# Wednesday Teaching Series

From the Claude memory export for the HighSignals brand project, 2026-09-06. The first concrete thing the vault knows about how the community actually runs.

## What it is

Weekly **Wednesday** teaching sessions for the HighSignals community, **running August through the end of the year**.

**Why it restarted:** Samuel had **paused community teaching to focus on video editing work**. This is the relaunch. That pause is the clearest evidence in any source of the tension named in [[brand-context]] — client work solves income and eats everything else.

## The curriculum

**20 topics across five thematic blocks:**

1. Why Visibility Matters
2. Clarity
3. Mindset
4. Consistency
5. Viral Strategies

**Action-oriented and workshop-style, not theory-heavy.**

Topics recorded in the source include: balancing content creation with full-time work · overcoming resource-based excuses · content planning · AI in the content workflow · scripting · ideation · storytelling.

**Scripnals was deliberately excluded from the curriculum** — it is not yet available to members, so teaching it would sell something nobody can use.

## The artefact

A clean curriculum document exists: a markdown file titled **"HighSignals Community — Wednesday Teaching Series"**, formatted for sharing directly with the community rather than as internal notes. It is not in this vault — it lives wherever Samuel saved it.
