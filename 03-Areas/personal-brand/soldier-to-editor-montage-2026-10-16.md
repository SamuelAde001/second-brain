## The reference reel

Posted 2026-02-25, caption *"Keep pursuing your dreams and be ruthless at it"*, 356 likes and 24 comments when checked 2026-10-16. Source file: `Instagram Samuel Signals\Edits\Keep pursuing your dreams and be ruthless at it.mp4` (720×1280, 68 s). The Resolve archive `My story, Miltary to entrepreneurship.dra` holds the project.

How it is built, from the file:
- **No voice-over.** One emotional song under the whole thing (Adele, *Skyfall*). The song carries the feeling.
- **12 chapters, one short caption each**, small white text in the upper third, 3-6 s per chapter, 2-4 photos or clips per chapter.
- Order: 2025 quit soldier job → started self-employed (empty room becomes a desk) → started working as a video editor → daily grind, sleepless nights → first USD 1,000 → started creating content → still felt like giving up → *"And then..."* → now living his dream life → first custom PC → 2026 starting Instagram from scratch → *"Lesson: Keep pursuing your dreams and be ruthless at it"*.
- The arc is the whole trick: rise, a dip at *"giving up"*, a pause on *"And then..."*, a payoff, a one-line lesson. No CTA.

Fact from his own caption in that reel: *"2025: Quit my job as a soldier"*.
