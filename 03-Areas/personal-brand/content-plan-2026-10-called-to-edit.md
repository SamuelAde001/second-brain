## Rules

1. **Goal is paid seats, not views.** Target about 200 at NGN 5,000; checkpoints in the playbook (30 by 10-10, 90 by 10-17, 150 by 10-21, 200 by 10-22). Follower count (5,000 by December) stays a secondary goal.
2. **Four Reels a week**, the same videos on TikTok. Stories don't count as posts but run every day.
3. **Reels only.** On his account Reels are 90.3% of views and feed posts 0.3%. Carousels get one slot: the webinar agenda. The playbook's two carousels a week are dropped.
4. **Each Reel has one job:** *Teach* (authority), *Story* (reach), *Proof* (trust), *Offer* (seats).
5. **Every Reel ends with one CTA.** Until the landing page exists: *"Comment PLAN and I'll send you the link"*, then Samuel (or a volunteer) DMs the Paystack link. A comment-to-DM turns a viewer into a conversation, and that is where seats are sold.
6. **Post in the morning.** His followers are most active 6am–12pm (peak 9am, on the Monday chart) and fall to a trough around 6pm. The old 7pm slot is the quietest hour. *Insights time zone assumed to be WAT; confirm.* Aim for 8–10am.
7. **Look the same.** One cover template ([[03-Areas/personal-brand/brand-guide|brand guide]] §4).

## Stories, every day (not counted as posts)

- A countdown sticker from Monday 10-05.
- A poll or question box once a week: *"What stops you getting clients?"* The answers are the webinar's hooks and the cohort's FAQ.
- Screenshots of real DMs and replies (with permission), proof clips, behind the scenes of building Called to Edit while working full time.
- A seats update, only if the number is real.

## The conversion engine (what actually sells seats)

1. **Comment PLAN → DM.** A reply with the link and one line of context. Answer within a day.
2. **Profile visits → registration.** Profile is the second step; the bio line and pinned Reels do the work. Today: 1,012 visits in 30 days, ~7 follows a day.
3. **Outreach.** The playbook's DMs, WhatsApp and communities do most of the seat-filling. Content gives people something to look at when a DM lands.
