# Creative director

Samuel onboarded a creative director on 2026-10-20, *"for my whole brand"*. She is a volunteer, like an intern: no salary. Instead he bought her a laptop on a loan, and the repayment is budgeted as her salary line ([[03-Areas/finances/obligations|obligations]], NGN 100,000/month, Nov 2026 – Apr 2027).

## What she's there for (his words, 2026-10-20)

- Edit his Shorts.
- Manage his social media pages.
- Come up with content ideas, and brainstorm plans for the brand with him.
- Have access to his AI and the Brand manager, to brainstorm from past plans.
- Make him accountable and push him to put in the work.

*"She would be working more with Brand manager later on."*
