# Ideas — personal-brand

The idea bank for this area. Every idea is dated and carries a status: **raw** / **shortlisted** / **used** / **dropped**. Used ideas link to what came of them. Dropped ideas say why — that is the part that stops the same bad idea coming back.

## The 30-story bank

All 30 entries below come from a single source document, "30 Stories — B-roll + Voiceover Series." Dated **2026-07** for every row — the month the source project was created. The source gives no exact day, so none is invented here. All 30 are unused: status **raw**, nothing recorded as shortlisted, used or dropped.

The source document's own framing, kept verbatim: *"Every idea below traces back to something Samuel actually said. Nothing invented. Each has a conflict and a resolution."* And: *"Titles say what the story is about. Hooks are drafts — narration-style openers, not slogans. Rewrite them in your own mouth before shooting."*

The source groups the 30 ideas into five themed sets. That grouping is preserved below as five separate tables rather than flattened into one, because the source uses it to organize the bank.

### Notes on the set

Kept from the source document because they govern how these ideas should be used, not just what they say:

- **#5, #13, #22, #30 have no clean resolution.** That's deliberate — they resolve into honesty rather than a lesson. Don't force a takeaway onto them; that's where guru tone creeps in.
- **Pairs from the same event:** #2/#3/#4/#29 all come from the near-quit. #6/#7 from the London client. #17/#18 from the corruption. #19/#20/#21 from Resolve. Shoot them apart in the calendar, not back to back.
- **Thin on wins.** 22 of these are struggles. Before the next batch, answer the "Still to mine" questions in the story bank — especially the mentor, the first international client, and the week after you left the military.
