# SOP — script review

**Trigger:** Samuel sends a script and wants it reviewed.
**Owner:** the content agent (Phase 4) — until then, whoever is asked.
**Last used:** unknown.

## Steps

Check the script against every line below, then hand back **what is weak and why**, followed by **a tightened version that keeps his tone and wording intact**.

- [ ] Does the hook state a clear stake, pain or dream in the first line or two?
- [ ] Is the hook bold without being dishonest or exaggerated?
- [ ] Does it follow Hook → Context → Conflict → Pivot → Resolution → Mantra, or a natural variation?
- [ ] Is there an obvious benefit to the viewer?
- [ ] Is the lesson or value clear — shown through the story, or stated directly if it is not obvious?
- [ ] Does the length fit the format? 60s tighter and punchier, 90s+ calmer talking-head storytelling.
- [ ] If Scripnals, the Academy or the community comes up, is it woven into the story rather than pushed as a hard sell?

## Done when

Samuel has both the criticism and the tightened draft. Not one without the other.

## Notes

The first two boxes are the two reasons his content flops, in his own ranking. Weight them accordingly.
