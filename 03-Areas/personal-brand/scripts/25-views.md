## Script

**HOOK**
25 views. 27. 28. 34. {his old Twitter posts with the view counts, from the webinar recording 1:47:06}
That's what my videos used to get.

**CONTEXT**
Back then, I was editing for my mentor. {him at the desk, side view}
And I asked him, how do I start earning in dollars?
He asked me a question back. Is your work anywhere people can see it? {close-up, thinking}
It wasn't.
So I opened a new Twitter account.
Instead of learning in private, I started learning in public. {typing, over the shoulder}

**CONFLICT**
Three posts a day. Timelines, time-lapses, practice edits. {timeline on screen, time-lapse of him working}
25 views. 27. 34.
Nobody was seeing me. {alone in the room, wide}
But I kept posting.

**PIVOT**
Then 100 views. Then 198. Then 600. {the counts climbing, from the recording}
And then one day, a DM. {phone lights up}
Someone who had been watching my posts.
Twenty dollars a video. My first dollar payment.
And years later, another DM. A company that had been watching my work, telling me how much they loved it. {reading on the phone, slow smile}
Today, they're my dream client.

**RESOLUTION**
Every client I've had, apart from referrals, came from those posts. {back at the desk, working}
The ones nobody was watching.
People watch what you do. People watch what you post.
Even when the views say they don't.

**CTA**
That's why, from today, I'm posting every single day for 60 days. {straight to camera, if he wants one face shot}
Follow along.
