# The Life of a Video Editor, Bad laptop

**Stage:** outline, 2026-09-23. Samuel writes the script from this. Kept for a later episode (Samuel, 2026-09-24: *"I would talk about the Network and the Computer issue in later videos"*); not straight after Ep 3, which is the same kind of story.
**Series:** [[03-Areas/personal-brand/series/life-of-a-video-editor|The Life of a Video Editor]]. On screen at the start, in bold: *The Life of a Video Editor, Episode N* (number set when it's scheduled). Target 1:30, up to 2:00.
**Bank:** #24, #19, #23 ([[03-Areas/personal-brand/personal-brand-ideas|ideas]]). **Leave out:** the military (see Open).

## Hooks (he picks one)

1. "My laptop crashed so many times, it made me hate video editing."
2. "The first money my current client paid me, I spent all of it on a PC."
3. "Right now, 200,000 naira cannot buy you a laptop that can edit properly."

## Outline

**Context**
- He bought his first laptop with his salary: an HP, NGN 150,000, *"when laptops were still cheap"*.
- Started straight on DaVinci Resolve. Simple videos worked.

**Conflict**
- Resolve was too powerful for it. Crashing and hanging, over and over. *"It has made me hate video editing because of that frustration."*
- Next, a gaming laptop, NGN 300,000. More power, but as the work grew it bottlenecked too.
- Then a NGN 900,000 laptop, bought with editing money. *"Really skyrocketed my career."* He used it for a long time.
- Even that one struggled with his current client's work: slow, always crashing.

**Pivot**
- The first money that client paid him went entirely on a PC build, last year (2025).

**Resolution**
- A year-plus on the PC. It handles AI work, 3D animation and heavy VFX that none of his laptops could.
- Today NGN 200,000 can't buy a laptop for proper editing; he checked. Even CapCut will hitch on one, *"but it's still a good place to start."*
- *"Video editors just have to manage what they have till they can get to where they want."*

## Kept, not in this outline

- He never edited on CapCut. He went straight to Resolve.
- The ladder: NGN 150,000 → NGN 300,000 → NGN 900,000 → a PC. It works as a carousel on its own.
