## Script

**HOOK**
Clients are not looking for skill. They're looking for experience. {On screen: "NOT SKILL. EXPERIENCE."}
And that's why a lot of good editors are still not getting paid.

**WHY ME**
I used to tell people I had five years of experience. {punch in}
Out of those five years, three years was mostly me working for free.
At most, I was earning 25,000 naira.
But no one knows that.

**THE MEAT**
You see, when a client wants to hire you, they don't just ask what you can do. {back out}
They ask, how long have you been doing this? Who have you worked with?
And here's the thing. It doesn't matter whether you worked with them for free.
It's still experience.
Our mindset is, I'm not perfect enough for people to pay me.
But how would you get perfect if you're not editing many videos?

**HOW**
So go to a small creator, or a small business close to you, and say: {On screen: the ask as a DM}
"Sir, please, I want to edit your videos for free. You don't need to post it. Just give me your raw talking head, let me be editing it."
Set a time. One month, or five videos.
And don't tell them you're learning. Tell them you're building your portfolio.
When it ends, if you did a good job, they won't want to lose you.

**EXAMPLE**
That's exactly how I got paid my first 100,000 naira. {punch in}
I was editing for my mentor for free, and he sent me the client.
I can never forget that client.

**TAKEAWAY**
So free work is not wasted work. It's experience. {back out}
And experience is what gets you paid.

**CTA**
Follow if you want to make money through video editing.
