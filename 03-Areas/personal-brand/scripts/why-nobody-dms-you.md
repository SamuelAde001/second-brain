1. "If no client has ever DMed you to edit their videos, this is why." *(used)*

3. "Clients won't DM you until these three things happen."

## Script

**HOOK**
If no client has ever DMed you to edit their videos, this is why. {On screen: an empty DM inbox}

**WHY ME**
Every client I've had, from when I started till now, apart from referrals, came from Twitter. {punch in}
I didn't chase most of them. They came to me.

**THE MEAT**
Before a client DMs you, three things have to happen. {back out}
They have to know you. They have to like you. And they have to trust you.
First, they need to know you. That means you're visible. You're posting. {On screen: KNOW}
And an editor posting every day will beat you posting once a week.
And then, they need to like you. {On screen: LIKE}
A client might know you're a video editor, but if they don't like you, they won't reach out.
So show the person behind the work. Your face. Your personality. Your behind the scenes.
Even you sitting down for five hours editing a video tells a client you're reliable.
And lastly, they need to trust you. {On screen: TRUST}
That's your past work. The videos you've done for people, even the free ones.

**EXAMPLE**
And you won't have what to post if you're not doing the work. {punch in}
There's no week I don't edit videos, even till now.

**TAKEAWAY**
So know, like, trust. {back out}
Miss one, and that DM doesn't come.

**CTA**
Follow if you want to make money through video editing.
