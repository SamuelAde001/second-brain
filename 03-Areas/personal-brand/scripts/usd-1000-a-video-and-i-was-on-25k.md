## Script

**HOOK**
Some editors were charging 1,000 dollars for one video. {scrolling a phone, close-up}
I was earning 25,000 naira a month.

**CONTEXT**
For two years, I edited for my mentor. {old photo of him, or him at the desk at night}
About four videos a month, for 25,000 naira. For data and expenses.
I was still in the military. Days in uniform, editing at night. {desk lamp, late, tired face}
And I didn't complain, because it was making me better.

**CONFLICT**
Then one day on Twitter, I saw editors charging 1,000 dollars a video. {phone screen, slow push in}
One video.
That was the day I knew something was wrong with my career. {still, staring at the screen}
And I knew there was something around the business that I didn't know.

**PIVOT**
So I locked in for six months. {getting up, going to the PC}
I paid 60 dollars from my salary to join a community that taught the business of editing.
I saw editors there earning 10,000 dollars a month, and I asked them what they were doing differently. {typing a DM}
And I started learning how to pitch myself.

**RESOLUTION**
Then my first international client. Twenty dollars a video. Then thirty. Then sixty. {working, focused, side view}
What I didn't know in three years, I knew in less than six months.
The skill was never the problem. {wide, him at the desk}
I just didn't know the business.

**CTA**
Follow if you want to make money through video editing.
