# The Life of a Video Editor, Ep 4: Bad network

**Stage:** outline, 2026-09-23. Samuel writes the script from this.
**Series:** [[03-Areas/personal-brand/series/life-of-a-video-editor|The Life of a Video Editor]]. On screen at the start, in bold: *The Life of a Video Editor, Episode 4*. Target 1:30, up to 2:00.
**Bank:** #25, #23 ([[03-Areas/personal-brand/personal-brand-ideas|ideas]]). **Leave out:** the military (see Open), the mentor's name.

## Hooks (he picks one)

1. "A client sent me 40 gigs. I was on mobile data. It downloaded all night and into the next morning."
2. "I've turned down client work because the files were too big for my data."
3. "When a download fails on Nigerian data, you start it again. And you pay for the data again."

## Outline

**Context**
- MTN, Airtel, Glo: he's been on all three. *"Irrespective of the network, it is bad."*
- Starting out, there's no money for the expensive routers. He spent NGN 10,000 a month on data (*"right now 10,000 a month cannot do anything"*), later NGN 20,000, only because he had another income. Most starting editors don't.
- Client files run to 30 or 50 gigs.

**Conflict**
- One 40GB download ran all night and into the next morning.
- Downloads failed halfway, again and again. Restart it and pay for the data again, while already short on data.
- He turned down client gigs because the files were too big.
- Sometimes the client wasn't even paying enough to cover it.
- His trick was downloading at night, when it's faster.

**Pivot**
- He moved into his mentor's house. His mentor already had Starlink.

**Resolution**
- 80–90GB now takes about 3–4 hours.
- *"Whenever I look at other video editors that don't have fast internet, I actually pity them… I can relate hugely."*
- The honest part: it's his mentor's Starlink, not his. When he moves into his own place, he'll need his own. *"This Starlink has really changed my life."*

## Kept, not in this outline

- The 20,000 was affordable because he was still in the military and earning a salary.
