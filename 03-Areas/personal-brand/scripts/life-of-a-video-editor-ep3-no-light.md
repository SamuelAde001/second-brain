# The Life of a Video Editor, Ep 3: No light

**Stage:** outline, 2026-09-23. Samuel writes the script from this. Goes live 2026-09-23, before 8:00pm WAT.
**Series:** [[03-Areas/personal-brand/series/life-of-a-video-editor|The Life of a Video Editor]]. On screen at the start, in bold: *The Life of a Video Editor, Episode 3*. Nothing spoken. Target 1:30, up to 2:00.
**Bank:** #16, #23 ([[03-Areas/personal-brand/personal-brand-ideas|ideas]]). **Leave out:** the military, the city.

## Hooks (he picks one)

1. "Sometimes I had two hours of light in a whole day. And I had client work to do."
2. "I used to carry my laptop to the Aboki corner, where people pay to charge their phones."
3. "I lost international clients before I ever worked with them. Because there was no light."

## Outline

**Context**
- Four years ago, when he started video editing.
- Some days, two hours of light in 24 hours. Client work still due.

**Conflict**
- The worst day: a client project he couldn't finish, waiting for light.
- Carried the laptop to the "Aboki corner", where people pay to charge phones.
- Worked from restaurants and bought food there, just to get light.
- Bought a small generator out of frustration. Loud, noisy.
- The real cost: international clients sent trial tasks he couldn't do. Lost gigs. *"They just expect that you always have light."*
- His existing clients understood. His mentor was one of the major ones.

**Pivot**
- His mentor helped him get a small power station, about NGN 300,000–400,000, enough to power the laptop. *"That was my first escape."* *"A life changer."*

**Resolution**
- Power station → more work → international clients → solar, later.
- Now he's gone a week, even two weeks, with no grid light (rain brings a line down, the national grid collapses) and kept working.
- *"Nigerian electricity is just bad… frustratingly bad. And it is costing a lot of Nigerians a lot of proper opportunities."*
- To the editor who can't afford solar yet: *"A power station is very, very important for a video editor."* No brand named.

## Kept, not in this outline
