## Script (about 90 to 110 seconds, casual pace)

**HOOK**
"I didn't call it Learn to Edit. I called it Called to Edit. And there's a reason."
{close, sitting relaxed, small smile}

**WHAT IT IS**
"So a lot of you have been seeing Called to Edit on my page, and some of you have been asking me, Samuel, what exactly is this thing? Let me just talk to you.
Called to Edit is for video editors. Not people learning editing just for fun. Editors who want to actually build a career out of it."

**WHAT THE NAME MEANS**
"Now, the name. For me, 'called' means two things. First, I believe this is what God called me to do. I've been editing for more than five years, and this skill has changed my life.
And then, it's a calling to the craft. If you're that person who just can't stop editing, even when nobody is paying you yet... you might be called to edit too."
{B-roll: him at the desk, timeline open, leaning back; matched to the calm, not the words}

**WHY I STARTED IT**
"So why did I start it? Everybody is teaching you this effect, that effect, this transition. And that's fine. But nobody is teaching you how to actually get paid for it.
Learning to edit is like two percent of the work. The remaining ninety-eight percent is everything outside the skill. Finding clients, knowing what to charge. And that's the part that took me years to figure out on my own. I didn't buy any course.
So that's what Called to Edit is about. I'll still teach you editing, but I'm teaching you how to build a career with it. I want to be that guy that helps video editors actually succeed in this thing."
{punch in on "nobody is teaching you how to actually get paid for it"}

**WHAT WE'RE DOING**
"We just had our first Called to Edit webinar. Almost 80 editors, three and a half hours. Omo, it was something else.
And this is just the start. Right now, the big thing is the Called to Edit Community. From November 1st, we're doing a 60-day challenge. Every day you post what you did to move your career forward, and I'll be right there guiding you. Go at your own pace, nobody is forcing anybody.
The Academy is coming soon. And one day, by God's grace, I want us to fill a room for a physical Called to Edit event."
{screen: the Meet grid from the webinar recording, names blurred; then a slow scroll through the Discord server}

**CTA**
"So if you're serious about making video editing your career, come join us. It's 5,000 naira a month, and the link is in my bio. And if you join after November 1st, no wahala, you start your own day one.
Come, let's build this thing together."
{back to close, hold a beat before the cut}

**For a 60-second cut:** drop the webinar line and the Academy / physical event lines. The video still runs name → why → community → CTA.

## Review (script-review-checklist)

- **Voice:** his own lines carry it: "this effect, that effect", "actually get paid", "two percent / ninety-eight percent", "I didn't buy any course", "that guy that helps video editors succeed". No triads, no slogan turns. Two Pidgin touches ("Omo", "no wahala"); cut one if it feels like too much.
- **Hook:** true and paid off (the name section answers it straight away). Weak spot: it's a curiosity hook, not a pain hook. If the first second needs more stake, hook 2 is the pain version.
- **Stakes and lesson:** an explainer, not a story, so the stake is the viewer's: editing well and still not getting paid. The lesson is the 2% / 98% line. It's the strongest line in the video; it's punched in on purpose.
- **No guru tone:** no promises of income, no "I have it all figured out".
- **Overlap:** the CTA repeats the [[03-Areas/personal-brand/scripts/webinar-recap-community-announcement|webinar recap]] announcement. Fine if they go out days apart; this one explains the brand, that one tells the webinar story.
