## Script

**HOOK**
Stop telling people you're a video editor. {On screen: ~~"I'M A VIDEO EDITOR"~~}
That sentence is costing you clients.

**WHY**
Because there are millions of video editors. {punch in}
Movie editors, podcast editors, TikTok editors, visual effects editors.
So when you say "I'm a video editor", the client still doesn't know if you're the one they need.
And generalists will always lose to specialists.
Think about it. A specialist doctor earns more than a general doctor, right?

**THE MEAT**
So instead, say what you do in one sentence. {back out}
First, who you help. A real person, with an occupation. Fitness coaches. Podcast creators. SaaS companies. {On screen: WHO}
And then, the result you get them. There must be a promise. More views, more sales. {On screen: RESULT}
And lastly, how you do it. {On screen: HOW}
So it becomes: {On screen, the full sentence}
"I help fitness coaches drive more sales through well-created engaging videos for their Instagram."
Do you see me saying "I'm a video editor"? No.
But now your editing has a purpose.

**EXAMPLE**
Mine is: I'm a long-form video editor that edits business videos for YouTubers. {punch in}
And I practise it. For my clients, I only edit long form.

**TAKEAWAY**
So put that sentence everywhere. Your bio, your banner, your pitch. {back out}
Be the editor for one kind of person.
That's how they choose you over the millions.

**CTA**
Follow if you want to make money through video editing.
