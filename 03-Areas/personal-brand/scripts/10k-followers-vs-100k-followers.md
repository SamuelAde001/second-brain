# T4 · 10,000 followers vs 100,000 followers: who pays you more (draft 1, 2026-10-29)

Week 1 teaching, seated, labels flipping on screen. Built from [[05-Knowledge/teachings/teachings-05-choosing-a-niche|Choosing a niche]]: the follower trap (*"A business guy can have 10,000 followers"* and possibly make USD 100,000 a month; an entertainer with 100,000 may not afford USD 500 or even USD 100 a video), *"they are mostly broke"*, *"In any niche, business guys pay more"*, live [0:32:13] *"most of us learn the skill of video editing that is only meant for entertainment guys"*, [0:48:16] business people *"literally budget for marketing"*, [0:53:05] finance videos pay well and aren't hard to edit. Format: **A vs B**. No voice-note ramble yet. Week: [[03-Areas/personal-brand/content-weeks/content-week-2026-11-01|week 1]].

About 250 words, roughly 1:30.

## Hooks

1. "This creator has 100,000 followers. This one has 10,000. Which one can pay you more?" *(used)*
2. "A creator with 10,000 followers can pay you more than one with 100,000."
3. "Most of us learn editing for the people who can't pay us."

## Script

**HOOK**
This creator has 100,000 followers. {On screen: 100K} This one has 10,000. {On screen: 10K}
Which one do you think can pay you more as a video editor?
Most people will say the 100k guy.
Wrong.

**CONTEXT**
I asked this same question at my webinar. {punch in}
And the answer is, it depends on what they do.

**A vs B**
{Label: ENTERTAINER · 100K}
The entertainer lives on brand deals, sponsorships and views.
To be honest, most of them are broke.
He may not even afford 500 dollars a video. Or even 100.
{Label: BUSINESS GUY · 10K}
The business guy is selling something to those 10,000 followers.
He could be making 100,000 dollars a month from them.
And business people already understand marketing. They literally budget for it.
And editing is part of marketing.

**THE PROBLEM**
But here's the problem. {back out}
Most of us learn the skill of video editing that is only meant for entertainment guys.
Their videos are hard to edit, they can't pay, and then we wonder why we can't build a career.
Meanwhile, finance videos pay well, and they're not even hard to edit.

**TAKEAWAY**
So in any niche, business guys pay more. {punch in}
SaaS, AI, real estate, anyone selling a product.
Don't look at the followers. Look at what they're selling. {On screen: "LOOK AT WHAT THEY SELL"}

**CTA**
Follow if you want to make money through video editing.
