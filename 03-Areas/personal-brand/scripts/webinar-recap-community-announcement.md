In his words: *"how the webinar was such a success and how we are now moving to the next step, which is to bring people into the community and keep them accountable to actually doing the work"* · *"this video is majorly more of an announcement to people that want to join my community"*. ## Review of Samuel's draft 2

**Works:** a real underdog arc, in order: "who would even pay attention to me" → only 5 paid → "Omo, is this how this thing is going to flop?" → the military video → the room fills. The "Omo" line is the most honest line in it. The pivot is a real event.

**Weak:**
1. **The hook is generic.** "I never expected what happened" gives no stake. The stake is the 5 payers, sitting in the middle. Move it into the hook.
2. **"Blew up" twice.** The early-October video blowing up isn't in the content log (his next best Reel after the 126K one is 11.8K). Two blow-ups make the real turning point land softer. Save "blew up" for the military video and give its number.
3. **"More than 80 paid participants."** The Brain has 78 seat holders (the thank-you email went to 78). "Almost 80" keeps the hook honest (non-negotiable 2) unless more paid after. "They all stayed": he must confirm; three and a half hours is on record.
4. **The webinar itself gets one line.** It's the payoff, and where the testimonials go on screen.
5. **The announcement is missing.** No 60-day challenge, no November 1st, no daily check-in, no price. "If that interests you" is a weak ask.
6. **No lesson for the viewer who won't join.** The story already has one (he nearly stopped at 5), so say it in one line.
7. **English:** first every → first ever · I build → I built · so much people → so many people · give a try → give it a try · what if you share → what if I share · DM's → DMs.

## Tightened version (about 80 to 90 seconds)

**HOOK**
"When I first announced my paid webinar, only 5 people paid. I never expected what happened next."
{talking head, close}

**CONTEXT**
"You see, at the beginning of this month, I posted a video about how I've been editing videos for 5 years. A lot of people saw it, and I got so many questions. People asking me how to actually get clients as a video editor. And I realised, this is something I know how to do very well. So an idea came to me: what if I share how to build a career in video editing?"

**STRUGGLE**
"At first I shrugged it off. Like, who would even pay attention to me? But on second thought, I decided to give it a try. I built a brand around it, called Called to Edit, and launched my first paid webinar. At first announcement, only 5 people paid. Omo, I was like, is this how this thing is going to flop?"
{B-roll: him checking his phone, face}

**PIVOT**
"Then I posted a video about how I quit my job in the military to become a full-time video editor. And bro, it blew up. Over 120,000 views. People started flooding my DMs, and the sign-ups kept increasing. I was shocked."
{screen: the Reel with its view count; DMs scrolling, names blurred}

**PAYOFF**
"Then the day of the webinar came. Almost 80 people paid, and we went for three and a half hours. [FILL: "and they all stayed", only if true.] And after the webinar, so many people came to my DMs telling me how much they had learned."
{screen: the Google Meet grid from the recording, names blurred; then 3 to 4 testimonials, each held long enough to read}

**LESSON**
"Honestly, I didn't expect it to come out this good for my first main project. If I had stopped at 5 people, none of this would have happened."
(The second sentence is the Brand manager's suggestion; keep it or cut it.)

**NEXT STEP**
"And a lot of people told me they're ready for the next level. So now I'm taking the next step. I want to build the best video editing community in Nigeria. Where instead of teaching people tips and tricks, I train video editors on how to actually get paid for their skill."
"On November 1st, we're starting a 60-day challenge inside the Called to Edit Community. Sixty days of getting clients, learning how to pitch yourself and present yourself, and getting paid. Every day you post what you did, and I'll be there guiding you."
{screen: scroll through the Discord server}

**CTA**
"It's 5,000 naira a month, and the link is in my bio. Join us, let's build your career in video editing."
