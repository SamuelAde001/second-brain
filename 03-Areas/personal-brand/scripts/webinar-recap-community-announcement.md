## Concepts pitched

1. **The 126K ad → the room** (recommended). Opens on a video he's already proved people watch, then shows what happened when they showed up.
2. **Cold open on his own line.** The recording clip at 1:04:31, *"Your video editing skill is useless if it is not aimed at a result"*, then cut to him at home. Used inside concept 1 as the turning point instead.
3. **"Okay, what next?"** Opens on a real attendee message asking what comes after. Only works if such a message exists.

## Script, concept 1 (about 75 to 90 seconds)

**HOOK**
{screen: the webinar ad Reel playing, the view count visible}
"This video got over 120,000 views. It was the ad for my first webinar ever."
{cut to Samuel, talking head}
"[FILL: what went through your head before Friday, in your words. E.g. were you scared nobody would pay, or that you'd freeze on camera?]"

**CONTEXT**
"Last Friday, 7pm, we went live. I taught for three and a half hours. Editors on their phones, editors on laptops, some people who have never had one client."
{B-roll: the Google Meet grid from the OBS recording, faces visible, names blurred; Samuel at his desk mid-teaching}

**THE PROOF**
"And after it, these are some of the messages I got."
{screen: 3 to 4 real messages, names blurred, each held long enough to read}
"[FILL: react to one in your own words. Which message hit you the most, and why?]"

**CONFLICT**
"But I noticed something. Most of the messages said the same thing: I'm motivated now."
"And I know how that goes. You're motivated on Friday. By next week, life comes back, and you're back to watching tutorials. No client. Nothing posted."

**PIVOT**
{clip: the recording at 1:04:31}
"I said it on the webinar: your video editing skill is useless if it is not aimed at a result. Knowing what to do is not the result. Doing it is."

**RESOLUTION: the community**
"So that's the next step. I've opened the Called to Edit Community. It's on Discord. We have a live call every week, you get feedback on your work, and you're around editors going the same way as you."
{screen: scroll through the Discord server, channel list and #general}
"[FILL, optional: how many people have joined so far, only if you want the number public.]"

**THE 60-DAY CHALLENGE**
"And on November 1st we start a 60-day challenge. Sixty days of building your career as a video editor: getting clients, getting paid, learning how to pitch yourself and how to present yourself. Every day you post what you did, and I'll be there guiding you."
"You go at your own pace. Nobody is going to force you. But when you see other people putting in the work every day, you'll want to do it too."

**CTA**
"It's 5,000 naira a month. The link is in my bio."
"[OPTIONAL: If you were at the webinar, your 2,000 naira link still works till Friday.]"
"If you join after November 1st, your day one starts the day you join. But I'd love you to start with us."
