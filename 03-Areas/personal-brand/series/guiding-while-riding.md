# Series — Guiding While Riding

Series reference dated 2026-08-11. **Status unverified:** the doc says this series replaces the 3×/week talking-head slot "for now". Whether it ever launched, and how many episodes exist, is not recorded anywhere in the vault yet.

## Concept

Recorded while riding a bicycle. A "yapping" format, but value-driven rather than freeform commentary. The bike is a deliberate attention-grabber — it stands out from the sea of talking-head content, and movement while talking is more engaging than a static shot.

**Core focus:** how to get opportunities, jobs and clients, especially in tech and creative work. Aimed at young people — particularly recent university graduates trying to make money online and land international clients or jobs.

## Structure

**Hook → Mantra → Tip → Steps → CTA**

- **Hook** — bold, pain-point-driven opening. No guru tone, no exaggeration.
- **Mantra** — episode marker, right after the hook, not at the end. Same placement as "Called to Create".
- **Tip** — one line naming what this ride teaches. Only needed if the hook has not already made it obvious.
- **Steps** — 2–3 concrete, doable actions. Most of the runtime lives here.
- **CTA** — closing line, every episode.

## Fixed lines

Mantra, stylised in caps like his other series branding:

> "Welcome to GUIDING WHILE RIDING, Episode X — [what this episode covers]"

Base line:

> "Welcome to Guiding While Riding — a series where I show you how to get opportunities online."

CTA, every episode:

> "Follow me so you don't miss our next ride."

## Topic bank

Open-ended; new topics can be added any time. Suggested order, grouped into phases so early viewers get a coherent path. Not a rigid rule — reorder whenever a hook calls for it.

**Phase 1 — Mindset & foundation:** thinking like an entrepreneur · choosing a field · solving Nigeria's limitations · personal discipline · time management · being patient

**Phase 2 — Building the skill:** where to learn · getting the right tools for your skill · learning from others · getting a mentor · use of AI · working for free to build expertise

**Phase 3 — Proof & presence:** building your portfolio · building a personal brand · visibility · building in public · using social media to your advantage · telling everyone about your skill

**Phase 4 — Selling yourself:** knowing how to sell yourself · learning sales · learning marketing · pricing yourself

**Phase 5 — Getting clients:** warm outreach · outreach by email · reaching out to clients · running ads · getting rich clients

**Phase 6 — Growing through people:** networking · going to real-life events · joining other communities · building a community

## Naming

Landed on "Guiding While Riding" after considering: Providing While Riding, Confiding While Riding, Striding While Riding, Gliding While Riding, Deciding While Riding.
