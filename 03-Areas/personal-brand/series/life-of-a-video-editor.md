# Series — The Life of a Video Editor

**The best-performing thing Samuel has made.** Confirmed by him 2026-09-20.

## What it is

A **storytelling B-roll style series** about the life of a video editor. Two episodes exist as of 2026-09-20.

## Why it matters more than anything else in this area

It is the identified cause of the only growth the Brain can measure:

| Platform | 2026-08-26 | 2026-09-20 |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 460 | **671** |
| TikTok | 550 | **673** |

His attribution, in his own words: *"I grew very fast because of my new Storytelling B-roll style series, and more focused on video editing content."*

**Both episodes "got good views."** No numbers recorded — see needs-input.

Everything else audience-facing that was running in the same period stopped: the Wednesday community teaching ended as *"no longer relevant to my audience"*, and the CapCut course paused. **This series is what the audience answered to.**

## Production, as it stands

- **B-roll is shot per episode.** Nothing is reused yet.
- **He intends to reuse B-roll from it in future** — which means the shot library is an asset that compounds, and episode 3 should cost less to make than episode 2 if it is organised for reuse.
- Recording setup, LUT workflow and audio chain: [[recording-setup]].

## Cadence

**Once a week**, currently. His own words on what changes that:

> "That cadence would improve once I speed up my video editing for my client using Claude."

That is the whole system in one sentence — see [[05-Knowledge/the-constraint-chain|The constraint chain]].
