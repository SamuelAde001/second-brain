## Hook options (his words, to rewrite in his voice)

1. *"I decided to become a video editor before I had a computer to edit on."* His own hook from the story bank. Real stake, honest.
2. *"In the last two years, I've edited over $20,000 worth of videos. Five years ago I couldn't even afford a laptop."* Proof first. Needs him to confirm the second sentence.
3. *"I left the military to edit videos. This is what the last 5 years paid me."* Stake in the first line.

Rule: the hook states a real stake and never exaggerates ([[03-Areas/personal-brand/script-review-checklist|checklist]]). Brand and webinar name come **after** the value, not before ([[03-Areas/personal-brand/brand-context|brand context]], *Brand after proof*).

## Structure (Hook, Context, Conflict, Pivot, Resolution, CTA)

| Beat | What goes here | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Locked from the options above | 0:00-0:07 |
| Context | Who he is, where (Abuja), the military, deciding to edit with no computer | ~20s |
| Conflict | Network, light, no computer. The early client that was hard. One spine struggle, picked by him | ~25s |
| Pivot | Posting his process in public, the DM, Resolve | ~20s |
| Resolution | Where 5 years got him: 500+ videos, international clients, over USD 20,000 in 2 years | ~20s |
| Turn | *"Now I'm giving everything I learned back."* What Called to Edit is, in one line | ~10s |
| CTA | One line, one action | ~5s |

About 100 to 110 seconds. One shoot, two cuts: the story cut for the feed, a 30 to 40 second cut for ads.

## How it is cut (the Editor's side)

- Voice-over or talking head, the Osmo setup from [[03-Areas/personal-brand/recording-setup|recording setup]]. VO cut by the waveform first, then mistakes ([[03-Areas/video-editing/sops/routerise-cut-workflow|cut workflow]]).
- B-roll is him at ordinary work-from-home life, matched to the feeling and never to the literal words. Proof overlays only for real things: the DM screenshot, the Resolve timeline, a Route Rise frame (his OK first).
- Bold captions on the hook and big lines. One cover template ([[03-Areas/personal-brand/brand-guide|brand guide]] section 4). Motion graphics follow [[03-Areas/video-editing/sops/fusion-build|the Fusion SOP]].

## Questions waiting

4. May he name Route Rise and the 1M+ YouTuber, or say "a foreign company"?
5. Talking head, voice-over with B-roll, or a mix? When can he shoot today?
