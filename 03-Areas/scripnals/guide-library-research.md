# Guide library: research and sources

The research behind the [[03-Areas/scripnals/guides/guide-library|guide library]], done 2026-09-22. Each guide entry lists the codes below in its `refs:` field, so any rule can be traced to where it came from.

**Rank of sources** (AGENTS.md rule 7): Samuel's own method (S codes) outranks everything. Instagram's own words (I codes) come next. Studies and creator frameworks (R codes) are suggestions. Craft rules with no single source are marked **G** and are the main session's suggestion until Samuel confirms them.

**What this research could not do:** watch Reels. Instagram blocks logged-out browsing and no model can watch video here. So "what works" comes from what Instagram says it measures, one large study, and creators who study hooks for a living, not from viewing top posts. The best next input is transcripts of 10–20 Reels Samuel rates as strong in each target niche (open question 75).

## Instagram's own words

| Code | Source | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| I1 | [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained), Instagram, 2023-05-31 | Reels ranking uses the viewer's activity, their history with the creator, the reel's content and popularity, and creator popularity. Watermarked reels get less reach. |
| I2 | [Recommendations and originality](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/recommendations-and-originality), Instagram Creators, 2024-04-30 | Only original content with no visible watermarks is recommended. Accounts reposting 10+ times in 30 days stop being recommended. **Not recommended:** clickbait, engagement bait, exaggerated health or money claims, unoriginal repurposed content. New reels go to a small audience first and widen if they perform. |
| I3 | [Mosseri's top signals](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/), Social Media Today, 2025-01-22 | *"The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends."* Likes weigh more with followers, sends more with non-followers. Watch time counts seconds watched, not only the percentage. |
| I4 | [Instagram's tips for recommendations](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-reels-recommendations-tips/741030/), Social Media Today on the Instagram Creators blog, 2025-02-26 | Post regularly. **Make reels self-contained**, so a stranger understands them. Use audio. Keep experimenting. |
| I5 | [Skip rate](https://metricool.com/instagram-reel-analytics/), Metricool on Instagram's Insights, 2025 | Instagram now shows **skip rate**: the share of viewers who swipe away in the first 3 seconds. Mosseri: the first three seconds are "super important". There is no official benchmark. |
| I6 | [Engagement bait](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait/), Meta Transparency Center | Meta demotes posts that ask for reactions, comments with set words, shares, votes or tags as a trick. Asking for real help or advice isn't bait. |

**One tension for Samuel:** Meta's list includes "comment with specific words" and "share with your friends". Two very common Instagram CTAs sit close to that line: *"Comment GUIDE and I'll send it"* and *"Send this to a friend who…"*. The guides allow both only when the viewer gets something real (a resource that exists, or content that truly helps the friend named), never as a trick. Open question 76.

## Studies and creator frameworks (suggestions)

| Code | Source | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | [Socialinsider Reels length study](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/), 2026-07-14 | 6 million brand Reels, January–June 2026. **45–60 s** had the highest engagement (0.35%) and median views (10,374, vs 4,700 under 30 s). Over 3 minutes dropped. *"A wide usable window rather than one single 'correct' length."* Brand accounts only. |
| R2 | [Kallaway: 6 hook archetypes](https://www.content.game/p/hooks), 2025-03-14 | Fortune teller, Experimenter, Teacher, Magician, Investigator, Contrarian. Hooks work through context → contrast. Any idea fits any archetype; the angle is the choice. |
| R3 | [Kallaway: hook alignment](https://x.com/kanekallaway/status/1912316912198943206), 2025 | The visual hook and the on-screen text matter more than the spoken hook, because eyes read before ears hear. All three must say the same thing. His four rules: alignment, speed to value, clarity, curiosity. |
| R4 | [Parker and Stone, NYU 2014](https://speakola.com/arts/matt-stone-trey-parker-nyu-writing-class-2014) | Beats joined by "and then" make a weak story. Join them with "but" or "therefore". |
| R5 | [Speaking speed](https://www.teleprompter.com/tools/speaking-speed-calculator) and [short-form script length](https://goteleprompter.com/blog/video-script-length-guide/) | Conversational speech runs about 125–150 words a minute. Energetic short-form runs 170–200. 150 words ≈ 60 s. |
| R6 | [OpusClip on Reels hooks](https://www.opus.pro/blog/instagram-reels-hook-formulas) | Curiosity, promise and pattern-interrupt hooks. Long intros ("hi, I'm…", "in this video") fail. Text on the first frame helps sound-off viewers. |
| R7 | [Inro: Reels engagement](https://www.inro.social/guides/increase-instagram-reels-engagement) | One call to action per reel. "Comment a keyword" plus a DM automation turns views into leads for businesses. |

## What changed in the library because of this research

1. **Hooks are judged hardest**, because the first 3 seconds now have their own metric (I5) and watch time is the top signal (I3).
2. **"Would someone send this?"** is a test in reviews, because sends are the top signal for reaching non-followers (I3).
3. **Self-contained** is a standing rule (I4).
4. **No clickbait, no engagement bait, no exaggerated health or money claims**, because Instagram stops recommending them (I2, I6). The AI never strengthens a claim.
5. **Visual, on-screen text and spoken hook must agree** (R3). This is why the library adds a **format** input.
6. **Default length stays 60 s / 150 words** for `shorten`. R1 supports 45–60 s and R5 the word count.
