Remove filler, repeats and weak lines. Change nothing else: order, hook and tone stay.
REMOVE: warm-ups ("so basically", "hey guys", "in this video"). Empty fillers ("like", "you know", "literally", "actually", "just", "really") where they add nothing. Hedges on points they clearly believe ("kind of", "I guess"). Lines that repeat the one before. Promises never delivered.
KEEP: catchphrases and personality words, deliberate repeats, an honest "I think" in an opinion, asides that are the joke.
Delete, don't reword. If a line only works reworded, leave it and mention it.
