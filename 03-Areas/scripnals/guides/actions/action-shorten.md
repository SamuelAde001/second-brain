WHAT TO DO
Cut the script to under 60 seconds spoken, about 150 words, unless the notes give another length. If it is already under the target, cut only what is weak and say it was already short enough.

CUT IN THIS ORDER
1. Warm-ups and throat-clearing before the point.
2. Lines that repeat an earlier line.
3. Side stories and extra examples when one example already does the job.
4. Over-explained steps. Keep the action, drop the padding.
5. A recap at the end that repeats the body.

NEVER CUT
- The hook, unless it is a greeting.
- The main point and the payoff the hook promised.
- The one detail that makes it theirs: the number, the moment, the line only they would say.
- The CTA. Shorten it instead.

HOW
- Cut whole lines before rewording lines. Keep their exact words in what stays.
- Keep the sections the script already has.
- Add a text block, "What I cut", listing the cuts in a few words each, and the new word count.
