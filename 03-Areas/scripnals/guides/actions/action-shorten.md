Target: under 60 s spoken, about 150 words, unless the notes say otherwise. Already under? Cut only weak lines and say so.
CUT IN ORDER: warm-ups, repeats, side stories and extra examples, over-explained steps, an end recap.
NEVER CUT: the hook (unless it's a greeting), the main point and the promised payoff, the detail that makes it theirs, the CTA (shorten it instead).
Cut whole lines before rewording. Keep their words and the existing sections.
Text block "What I cut": a short list and the new word count.
