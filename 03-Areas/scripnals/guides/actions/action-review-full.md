HOW TO SCORE
Five areas, 0 to 20 each. Score what is on the page, not what it could become. Be honest: a high score the script has not earned helps nobody.

HOOK
17-20: the first line makes the topic and the reason to stay clear within about 3 seconds, is specific, is true, and sounds like the user.
11-16: the topic is clear but the line is slow, vague or gives no reason to stay.
6-10: the hook comes after a warm-up, or is generic.
0-5: no hook. It opens with a greeting, a name or "in this video".

CLARITY
17-20: one main idea. A stranger follows it first time. Every line is easy to say out loud.
11-16: clear overall, with one confusing or overloaded part.
6-10: two ideas compete, or steps are missing.
0-5: the point is hard to find.

AUDIENCE FIT
17-20: speaks straight to the profile's dream client or follower, in their words, about what they care about.
11-16: right topic, but the benefit to the viewer is not obvious.
6-10: about the user more than the viewer, or aimed at "everyone".
0-5: wrong audience.

PACING
17-20: the length suits the idea, every line moves forward, the payoff lands on time.
11-16: one slow patch or repeat.
6-10: long setup, repeats, or the payoff comes too late.
0-5: drags from start to end.

CALL TO ACTION
17-20: one clear ask that fits the goal and the video, or a deliberate strong ending.
11-16: an ask that works but is generic or bolted on.
6-10: several asks, or a vague one.
0-5: missing, or engagement bait.

THE FIXES
- 3 to 5 fixes, biggest first. The hook comes first if it scored under 15.
- Each fix quotes the exact line, says what goes wrong for the viewer, and gives the fix in the user's voice.
- Also check: would someone send this to a friend? Is any claim exaggerated? Did anything unclear need a question instead of a guess?
- You may add one short text block, "Keep these", quoting 1 or 2 lines that already work, so the user knows what not to change.
