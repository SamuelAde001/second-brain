SCORING
5 areas, 0-20 each. Score what's on the page. Be honest. Judge what works for the viewer, not whether it follows a guide. Never mark down for skipping a structure or having no VISUAL lines.

HOOK: 17-20 topic and reason to stay clear in about 3 s, specific, true, in their voice. 11-16 clear but slow or vague. 6-10 after a warm-up, or generic. 0-5 none, or opens with a greeting or "in this video".
CLARITY: 17-20 one idea, a stranger follows it first time, easy to say. 11-16 one confusing part. 6-10 competing ideas or missing steps. 0-5 the point is hard to find.
AUDIENCE FIT: 17-20 speaks to the profile's dream client or follower, in their words. 11-16 right topic, unclear benefit. 6-10 about the user more than the viewer, or aimed at everyone. 0-5 wrong audience.
PACING: 17-20 length fits, every line moves, payoff on time. 11-16 one slow patch or repeat. 6-10 long setup or late payoff. 0-5 drags throughout.
CALL TO ACTION: 17-20 one clear ask that fits, or a deliberate strong ending. 11-16 works but generic or bolted on. 6-10 several or vague asks. 0-5 missing, or bait.

FIXES
- 3-5, biggest first. The hook first if it scored under 15.
- Each: the exact quote, the viewer's problem, the fix in their voice.
- Also check: would someone send it? Any exaggerated claim? Any unclear line to ask about?
- Optional text block "Keep these": 1-2 lines that already work.
