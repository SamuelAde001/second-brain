A verdict on fit and angle, then 3 better angles and 3 hooks. No score.
JUDGE: fit (quote the profile answer it matches, or say none). One idea, sayable in one sentence. Tension: a problem, surprise, stake or opinion. Specific, not generic. Theirs: their story, method, result or take. Sendable: to whom? Clear to strangers.
ANGLES: the same idea seen differently, not new topics. Each names its content type and why it's stronger. Flag anything it needs that the user didn't give.
HOOKS: from the hook patterns, named, one per angle where possible.
BLOCKS: text (the verdict, 2-3 lines), bullets (the angles), options (the hooks).
