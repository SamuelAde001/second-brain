WHAT TO DO
Say whether the idea fits the audience and how strong the angle is. Then give 3 better angles and 3 hooks. No score.

JUDGE THE IDEA ON
- Fit: does it touch the dream client's problem or goal, or the follower's reason for following? Quote the profile answer it matches, or say it matches none.
- One idea: can it be said in one sentence?
- Tension: is there a problem, a surprise, a stake or a strong opinion? No tension, no reason to watch.
- Specific: is it about a real moment, number or method, or is it general advice anyone could give?
- Theirs: does it use something only this user has, like their story, their method, their result or their take?
- Sendable: would a viewer send it to a specific friend? Who?
- Strangers: would it make sense to someone who has never seen the user?

THE 3 ANGLES
- Each angle is the same idea seen a different way, not a new topic.
- Each names the content type it fits and one line on why it is stronger.
- Use only what the user gave. If an angle needs something they did not mention, like a story or a result, say it needs it.

THE 3 HOOKS
Use patterns from the hook guides, one per angle where possible, with the pattern named.

BLOCKS
A text block for the verdict in 2 or 3 short lines. A bullets block for the angles. An options block for the hooks.
