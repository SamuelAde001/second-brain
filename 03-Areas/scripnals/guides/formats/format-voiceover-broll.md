HOW THE SCRIPT CHANGES
- Write the voiceover and the shots together. Every one or two lines of voice gets a VISUAL line saying what is on screen.
- The picture should show what the words say, or add to it. Never contradict it.
- Voiceover can be calmer and more written than talking to camera, but still short lines, easy to say.
- First frame: the strongest shot, matching the first spoken line and the on-screen text.
- Leave room: a line of silence over a strong shot can land harder than words.
AVOID
Describing what the viewer can already see. Shots the user can't film. Suggest simple shots from their own life and work.
