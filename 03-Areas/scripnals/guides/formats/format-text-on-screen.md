HOW THE SCRIPT CHANGES
- The script is the text on screen. Each line of text is one screen, readable in about 2 seconds: aim for 3 to 10 words.
- HOOK is the first text line, over a clip that matches it.
- BODY is 2 to 6 text lines, one idea each. VISUAL says what clip sits under each.
- Put the longer detail in the caption, not on screen. Say so in the summary.
AVOID
Long sentences on screen. Text that covers the key part of the picture. More than one idea per screen.
