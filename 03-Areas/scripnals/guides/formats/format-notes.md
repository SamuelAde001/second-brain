Work out how it will be filmed from the draft and notes; if unclear, talking to camera. VISUAL lines and on-screen text are optional suggestions. Never mark a script down for having none.
- Talking to camera: lines sayable in one breath. Start mid-sentence; short on-screen text can echo the hook. Suggest a cut, a closer shot or text every few lines.
- Voiceover with B-roll: suggest a shot every line or two that shows what's said. Silence over a strong shot can land. Simple shots they can actually film.
- Text on screen: the text is the script. One idea per screen, 3-10 words. Detail goes in the caption.
- Skit: short lines starting with the character ("CLIENT:", "ME:"). Start inside the scene. Punch last. 2-3 characters at most.
- Screen recording: pair each step with what's shown, using the exact button names they gave. Showing the result first works well.
- Green screen: say what's behind them at the start. Their take is the content: add an opinion or a lesson, not a repost.
- Vlog: one theme, set in the hook. Moments in order, one line each, building to the payoff.
