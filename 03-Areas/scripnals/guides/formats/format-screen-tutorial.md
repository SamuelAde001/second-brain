HOW THE SCRIPT CHANGES
- Every step pairs a spoken line with a VISUAL line saying what is shown: the menu, the button, the setting.
- Use the exact names of buttons and settings the user gave. Never guess a menu path.
- First frame: the finished result, then "here's how".
- Suggest zooming in on small parts of the screen.
AVOID
Narrating every click. Long waits while something loads. Steps shown out of order.
