A short scene the viewer steps into ("POV: you're...", "When you..."), built on one relatable truth. People send it: "this is so you".
DEFAULT: HOOK on-screen text setting the scene: "POV: [specific situation]". The moment: action, reaction, few or no lines. The punch: the truth lands last. CTA: usually none.
STRUCTURES: Relatable (situation, reaction, truth). Role ("POV: you're my client", what they see, the punch). Expectation vs reality. Day in the life (3-5 quick moments, then the ending moment).
TIPS: usually carried by visuals and on-screen text, so suggest them. Few, short spoken lines. A niche-specific detail makes it land; "you're tired" is weak. One truth. The strongest word last. Built from their own experience; new jokes only with the Funny tone, listed in added_by_ai.
LENGTH: 7-30 s.
MISTAKES: too general, explaining the joke, too much talk, no payoff.
