WHAT IT IS
A short scene the viewer steps into: "POV: you're..." or "When you...". It is built on one relatable truth, usually shown more than said.

WHEN IT WORKS
Sends and relatability. People send it because "this is so you". It works for businesses too: the client's daily pain, or the moment the user solves it.

DEFAULT BEATS
HOOK: the on-screen text setting the scene: "POV: [a specific situation the audience lives]".
BODY: The moment played out: action, reaction, a few lines of dialogue or none → The twist or punch: the relatable truth landing at the end.
CTA: usually none, or one short line. Let the ending carry it.

STRUCTURES TO CHOOSE FROM
- Relatable POV: the situation → the reaction → the truth everyone recognises.
- Role POV: "POV: you're my client / my editor / my mum" → what that person sees → the punch.
- Two sides: expectation → reality.
- Day in the life POV: 3 to 5 quick moments on one theme → the ending moment.

RULES
- Mostly VISUAL and on-screen text. Keep spoken lines few and short.
- The situation must be specific to the niche. "POV: you're tired" is weak. A detail only this audience knows makes it land.
- One truth per video.
- The punch goes last. Put the strongest word at the end of the last line.
- Built from the user's own experience and humour. Don't invent a joke unless the tone is Funny, and then list it in added_by_ai.

LENGTH
7 to 30 seconds.

COMMON MISTAKES
A situation too general. Explaining the joke. Too many lines of talk. No payoff at the end.
