A numbered set: tips, mistakes, tools, signs, lessons. Easy saves and sends.
DEFAULT: HOOK the number, the topic and why it matters. Items: the name in one line, then 1-2 lines on why or how. CTA: save, follow, or "which one do you do?".
STRUCTURES: N ways to a result. N mistakes (mistake, cost, fix). N tools the user uses. N things I wish I knew. N signs.
TIPS: 3-5 items for under 60 s; keep the strongest and say what was cut. Same topic, parallel phrasing. A strong first item; a best-for-last teased early, if true. The hook's number matches the list. Never add items to round a number.
LENGTH: 20-60 s.
MISTAKES: too many items, overlap, long explanations, a number with no reason to care.
