WHAT IT IS
The user disagrees with common advice or a common belief, says so plainly, and backs it up.

WHEN IT WORKS
Comments, sends and standing out. The user truly holds the view and has a reason.

DEFAULT BEATS
HOOK: the take, stated plainly in the first line. No warm-up.
BODY: What most people believe or say → Why it's wrong or incomplete: 1 or 2 real reasons → What to do or believe instead → The fair counterpoint in one line, and why the take still stands.
CTA: a real question ("agree, or am I wrong?") or a follow. For a business, one line on how they help.

STRUCTURES TO CHOOSE FROM
- Myth buster: The myth → Why it's wrong → What to do instead → Light promotion → CTA.
- Hot take: The take → Reasons → The counterpoint → What to do with this.
- Reaction: A short clip or quote from someone else → The user's verdict, true or false → Why → What to do instead. Name what is being reacted to. Don't reuse the whole clip.
- Stop doing this: The common habit → What it costs → The better way.

RULES
- The take must be the user's real opinion. Never make it more extreme than they said it.
- Attack the idea, never a person or a group.
- The reasons carry it. A take with no reason is just bait, and bait gets pushed down.
- "Instead" is the value. Always give the better way.

LENGTH
20 to 60 seconds.

COMMON MISTAKES
Hedging the take ("some people might say..."). Only complaining with no alternative. A take so mild nobody disagrees. A strawman of the other side.
