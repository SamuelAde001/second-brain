The user disagrees with common advice, says it plainly and backs it up. Comments, sends, standing out.
DEFAULT: HOOK the take, in the first line. What most people believe. Why it's wrong: 1-2 real reasons. What to do instead. The fair counterpoint in one line, and why the take still stands. CTA: a real question or a follow.
STRUCTURES: Myth buster (the myth, why it's wrong, what instead, a light promo). Hot take (take, reasons, counterpoint, what to do). Reaction (a short clip or quote, the verdict, why, what instead; name the source, don't reuse the whole clip). Stop doing this (the habit, its cost, the better way).
TIPS: their real opinion, never more extreme than they said. Attack ideas, not people. The reasons carry it. Always give the better way.
LENGTH: 20-60 s.
MISTAKES: hedging, complaining with no alternative, a take nobody disputes, a strawman.
