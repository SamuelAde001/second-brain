WHAT IT IS
A real moment told so the viewer feels it: a problem, what it cost, what turned it, what changed. The lesson comes out of the story.

WHEN IT WORKS
Building trust and connection. The user has lived it, or watched a client live it.

DEFAULT BEATS
HOOK: the most intense or surprising moment, dropped in without setup.
BODY: Context (one line: when, where, who) → Struggle (what went wrong, what it cost, with real details) → Turn (the exact moment or realisation that flipped it) → Result or lesson (what changed, what they learned).
CTA: soft. A follow, a question, or end on the lesson. A named series may close on its catchphrase.

STRUCTURES TO CHOOSE FROM
- The lesson: Setback → What I tried → What worked → What it taught me.
- The breakthrough: Doing everything and still failing → The realisation → The fix, step by step.
- Challenge to victory: Someone said I couldn't → The struggle → The turning point → The method → The before and after → The myth it busts.
- Client story (business): Their problem → The pain → What failed → What worked → Their result → How I help people like them.
- About me: Normal life → The event → The realisation → The change → My mission now.
- Big goal: The dream → Starting for real → Progress so far → Help me get there.

RULES
- Join beats with "but" and "so". "And then" means the story has no tension.
- Real details only: numbers, times, feelings, exact words said. Never invent one. Ask with a bracketed gap.
- The turn must be specific, a thing seen, heard or done, not "I realised I had to change".
- Vulnerable details are usually the best lines. Keep them.

LENGTH
45 to 90 seconds. Tight versions 30 to 45.

COMMON MISTAKES
Starting at the beginning of the day instead of the moment. Too much setup. A lesson that doesn't follow from the story. Ending on a vague moral.
