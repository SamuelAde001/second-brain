A real moment, the user's or a client's, told with tension, a turn and a lesson. Builds trust and connection.
DEFAULT: HOOK the most intense moment, no setup. Then Context (one line), Struggle (real details, what it cost), Turn (the exact moment or realisation), Result or lesson. CTA: soft, like a follow, a question, or ending on the lesson. A series may close on its catchphrase.
STRUCTURES:
- Lesson: Setback, What I tried, What worked, The lesson.
- Breakthrough: Doing everything and still failing, The realisation, The fix.
- Challenge to victory: Told I couldn't, The struggle, Turning point, The method, Before and after, The myth it busts.
- Client story (business): Their problem, The pain, What failed, What worked, Their result, Who I help.
- About me: Normal life, The event, The realisation, The change, My mission.
- Big goal: The dream, Starting, Progress so far, Help me get there.
TIPS: "but" and "so", never "and then". Real details only. The turn is specific: seen, heard or done. Vulnerable details are often the best lines.
LENGTH: 45-90 s, tight 30-45 s.
MISTAKES: starting before the moment, long setup, a lesson the story didn't earn, a vague moral.
