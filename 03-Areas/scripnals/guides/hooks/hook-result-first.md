FORMULA: [Real result] in [real timeframe]. Here's what did it.
USE WHEN: the user gave a real result: a number, an outcome, a client win.
NEEDS: the result, stated in the user's text. If there isn't one, do not use this pattern.
AVOID: rounding up, or implying the viewer will get the same.
