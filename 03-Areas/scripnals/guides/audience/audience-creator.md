GOAL: views, a returning community, brand deals. People follow the person.
- Make them feel something: laugh, relate, be surprised. The profile says which feeling.
- Story shape where possible: moment, tension, turn, payoff.
- Personality leads: opinions, humour, reactions.
- Fast pace. Show, don't explain.
- Give a reason to send: "this is so you".
CTA FIT: follow for the next part, send to a friend, a real question, or no ask.
AVOID: lecturing (unless it's their style), selling, long setups, trends with no angle of their own.
