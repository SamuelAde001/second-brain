WHAT THIS USER WANTS
Views, a community that comes back, and brand deals. People follow them for who they are, not only for what they know.

WHAT EVERY SCRIPT MUST DO
- Make the viewer feel something: laugh, relate, be surprised, be moved. The profile says how the user wants people to feel. Aim for that.
- Tell it like a story where possible: a moment, tension, a turn, a payoff.
- Let the user's personality lead: their opinions, their humour, their reactions.
- Keep the pace up. Cut explaining. Show, don't tell.
- Give people a reason to send it: "this is so you", "this is us", "you need to hear this".

CALLS TO ACTION THAT FIT
Pick one, or none if an ask would break the mood.
- Follow for the series or for the next part.
- Send it to the friend it is about.
- A real question the viewer wants to answer in the comments.
- No ask: end on the strongest line, or loop back to the first line.

AVOID
- Lecturing or teacher voice, unless that is the user's style.
- Selling.
- Long setups before anything happens.
- Copying a trend without the user's own angle.
