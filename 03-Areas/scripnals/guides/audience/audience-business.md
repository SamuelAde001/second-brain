GOAL: leads and trust. The dream client should think "they get my problem and can fix it".
- Speak to the client's main problem or dream outcome from the profile, in the client's words.
- Show expertise through specifics: how, why, what they've seen.
- Give real value in the video itself.
- Keep the focus on the client's problem. Use the user's story only if it proves they understand it.
- The offer: one light line at most, unless it's a promo.
CTA FIT: cold viewer: save or follow. Viewer with the problem now: keyword comment or DM. Promo: the offer, one step.
AVOID: a hard sell every video, jargon, unbacked claims, talking to "everyone".
