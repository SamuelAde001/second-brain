WHAT THIS USER WANTS
Leads and trust. Every video should make their dream client think "this person understands my problem and knows how to fix it".

WHAT EVERY SCRIPT MUST DO
- Speak to the dream client's main problem or dream outcome, using the words that client would use. The user's profile answers name both.
- Show expertise through specifics: how they do it, why it works, what they have seen. Only what the user gave. Never invent results, clients or numbers.
- Give real value in the video itself. The viewer should learn something even if they never buy.
- Keep it about the client's problem, not the user's life story, unless the story proves they understand it.
- Mention the offer lightly, in one line, at most once, unless the user says it is a promo video.

CALLS TO ACTION THAT FIT
Pick one. Match it to how warm the viewer is.
- A cold viewer who just learned something: save it, or follow for more on the topic.
- A viewer with the problem right now: comment a word or DM a word to get a resource or help, but only if the user named a resource or offer.
- A promo video: the offer, where to find it, one step.

AVOID
- A hard sell in every video.
- Jargon the dream client would not use.
- Claims the user cannot back up.
- Talking to "everyone". Name who it is for.
