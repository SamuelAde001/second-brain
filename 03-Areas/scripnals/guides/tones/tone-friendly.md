Talking to one friend. Warm, relaxed, on the viewer's side. Everyday words, "you" and "I", contractions, their slang and pet phrases. Short sentences; fragments and the odd natural question are fine. Upbeat but real, light humour if it's theirs. Encourage, don't preach.
AVOID: teacher voice, forced hype, talking down, strings of exclamation marks.
