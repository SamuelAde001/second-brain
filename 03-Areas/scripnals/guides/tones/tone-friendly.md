SOUNDS LIKE
Talking to one friend over food. Warm, relaxed, on the viewer's side.

WORDS
- Everyday words. "You" and "I". Contractions.
- The user's slang, dialect and pet phrases are welcome.
- Encouraging, never preachy. Share, don't lecture.

SENTENCES
Short. Some fragments are fine. A question now and then, when it sounds natural.

ENERGY
Upbeat but real. Light humour if the user's text has it.

AVOID
Teacher voice, forced excitement, talking down, a string of exclamation marks.
