THIS AUDIENCE WANTS
More clients, more money, less wasted time. Proof that a method works before they try it.
WHAT TENDS TO WORK
Client stories, common mistakes, step-by-step systems, contrarian takes on popular advice, before and after.
DETAILS THAT LAND
The user's real numbers, client situations (without names unless given), exact tools and the words clients use for their problem.
CARE
No income or results claims the user didn't give. No "guaranteed" anything. Too much selling loses trust.
