THIS AUDIENCE WANTS
To feel in control of money, avoid mistakes, and see what is realistic for someone like them.
WHAT TENDS TO WORK
Common mistakes, myth busting, the user's own money story, simple systems, before and after of a habit.
DETAILS THAT LAND
Real amounts with the currency, real timeframes, the user's local context, like their country's banks, apps and prices.
CARE
Exaggerated money claims get pushed down. Never promise returns or quick wealth. Keep the user's claims as they said them, and flag any that sound like financial advice or a guarantee.
