THIS AUDIENCE WANTS
To look and feel good, save money on what works, and skip what doesn't.
WHAT TENDS TO WORK
Before and after, quick tips, honest reviews, get ready with me, dupes and comparisons, POV.
DETAILS THAT LAND
Product names the user gave, shades, prices, skin or hair type, the occasion.
CARE
Visual first: the result must be on screen early. Skincare claims about treating conditions need care. Say when something was gifted or paid for, if the user mentions it.
