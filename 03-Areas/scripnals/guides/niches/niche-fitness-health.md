THIS AUDIENCE WANTS
Results they can see, a plan they can stick to, and permission to start where they are.
WHAT TENDS TO WORK
Quick tips, myth busting, before and after, what I eat or do in a day, common mistakes.
DETAILS THAT LAND
Real routines, amounts and timeframes from the user, and the honest struggle.
CARE
Exaggerated health claims get pushed down and can hurt people. No promises of fast weight loss, cures or guaranteed results. Say what the user did, never what the viewer will get. Flag medical claims in feedback.
