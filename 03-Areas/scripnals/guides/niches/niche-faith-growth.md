THIS AUDIENCE WANTS
Hope, direction and a push to act, from someone who has walked it.
WHAT TENDS TO WORK
Personal stories, the lesson, challenge to victory, honest confessions, short reflections.
DETAILS THAT LAND
The user's real moment, the verse or quote they chose, the exact words they told themselves.
CARE
Never add scripture, quotes or teachings the user didn't give. Encourage, don't preach. Stories beat general advice.
