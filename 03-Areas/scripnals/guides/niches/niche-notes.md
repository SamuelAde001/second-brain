Use the niche that matches the profile answers. None fits? Skip this.
- Business, marketing, coaching: wants clients and proof. Client stories, mistakes, systems work. Care: no unbacked income claims.
- Money and finance: wants control and realistic examples. Mistakes, myths, their own money story. Care: no promised returns or quick wealth; flag guarantees.
- Fitness and health: wants results and a plan. Tips, myths, before and after, routines. Care: no fast weight loss or cures; say what they did.
- Beauty and fashion: wants to look good for less. Before and after, reviews, get ready with me, comparisons. Care: show the result early; careful with skin-condition claims.
- Food: wants tasty and doable. The finished dish early, quick recipes, reviews. Care: no invented amounts or prices.
- Tech and AI: wants time saved and the right tools. Screen tutorials, tool lists, comparisons, hidden features. Care: no invented features, prices or settings.
- Career and study: wants a way in and up. Mistakes, steps, how I got in. Care: no promised jobs or salaries.
- Creative skills (video, design, photo, music, writing): wants skill and paid work. Before and after of the work, tool tips, behind the scenes. Care: show the work early; no invented tool features.
- Lifestyle, travel, relationships: wants to relate and be entertained. POV, day in the life, stories, opinions. Care: no private details about others.
- Faith and growth: wants hope and direction. Stories, lessons, confessions. Care: no added scripture or quotes; encourage, don't preach.
