# Called to Edit

## What this is

**Called to Edit is Samuel's brand**, the umbrella over everything he sells to video editors: the webinar, the Academy cohort, the community, mentorship, later a website and a physical seminar. He is its founder. It replaced HighSignals as the umbrella on 2026-10-20.

Samuel, 2026-10-20: *"instead of High Signals, I'm moving my brand to Call to Edit fully"* · *"Instead of known as the founder of High Signals, I'll be known as the founder of Call to Edit"*. The name is **Called**, never "Call" or "Code" (his voice typing writes both; confirmed 2026-10-20: *"Yes, it's Called not Call"*).

What it stands for, the audience, naming rules and the look: [[03-Areas/called-to-edit/called-to-edit-brand-guide|Brand guide]] (v1, 2026-10-21) · [[03-Areas/called-to-edit/called-to-edit-brand-foundation|brand foundation]].

## Why it matters

It's the business his ambition sits on: *"I want to become the best or the most known or the most influential video editing coach in Nigeria"*. Every branch that had a direction was already heading toward video editing, and the one thing that grew in September and October was video-editing content. Called to Edit puts one name on that.
