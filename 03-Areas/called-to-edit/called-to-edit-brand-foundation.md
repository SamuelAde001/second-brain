# Called to Edit: brand foundation

What the brand is, in Samuel's words. Everything above "Brand manager suggestions" is his; the suggestions are not decisions until he confirms them (AGENTS.md §5 rule 7). Back to [[03-Areas/called-to-edit/called-to-edit|Called to Edit]].

## 1. The name

- **Called to Edit.** Never "Call to Edit", never "Code to Edit". Samuel, 2026-10-20: *"Yes, it's Called not Call"*.
- Products carry the name: Called to Edit Webinar, Called to Edit Academy (the cohort), Called to Edit Mentorship, Called to Edit Community. His list, 2026-10-20: *"Call to Edit Webinar, right? Call to Edit Masterclass, Call to Edit Cohort ... Call to Edit Community"*.
- The sister name, if HighSignals Community continues in 2027: **Called to Create**.
- Samuel is **the founder of Called to Edit** (public after the webinar).

## 2. Who it's for

- Video editors **building a career**: working for clients, getting content creators. *"not people that are just learning video editing for fancy sake, that are trying to build a career around video editing"* (2026-10-20).
- **Mostly Nigerian, not only.** *"video editors does not mean only Nigerian video editors, but my more dominant audience will be Nigerian video editors"* (2026-10-20). Same line as 2026-10-04: *"my main audience right now are Nigerian, but I want other people outside to not feel left out"*.
- Two kinds (2026-10-04): (1) wants to start editing and has no plan or first step; (2) already learning but *"doesn't know how to get clients ... how to position himself ... how to get people to pay him"*.

## 3. The position: the contrarian take

Samuel, 2026-10-20: *"every video editing coach will come and train you on how to do flashy edits, but me, I'm coming in to teach you how to build a career in video editing. I want to be that guy that helps every video editor succeed in video editing as a career."*

- He coaches **the business**: *"I will coach them on building the business"*.
- He still teaches **the skill**: *"I will still coach them on the skill of video editing, right? But with a better mindset towards career growth"*. His craft is DaVinci Resolve (*"the methods I use in DaVinci Resolve"*, 2026-10-04).
- The promise: *"Call to Edit is aimed to transform people into that career state"*.
- One line already in use (bio, 2026-10-04): **"I coach video editors to get paid."**

## 4. The ambition

*"I want to become the best or the most known or the most influential video editing coach in Nigeria"* · *"I want to one day be able to host a Call to Edit seminar, a physical seminar that people gather"*.
No number yet (2026-10-20): *"I want it to be that when they think of a video editing coach, they think me first, we would keep building gradually"*.

## 5. Brand architecture

- **Samuel is the face.** @SamuelSignals stays his personal brand and funnels into Called to Edit (and Called to Create).
- **No Called to Edit social account yet**: *"later on, maybe Call to Edit might have his own social media profile, but right now I don't have the capacity for that"*.
- **A website eventually**: *"eventually I'll have the Call to Edit website"*. Today it's the webinar landing page, calledtoedit.netlify.app ([[04-Projects/called-to-edit-landing-page-build|build]]).
- **Scripnals stands alone**, not under Called to Edit, and isn't the focus now.

## 6. The look

- **The current branding carries over**: *"This branding I'm already using for High Signals, and I will use it for Call to Edit"*. Which one he means is open (question 115). The Brain holds three:
  - the navy / gold / cream working system in the [[03-Areas/personal-brand/brand-guide|brand guide]], with a *Called to Edit* gold-serif lock-up;
  - the deep blue / yellow / white webinar flyer look he picked on 2026-10-16 for webinar designs;
  - the cobalt `#0540AD` / Poppins system in [[03-Areas/personal-brand/visual-identity|visual identity]], filed as possibly HighSignals (question 108).
- **A logo, eventually**: *"I will eventually need to have Call to Edit logo that we can work with, so that we can be able to build something around it"*. Who makes it and when: *"I haven't decided yet"*.
- **Creative director:** [[03-Areas/personal-brand/creative-director|Ifeoma]], eventually for Called to Edit.
- **What he likes (2026-10-21):** *"I like the color Navy blue or so and also the color of the text in my Life of a Video Editor video, but I don't know if they work together"*. On the title font: *"I like that font actually, but I don't know if that is the best font for my brand"*. - **2026-10-21: the brand ladder** (Samuel): personal brand → webinars → Community → Academy → Mentorship → a Called to Edit physical event (one day). Replaces the order in suggestion 1 below.

## 7. Timing

- The rebrand goes public **after the webinar** (2026-10-23): *"I would announce it all after the webinar"*.
- Bio stays as it is until then: *"Bio would wait to change after the webinar"*.

## Brand manager suggestions (not decisions)

Given to Samuel on 2026-10-20. He confirmed none of these yet.

1. **Build the ladder one step at a time**: content → webinar → cohort → alumni community → mentorship → seminar once there are enough alumni to fill a room. Drop "masterclass" unless it means something the webinar doesn't. *(2026-10-21: Samuel set his own ladder, recorded above under the look.)*
2. **The contrarian take needs proof.** Cohort 1's real job is 3-5 case studies of editors who got paid. They become the content, and later the content of a Called to Edit account.
3. **Say "Called" on purpose.** Samuel said "Call to Edit" throughout the 2026-10-20 voice note; the audience will too. Make "Called" clear in the logo, and secure the handle and domain for both spellings.
4. **The logo is a simple wordmark** in the look he picks, ready before cohort certificates are handed out.
5. **Leave HighSignals Community as it is until December**, then decide on Called to Create.
