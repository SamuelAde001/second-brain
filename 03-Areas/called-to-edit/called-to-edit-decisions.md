## 2026-10-21 — The webinar upsells the Called to Edit Community, not the Academy

Samuel, voice note for the webinar close: *"I am not upselling them to call to edit academy anymore. I'm upselling them to call to edit community."*
- **Why:** after the webinar people will want to put it into practice, ask more questions and get his feedback on their emails, outreach, profile and niche: *"they will need kind of a place, a community where they can be able to give themselves feedbacks on everyone, and everyone is supporting each other."* *"I think the best thing would be the community first."*
- **Price:** *"5,000 naira per month"*, a monthly subscription. *"It's not going to be free."*
- **Who:** webinar attendees, and *"other people outside of that didn't come for the webinar can still join."*
- **When:** set up before the webinar (Fri 2026-10-23): *"we need to work on the community before the webinar, so that the community is already ready and set up before the webinar."*
- **Inside it:** his feedback, corrections and guidance; calls; a 30- or 60-day challenge built from the webinar (length not decided).
- **Academy:** *"The call to edit academy would be coming soon."* Announced as coming soon, not sold at the webinar. The NGN 50,000 cohort plan stands down until he says otherwise.
- **Changes:** the Community was planned for 2027; it now launches at the webinar.
- **Who decided:** Samuel.

## 2026-10-22 — The Community is a place to grow, not the 60-day challenge

Samuel, word for word: *"you built the whole community around the 60 days challenge, the 60 days challenge is just one of the many activities that would hold in the community"* · *"The aim of the community is a place where editors can grow with others, be kept accountable, share resources, share opportunities, share client works, archive of useful tutorials, editing hacks, etc, we would have weekly live calls, where I would guide them, and teach sometimes"* · *"I don't want the community to feel overwhelming with too much channels ... let's have the essential channels that would meet our needs"* · *"I want the bot to be called \"Editors bot\" they can ask it questions from my teachings, it can respond to them like an AI agent ... it should be able to answer their questions when called, and the bot should have it's own commands"* · *"Make this community to become the best community for editors, to actually thrive and grow"*.
- **Decided:** the Community's purpose is growth with others: accountability, resources, opportunities, sharing client work, a tutorial and editing-hacks archive. **Weekly live calls: yes** (he guides, sometimes teaches); day and time not given yet. The 60-day challenge is one activity among many. Fewer, essential channels. The bot is **Editors Bot**: an AI agent answering from his teachings, with its own personality and commands.
- **Changes:** the 2026-10-22 server layout and the /community page, both built around the challenge, are redone. → [[04-Projects/called-to-edit-community-launch|community launch]]
