Samuel, voice note: *"instead of High Signals, I'm moving my brand to Call to Edit fully"*. Full record in his words: [[03-Areas/highsignals/highsignals-decisions|HighSignals decisions]], 2026-10-20.

## 2026-10-20 — His answers on the structure

- **Name:** *"Yes, it's Called not Call"*. Called to Edit, Called to Create.
- **Mentorship:** *"Yes it becomes Called to edit mentorship"*.
- **Scripnals:** *"Scripnals would be a stand alone product, but focus won't be on it for now"*. Not under Called to Edit.
- **Creative director:** *"Her name is Ifeoma, she would have access to my account everntually, but we would sort that out later"*.
- **The ambition, no number:** *"No number yet, but I want it to be that when they think of a video editing coach, they think me first, we would keep building gradually"*.
- **Bio:** *"Bio would wait to change after the webinar"*. "Founder of HighSignals" stays until after 2026-10-23.
- **Logo and announcement:** *"I haven't decided yet, I would announce it all after the webinar"*.
- **Brain:** a new area, `03-Areas/called-to-edit/`, is the umbrella. Academy, Mentorship and Community point to it; HighSignals keeps its history and the community that may become Called to Create; Scripnals stands alone. Folder names unchanged.
- **Who decided:** Samuel. The Brain layout is the Brand manager's, following his answers.

- *"Remove the line " If two things are gold, one of them is wrong.""*

- *"Remove anything someone who isn't me can't understand, some words you use are things only I understands in the brand guide"*
- *"Remove "The logo is built the way Samuel builds his Life of a Video Editor titles: " a new designer won't know my series"*

- *"I don't like the Called to Edit Webinar and Called to Edit Academy, the extra words are byt the side, let's put it under so it's easy to put in a box or a profile picture"*

- **Decided:** the brand ladder is personal brand → webinars → Community → Academy → Mentorship → a Called to Edit physical event (one day). This replaces the order in the brand foundation. The marker and the normal round dot on the i are interchangeable. Product names go under the logo. The guide holds no personal details (no room, no series, nothing only Samuel would understand). The guide is a PDF.
