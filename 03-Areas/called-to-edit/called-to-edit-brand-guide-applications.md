# Called to Edit: brand guide, applications

Video, social and print rules from the [[03-Areas/called-to-edit/called-to-edit-brand-guide|brand guide]] (v1, 2026-10-21; the Brand manager's v1 waiting on Samuel's review). Live examples: https://claude.ai/artifact/FFdUdBWirJXJ96dSWa2bvm. Back to [[03-Areas/called-to-edit/called-to-edit|Called to Edit]].

## Video

Samuel's videos are where the brand was born. These are the rules for titles, captions and lower thirds in DaVinci Resolve (Fusion Text+), sized for a 1080 × 1920 frame.

## Title card

The Life of a Video Editor build, used for every Called to Edit video:

1. **Kicker:** Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold, white, 0.3 × the title size.
2. **Title word:** Gloock, champagne `#FCDD9E`, 180–260 px, tracking −5 %.
3. **Tag (optional):** Plus Jakarta Sans Bold caps, champagne, top right of the title. EP 1, LESSON 3, LIVE.
4. **Subtitle (optional):** Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold, white, 44–52 px, one line.

Place it in the top third, left-aligned, at least 96 px from the left edge. Hold it for 2–3 seconds at the start.

## The title shadow

Over footage, put one soft shadow under the whole title or logo: black-navy `#071226` at 60 %, blur about 12 px, offset 2 px down. That's the only effect allowed. No outlines, strokes or glows.

## Captions

- Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold, 46–54 px, white, centred, in the middle third of the frame.
- One key word per caption line in champagne: the word that carries the line.
- Short chunks, one to four words, the way he speaks.
- Same soft shadow as the title when the background is bright.

## Lower thirds

- The one-line logo or the marker on the left, then the name in Plus Jakarta Sans Bold 44 px white, the role under it in Mist 32 px.
- Sits on a midnight band at 70 %, square corners, no border.
- Bottom-left, 96 px from the left edge, above the caption zone.

## Grade

Grade toward the room: navy shadows, warm highlights, skin kept natural. Champagne text then sits on top without fighting the picture.

## Motion

- Things move one at a time. Titles rise in or fade in over 8–12 frames with a soft ease, hold, and leave the same way.
- Nothing bounces, spins or glitches.
- The marker on the i can drop in last, like a playhead landing, as the one small flourish.

## Outro and corner bug

- Outro: stacked logo centred on Called Navy, with one call to action under it in Plus Jakarta Sans. Hold 2 seconds.
- Corner bug (only when needed): white one-colour logo, 220–300 px wide, top right, 60 % opacity.

## Social and print

## Flyers (4:5, 1080 × 1350)

- **Background:** Called Navy, filled top to bottom. No empty bands.
- **Top left:** the product lockup (for example the Webinar logo).
- **Headline:** Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold, white, under the logo, the one promise in a sentence.
- **Samuel:** black-and-white cut-out on the right, large, with soft edges. No outline around him.
- **Details row:** labelled cells (Date, Time, Where, Fee) split by thin champagne lines.
- **Benefits:** three at most, short.
- **Urgency:** one box, champagne or white, navy text: "100 seats".
- **Call to action:** one champagne button with navy text, and the link written out.
- **Handle:** @samuelsignals01 until Called to Edit has its own account.

## Carousels (4:5)

- **Cover:** a kicker and one Gloock word, or one Gloock number, on navy. Nothing else but the slide count.
- **Inside slides:** a label, a Jakarta headline, at most three points with the marker as the bullet.
- **Last slide:** one call to action.
- Same margins on every slide: 96 px on 1080 wide.

## The marker

The playhead marker from the logo is the brand's one graphic device:

- as the bullet in lists,
- as the "you are here" pointer on a roadmap or step list,
- as the dot over a timestamp or a price.

Champagne on dark, bronze on light. Never more than one marker per item, never as decoration on its own.

## Profile pictures

- **Everywhere:** `called-to-edit-avatar-champagne.png`, the word Edit in navy on a champagne disc. It stands out in a feed of dark profile pictures and reads down to 32 px.
- **On a dark page where a bright disc is too loud:** `called-to-edit-avatar-navy.png`.
- Samuel's own accounts keep his photo. The Called to Edit avatar is for Called to Edit accounts, the WhatsApp group, the community and Google Meet.

## Certificates

- White paper, navy text, bronze accents.
- The seal top centre, the student's name in Gloock navy, the course line in Plus Jakarta Sans.
- Signed by Samuel Adebayo, Founder, with the date and cohort.

## Website and email

- The website follows the same tokens: Called Navy page, champagne for the one word and the main button, white text, Mist for small print.
- The one-line logo in the header. The icon as the favicon.
- Emails: white background, the light one-line logo at the top, bronze for links, one button.

## Photos

- Real photos of Samuel, his room and his desk. Black-and-white cut-outs on flyers, colour in his room for content.
- No stock photos of people.
- Darken a busy photo with a navy-to-midnight gradient behind any text.
