# Called to Edit: brand guide, applications

Video, social, print and web rules from the [[03-Areas/called-to-edit/called-to-edit-brand-guide|brand guide]] (v1.0, 2026-10-21; the Brand manager's v1 under Samuel's review). The mockups are in the PDF, `_attachments/called-to-edit-brand/Called-to-Edit-Brand-Guidelines.pdf`, pages 22–26. No personal details here either (Samuel, 2026-10-21). Back to [[03-Areas/called-to-edit/called-to-edit|Called to Edit]].

## Vertical video (1080 × 1920)

- **Title:** a top line in Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold, the title word in Gloock champagne at 180–260 px, an optional one-line subtitle. Top third, left-aligned, 96 px from the edge, held 2–3 seconds.
- **Captions:** Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold, 46–54 px, white, centred. One key word in champagne.
- **Effects:** only one soft shadow over footage. No outlines or glows. Things move one at a time and never bounce.
- **Outro:** the stacked logo on navy, one call to action under it.
- **Marker or dot in video:** where the marker can't be built in the title tool, use the round-dot version of the word.

## Landscape video (1920 × 1080)

- **Lower third:** the marker, the name in Plus Jakarta Sans Bold white, the role under it in Mist, on a square midnight band at 70 %. Bottom left, 96 px from the edge. No border, no rounded corners.
- **Corner logo:** the white one-line logo, top right, 60 % opacity, only when needed.

## Social

- **Flyer (4:5):** product logo top left, an urgency box top right (white, navy text), a Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold headline, up to three points with the marker, a midnight details row (date, time, where, fee) split by thin champagne lines, one champagne button. Photo of Samuel in black and white on the right when the page is busy.
- **Carousel (4:5):** cover = a top line and one Gloock word, a one-line subtitle in Mist. Inside slides = a label, a headline, up to three points with the marker. Slide count bottom left, the profile picture small bottom right. One call to action, on the last slide only.

## Print

- **Certificate (A4 landscape):** white paper, navy text, bronze accents and a thin bronze inner line. The seal at the top, "CERTIFICATE OF COMPLETION" in bronze caps, the student's name in Gloock, the course in Plus Jakarta Sans, signed by the founder (Samuel Adebayo, Founder, Called to Edit) and dated.
- **Seal:** for certificates, merch and stickers. It never replaces the main logo.

## Web

- **Header:** the one-line logo left, navigation in Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold, one champagne button.
- **Hero:** a label in champagne caps (what, when), the signature build (top line + one Gloock word), a short paragraph, one champagne button and one outlined button. Photo of Samuel on a navy-to-midnight background, on the right.

## Chat and profile pictures

- Group and account pictures use the profile picture, or the product profile picture for one product.
