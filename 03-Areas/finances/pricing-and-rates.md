## Landing short-form retainer clients — proposed, never confirmed

Tactics suggested, in the order the assistant ranked them by yield:

1. **Mine existing clients first** — pitch a per-project client on a flat monthly volume deal instead of per-video quotes; ask every past client for one introduction.
2. **Target buyers with a recurring content need** — podcasters/long-form YouTubers needing clip repurposing (called the best fit), founder-led B2B/SaaS teams, coaches/consultants/agency owners posting 3–5×/week, agencies needing white-label overflow.
3. **Cold outreach with proof, not a pitch** — edit one of 25–30 target prospects' existing videos unasked and send it, or a short Loom breaking down what's wrong with their current shorts. Framed as converting far better than a portfolio link.
4. **Sell a monthly deliverable package, not an edit or an hour** — e.g. "12 shorts a month, 48-hour turnaround, hook-tested," priced as a product.
5. On Instagram specifically: editing-tips/typography content attracts other editors, not clients — that's an audience for a course (a different revenue engine), not for retainer acquisition. Client-facing content needs to be results and case studies aimed at founders.
6. **Get paid properly for international work** — Wise or Payoneer for USD, invoice monthly in advance once trust is established, 50% upfront in month one, never bill USD work in Naira.

**Proposed price bands (unconfirmed):**
- For Samuel himself: USD 1,500–3,000/month "realistic band for a strong editor with US clients"; USD 800–1,200/month "if you're still building US testimonials."
- For the other editor (earlier in proof-building): USD 600–2,000/month depending on volume and edit density, structured as deliverables not hours (example given: "16 shorts/month, $1,200"), converting from a paid 3-video trial.
- Framed as "a volume game for the first 60 days" — the estimate given was that roughly twenty spec edits gets one retainer.

## Paid mentorship / coaching calls — proposed, never confirmed

Samuel's stated need: a bookable, paid 1:1 link, working from Nigeria, for a mostly-Nigerian audience with occasional international clients.

**Platform proposed: Coachli** — Nigerian, built for paid 1:1 session booking, said to take NGN via Paystack/Flutterwave and USD/GBP/EUR via Stripe on one link, with no company registration required. Vendor's own stated fees (explicitly flagged in the chat as unverified — "treat those rates as their own claim and verify in the dashboard before you publish a price"): no monthly fee, ~4% + NGN 50 on naira transactions, ~7.5% on USD transactions.

**Alternatives discussed and set aside:**
- Direct international payments via Paystack or Flutterwave — requires a Zenith Bank USD domiciliary account, a CAC certificate, and standard KYC (none of those identifiers were given in the chat and none are recorded here). Approval takes a few business days. Flutterwave was called the better of the two for actually holding USD rather than auto-converting to naira. Not pursued — "don't go build a whole second system for it" unless international bookings reach roughly 20/month.
- A US LLC with real Stripe access — Stripe does not support Nigerian-registered businesses. Estimated USD 300–500 plus ongoing compliance/tax filing. Explicitly called "not worth it for mentorship calls alone."
- Ruled out entirely: Calendly + Stripe (needs a foreign entity), PayPal (unreliable payout into Nigeria). Selar was called workable only if the audience already buys there, since it's built for digital products, not scheduled calls.

**Suggested operating details (proposed, not confirmed):**
- Payment required before a booking confirms, never after.
- Availability set in WAT, tool handles conversion for other time zones.
- Two or three intake questions before the call (what they're editing, where they're stuck, what software).
- 45-minute sessions, with a 15-minute buffer built in after.
- A written no-refund-for-no-show line in the booking description.
- One published price, no separate "discovery call" tier.
- Occasional international bookings (2-3/month) handled manually via a Flutterwave or Grey payment link rather than a second payment system.

**Suggested NGN price band (proposed, not confirmed):** NGN 15,000–40,000 for a 45-minute session, cited as roughly where Nigerian editors coaching beginners land, with room to raise once the calendar fills.
