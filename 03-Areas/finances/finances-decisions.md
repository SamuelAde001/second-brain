## 2026-09-23 — Money he gives people is given, never a loan

- **Decision:** anything Samuel sends to a person is logged as given. Nothing is logged as a loan, and no repayment is counted on for a pot or a plan. His words: "No, anything I give I won't collect back anymore".
- **Why:** the NGN 10,000 friend loan (2026-09-22) and the NGN 5,000 sent 2026-09-23 were both given outright; the loan had been counted on to refill the Buffer.
- **Rejected:** keeping a loans category with repayments flowing back into the Buffer.
- **Who:** Samuel.
