# The money rules

**These are active.** Samuel confirmed on 2026-09-20 that the Accountability Engine's financial system is the current one and its rules carry into the Brain. They are his rules, written by him, most of them dated 2026-08-26.

---

### 1. Savings are untouchable
The only permitted withdrawals: **medical emergency, a building-project shortfall that would stop work, family emergency.** Nothing else.

> "A girlfriend's visit is not an emergency."

### 2. Every withdrawal is logged the same day
Amount and reason, in his own words, in the money ledger. **No silent withdrawals.** Unchanged — this rule is about savings withdrawals, and it stands as written.

> **Changed 2026-09-20 — what "logged" means for ordinary spending.** The engine's weekly minimum was *"every naira in and out logged daily, zero unlogged days."* That is no longer the rule. Samuel: *"I would only log major spendings, but minor ones would just be logged in bulk."*
>
> **The rule now:** every **major** spend gets its own dated row. **Minor** spends are logged **in bulk** — a single periodic row covering them. What counts as major is his judgement, not a fixed threshold, until he sets one.
>
> The cost of this is real and worth stating once: bulk rows hide where small money goes, and small money is exactly what the NGN 10,000 personal/misc line and the NGN 40,000 household line are made of. Rule 6 still applies — a bulk row must reflect money that actually moved, not an estimate written to make the month balance.

### 3. Savings move on a payday — never at month end
> "Money saved at the end of the month is money that was never saved."

Cowrywise moves on **Payday A**. Goal 1 and the Buffer move on **Payday B** — his call, 2026-08-26, because Payday A is nearly always full.

**Exception:** if Payday A leaves more than NGN 50,000 free after its commitments, that excess moves the same day. Six figures sitting loose for fourteen days is how NGN 595,250 disappeared.

### 4. The building project is paid in full before any discretionary line
**NGN 500,000, every month.** August 2026 was NGN 200,000 — a NGN 300,000 shortfall, on the record.

### 5. No new work below USD 333 per video
Whether it is a new Route Rise end client or a new payer — not without logging the reason in the ledger. The August batch lost roughly **NGN 432,000** to the USD 175 rate.

### 6. The ledger is the truth; the spreadsheet is a plan
> "A budget line that says money was saved when no money was saved is a lie in a spreadsheet."

If the two disagree, **the ledger wins.**

### 7. The investment is ring-fenced
**NGN 100,000/month to Cowrywise until the year ends.** Not touched for the NGN 1M, the NGN 3M, the building, or anything else. New savings and the emergency fund are built **outside** it. It does not pay out until January 2027, when it moves into actual stocks.

Settled once, 2026-08-26, and not to be re-argued: **it never pauses.** If December comes up short, the house move slips rather than the contribution. His trade, made with the numbers in front of him.

> ⚠️ **Rule 7 was broken in September 2026** — the Kaduna trip cost roughly NGN 430,011 against NGN 80,000 planned, and NGN 368,041 came out of the Cowrywise investment.
>
> **Resolved 2026-09-20, his call: it does not get refilled.** *"I won't refill it, I would just continue the normal savings."* The NGN 100,000/month contribution continues as normal from here; the withdrawal is absorbed, not repaid.
>
> **What that costs, stated once:** the investment ends 2026 near **NGN 336,959** instead of the NGN 705,000 the plan assumed — a **NGN 368,041** permanent hole in the pot that was never supposed to be touched. Rule 7's "it never pauses" survives; "it is never touched" did not. The rule now has a precedent, and the next urgency will find it.

### 8. The Buffer funds urgencies — never savings
**NGN 50,000/month to a NGN 200,000 target**, plus any month-end underspend, plus the whole excess of any 4-video month.

When a month lands short, **the cut order is already decided**:

> personal/misc → creator visits → household down to NGN 20,000 → the buffer covers the rest → **and only then a conversation.**

If the buffer is empty, an urgency gets **negotiated, not funded**. It never gets funded from Goal 1.

---

## The trade behind Rule 7, stated once

He puts NGN 100,000/month into a locked pot he refuses to count toward his goals, while holding very little liquid and a NGN 1M target with thin margin. That is long-term wealth bought with short-term fragility. **It is his call.** It is written down so that if December comes up short, the reason is on the record and not a mystery.

## What these rules exist to prevent

June–August 2026 the sheets booked **NGN 900,250** to savings and emergency funds. **NGN 305,000 survived**, as the Cowrywise investment. **NGN 595,250 is gone.**

His words: *"unplanned event came in and I used all the money saved."*

The August building payment dropped from NGN 500,000 to NGN 200,000 as a direct consequence — a NGN 300,000 shortfall on the one obligation the entire NGN 1M target exists to serve.

**A third became a real asset. Two thirds evaporated because no rule existed saying what savings are for.** That is what Rules 1, 2, 3 and 8 are: the fix. Not a tighter budget.

## Still unenforced

There is **no consequence for breaking the savings rule.** Open since 2026-08-26. Rule 1 has no teeth, and September proved it when Rule 7 broke with nothing following. **Answered 2026-09-22:** asked what breaking it costs, Samuel: *"It cost me financial stability and security."* A natural cost, not an enforced penalty. Self-enforced penalties *"don't work"*; a better consequence may come later ([[stakes-and-accountability]]).
