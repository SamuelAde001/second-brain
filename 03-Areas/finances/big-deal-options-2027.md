# Big-deal options for 2027 (NGN 10M–20M, one payment)

Samuel, 2026-11-05: *"is there any thing I can offer to someone, a business or a brand or an individual, a big project or contract I can do for someone that can make me sign a deal of 10Million to 20million one time payment"* · *"I don't want to have to wait monthly for my editing to give me that, I would love to land one big deal for that"*.

**This is General Manager research from web searches on 2026-11-05. All of it is a suggestion. None of it is a decision** (AGENTS.md §5.7). Prices are estimates. No Nigerian rate card was found for any of these.

## The size of the target

- At about NGN 1,375–1,450 per USD (the last rates found; no November 2026 figure was found), **NGN 10M ≈ USD 7,000 and NGN 20M ≈ USD 14,000.**
- That works out to about **21–42 Route Rise videos** at USD 333.33. It's about 7–14 times the biggest single invoice in the Brain (Mshel, NGN 1,397,500, unexplained, [[03-Areas/video-editing/video-editing|video editing]]).
- Deals of this size go to a **registered business** that can show it has already **delivered something of the same size**. The deal isn't the first step. Getting him ready to be that seller is.

## The options, best fit first

### 1. Sell the Academy as a training contract (B2B or B2G)
- **What:** a Called to Edit cohort on content creation and video editing (CapCut, then DaVinci Resolve), with a portfolio at the end. It's sold to a funder or programme that pays for the trainees.
- **Why now:** in October 2026 the federal iDICE programme announced 50,000 youths in the North-West with Conclase Consulting, with content creation in the curriculum ([allAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202610270644.html)). It also selected six organisations to train 300,000 youths across the six zones ([Business Tech Africa](https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/nigeria-launches-digital-skills-programme-targeting-300-000-young-people)). Ebonyi's IDEAS-TVET is training 100+ in video editing through an outside firm ([headtopics](https://ng.headtopics.com/news/fg-to-train-100-ebonyi-youths-in-creative-media-digital-88413225)). Other buyers: bank and telco CSR programmes, churches, state ICT and youth ministries, foundations.
- **The arithmetic (estimate):** 50 trainees × NGN 200,000 = NGN 10M; 100 × NGN 200,000 = NGN 20M. The usual route is as a **subcontracted training partner** to the firm that already holds the contract.
- **Fit:** strongest. It's his own business, and the curriculum already exists ([[05-Knowledge/teachings/teachings-archive|teachings archive]]). It builds Called to Edit instead of competing with it.
- **Risk:** government money pays late. 3MTT-type providers had to place 50 % of fellows in jobs within 3 months ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2023/10/19/3-million-technical-talent-portal-features-faqs-and-more/)). Expect conditions on outcomes.

### 2. A documentary and content partner for NGOs and UN agencies in Abuja
- **What:** end-of-project and impact documentaries, plus multi-year video long-term agreements (LTAs).
- **Evidence:** in 2026 alone: a UNICEF Nigeria 2-year videography LTA, two UNDP Nigeria media RFPs, CBM, IRC, Mercy Corps and ActionAid documentaries ([UNGM UNICEF](https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/294179), [UNGM UNDP](https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/299411), [ngojobsinafrica](https://ngojobsinafrica.com/?p=320367)). One World Bank-financed Nigerian state documentary went to an individual consultant for **USD 24,285** (FY2020). Brand documentaries abroad run USD 15,000–50,000 ([Viva Media](https://vivamedia.ca/blog/corporate-video-pricing)).
- **Fit:** his skill is the hard half: story and post-production. Abuja is where these buyers are.
- **Gap:** he has no film crew or cinema camera; his gear is the Osmo Pocket 4 and the Mic Mini. Most RFPs want a registered firm, a tax clearance and past documentaries. **Fix:** team up with an Abuja videographer and bid together, Samuel as director and editor.
- **Payment shape seen:** 60 % at start and 40 % on completion. Withholding tax is 10 % for a firm and 5 % for an individual.

### 3. Election-season media desk (short window, high risk)
- **Timing:** the presidential and National Assembly elections are on **2027-01-16** and the governorship and state assembly elections on **2027-02-06**. Campaigns have been running since 2026-08-19 and 2026-09-09 ([Voice of Nigeria](https://von.gov.ng/2027-elections-inec-releases-official-campaign-timetable/), [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/news/article/from-primaries-to-elections-important-dates-from-inec-2027-timetable/)).
- **What:** 8–12 weeks of daily campaign videos, rally edits and a biography film for one Senate, House or governorship candidate. Then, if they win, a **"first 100 days"** content contract after the 2027-05-29 inauguration.
- **Risk:** campaigns are known for paying late or not at all. Get **at least 50 % upfront**. There's also a brand risk to an "inspire first", faith-led name ([[03-Areas/personal-brand/faith-and-content|faith and content]]), and a time clash with Route Rise in Jan–Feb. **Only worth it if he already knows someone inside a campaign.** Otherwise the window is too short.

### 4. A premium course production for a coach, business school or edtech
- A whole course (40–60 lessons) plus a launch trailer and short clips. Marketplace prices are low, USD 400–800 per course on Fiverr ([Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/joeka24/edit-your-online-course)), so NGN 10M needs an institution, not a solo creator. It's a lower fit as a single deal and a better fit as a line on the training offer.

### 5. An AI post-production setup for an agency or media house
- What he runs now (AI-assisted editing pipeline, agent system, scripted Resolve) is rare. Sold as setup + training to a Nigerian agency, it could be priced as a project. **No buyer and no price evidence was found.** It's a bet, not a lead.

## Capacity, the real constraint

He ruled out more clients because one client's workload is already high ([[income]]). A NGN 10M+ deal means one of two things: **lighter Route Rise volume for the length of the project**, or **a delivery team**: Ifeoma plus trained editors from the Called to Edit Community, on the AI pipeline. Option 1 needs his hours. Options 2 and 3 need a team.

## What has to exist before any pitch

1. **Business registration (CAC)** for Called to Edit, or a firm name for production, with a corporate account. Most of these buyers can't pay a person.
2. **A one-page capability statement** and a **showreel**. Route Rise work probably needs the agency's OK to show. Ask, don't assume.
3. **A crew partner** in Abuja for anything that's filmed.
4. **A contract template**: deposit, milestones, revisions, kill fee.
5. **A proof project** at NGN 1–3M first, so the 10M pitch has a precedent.
