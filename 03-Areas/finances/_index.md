# Finances

## What this is

Samuel's money: freelance income (variable, split between NGN and USD, USD reaching NGN through Cleva), the budgeting system he runs it through, how he invoices clients, and what he charges for editing and mentorship. It does not hold banking credentials or account numbers — see AGENTS.md §6 — and it does not hold day-to-day task tracking, which lives in TickTick.

## Why it matters

Income is freelance and irregular by nature — variable per-client amounts, partial payments (a recurring 70/30 split, 70% at month-end and 30% mid-following-month), and two currencies. Without a system, that variability turns into guesswork about what's actually safe to spend. This area exists to remove that guesswork.

## Current status — as of 2026-09-20

Built from four Claude-export chats (`01-Inbox/_imports/processed/shortlist/finances/`), covering four separate threads: a percentage-based budget rebuild, one real client invoice, and two open questions about pricing (USD retainer clients; paid mentorship bookings). None of these chats confirm whether their proposals were ever adopted — that is the biggest gap in this area right now.

Separately, an **archived, unreviewed** set of money rules exists from the old Accountability Engine (`00-System/legacy-review.md`, items M1–M8, G2–G5) — a stricter, fixed-NGN-amount system (specific savings vehicles, a NGN 4,000,000 goal by 31 July 2027, a NGN 500,000 building-project rule). **None of it is active.** It predates and does not obviously reconcile with the percentage-based system in [[budget-system]] — notably, both systems name a "Building Project" but describe it differently (a fixed NGN 500,000 paid first, vs. a 27.7%-of-income bucket). That reconciliation is Samuel's to make, not assumed here.
