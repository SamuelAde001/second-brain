## ⚠️ Two name collisions, both deliberate, both dangerous by December

1. **Cowrywise holds two pots that mean opposite things** — the ring-fenced investment that never counts toward any goal (Rule 7), and the Goal 1 house money that *is* the goal. In December the app will show one blended balance. Two questions were raised and still need answering: **is Goal 1 a separate plan from the investment or the same one**, and **what is the maturity date?** The NGN 1M must be saved *and still there* on 31 Dec 2026 and then spendable on the house — a lock that runs past December protects the target and blocks its purpose.
2. **The account called "Emergency" holds the Buffer, not the emergency fund** — that pot does not start until January 2027. From January two different pots would share one account and the balance stops meaning anything.

Neither is wrong. Both read fine today and are unreadable in December.

## Does the NGN 1M close?

At the August mix, the plan closes December at roughly **NGN 1,004,000–1,007,000 against NGN 1,000,000** — a margin of a few thousand naira, with the buffer landing on NGN 200,000 the same month.

**Goal 1 is budgeted per month, not as a flat average** (set 2026-08-28). An average asked September for money September did not have, because September carried NGN 140,000 of one-offs the other months do not. Same finish line, four honest numbers instead of one convenient one.

That margin is thin on purpose. NGN 207,000 of margin with no buffer means the first urgency gets paid out of savings — which is exactly how NGN 595,250 disappeared. **Margin has never stopped a raid. A buffer does.**
