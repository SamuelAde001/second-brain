# Finances

## What this is

Samuel's money: one payer, two paydays, a fixed obligations floor, four pots and two dated goals. Plus the rules that exist because NGN 595,250 of "savings" evaporated in three months.

**No account numbers, card details or credentials live here** — accounts by nickname only, AGENTS.md §6.

## Why it matters

Income is variable, USD-denominated, paid in two stages, and comes entirely from **one payer whose volume decisions Samuel does not control.** The obligations floor is NGN 936,800/month and does not flex. Between those two facts sits every goal he has.
