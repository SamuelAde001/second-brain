## How something comes off the list — confirmed 2026-09-22

1. The month closes, with bills, one-offs, Cowrywise, the Buffer and Goal 1 all funded.
2. The surplus is real — the ledger says so, not the plan.
3. The item has a price on it.
4. It is logged like any other spend, and named here.

Anything that skips step 1 is not a purchase, it is a raid on savings — the shape of [[patterns|P3]]. A wish bought out of Goal 1 is the house move slipping ([[03-Areas/finances/finances-goals|Finances goals]]).

The engine's note on pricing: an unpriced want cannot be planned for, and "spare cash" plus an unpriced want is how an impulse gets justified after the fact. Price items as it becomes convenient.
