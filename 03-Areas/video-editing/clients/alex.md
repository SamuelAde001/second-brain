## The work

Long-form talking-head YouTube tutorials with live screen-recorded demo sections — B2B lead-generation and cold-outreach content, plus AI-ecommerce and dropshipping tutorials. Full pipeline: editorial cutting, Fusion motion graphics, colour, delivery prep.

Client footage typically arrives as **a single OBS capture** combining A-roll talking head and screen recording, and **the presenter freestyles significantly from the script** — which is why cut sheets key to the subtitle file rather than the script.
