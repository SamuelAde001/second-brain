## 3. Rhythm
- **Chaining.** Each visual ends exactly where the next starts, or on a V1 cut. There are no slivers of A-roll. The longest unbroken runs:
  - 0–49.4: the hook, 14 visuals.
  - 149.4–214.5: 9 UI renders back to back.
  - 243.7–391.2: a card, 3 UIs, 118 s of Tella, a card, a website.
- **Breathing.** About one plain A-roll gap per 46 s, median 3.6 s. Gaps of 1.1–1.5 s are only cut buffers between two visuals: 49.38, 223.64, 242.45. The long gaps (7–11 s) follow dense runs and carry caveats or the outro.
- **During plain A-roll nothing moves.** There are no subtitles, no zoom keyframes and no overlays; the jump cuts every ~2.6 s are the only motion. The one exception is the 20-frame zoom-out reveal at section returns. The V2 adjustment clips over these gaps (69.44, 214.46, 495.37, 583.54) render as pass-through.
- **Hold lengths:**
  - card reveal 2–4 s
  - UI render ~5.4 s
  - motion graphic 5–8 s (multi-step diagrams split into 3.5–8 s steps)
  - glass panel 6–16 s
  - caption 1.5–4.7 s
  - montage clips 0.75–2 s
- **Inside comps, every element lands on its spoken word.** Neo Anim InOffsets are set to word frames (see the triggers above). Elements stagger 3–6 frames apart in a set, and are spaced by the speech between items.
- **Tella stretches.** V4 is cut at every sentence (87 pieces). V5 holds a static punch-in per paragraph. V6 spotlights each named UI element (1.3–12.7 s, median ~3 s). The V8 bubble is re-rendered per cut.

## 5. Signature moves and taste
1. **Real over abstract, proven by what he switched off.** Disabled V3 comps at 195.86 ("PRODUCT → EXPERTISE") and 205.41 ("THEIR QUESTION → YOUR ANSWER") were replaced with real UI: a playbook doc, and a Google search that leads into a buying-signal checker. He also disabled the 4 separate recap-tile comps (575.99–583.54) for one combined comp, and a "HUMAN REVIEW" pill comp at 544.59.
2. **Real logos and real sites everywhere:**
   - 20 app tiles in the hook
   - logos in every card, hub and tile
   - live recordings of railway.com and wisprflow.ai
   - Anthropic's own launch video
   - Frontal's own homepage
3. **One reusable section device.** The same "THE 4 TOOLS" card set returns 5 times: teaser (mosaicked), each reveal, then all lit. It doubles as a progress bar.
4. **Hide what hasn't been said yet.** MosaicBlur on future tools, future steps and not-yet-found data, de-pixelated on the word.
5. **Two-tone on everything, with colour carrying meaning.** White setup, orange payoff word, red for negatives (✕, "DON'T ASSUME", "MOST IMPRESSIVE DEMO", filler words), green for success.
6. **Every element arrives with Neo Anim on its word**, staggered 3–6 f, then gets a 10-frame shine swipe. Premium stack on every pill and card: NeoGlow, static NeoLightSweep Pro, DropShadow, NeoBevel on cards, NeoReflection under cards. DreamGlow on the hero.
7. **Depth.** Glass panels refract and blur the footage behind; captions dim the footage (Gain 0.34); prompts dim and blur the UI; MagicMask puts graphics behind Alex.
8. **Overlays never cover his face.** Glass panels sit in the left third (x ≈ 0.18–0.21) or right third (x ≈ 0.76). Captions and the recap sit in the bottom band. Number graphics flank him.
9. **Section returns get the zoom-out-with-blur slam.** Plain A-roll otherwise stays untouched.
10. **Render in place for heavy comps** (the workflow graphics, the bubble). Editable Fusion stays for the rest.

What he never does here:
- no running subtitles
- no zoom keyframes on A-roll
- no animated zoom moves on screen recordings (static punch-ins re-set at cuts, plus spotlights)
- no out-animation on B-roll (it cuts straight to the next visual)
- no gaps or slivers between visuals
- no AI B-roll
- no non-orange grounds
- no labels or pills on UI renders (the only text over UI is the Box main claim at 6.17, the teaser title at 24.65 and the dictation overlays)
- no screenshot thumbnails standing in for logos
- stock footage only once, for a "busy founder" mood beat

- **The workflow (C19) and number (C20) comps are render-in-place outputs.** Their Fusion sources sit inside the Resolve project, not in the exported comps.
