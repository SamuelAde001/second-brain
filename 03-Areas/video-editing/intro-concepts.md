# Video intro visual concepts

Visual-concept brainstorming for the hook + Week 1 cost-breakdown of a talking-head video, and later the Week 2 intro of the same video — captured across one Claude conversation on 2026-08-20/21/24/25. **This is almost entirely proposed ideas, not decisions** (AGENTS.md §5.7) — very little here was confirmed by Samuel as final. Treat every visual idea below as a suggestion unless explicitly marked settled.

**Which project this belongs to isn't stated in the source conversation** — the video's own working content (a 4-week "$0 to $10,000 dropshipping using Claude as your team" structure) doesn't match Routerise/Frontal or any other client documented elsewhere in this vault. `status: needs-input` reflects that; this should be logged to `00-System/open-questions.md` in a future pass.

## What's settled (facts drawn from the video's own transcript/script, not visual ideas)

- The video Claude was reading from is structured as a 4-week plan. Per the video's *own* section titles (not Claude's invention): **Week 1 — Research, Week 2 — Build, Week 3 — Launch, Week 4 — Scale.**
- Section boundaries, per Claude's read of the uploaded transcript: Section 1–2 (hook + Week 1 cost breakdown) runs **00:00 → 01:08**, ending where the first screen recording begins. Section 11 (Week 2 intro, "Don't Lose to a Template") runs **07:46 → 09:20**, ending where the second screen recording begins.
- Samuel's explicit redirect, first round of ideas (2026-08-21): *"Don't worry about the assets and macros, I just need visual ideas for the hook and intro before the screen recording."* Concepts only, no macro-building — for that pass. Whether he wants the macros built now is not established here.
- A likely transcript/caption error was flagged in the video's own script, not corrected in the source: the line at the "one person trying to do the work of a handful of different jobs" beat reads **"…WHILE WE'RE A COMPLETE BEGINNER,"** almost certainly meant **"while they're a complete beginner"** (the surrounding sentence is about other people throughout). Worth checking before pulling on-screen text or burning captions from that line — not confirmed fixed.

## Proposed recurring visual system (not built)

Three "hero asset" concepts were proposed as reusable Fusion macros — build once, reuse across the video, rather than a one-off graphic per beat. **None were built or confirmed during this conversation** (see the redirect above).

| Asset | Concept | Where it would recur |
|---|---|---|
| **A — 30-day calendar** | 6×5 grid of 30 rounded squares; a proposed "active week" slider fills the current week's days in Claude-orange | Chapter card at the start of each week (00:06, 00:40, 07:52...) |
| **B — org chart** | One human node at top, connector lines down to job/agent nodes below; proposed "state" slider drives broken (red flicker, failing) vs. clean (Claude-orange, labelled) from one node group | The hook's "one person doing 4 jobs" beat, its "erased" turn, its "clean team" reveal, then echoed when the real subagents get built on screen later in the video |
| **C — three paths** | Three vertical lanes (Designer / Template / Claude), proposed per-lane state control (dim / highlighted / expanded / struck-through) | Week 2's "old ways vs. Claude" comparison beat; flagged as reusable for any later "old way vs. Claude way" beat (e.g. an ad-strategy comparison) |

Proposed palette for all three: Claude clay/terracotta on warm cream or near-black. White-on-dark for "broken/before" states, clay for "fixed/after" states — deliberately avoiding a third accent color so color alone carries the before/after argument.

## Section 1 — Hook (00:00–00:40), proposed beats

Condensed; full line-by-line detail lives only in the source conversation.

| Beat (line) | Proposed visual |
|---|---|
| "Not going to show you my car" / "convince you I'm rich" | Brief flash (4–8 frames) of real car/wealth B-roll, then a "reject" treatment — desaturate, freeze-frame with a red X, or a fast zoom-yank — before cutting back to plain talking head. The joke only works if it's real footage of him, not stock (see reaction note below). |
| "The exact 30-day plan..." | Asset A's debut — calendar draws on, then four week-blocks group under RESEARCH / BUILD / LAUNCH / SCALE labels |
| "Seven-figure e-commerce brand" | Restrained text only — **7 YEARS / 7 FIGURES** as a quick small lower-third hit, gone fast. Explicit flag: don't cut to a revenue dashboard here — he just told the audience he isn't going to prove he's rich, so proof imagery here would contradict the line he just said |
| "Hopeless store from zero to $10,000" | Two-part: a literal empty/placeholder Shopify store screen-record, then a counter animating $0 → $10,000. Proposed Fusion mechanic: Text+ with a keyframed Number slider and a comma-formatting expression, monospaced digits so they don't jitter width while rolling |
| "Isn't because they picked the wrong product" | Fast, unreadable scroll of generic product-research screenshots, then a hard stop and a strike-through/cross-out |
| "One person trying to do the work of a handful of jobs" ⭐ | Asset B, broken state — one avatar, connector lines to job boxes, one failure signal only (proposed: ticking unread-badge counters, "the quietest and reads fastest on a phone") |
| "But Claude just erased that part" | A wipe across the broken org chart, revealing clean underneath |
| "Now you've got a full team ready to be trained" | Asset B, clean state — same coordinates as the broken version, so it reads as the same chart repaired, not a new graphic. Labelled with the four roles the video actually builds later, so there's a recognition payoff when that happens on screen |
| "You just have to know how to hire and manage them" | No graphic — punch in and hold on him, a breath before the chapter break |

## Section 2 — Week 1 cost breakdown (00:40–01:08), proposed beats

- Chapter card: Asset A returns, Week 1 fills in, "WEEK 1" typeset large.
- Cost stack designed as a **thermal receipt** motif — narrow strip, monospace type, dotted rules, slight paper texture — proposed specifically as "the anti-Lamborghini," a tonal callback to the hook's restraint rather than a clean fintech card. Line items (Claude $20/mo, Shopify $1/mo, $250 ad budget, $100–200 buffer) print in one at a time, then a dotted rule draws and TOTAL counts up to ~$500 reusing the same counter mechanic from the hook.
- Handoff: receipt slides off, Asset B's clean chart returns briefly, then a push-through transition into the first screen recording.

## Week 2 intro (07:46–09:20), proposed beats

Condensed from the second round of ideas (2026-08-24), which reuses established motifs rather than inventing new ones — flagged in the source as the right call once a video's visual language is established.

- Recap: a callback cut to actual Week 1 screen-recording footage rather than a new graphic.
- Asset A returns, Week 2 block fills in.
- "My first store took weeks to learn" — proposed callback to the hook's "seven years ago" line via a desaturated, dated-UI mockup with a "7 YEARS AGO" corner label.
- The "uglier store that already launched" beat ⭐ — flagged as the strongest visual in this section: a split-screen race, "YOU" still spinning vs. "COMPETITOR" stamped LAUNCHED with a filling market-share bar. Proposed to reuse the hook's digit-roll counter mechanic for a days-elapsed counter, tying the two sections together without the viewer consciously noticing why.
- "The market rewards speed, not effort" — a restrained pull-quote beat (big kinetic type only, no icons), explicitly modeled on the hook's "7 YEARS / 7 FIGURES" restraint.
- Asset C's debut — Designer and Template lanes shown failing (proposed reuse of the hook's strike-through/cross-out gag on each, "a visual rhyme that tells the audience this is the same kind of wrong without saying it"), then both collapsing into the Claude lane.
- Handoff: push through the Claude lane into the second screen recording, reusing the same transition device as Section 2's handoff — proposed to become the video's standing scene-transition signature by its third use.

**Flags carried on this section:** 94 seconds of unbroken talking head (longer than the entire cold open) — proposed mitigation is a cutaway roughly every 6–8 seconds via the beats above, plus checking whether a B-cam exists in the client footage so some beats could run as a punch-in instead of a full graphic.

## Reaction note — the one real back-and-forth in this conversation

Samuel asked directly: *"I have B-rolls of his car and him being rich, should I show that at the hook, or I should use generic vids on internet?"*

Claude's recommendation: use his own real footage, not stock — because the hook line only lands as a joke if the footage is genuinely his to dismiss; cutting to a stranger's stock Lamborghini reads as a generic influencer-flex reference instead of self-aware restraint, and risks implying footage that isn't his is. Proposed treatment: a genuine flash (4–8 frames, well under a second) with a visible "reject" treatment (desaturate, freeze-frame with a strikethrough, or a fast zoom-yank) so it reads as dismissal rather than an actual flex.

**Not confirmed** — there is no reply from Samuel in this conversation after the recommendation, so this stays a proposal, not a decision.
