## The rules from this video

1. Visual frequency: a new visual every second in the hook, every 2 s in the intro, every 5-10 s in the body (15 s max).
2. Triple attack every claim: he says it, text says it, a graphic or real example shows it.
3. Darken behind every graphic and every text block (background footage dimmed) so it pops; use dark backgrounds with a gradient and glow.
4. Focus with the lighting trick + zoom trick: zoom to the spot, darken the rest, shine or unblur it. On text, use the double highlight: highlight plus darken (and blur) everything else.
5. Colour codes meaning: red (plus a hit SFX and shake in the hook) for negative, green for money, grey for dead, competitor or not used; white for text and graphics; real logos in their real colours.
6. Tease with blur: blurred chapter names, items and tools, plus a "?", then reveal later with zoom, darken, shine, unblur.
7. Title on top of every list or diagram so the viewer knows what they're looking at; chapter cards with number and title.
8. Constant moderate movement: slow zoom in or out, slow pans on every B-roll and still, smooth slide-on and slide-off; never static, never frantic.
9. Build for mobile: big bold text centred, one or two things on screen at a time, nothing small, keep the eye in the middle.
10. Real proof first: the creator's own footage, real screen recordings and real articles beat mocks; then AI B-roll made to match exactly what he says.
11. Give text texture: keyword colour, a subtle gradient, a second style for numbers or years, sometimes italic. Titles in all caps; numbers as 1, 2, not 01, 02.
12. Layer the aesthetic: concentric circles, shapes at different sizes and shades, glows, a slight side angle; solid custom icons, never hollow cheap ones.
13. Music: change the song at chapters or emotional shifts (roughly every 1-2 min), fade out and in, never mid-sentence; voice always above music; tension risers before reveals.
14. SFX: more in the hook, fewer after but still there; vary them and keep them quiet.
15. Logo in the corner of every tool B-roll; on screen recordings add zooms and lighting tricks.

## What this means for Samuel's Alex videos

- Agrees with the Frontal look: dark backgrounds, glowing boxes, white text, real logos, darken behind graphics, title-on-top cards. Frontal's blue can stand in where Nate's videos use teal-blue gradients; keep shades of one hue plus glows.
- Clash to manage: Nate's cards are rounded, angled glass with soft gradients and concentric circles. A square Frontal brand keeps its square boxes but can still borrow layering, shades and glows inside that shape. Colour coding (red, green, grey) has to sit on top of the blue base.
- Check every Alex timeline against the visual frequency numbers (1 s hook, 2 s intro, 5-10 s body) and the triple attack.
- Add the double highlight with darkening on every article or screenshot, blur-to-tease on lists and chapters, and a tool logo in the corner of screen recordings.
- Music: song changes only at chapter breaks with fades, voice always on top; numbers as 1, 2, never 01.
