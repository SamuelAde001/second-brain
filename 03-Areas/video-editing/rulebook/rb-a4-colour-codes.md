# A4 · Colour codes

> **Read when:** colouring sections after the cut, reading Samuel's markers on a review, or putting the Editor's own clips and markers on a timeline (Editor, sections step and every revision). **Client values:** none. **Status:** approved by Samuel rule by rule, 2026-11-04.

## Rules
### Section colours (clip colours)
- **A4.1** After the cut, split the video into sections and give each section its own clip colour, in one unbroken run of clips. 🔁 (3 times: 2026-09-24, 09-25, 10-06) ✅ *"Colour code each section of the video"* (2026-09-24) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A4.2** Clip colours are the map of the video: one colour per chapter, not a rotating cycle. 🎬 ✅ (2026-09-20) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A4.3** Split a section's talk from its demo with two colours (e.g. a tool's talk Blue, its demo Green). 🎬 (2026-09-25) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A4.4** Order in use on every job since 2026-09-25: Hook Yellow first; then Blue, Green, Purple, Teal, Navy, Tan, Lime, Brown, Olive, Orange as needed; Mid-CTA Pink; Outro or CTA Violet. Pink is kept for the Mid-CTA. ⚙️ 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A4.6** Music changes on the same frame as the section colour (D1). 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A4.7** The transcript doc uses the same section colours (A5). ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-04

### Content colours
- **A4.19** Content colours for clips above the A-roll, chosen by the Editor at Samuel's word: Orange = motion graphic (Fusion comp, text, list, card); Teal = screen recording or UI demo; Navy = website, article or screenshot page; Green = client B-roll or stock; Lime = AI B-roll; Olive = YouTube / IG / TikTok pull; Tan = logo; Purple = graph or stat; Violet = section card or chapter pill; Apricot = adjustment clip (zoom, media adj, highlight); Chocolate = 3D element. Audio: Yellow = SFX, Brown = music. Pink stays only for the Mid-CTA section (A4.18). Tracks have no colours (Q-A4.c). ✅ *"colours can have meaning, you can choose"* (2026-11-04) · ✔ 2026-11-04

### The Editor's own colours
- **A4.8** Clip colours on the visual tracks say **what is on the clip**, not who made it: the content colours in A4.19. The Editor's visuals still sit in the bin `Fusion Comps and Adjustments / Claude visuals vN (Editor)`. ✅ *"I want more variety so it can tell me what is on the clip rather than if Claude did it"* (2026-11-04) · ✏ 2026-11-04
- **A4.9** The Editor's markers: blue "Lip-sync check" and green "Screen sync check" on the raw sync timeline only (A2.20). Nothing else of the Editor's is left on a timeline he reviews, except attention markers (A4.15, A4.17). ⚙️ (section duration markers dropped with A4.5) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A4.10** Doubtful takes: decide yourself. Leave no colour flags or markers on cut clips for him to judge. ✅ *"clutter"* (2026-09-25) Replaces the same morning's "leave it on the timeline with a different color" (2026-09-25). · ✔ 2026-11-04

### Samuel's markers
- **A4.11** His markers are instructions to act on. The instruction sits in the marker name, sometimes in the description. A marker with a duration shows where the visual goes. 🔁 (6 times, 2026-09-27 to 10-03) ✅ *"on red markers, I wrote the corrections on the names of the markers and some in the description"* (2026-09-28) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A4.12** Red was his correction colour on the first jobs; on later reviews he used Blue (LinkedIn v5, video 2 revisions 3 and 4). So colour is no filter: snapshot the markers before work, and treat every marker added after that snapshot as a request. ⚙️ (2026-10-29) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A4.13** His blue markers can also mark a note or a section start (build, demo start). Read the name before acting. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A4.14** Read his markers on the timeline he wrote them on: copied to a duplicate, they sat 96 frames late. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-04

### Marker palette
- **A4.15** The Editor's own marker colours don't matter to him. To bring his attention to something he should check, use a **red** marker named `Editor: <what to check>`, so it can't be mistaken for one of his. Marker colours are not clip colours (there is no Orange marker); one marker per frame. ✅ *"if you want to bring my attention to check something you can use red markers"* (2026-11-04) · ✏ 2026-11-04

## Never (strict)
- **A4.16** Colours carry only the meanings in this section: section colours (A4.4) and content colours (A4.19). Never use a colour for anything else, and never change a meaning without his word. ✅ *"colours can have meaning, you can choose"* (2026-11-04) · ✏ 2026-11-04
- **A4.17** Never leave review flags or doubt colours on a cut for him to sort out. A red attention marker (A4.15) is allowed to point him at an area, but only after the Editor has already acted on it. ✅ *"you can put markers though to bring my attention to an area, but act on it before then"* (2026-11-04) · ✏ 2026-11-04
- **A4.18** Never use Pink for anything but the Mid-CTA section. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-04

## Questions for Samuel
- **Q-A4.a** Should section colours mean something? Your 7M final opened on a Purple hook (2026-09-14); a meaning set (Purple hook and outro, Orange problem, Yellow teaching, Teal demo, Pink emphasis, Blue client B-roll) was proposed 2026-09-20 and never approved; every job since 2026-09-25 runs a fixed order (Hook Yellow … Outro Violet). → Options: (1) keep the fixed order (2) colours by meaning. My pick: (1). → Samuel: colours can have meaning, the Editor chooses (2026-11-04): sections keep the order in A4.4; clips carry the content colours in A4.19.
- **Q-A4.b** Doubtful takes: "leave it with a different color, I would handle it" and "clutter, decide" were both said 2026-09-25; the Editor profile still says ask on genuine judgement calls. → Options: (1) never flag on the timeline; decide, and list the hard calls in the report (2) Orange clip colour only for true judgement calls. My pick: (1). → Samuel: answered by A3.50 (2026-11-03): decide, then ask in chat with the timeline timecode; nothing flagged on the timeline.
- **Q-A4.c** Track colours: nothing in the sources gives tracks a colour code. → Options: (1) no track colours (2) you name a scheme. My pick: (1). → Samuel: no track colours (2026-11-04).
