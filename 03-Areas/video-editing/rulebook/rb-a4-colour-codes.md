# A4 · Colour codes

> **Read when:** colouring sections after the cut, reading Samuel's markers on a review, or putting the Editor's own clips and markers on a timeline (Editor, sections step and every revision). **Client values:** none. **Status:** draft, not yet approved by Samuel.

## Rules
### Section colours (clip colours)
- **A4.1** After the cut, split the video into sections and give each section its own clip colour, in one unbroken run of clips. 🔁 (3 times: 2026-09-24, 09-25, 10-06) ✅ *"Colour code each section of the video"* (2026-09-24)
- **A4.2** Clip colours are the map of the video: one colour per chapter, not a rotating cycle. 🎬 ✅ (2026-09-20)
- **A4.3** Split a section's talk from its demo with two colours (e.g. a tool's talk Blue, its demo Green). 🎬 (2026-09-25)
- **A4.4** Order in use on every job since 2026-09-25: Hook Yellow first; then Blue, Green, Purple, Teal, Navy, Tan, Lime, Brown, Olive, Orange as needed; Mid-CTA Pink; Outro or CTA Violet. Pink is kept for the Mid-CTA. ⚙️ 🎬
- **A4.5** Put one duration marker per section on the sections timeline, named after the section, in the nearest marker colour. Samuel removes the section markers at approval and keeps the clip colours; his finals carry no markers. 🎬 ⚙️
- **A4.6** Music changes on the same frame as the section colour (D1). 🎬
- **A4.7** The transcript doc uses the same section colours (A5). ⚙️

### The Editor's own colours
- **A4.8** The Editor's rendered visuals and Fusion carrier clips are clip colour Apricot, on their own track, in the bin `Fusion Comps and Adjustments / Claude visuals vN (Editor)`. ⚙️
- **A4.9** The Editor's markers: blue "Lip-sync check" and green "Screen sync check" on the raw sync timeline only (A2.20); section duration markers on the sections timeline. Nothing else of the Editor's is left on a timeline he reviews. ⚙️
- **A4.10** Doubtful takes: decide yourself. Leave no colour flags or markers on cut clips for him to judge. ✅ *"clutter"* (2026-09-25) Replaces the same morning's "leave it on the timeline with a different color" (2026-09-25).

### Samuel's markers
- **A4.11** His markers are instructions to act on. The instruction sits in the marker name, sometimes in the description. A marker with a duration shows where the visual goes. 🔁 (6 times, 2026-09-27 to 10-03) ✅ *"on red markers, I wrote the corrections on the names of the markers and some in the description"* (2026-09-28)
- **A4.12** Red was his correction colour on the first jobs; on later reviews he used Blue (LinkedIn v5, video 2 revisions 3 and 4). So colour is no filter: snapshot the markers before work, and treat every marker added after that snapshot as a request. ⚙️ (2026-10-29)
- **A4.13** His blue markers can also mark a note or a section start (build, demo start). Read the name before acting. ⚙️
- **A4.14** Read his markers on the timeline he wrote them on: copied to a duplicate, they sat 96 frames late. ⚙️

### Marker palette
- **A4.15** Marker colours are not clip colours. There is no Orange marker. Markers: Blue, Cyan, Green, Yellow, Red, Pink, Purple, Fuchsia, Rose, Lavender, Sky, Mint, Lemon, Sand, Cocoa, Cream. One marker per frame (adding a second fails). ⚙️

## Never
- **A4.16** Never give colours a meaning (e.g. Purple = hook, Teal = demo) until Samuel approves one (Q-A4.a). 💡
- **A4.17** Never leave review flags, check markers or doubt colours on a cut for him to sort out. ✅ (2026-09-25)
- **A4.18** Never use Pink for anything but the Mid-CTA section. ⚙️
