# C1 · Aesthetics and the quality bar

> **Read when:** planning a new look, reviewing any beat or a whole cut, or deciding whether a visual is good enough to send. Every agent. **Client values:** none here. Colours, fonts and box shape are in the client overlays (K2 Alex/Frontal, K3 Gamma). **Status:** approved by Samuel, 2026-11-05 (issues answered one by one, the rest approved in one go).

## Rules
### The bar
- **C1.1** Aim for zero corrections: a first version that neither Samuel nor the creative director would correct. 🔁 (4: 10-20, 10-26, 10-28, 11-01) ✅ *"I don't want to make any corrections at all. I don't want to make one single correction."* (2026-10-20) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.2** The bar is his latest approved final for that client, done in the current brand. For Alex: the LinkedIn Training final is still the best so far, redone in Frontal branding; Cold Email Goldrush is the reference for how the new branding should look. *"Linkedin still the best so far but cold email did good to for his new branding"* (2026-11-05) For Gamma: see C16.3. ✅ *"I want the quality of that video, but in this video and with good branding"* (2026-10-28) · ✏ 2026-11-05
- **C1.3** Every video beats the one before it. Quality never drops between videos. 🔁 (3) ✅ *"We're not dropping quality ... we need to top up what we've done and become better and better."* (2026-10-13) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.4** Video 2 (10,000 SaaS) is a starting point to build on, not the bar. ✅ *"Though video 2 is done, I am still not happy with the output"* (2026-10-28) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.5** His earlier finals ($7M Founder, TSB, 4 AI Tools, Apollo, AI B2B V2) still define his constructs. Build on them, don't stop at them, and don't repeat one design beat after beat. ✅ *"the finished videos of the last 3 are okay, just that we need more better and different types"* (2026-09-30) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.6** Every past correction is a standing rule. Never make him correct the same thing twice. Never bring back something he rejected. 🔁 (4) ✅ *"Anything I've corrected on in the past, right? I don't want to recorrect them."* (2026-10-20); *"If I have rejected something in the past, don't repeat them"* (2026-10-03) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.7** Quality never drops later in the video. The last minute gets the same care as the hook. 🏢 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.8** A new brand changes only the look: colours, fonts, shapes and the brand's own rules. His editing fundamentals, constructs and finish stay the same. 🔁 (4) ✅ *"the fundamentals of how I edit my videos must still kind of remain the same ... I don't want to see random."* (2026-10-21) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.9** A brand guide is never an excuse for a plain visual. Take the brand's style and craft it up to his standard. ✅ *"using their brand guide doesn't mean you should do very medicocre looking visuals, use the style and work on it"* (2026-10-21) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### What his words mean
- **C1.10** **Cheap** = brand shapes with no craft: a greyed page with text on top, one flat layer per frame, small type on a big field, new devices instead of his own constructs. ✅ *"the visuals look cheap bro"* (2026-10-21) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.11** **Mediocre / shallow / stale / basic** = the same design and colour ratio every beat, small cards with tiny text, abstract metaphors (seesaw, gauge, coins), generic boxes, misaligned text, no depth. 🔁 (8+) ✅ *"You tend to use the same Design every time, the same color ratio"* (2026-09-30) 🎯 *"very mediocre… shallow… didn't have depth"* (2026-09-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.12** **Flat** = no depth, and flat is what makes a video look AI-made. Depth comes from real effect nodes (shine, light sweep, glow, glass) and from layered shapes in different sizes and shades. Where and how much: C7. ✅ *"It looks like AI did it, right? Because it's flat."* (2026-10-22) 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.13** **AI edited** = static: everything on screen at once or popping on at random, plain boxes in a row, flat boxes with no sheen. In his edits every element is animated on its own and lands on its word. 🔁 (2) ✅ *"Nothing just comes on screen randomly. Everything is animated individually and well-timed."* (2026-10-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.14** **Looks AI** also = bouncing or overshoot animations, too much text, random text or text boxes popping up, and devices he never uses (stickers, toasts, checklists, counter chips, float pills, marker sweeps). ✅ *"the visuals all look AI, with bouncing animations and too much text"* (2026-11-01) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.15** **Abandoned my style** = inventing a style for a client. Being creative means new combinations inside his styles, his library, the CD teachings and the node studies, never things he doesn't do. 🔁 (3: 10-06, 10-14, 11-01) ✅ *"being creative doesn't mean you should do things I don't normally do in any of my videos"* (2026-11-01) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.16** **Subpar / not up to standard** = below the last approved video, even when it's "a good job". ✅ *"you've done a good job on the intro, but it's still not up to standard"* (2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.17** **Vague** = abstract. Make it concrete: real dashboards with real-looking values, real UI, real logos. ✅ *"very, very bad. I need something much more concrete"* (2026-10-10) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### What premium looks like (his finals)
- **C1.18** Show the real thing (UI, web page, logo, B-roll) before an icon-and-text graphic. Show what is said. Concept-arrow graphics were swapped for real UI in his finals. 🎬 ✅ *"show and not tell"* (2026-10-31) → C8 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.19** Few words: chips of 1-4 words, never a sentence on a card. Text that only repeats what's on screen gets deleted. 🎬 → C4 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.20** Big, bold and centred, made for a phone: big type, cards that fill a side or the frame. Never small, dark, sparse or off-centre. ✅ *"viewers are watching this on their mobile phone, so things need to be more bolder and more specific"* (2026-10-22) 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.21** Depth on every element, from real stacked effect nodes: glass for panels over the speaker, glow on borders and rings, light sweep, drop shadow. Layers stack (zoomed A-roll + card beside him + caption + vignette). Nothing faked. 🎬 → B3, C7 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.22** One focus at a time. The viewer looks at one element; the rest are dimmed (Gain ~0.5). ✅ *"the veiwer needs to focus on one thing at a time"* (2026-10-24) 🎬 → C6 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.23** Overlays never cover his face. They sit beside or around him, never parked in a corner. 🎬 → B1 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.24** Something always moves, gently: a slow push on every shot and graphic (1 → 1.04-1.05). Never static, never jittery. 🎬 🎯 → C2, B5 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.25** Smooth, slow and easy to follow. Never several things at once, never lots of fast things. ✅ *"I need things to be smooth. I need things to be slow and easy for the viewer to follow"* (2026-10-24) → C15 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.26** Every visual looks unique, makes sense and still looks cool. Vary pills, boxes, circles and colour ratios across the video. 🔁 (6+) ✅ *"Each visual should look unique"* / *"You visuals should make sense but still look cool"* (2026-10-03) → C3, C5 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.27** Every effect, cut, caption and B-roll has a job: help the viewer understand or feel something. No decoration. ✅ *"every effect, every cut, every caption, every B-roll should have a function"* (2026-10-19) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.28** Edit toward the client's goal (sales or views) and the target audience, not toward flashiness. ✅ *"put into consideration the target audience"* (2026-10-20) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.29** Colour carries meaning. Backgrounds are never flat black. 🎬 → C5 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.30** The look itself is a quality lever: glow, contrast, layering and shades make the agency look recognisable. Graphics must look custom. 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.31** Keep comps simple (median 3 tools in $7M). Save big set-piece builds for 3-4 hero moments a video. 💡 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.32** The three-dimensional look, layered effects and creative lines are tools to use when they help, not on every beat. ✅ *"making things look 3D when needed ... Adding some layers of effects when needed"* (2026-10-20) → C11 · ✔ 2026-11-05

### When a visual fails
- **C1.33** If a visual confuses, is badly synced or adds nothing, delete it and leave A-roll. A-roll beats a weak visual. 🔁 (5) ✅ *"remove this visual, I don't like it,not well synced, just show A-roll instead"* (2026-10-15) 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.34** Remove elements nobody needs (decor walls, handshakes, extra icons, boxes he didn't ask for, extra lines). 🔁 (4) ✅ *"I don't like those inititail boxes you added"* (2026-10-25) → C3 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.35** Every visual shows something meaningful. When unsure, scroll the real page and write its main text as an overlay. ✅ *"it doesn't show anything meaningful, just scroll through his page"* (2026-10-14) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Before anything leaves (the review questions)
- **C1.36** Ask his three questions of every visual: (1) Is this what the best video editors make? (2) Would my creative director like it? (3) Does it belong on a 10M-follower channel? (He named "Dan Mattel", most likely Dan Martell.) ✅ *"does this look like the best video editors type of creative video? does this look llike something my creative director would like"* (2026-10-21) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.37** Then ask "would Samuel correct this?" Render the range and look as he would: size, readability, overlap with him, empty space, edges touching, text alignment, dimensions. 🔁 (3) ✅ *"ask, would Samuel correct this, pay attention to little details"* (2026-10-03) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.38** Then ask "does this look like Samuel cut it?" A rule check alone doesn't catch an edit that looks AI-made. ✅ *"this edit doesn't look like mine, it looks AI edited"* (2026-10-25) → E2 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.39** Cross-check against his approved finals and the top videos in his company. 🔁 (3) ✅ *"crosscheck your design with the top videos in my company"* (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.40** A polished cut goes to the creative director, so it has no mistakes. ✅ *"Make sure there is no mistakes as it is polished cause i would send it to my creative director"* (2026-10-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.41** Keep going until it matches his finals. Time is not the limit (token rules: F3). ✅ *"Don't worry about time, don't stop till you have a perfect video"* (2026-10-07) · ✔ 2026-11-05

## Never (strict)
- **C1.42** The same design or colour ratio repeated beat after beat. ❌ *"We are repeating this bad visuals everytime"* (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.43** Generic boxes, or plain boxes in a row. ❌ *"You are using the same generic boxes, be more creative"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.44** Small text, no logos and no polish. ❌ *"we don't have small text like thism and no logos or shines"* (2026-10-14) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.45** Effects with no reason (shines everywhere). ❌ *"you put shines wrongly all throughout the video without any reason"* (2026-10-24) → C7 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.46** Two things happening on screen at once. ❌ (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.47** Bouncing animations, too much text, invented devices. ❌ (2026-11-01) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.48** One client's designs copied onto another. ❌ *"I told you to make better designs not copy frontals designs"* (2026-10-31) → C16 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.49** Never overuse an old template. Reusing one is fine; don't lean on the same one again and again. ✅ *"you can use an old template, just make it not overused"* (2026-11-05) · ✏ 2026-11-05
- **C1.50** Placeholder logos, misaligned text, text outside its box. ❌ (2026-10-07) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C1.51** Major motion graphics go full screen on their own main background (K3: the Gamma navy), centred: diagrams, roadmaps, money runs, crowds and groups, any multi-element build. Only minor visuals sit on the A-roll: lists, pills, chapter pills. ✅ *"Ensure major motion graphics is on fullscreen with it's own main BG and centered / Only minor visuals on screen like lists, pills or chapters"* (2026-11-05; the small group, the roadmap and the 4-a-year run were on her screen). Refines Q-C1.a.
- **C1.52** Spread minor visuals around her: left, right and bottom, not always the right side. ✅ *"looks like you are putting a lot of visuals by just the right side, and not left or down on her screen"* (2026-11-05)
