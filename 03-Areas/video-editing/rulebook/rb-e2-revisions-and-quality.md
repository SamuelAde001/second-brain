# E2 · Revisions and quality management

> **Read when:** at the start of every job (the bar and "never repeat a correction"), whenever Samuel leaves markers or the CD sends notes, before any version goes to him, and on delivery day (end-of-job review). Every builder and reviewer. **Client values:** none. **Status:** approved by Samuel, 2026-11-05 (issues answered one by one, the rest approved in one go).

## Rules
### The bar
- **E2.1** The goal is zero revisions: no corrections from Samuel and none from the Route Rise CD. Videos before video 2 had none. 🔁 ×4 ✅ *"a great quality Job that never needs my correction nor the creative directors corrections, that is the biggest token saver"* (2026-10-28) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.2** Why it pays: Route Rise pays a bonus of USD 50 for a no-correction video and USD 25 for under 5 corrections. ✅ (2026-10-30) 🏢 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.3** Polished before it leaves: cross-check everything so he has nothing to correct. 🔁 ×6 ✅ *"Please make sure you cross-check the works and don't make any mistakes that I will have to correct."* (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.4** Catch faults in the plan, not after the timeline is built: late corrections make the video worse. ✅ *"I don't want to have to make corrections that will make the video look bad."* (2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.5** This rulebook is the number one rule unless he says otherwise. Don't guess: if it is silent, ask (or pick, flag it and tell him). ✅ *"This rule book would be your number one rule, unless I tell you not to"* (2026-11-03) See Q-E2.a. · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.6** Verify facts that appear in visuals (which apps the client really uses, names, numbers) before building. ✅ *"are you sure this are the apps. he uses in his prospecting system? crosscheck that"* (2026-10-01) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Never make him repeat a correction
- **E2.7** Anything he corrected in any past video applies to every later video. Read every past correction before you start. 🔁 ×6 ✅ *"Anything I corrected in the last video or in the last previous videos, I should not repeat myself."* (2026-10-13) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.8** Write every new correction down the same session: one dated line on [[03-Areas/video-editing/samuel-review-card|Samuel's review card]] and the rule in its rulebook section. A rule that lives only in chat is lost. 🔁 ×3 ✅ *"take note of everything I said so if I say resume in another session, you won't do this rubbish again"* (2026-10-21) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.9** The same fix needed twice means the rule did not land where the Editor reads it. Move it there. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05

- **E2.43** Whenever the Editor asks Samuel about, or reports on, a spot in the video, it names the timeline timecode (and the timeline), never only a beat id, clip number or marker number: he finds things by time. ✅ *"when asking me for something ussure mentioned the time in the sequence not the beat, cause I can easiliy find things by time stamp not numbers or beat, but make sure you decide but ask me in chat"* (2026-11-03) · ✔ 2026-11-03

### Reading his markers
- **E2.10** His markers on version N are the brief for N+1. Implement every one, track each to done, and learn from them how he wants the work done. 🔁 ×3 ✅ *"look through the corrections I made and understand how you, I really want you to work"* (2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.11** Read all markers first, in context, before planning: a point marker vs a range marker, the words at that moment and a still at each marker. Know which visual he means. Then plan, then fix. 🔁 ×2 ✅ *"Some I put a marker over a range. You have to understand what is that, what's the visual"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.12** A correction is a pattern: apply it everywhere in the video it fits, marked or not, and in the next videos. He often marks only the first part because notes repeat. 🔁 ×8 ✅ *"if I corrected one place and you think that correction might be needed in other places ... repeat that correction"* (2026-10-10) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.13** An earlier marker applies to later beats in the same video. 🔁 ~10 in one video ✅ *"Whatever you changed in the intro change it here too"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.14** Don't change what he didn't correct, beyond carrying a correction to similar places. ✅ *"no need to change those things I didn't correct explicitly."* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.15** "Make it premium" means keep the design he liked and raise its finish. Never redesign it. 🔁 ×5 ✅ *"I liked the design of the v1 I only said make it premium"* (2026-09-27) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Running a revision
- **E2.16** Revisions are top priority. ✅ *"Revisions happen and can't be controlled, but they become top priority."* (2026-09-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.17** Revise what is built. Never redo the video from scratch; rebuild only a visual that needs a complete redo. 🔁 ×3 ✅ *"I don't want us redoing this video from scratch"* (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.18** After the revision plan, give him a time estimate for the whole revision. ✅ *"How long is the whole revision going to take?"* (2026-10-10) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.19** He fixes small things on the timeline himself and marks the main ones. Take a baseline snapshot, stay off Resolve until he says done, then diff his changes (`finish\tl_snapshot.py`, `tl_diff.py`) and read his markers. ✅ *"I would be making some adjustments myself on the timeline itself for things I can fix myself"* (2026-10-29) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.20** Learn from his diff, then still sweep the whole video for anything he missed. ✅ *"still go through the whole video incase there is something I missed"* (2026-09-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.21** Gather his notes and your own check findings into ONE round. He gives small corrections in one batch. Re-render only the chunks touched. ✅ *"I want to do some small corrections so that you can add them at once"* (2026-10-29) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.22** Close to final: use the fixes already done, leave the rest for his last review rather than holding him up. ✅ (2026-10-16) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.23** CD notes: they arrive as Frame.io comments (logged in on his browser). They count as his own corrections. Write each down, understand its context, ask him if unclear; a ticked comment is already done by him. Carry them into the job in progress too. ✅ *"go through all the revisions, write them down and understand the context of it"* (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.24** Order when sources disagree: his corrections and markers > CD standard > house notes > client brand kit (see K0). ✅ (2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### The checks (before anything reaches him)
- **E2.25** Cross-checking stays. Scripts may replace a reviewer agent only if they catch as much; a look at readable size stays for taste. ✅ *"if the scripts can do the checking as well as the agents did, that's fine"* (2026-10-12) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.26** Independent review: builders don't see their own faults. A fresh reviewer judges each beat's rendered stills against the review card and the job card. Self-review alone is rejected. ✅ *"The reason the verify is needed is so we don't get mistakes"* (2026-10-28) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.27** Per beat, in order: `beat_lint.py` + `checks\card_check.py` + `checks\accent_scan.py` (no tokens) → Sonnet checklist reviewer on the filmstrip (pass/fail with frame evidence; taste items marked unsure) → Opus section pass on the whole section. Never re-review a passed beat; after a fix re-check only what changed. ⚙️ Who runs what and the calibration: [[00-System/model-routing|model routing]] → On Max 20x. · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.28** Verify every blocking fault with Opus before writing its fix (24 % of flagged faults on video 2 were false). Reviewers mark faults `blocking` or `cd-risk`, taste `unsure`, and never fix while reviewing. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.29** A render or a rendered still is the only proof. Never call a fix done from file checks; check the install list, not the folder (video 2 shipped a fix that was never installed). ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.30** Look at real frames: stills from the timeline render at landing, middle and end of every visual (take heavy comps twice; the first frame can be stale), one contact sheet per beat. Never hand the visual check to Samuel. ✅ *"always check your work with the view"* (2026-09-27) How to grab stills safely: [[03-Areas/video-editing/sops/fusion-build|Fusion build]] §5, [[03-Areas/video-editing/resolve-automation-lessons|Resolve automation lessons]]. · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.31** Install gates and render scans (identity, raw line breaks, chain, crosscheck, timeline check, flash and media-offline scan): run them after every install and every render. Fix media offline before review. ✅ *"There is media offline showing around here"* (2026-10-25) Commands: [[03-Areas/video-editing/sops/video-pipeline-tools|video pipeline tools]]. · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.32** Report what you saw, never a guessed cause. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Versions and hand-over
- **E2.33** Reviews come in finished versions; he does not babysit steps. ✅ *"I want to do it all at once and have reviews in versions"* (2026-10-13) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.34** Each build is a new timeline, `Visuals vN (Editor)`. The version he marked stays as his reference, untouched. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.35** Done means: built, reviewed, installed, gates pass, stills sheets looked at. Then send him the stills and a short report (what changed, what is unsure, what broke, which timeline to open, every open question with the default chosen). Leave the playhead on something for him to check. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.36** Render naming and delivery: [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-e1-rendering|E1]]. · ✔ 2026-11-05

### End of job
- **E2.37** On delivery day run the end-of-job review (~15 min): a `Fixes: N (setup a · cut b · visuals c · Fusion d · other e)` line; each new rule folded into one home and the review card; scripts sorted; comps he approved unchanged harvested into the client library; job cost recorded. ✅ (Samuel's yes, 2026-09-28) Steps: [[03-Areas/video-editing/sops/end-of-job-review|end-of-job review]]. · ✔ 2026-11-05

## Never (strict)
- **E2.38** Never make him repeat a correction. ❌ 🔁 ×6 ✅ *"I don't want to find myself repeating myself in future videos."* (2026-10-16) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.39** Never change what he didn't mark (beyond carrying a correction). ❌ ✅ (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.40** Never rebuild from scratch on a revision. ❌ ✅ (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.41** Never guess. ❌ ✅ *"if I tell Editor to fix the video it would make a mess again cause it is doing guess work"* (2026-11-03) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **E2.42** Never let the CD see an unfinished version. Ifeoma never touches Route Rise edits. ❌ ✅ (2026-10-28) · ✔ 2026-11-05

## Questions for Samuel
- **Q-E2.a** When the rulebook is silent, what should an agent do? You said "never guess" (2026-11-03), but overnight runs can't wait for you. → Options: (1) stop and ask; (2) pick the closest rule, flag it in the report, keep going; (3) (2) overnight, (1) when you are at the PC. My pick: (3). → Samuel: (3), overnight pick the closest rule, flag it and keep going; stop and ask when he is at the PC (2026-11-05).
- **Q-E2.b** Review card vs rulebook. The card says *"If this card and a long note disagree, the long note wins"* (2026-10-24). Now the rulebook is the number one rule. → Options: (1) the rulebook is canonical; the card becomes its one-page digest, both updated the same session; (2) retire the card. My pick: (1); reviewers need the short version. → Samuel: (1), the rulebook is canonical; the review card is its one-page digest, both updated the same session (2026-11-05).
- **Q-E2.c** Version names for deliveries to Route Rise: only "v1/v2" as you name them. See Q-E1.b. → Samuel: settled by Q-E1.b: `<video title> v<N>.mp4` (2026-11-05).
