# C8 · Screen recordings, demos, screenshots, UI, articles, websites

> **Read when:** planning or building any beat that shows a tool, app, website, article, social post or call; reviewing those beats. **Client values:** none here; brand values live in the client overlays (K2 Alex, K3 Gamma). **Status:** approved by Samuel, 2026-11-05 (issues answered one by one, the rest approved in one go).

## Rules
### Real, full screen, a live demo
- **C8.1** Show, don't tell. When the speaker names or describes a tool, website, workflow or action, show it on screen in its real UI, unasked. ✅🔁4 *"My priority is always show and not just tell"* (2026-10-06); *"Show real UI as we normally do"* (2026-10-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.2** Every screen recording looks like a live demo walking the viewer through: cursor clicks, dropdowns, typing, pages opening, timed to his words. Static UI shots that don't demo are rejected. ✅🔁3 *"properly demoed with cursor clicking on it, drop downs, interactions"* (2026-10-31) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.3** Full screen, as it looks when browsing: the page viewport only. No Chrome tabs, address bar, Windows UI, wallpaper, floating or tilted window, half-screen page. ✅🔁8 *"it must all be fullscreen of the UI visuals."* (2026-10-13); *"make this visal page full screen than half"* (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.4** The UI is one-to-one with the real product, down to the last dot: real layout, real icons, real logos. ✅🔁4 *"The UI must be accurate down to the last dot"* (2026-10-20); *"the UI must be one-to-one identical"* (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.5** The standard is the screen recordings in the videos before video 2 (B2B, LinkedIn). ✅ *"those screen recordings were good."* (2026-10-28) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.6** Motion graphics only where no real UI is needed to explain. Judging which is which is part of the job. ✅ *"when he's explaining something that doesn't necessarily need a real UI, then you will use motion graphics"* (2026-10-06) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.7** Prefer real visuals over abstract graphics: call recordings, team, notes, shown full screen. ✅ *"I want more real visuals, screen recordings, showing things"* (2026-10-03) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.8** A UI demo replaces the speaker: take his video off the screen while it runs. ✅🔁3 *"Remove his video from the screen"* (2026-10-02) (see Q-C8.b) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Where the UI comes from, in this order
- **C8.9** (1) A live capture in his signed-in Chrome (Flow and logged-in sites allowed). (2) The product's real screenshots from Google Images, vendor help-centre images first, none with arrows, callouts or marketing frames. (3) A 1:1 HTML rebuild, measured off a real screenshot, for private screens, things that don't exist, or a demo that needs a scripted cursor. ✅🔁3 *"always use my browser"* / *"Get UI screenshots from Google"* (2026-09-27); *"Yes you can use flow and all the sites needed"* (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.10** Real screenshots with highlights beat a mock-up that only looks like the app (TSB: Claude's mock-ups switched off). 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.11** "Build a UI demo" means build it from scratch to look like the real product. Never cut it from the client's own recording (Tella) or copy his visuals unless Samuel names them. The Tella is a source of facts only. ✅🔁4 *"I said build your own UI Demo from scratch, you are busy using Alexes Demo."* (2026-10-02) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.12** A system with no screen of its own (GTM OS) gets an original app UI, dark mode by default, not a cursor page. ✅ *"Generate a UI that shows the GTM OS, not a cursur page ... make it dark mode also"* (2026-10-02) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.13** Show the tool the way the client uses it. Claude Code = the Mac Claude desktop app, Code view, light theme, not a terminal. Rebuilt apps (Gmail etc.) are checked 1:1 against the real one. ✅ *"Desktop app, as Alex uses it"* (2026-10-20) ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.14** Show the real product surface, never a cropped marketing illustration. Pages behind a bot check are not bypassed: use a stat card with its source. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.15** Reuse UI captures already made when they make sense and are well timed. ✅ *"all the UIs that you've actually generated before that makes sense and is well timed, you can still reuse them"* (2026-10-31) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.16** Put real full-frame UI on any A-roll stretch that talks about a UI, without being asked. ✅ (2026-09-27) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Framing, zoom and movement
- **C8.17** Frame like a live demo: not too zoomed in, not too zoomed out. Keep enough context to know where you are; focus on the part he talks about. ✅🔁3 *"I don't want it to be too zoomed in, but also not too zoomed out"* (2026-10-26); *"please zoom out a bit"* (2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.18** Screen standard, measured on the approved LinkedIn captures: read text 30-52 px at 1080 from frame 1; 1.6-1.9 frame px per CSS px (record 1536 CSS px at 2880x1620, crop about 1.42x); the read line spans 45-80 % of the width and sits at least 40 px from the edges; each page shot 2.5-8 s. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.19** Scroll slowly and cleanly, readable: hold, then a 16-32 f eased move, then hold at least 48 f. ✅ *"not too fast, but not too slow"* (2026-10-15) 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.20** Cursor only for actions; smooth and unhurried; it lands at least 3 f before the word. ✅ *"nothing should be too fast and everything must be very smooth"* (2026-09-27) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.21** Treatment on every screen recording: highlight the focus area (the rest darkened), cursor movement, and the adjustment clip (NeoAnim entrance, small vignette, gradual zoom). See B2, B5 and C6. ✅ (2026-11-01) 🎯 (the creative director adds progressive unblur) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.22** Inside a demo the shot never sits still: a zoom or spotlight move every 1.5-3.5 s. 🎬 No more than about 30 s on an unchanging screen, then cut to his face or B-roll. 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.23** One new cut of the real UI per spoken clause (TSB: 2.0 / 2.6 / 1.2 / 1.0 / 2.5 s). Tool-by-tool shots are full screen and hard-cut on each tool as he names it. ✅ *"I want it to all be full screen ... as he mentions each"* (2026-09-27) 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.24** Fit a real page load to the words by cutting on the page change (click, spinner, skeleton). ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Websites, articles, companies
- **C8.25** A company, tool or site he names gets its real website or logo within the same sentence, never a stand-in or a text pill. ✅🔁2 *"If he's saying something, show the website."* (2026-10-07) 🎬 (3 finals) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.26** A study, statement or figure is shown on its real page with the line highlighted. ✅🔁6 *"Show the actual study on the website, highlighted"* (2026-10-08); *"SHow the amount they sold it, on the page"* (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.27** Introducing a company = its logo and name only: no website page, no shine. ✅🔁2 *"Just show their logo and name, no need for the websiite page, and no need for this shine over it"* (2026-10-24) (see Q-C8.d) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.28** Article title centred; a montage of pages runs about 1 s a page. 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.55** Tool B-roll carries the tool's logo small in a corner. Not on screen recordings or UI visuals (C8.50). ✅ *"tool logos would be nice in the corner"* (2026-11-05) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.29** Never make up a number that contradicts a readable source page; if the live page contradicts the speaker, look for an archived page and ask Samuel. Blur prices and numbers the speaker never says; mosaic only those lines and keep the page sharp. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Social posts, comments, YouTube
- **C8.30** Every post he mentions is the real post from his page (Notion links first, then search his page). Made up only where none exists, and then exactly like the platform. ✅🔁3 *"you should not just make up things. Go to his LinkedIn page and find the actual post."* (2026-10-13) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.31** Zoom posts out so they read as a post: whole card, header and a little feed, not so far out that it is mostly platform UI. ✅🔁8 *"enough for us to know it is a post"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.32** Made-up posts get a profile picture and filler content; one face per name across the video. ✅🔁5 *"Add a profile pic to the linkedin post so it doesn't look bare"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.33** Show only the post he talks about. ✅ *"why do we have two posts here, when he is talking about one"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.34** Comments: show the real comments UI; build one if the real ones don't exist. ✅ *"Show the actual UI of the comments, even if we don't have the exact comments, create one"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.35** YouTube: the proper dark-theme UI, a long real search or feed scrolled at reading speed, framed on the videos, not the whole UI. ✅🔁2 *"Show proper Youtube dark theme UI scrolling, don't show the whole UI, focus on the video parts"* (2026-10-14) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.36** Show a public figure's profile, never a private person's. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Calls
- **C8.37** A sales call is a Google Meet screen: Alex's own footage in one tile, the prospect blurred, a message box with the prospect's words timed to the line. Not a call-recording page. ✅🔁3 *"do this for the others"* (2026-10-08) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.38** Use Alex's real podcast footage with the guest as the prospect, "name - position" tags, the name blurred. 🎯 (passed on by Samuel, 2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Privacy and staging
- **C8.39** Private data never on screen: his inbox, DMs, other people's names. Use made-up content in a 1:1 rebuild. Never connect apps, create accounts or spend credits in his accounts; posting or sending as him is his call. ✅ (2026-10-26) ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.40** Captures from his Chrome carry the client's identity, never Samuel's: paint the client's avatar over his photo (nav, dock, comment box), hide Follow and degree, hide the messaging dock. Check it in the pixels, not the page code. Keep the originals. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.41** Blur personal data: emails, phone numbers, cards, business addresses, store URLs. Flag real people's names and LinkedIn links in demo results to Samuel. 🏢 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.42** One fictional cast per video (one client name everywhere, the same names and numbers); check made-up names don't belong to real companies. No strangers' faces on plates. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.43** Own browser inserts are recorded in Chrome full screen (F11) so no window frame shows Windows next to the client's macOS footage. 🏢 · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Demo sections and build method
- **C8.44** A long demo section strips down: no music, no SFX, 1-4 graphics per 30 s. 🎬 ($7M final) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.45** Recording, capture and render how-tos (demo recorder, crisp region captures, Chrome grabs, page size, render format): open [[03-Areas/video-editing/resolve-automation-lessons|Resolve automation lessons]] and [[03-Areas/video-editing/sops/video-pipeline-tools|Video pipeline tools]] before recording or rebuilding a UI. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.46** Known build faults: render HTML at the layout's real page size (or a quarter goes empty); no sparse or empty pages; no tiny UI text; never name plate files with numbers Resolve reads as an image sequence (Media Offline); helpers close their own headless Chrome. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05

## Never (strict)
- **C8.50** No motion graphics or text that isn't part of the UI on a screen recording or UI visual: no labels, timer or counter chips, pills, shine, text at the top. Text that goes with a capture sits centred and big in its own visual. One exception: a step number (Step 1) or a major numbering may sit on it (Q-C8.a). *"Nothing on a screen recording except when there is a step 1 or major numberings"* (2026-11-05) ✅🔁15 *"Since when did we start putting motion graphics on screen recording visuals"* (2026-11-01); *"never put text on a screen record up, I don't do that"* (2026-10-21) · ✏ 2026-11-05
- **C8.51** No windowed UI on a background, no tilt, drop-in or camera push. A windowed UI is allowed only as one element inside a motion graphic. ✅ *"It must be a fullscreen visual, not with any background."* (2026-09-27; his same-day windowed layout was reverted, later wins) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.52** Never the client's own demo footage or visuals unless Samuel names them (C8.11). ✅ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.53** Not the video 2 page faults: crops so deep the lines ran off the frame, a whole page at 1:1 with small text, soft-blur bands instead of lighting, focus baked into the comp, 1.4x jumps inside a beat. ❌ (2026-10-28) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.54** No capture that shows Samuel's own avatar, inbox or private data. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C8.56** The demo standard is the Gamma Ep. 12 connector demo (sample S4): her real-UI rebuild, one cut per spoken step, the light on each target as she names it, the dialog framed to fill the frame, nothing of ours on the UI. ✅ *"The demo u showed, I love it, let's keep that standard for the demos"* (2026-11-05)
- **C8.57** Never reveal what the video hasn't shown yet: mosaic-blur it with an adjustment clip and put COMING SOON on top. ✅ *"Blur all this here with a mosaic blur adjustment clip, and put a text on top saying coming soon, Never reveal things not yet shown"* (2026-11-06)

## Questions for Samuel
- **Q-C8.a** Can anything sit over a screen recording? For: *"you can add numbers on the screen above the visual"* (2026-10-15); small stats as pills at the bottom of a page (2026-10-25); boxes over a scrolling website (2026-09-27). Against: *"never put text on a screen record"* (2026-10-21), *"Since when did we start putting motion graphics on screen recording visuals"* (2026-11-01). → Options: (1) nothing ever sits on a live screen recording; numbers and boxes get their own motion-graphic beat, where a darkened website may be the background; (2) one number or bottom pill over a darkened page, only on the word he says. My pick: (1). → Samuel: nothing on a screen recording except a step number (Step 1) or a major numbering. *"Nothing on a screen recording except when there is a step 1 or major numberings"* (2026-11-05) C8.50 updated.
- **Q-C8.b** Speaker during demos: *"Remove his video from the screen"* (2026-10-02) vs the creative director's small corner cam (2026-10-22) and the $7M final (screen + PIP). → Options: (1) never in Route Rise UI demos; (2) small corner cam on demos longer than about 10 s. My pick: (1). → Samuel: (1), never in Route Rise UI demos (2026-11-05).
- **Q-C8.c** The creative director puts the tool's logo in the corner of every tool shot (2026-10-22). You never ruled on it, and you ban text at the top of captures. → Options: (1) no corner logo; (2) corner logo on tool B-roll only. My pick: (1) until you say. → Samuel: (2), corner logo on tool B-roll. *"tool logos would be nice in the corner"* (2026-11-05) New C8.55.
- **Q-C8.d** Company intro = logo and name only (2026-10-24). Does that also retire the AI4 brand beat (real homepage blurred under logo and tagline, 2026-10-07 final)? → My pick: yes, logo and name only, unless the website itself is the point. → Samuel: yes, logo and name only, unless the website itself is the point (2026-11-05).
