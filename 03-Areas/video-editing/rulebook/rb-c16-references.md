# C16 · References and creativity

> **Read when:** starting a job or a new client, before planning a look, when a beat needs a construct, when studying a reference video. Planner and builders. **Client values:** which reference video belongs to which client is listed here; the brand values themselves are in the client overlays (K2 Alex/Frontal, K3 Gamma). **Status:** approved by Samuel, 2026-11-05 (issues answered one by one, the rest approved in one go).

## Rules

### How to study a reference
- **C16.18** Study the timeline, not the render: layout, comps, node structure and settings, colours, animations, screen UI visuals. Then watch it. 🔁 (4: 10-06, 10-07 ×2, 11-01) ✅ *"Not the finished video, not the rendered video. Study the timeline."* (2026-10-06); *"study the timeline in resolve, the comps the nodes the colors, the animations the screen UI visuals"* (2026-11-01) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C16.19** Studying means copying his grammar 1:1. Measuring box shapes alone is not studying. ❌ *"you said you studied it but did the exact opposite"* (2026-11-01) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C16.20** Study each reference once, deeply. After that, work from the notes (edit grammar, templates, gallery) and open a template or still only to copy a construct. Go back to a video only when unsure. 🔁 (3) ✅ *"without it learning again from the old videos"* (2026-10-08); *"we overalysed prevoius videos"* (2026-10-12) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C16.21** Study by script, not by eye: parse comps and rows (coverage, triggers, rhythm, construct settings); render the reference's comps and measure values (corner radius, glow, sizes); make contact sheets of finished frames (one per 1-2 s, or ffmpeg scene detect `gt(scene,0.22)` with a frame 1.2 s after each change); map visuals to the subtitle track. ⚙️ → [[03-Areas/video-editing/sops/visual-sheet-pipeline|visual sheet pipeline]], [[03-Areas/video-editing/cut-sheets|cut sheets]] · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C16.22** Read what he switched off: the disabled clips in his XML show what he rejects. ⚙️ 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C16.23** Learn from his fixes: diff his changes against the delivered render, and our original comps against the comps in his final. When he rebuilds a comp, study it and tell him what you notice. ✅ *"Just study to see what you notice and tell me what you think about the visual"* (2026-09-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C16.24** Play YouTube references in Samuel's Chrome (the in-app browser gets ads, embeds fail with error 153). ⚙️ → visual sheet pipeline · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C16.25** Use what you already know (the CD teachings, his past corrections, what made him angry) and don't make him repeat himself. 🔁 (4: 10-24, 10-25, 10-28, 11-01) ✅ *"You've seen what I have said in the past, the corrections I've made in the past, the things I have been angry about in the past."* (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
