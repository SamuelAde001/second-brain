# C15 · Pacing

> **Read when:** writing the visual sheet (which sentences get a visual), planning density per region, reviewing a whole cut for rhythm. Planner and section-pass reviewer. **Client values:** none. **Status:** draft, not yet approved by Samuel.

## Rules
### Match his normal pacing
- **C15.1** Pacing matches his finals. A new brand doesn't change it; density stays as in the previous video. 🔁 (6) ✅ *"the pacing of the video, very important. Make sure that the pacing is on par"* (2026-10-06); *"Density remains the same"* (2026-10-12); *"the pacing should be match our normal pacing"* (2026-10-20)
- **C15.2** Plan pacing sentence by sentence, the way he did: which sentences get a visual, which stay A-roll. ✅ *"understand how I paced the video, the frequency of visuals, per sentence"* (2026-10-03)

### How often something new comes in
- **C15.3** A new visual about every 1 s in the hook, every 2 s in the intro, every 5-10 s in the body, and never more than 15 s without one. 🎯 (CD04 12:46, CD05 23:52, 2026-10-22)
- **C15.4** The count includes elements entering inside a visual, not only cuts. 💡
- **C15.5** In the hook, keep the montage pace but give each shot only one thing to read, landed early. Drop second reads. 💡 → C14.13
- **C15.6** For scale, his finals: Apollo median 3.5 s between beat starts (13 in minute one, 1 in the last minute); 4 AI Tools body 10.2 visuals a minute (one every ~5.9 s); TSB 11.6 a minute (13.5 in his own part); the hook wall to wall. 🎬
- **C15.7** Fast at hooks, topic starts and new lists; calmer through explanations. 🏢 🎬

### Coverage and breaths
- **C15.8** Visuals cover ~85-90 % of the runtime when the video has demos and UI (4 AI Tools 88.9 %, Apollo 84.8 %, AI B2B final ~91 %). A 20+ minute talking head with no demo sits nearer ~75 % (71-81 %). 🎬
- **C15.9** Plain A-roll breaths come about every 40-60 s in explanations (every ~19 s in his own TSB part), 2-6 s long (median ~3 s), rarely past 8 s, and none in the middle of a build-up. 🎬
- **C15.10** Caveats, asides, advice, closing opinions and the outro get plain A-roll (7-13 s). A long breath follows a dense run. 🎬
- **C15.11** Many sentences get no visual. Let the A-roll breathe. ✅ (his NONE rows on RR3, where he did the punch-ins himself, 2026-09-27)
- **C15.12** Where a sentence needs a visual, there must be one. Where it doesn't, leave it. 🔁 (2) ✅ *"Anytime there needs to be visuals, there must be visuals."* (2026-10-13); *"you are doing visuals in between ... words that don't need it"* (2026-10-07)
- **C15.13** Never a long stretch without something happening. Even a plain talking-head stretch gets a slow zoom. 🏢 🎯 (the CD's one criticism of his Alex videos was a stretch without one)

### How it should feel
- **C15.14** Calm: smooth, slow, easy to follow, "simple yet there". One thing at a time, never lots of fast things, never static. 🔁 (3) ✅ *"I don't want too many fast things. I don't want the whole thing to be buggy and annoying for the viewer to follow"* (2026-10-24); *"it needs to feel simple yet there"* (2026-10-24)
- **C15.15** Too fast is a fault. If the viewer can't follow, slow it down, use fewer elements, hold longer. 🔁 (5) ✅ *"This is too fast and too jawing, viewer can't follow it"* (2026-10-08) 🎯 (review card C4)
- **C15.16** Movement is constant but gentle: a slow push or pan on every shot, still or graphic. 🎯 → B5
- **C15.17** Prefer visuals over the A-roll to cutting away from him (size: Q-C1.a). ✅ *"I like visuals on top of the A-roll more"* (2026-10-03)
- **C15.18** Recurring motifs carry the video, with plain A-roll between visuals. ⚙️
- **C15.19** In his finals A-roll cuts run a median 2.4-3.2 s and zoom adjustment clips come 1.8-4.4 a minute. 🎬 → A3, B5
- **C15.20** Route Rise mix, as a guide: fast parts 50 % B-roll and text, 40 % animation, 10 % zooms; slow parts 40/30/30; some change about every 3 s. 🏢

## Never
- **C15.21** Visuals dropped between words that don't need them. ❌ (2026-10-07)
- **C15.22** Pacing so fast the viewer can't follow. ❌ *"Bad pacing"* (2026-10-08) / *"This whole visual is bad and not well paced"* (2026-10-08)
- **C15.23** Zooms that are too fast and jerky. ❌ *"zooms too jawing and fast"* (2026-10-24) → B5
- **C15.24** Long gaps with nothing happening. ❌ 🏢
