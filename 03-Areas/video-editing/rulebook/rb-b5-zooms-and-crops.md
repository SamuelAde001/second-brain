# B5 · Zoom in, steady zoom, crop-ins

> **Read when:** the MagicZoom pass, any focus zoom on UI or pages, framing pages, posts and screen recordings, and reviewing motion. **Client values:** corner shape and outline colour of the shrink-to-card move (client overlay). **Status:** approved by Samuel, 2026-11-05 (issues answered one by one, the rest approved in one go).

## Rules

### Nothing is static
- **B5.1** Everything moves slowly: every A-roll stretch, B-roll, screen recording, page, screenshot and motion graphic. Not fast, not still. 🔁 ×2 ✅ *"Let it not be too fast, but let it not be static ... nothing should be static."* (2026-10-28) 🎯 a slow push on every static stretch (2026-10-22). Replaces the still plain A-roll seen in AI4. · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.2** Motion graphics: one push Transform over the whole comp, Size 1.000 → 1.036 (never past 1.06) across the clip. ✅ *"Even the motion graphic area should slowly zoom in."* (2026-10-28) 🎬 (TSB 1.036-1.052) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.3** Media drift comes from the MagicZoom in the media adjustment clip (B2.7): Standard (Type 0), pivot centre, depth by clip length: under 36 f 0.02 · 36-119 f 0.03 · 120-199 f 0.04 · 200 f and over 0.048. Focus zoom × MagicZoom stays ≤ 1.25 in total. ⚙️ (video 4) See Q-B5.b. · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.4** The hook: push the whole composite to ~×1.11 in under 1 s, then hold. 🎬 (his own change on the B2B final, 2026-10-13) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### A-roll punch pass (MagicZoom)
- **B5.5** A-roll zooms and punch-ins are MagicZoom V3 on adjustment clips. ✅ *"Zoom and hold with magic zoom like we did in other videos"* (2026-10-14) 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.6** Run the pass after the visuals are final and before sound. Plan it for whatever A-roll is still plain, and re-fit it whenever beats change length (`finish\mz_build.py`, `mz_media.py`, `mz_fit.py`). ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.7** One MagicZoom per plain stretch of 24 f or more, starting and ending on V1 cuts, covering at least 75 % of plain A-roll. 🎬 (his finals: TSB 77 %, LinkedIn 74 %) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.8** Settings. On the A-roll: zoom type **Standard**, curve **Linear** (*"the magic zoom settings should be set to zoom type, standard and curve linear for A-roll, only time we use the usual zoom and hold is for empasis statement areas"* (2026-11-05)). Zoom & Hold only on emphasis statements: 0.071, or a 0.111 punch on emphatic lines of 45 f or less (at most 1 in 4, never two in a row). Standard 0.03 under text and cards · an occasional 0.053 inverted zoom-out. Pivot (0.507, 0.62). Depths from his 170 instances. ⚙️ (reworded 2026-11-05 to follow B2.13; still for his review in B5) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.9** A-roll zooms centre on his face. ✅ *"Zoom focus most be to his face"* (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.10** No MagicZoom under a full-frame visual, and none in frames 0-28 (the intro adjustment clip owns them, B2.10). ⚙️ ✅ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.11** A zoom that ends on a cut to plain A-roll uses MagicZoom Mirror (Type 3: in, then eased back to 1.00 by the clip end), or the next MagicZoom starts from the cut, so the next shot never snaps. ✅ *"this doesn't smoothly"* (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.12** Section starts, and returns to camera after a full-frame run, get the 28 f zoom-out reveal (Size 1.594 → 1.0, B2.12). 🎬 🎯 open on the creator with a slow zoom out (2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.13** Punch in slowly on the emotional or personal beat. 🎯 (2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Speed and depth
- **B5.14** Every zoom is slow and gradual: never fast, deep, drastic or jittery. 🔁 ×25+ (mostly the video 2 markers) ✅ *"your zooms in the whole video is just too fast"* (2026-10-24); *"If it is too fast, it doesn't allow the viewer to digest the information."* (2026-10-14) 🎯 *"not moving too fast... not moving super super slow boring"* (2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.15** Start a little closer and ease in. Don't zoom far out before zooming in. ✅ *"don't zoom out too much it makes zooming in too drastic"* (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.16** Targeted zooms on UI and pages: each move ~18 f, eased; keys only where a move starts or ends (flat handles at thirds, Size and centre on one curve), holds in between. Zoom ≤ 1.25, never over 1.3. 🔁 ×2 ✅ *"Don't do fast zooms on UI visuals, make sure everything looks clear and premium"* (2026-10-03) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.17** When the words come faster than an 18 f move, hold the zoom and move only the highlight (≥ 10 f per hop, ≥ 30 f between hops). Where parts sit close together, zoom 1.0 and move only the highlight. 🎬 💡 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.18** Where he marks several moves, use one smooth zoom. ✅ (2026-10-10) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.19** Not every visual needs a targeted zoom; the slow drift is enough. 🔁 ×2 ✅ *"no need to zoom here at all"* (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Framing pages, UI, posts and screen recordings
- **B5.20** Pages and UI open slightly wider than the line being read (never the whole page, never right on the sentence), then ease in across the beat. 🔁 ×6 ✅ *"I didn't mean zoom out to the whole page ... just don't be too close to the exact sentence"* (2026-10-24; replaces "zoomed in from the first frame", 2026-10-14) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.21** Context first: the viewer must see what it is (a post, a job ad), then the area lights up, then the words are highlighted (C6). ✅ *"zoom out so we understand the context, then highlight the area of interest"* (2026-10-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.22** Screen recordings are framed average, like a walkthrough. ✅ *"I don't want it to be too zoomed in, but also not too zoomed out."* (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.23** Zoom to where you are highlighting. Never so close that text crowds or is cut at the frame edge; spotlight edges never slice text. ✅ *"Zoom to the place you are higjlighting"* (2026-10-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.24** Social posts (LinkedIn and the like): whole card width and its header in frame, enough to read as a post, little platform UI around it. ✅ *"Zoom out all linkedin posts, so we know it is a post"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.25** Web pages and captures fill the frame: scaled up, browser bars cropped off, still pushing (e.g. 1.07 → 1.10). 🎬 (2026-10-13) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.26** Frame every shot well. ✅ *"This is not well framed, please frame it well"* (2026-10-14) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.27** Tella full-screen framing: scale = 0.5·Z, Pan = −(cx−1920)·0.5·Z, Tilt = (cy−956)·0.5·Z (e.g. Zoom 1.65, Pan −372, Tilt 60). ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.28** Reused B-roll may be re-framed by zoom and pan (1.22-1.8) to show the action. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.29** Line-marker strokes sit under the zoom (upstream of Transform_Zoom), or the zoom holds still while a stroke is on screen. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.30** The tool reveal: zoom into the named tool while the others desaturate and drop opacity. ✅ *"I zoomed in to CLay and made the others less saturated and opacity reduced"* (2026-09-27) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.31** Opening move into a motion graphic: the live A-roll frame shrinks into a card (W 1 → 0.34, H 1 → 0.58 over 38 f; or Size 1 → 0.756 over 27 f). Corner shape and outline: client overlay. 🎬 (Apollo, TSB) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.38** An A-roll zoom adjustment clip runs through a whole statement, across jump cuts; it never cuts mid-statement. A long stretch splits only at a sentence end. ✅ *"Adjusment clips fo zoom should last though a major statement"* (2026-11-06); *"The adjusment clip for the zooms are cuuting when they shouldn't be cutting"* (he merged two zones at 6334-6419 himself)

## Never (strict)
- **B5.32** Never a jump, 2× or punchy zoom on pages and UI. 🏢 "Never jump zooms" (2026-09-25) ✅ (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.33** Never zoom frame by frame after the cursor: it looks janky. ✅ (2026-10-03; replaces the cursor-following zoom of 2026-10-02) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.34** Never zoom onto a text line for its own sake. ✅ *"Why is zoom in on the text here, that's not how we do things"* (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.35** Never MagicZoom on a source clip, and never a Fusion comp for a zoom-in (B2.26, B2.27). 🎬 ✅ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **B5.36** Every screen recording and page B-roll carries subtle camera motion inside its adjustment clip (drift, B5.3 table), and the review clip must show it. ✅ *"In the Gumroad B-roll, there is no camera motion, add a subtle camera motion there inside the adjustment clips there and in any other Screen recording that would need that"* (2026-11-05; the sample render skipped the MagicZoom)
- **B5.37** The A-roll slow zooms and emphasis punch-ins (B3.23, C15.13) go in with the build, not after; a sample over her shows them. ✅ *"I am not seeing slow zooms and emphasis zooms on the main cam"* (2026-11-05)
- **B5.39** A transform or scale never shows the frame edge or a cut line: overscan the plate or animate inside the frame. ✅ *"Can you see the edge cut down, it should animate without showing the edge, you are transofrming it the wrong way"* (2026-11-06)
