# K4 · New client template

> **Read when:** Route Rise sends a client we have not edited for in the Brain, or a client sends a new brand kit. The Editor at job setup. **Client values:** none yet; this note makes them. **Status:** draft, not yet approved by Samuel.

## Rules
### The look comes from that client's own reference
- **K4.1** A client's look comes from that client's own reference: Samuel's last finished edit for them. Never from another client's kit or overlay. Mechanics (scripts, node structures, timing) carry over; looks never do. 🔁 (2026-10-31, 2026-11-01) ✅ *"you are using the same design of Alex for Gamma, I showed you her video I edited this is wrong design"* (2026-10-31)
- **K4.2** Until that style is studied, build only plates: screen recordings, screenshots, website and UI demos. No text, pills, boxes, motion graphics or adjustment-clip styling. ✅ *"cause you don't know the style of this client, I know the style"* (2026-10-30)
- **K4.3** Samuel supplies the colour palette, the font and an example of a finished edit. Ask him for all three on day one. ✅ *"I'm going to give you the colors and I'm going to give you the style, the font"* (2026-10-31)
- **K4.4** Study the reference by opening its Resolve timeline, not only by exporting comps: census every track (what sits over each screen recording, over the presenter, how many, how long), read comps, nodes, colours and animations, and watch it through. Render its comps beside every new proof. Other clients' finals are side references only. 🔁 (2026-10-31, 2026-11-01) ✅ *"study how I edited the gamma video"* (2026-11-01)
- **K4.5** Being creative means staying inside his styles, his library and the CD's teachings, not inventing new ones. ✅ *"being creative doesn't mean you should do things I don't normally do in any of my videos"* (2026-11-01)

### The checklist (collect before any graphics)
- **K4.6** Collect and write into the new overlay: ⚙️ 💡 · ✔ 2026-11-05
  1. **Colours**: every brand hex, which one is the accent, what carries positive/negative.
  2. **Fonts**: names, weights, case, and any licence limit (who may use it, where).
  3. **Corner shape**: square, rounded (radius) or pill; any frame or bracket style.
  4. **Reference edit**: Samuel's last finished video for this client, its Resolve project and timeline name, frame rate.
  5. **Brand kit**, if any: the whole folder (logos, colour swatches, fonts, rules). Where it lives.
  6. **Logos and names**: may their logo, their clients' logos and company names be shown? Settled with whom?
  7. **Presenter**: name (or "never name"), camera and mic setup, number of cameras, resolution, fps, where the face and free side sit in frame.
  8. **App access**: which tools shown on screen are logged in on his Chrome.
  9. **Brief**: how it arrives (ClickUp, script doc, Drive), deadline, rate (from Samuel, never guessed), revision terms.
  10. **Audience**: the niche and viewer age. Older viewers in teaching niches need simpler, clearer visuals. 🎯 (CD, 2026-10-22)
- **K4.7** Anything missing stays `needs-input` in the overlay and goes in one question to Samuel. Never guessed. ⚙️

### When a brand kit arrives
- **K4.8** Study the whole kit folder before going on with the video. Then test the design in Resolve nodes, and build a variety library in the new look (lists, chapters, lower thirds, motion graphics, icons, animations). ✅ *"We would have to do this study before we can move forward on this video"* (2026-10-20)
- **K4.9** The kit re-skins his style; it never replaces his fundamentals (pacing, reveals, shines, icons, adjustment clips, quality). Where a kit contradicts itself, follow its brand book. ✅ (2026-10-20, 2026-10-22)
- **K4.10** Brand copies of his macros (BGFRONTAL, BGGAMMA) are new files beside his. Never edit, rename or overwrite his own macros. ✅ (2026-10-20)

### Making the overlay
- **K4.11** Write the new client's overlay as `rb-k<n>-<client>.md` with the same headings as [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k3-gamma|K3 Gamma]] (Who and what, The reference, Colours, Font, Shape, Over the presenter, Never, Questions), add it to the index and the changelog, and make a client note in `03-Areas/video-editing/clients/`. ⚙️
- **K4.12** Brand values go only in that overlay; no other section names them (K0.20). ⚙️

## Never
- **K4.13** Never build a new client's graphics in another client's look, and never invent motion graphics before his style is studied. ❌ 🔁 (2026-10-30, 2026-10-31, 2026-11-01)
