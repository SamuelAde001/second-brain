# The editing rulebook

> Samuel, 2026-11-03: *"this rule book will govern every edit from Routerise ... what would change between clients are the fonts, colors and for alex, square boxes instead of rounded corners"* · *"This rule book would be your number one rule, unless I tell you not to"*

Every Route Rise edit follows this rulebook: Alex (Frontal), Gamma and any client Route Rise sends. It was built on 2026-11-03 from every video so far: Samuel's 529 editing messages, his 359 timeline markers, his finished timelines, the creative director's breakdowns and corrections, every job note and everything the Editor learned. Samuel approves it **section by section**; until a section says `status: approved`, it is a draft and the older notes it names under *Sources* still apply where it is silent.

**It is the standard guide, so old videos are not studied again** (Samuel, 2026-11-04: *"I want us to have a standard guide that doesn't need us to study old videos, cause we can't be studying them over again that's what this guide book is meant to help"*). Plan from it; open a past video only where it is silent or you are unsure (A6.2, A6.5), and write what you learn back into it.

## Order of authority

1. **Samuel's live corrections and markers** on the job in hand.
2. **This rulebook** (approved sections).
3. The client overlay (K2, K3 …) for fonts, colours, shapes and brand rules.
4. Older Brain notes, only where the rulebook says nothing.

Full rule: [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k0-authority|K0 Order of authority]]. A new correction from Samuel is written into the right section **in the same session**, and logged in the [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rulebook-changelog|changelog]].

## How the review works

Samuel decides **every rule** himself (2026-11-03: *"bring each rule to me to approve correct or dismiss"*). The Editor brings them in small numbered batches; each line then ends with its mark: ✔ approved, ✏ corrected (his wording), ✖ dismissed, with the date. **Dismissed and replaced rules leave the rulebook** (Samuel, 2026-11-03: *"The replaced rules should be replaced and removed from the rule book"*): they are kept word for word in [[08-Archive/brain-build/rulebook-removed-rules|removed rules]], which no agent follows, and their numbers are never reused. **Every rule under "Never" is strict** (Samuel, 2026-11-03: *"The never should be there as strict"*): no exception without his word. Questions end with "→ Samuel: <answer>". A section turns `status: approved` only when every rule and question in it is decided. Where the review stands: `python 03-Areas/video-editing/scripts/rulebook/rb_status.py` (scripts: [[03-Areas/video-editing/scripts/rulebook/README|rulebook scripts]]).

## How Samuel changes a rule

Edit the line in Obsidian, or tell any agent "change C6.3 to …". The agent edits the rule, keeps the number, adds a line to the [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rulebook-changelog|changelog]], and commits. A removed rule goes to [[08-Archive/brain-build/rulebook-removed-rules|removed rules]] and its number is never reused, so numbers never shift. Pictures live in `_attachments/rulebook/`.
