# C9 · B-roll

> **Read when:** planning a beat that shows people, actions, metaphors, anecdotes or lists; finding, downloading or generating B-roll (Google Flow); reviewing B-roll beats. **Client values:** brand imagery limits live in the client overlays (K2 Alex, K3 Gamma). **Status:** draft, not yet approved by Samuel.

## Rules
### Real first
- **C9.1** Show, don't tell. Anything said that can be shown is shown, clearly. ✅🔁2 *"Show and not tell. Don't be weird there. You need to show something, let it be clear."* (2026-10-20)
- **C9.2** Real footage beats AI footage: the client's own footage, real YouTube, TikTok or Instagram clips, real pages. For a real action (a cold call), find real footage. Remove AI B-roll he flags. ✅ (2026-10-10) 🎯 (research the exact clip; it takes time, that is the quality bar)
- **C9.3** Gather the B-roll, recordings and pictures yourself, from the web in his Chrome. Downloading real footage is fine (a podcast, a Hormozi clip, faces for made-up posts). ✅🔁2 *"go into Google Chrome and get all the resources you need, B-rolls, screen recording, pictures"* (2026-10-20)
- **C9.4** "No stock" means no stock websites only. Real UI, websites, YouTube and TikTok pulls and AI B-roll are all fine. ✅ *"Stock B-roll just applies to stock B-roll from stock websites ... even AI B-roll is fine"* (2026-10-20) (replaces "YouTube and TikTok only", 2026-09-22)
- **C9.5** Every clip shows the point of the line. No generic or surface-level B-roll. B-roll shows; animation explains. 🎬 (TSB: generic startup cutaways removed) 🏢
- **C9.6** Custom or movie footage over stock; no slow-motion stock of people talking. 🏢

### People
- **C9.7** A person he names gets their real footage (or an animated still) and their name. Example: real Hormozi B-roll with the text by his side in a glass box. ✅ *"Show a real B-roll of Alex hormozi here and the the text by his side with a glass box"* (2026-10-15) 🎬
- **C9.8** A co-worker or team member he names: their real photo from the company website. ✅ *"go to his website, check the person's picture to make sure that you show the actual person"* (2026-10-06)
- **C9.9** A quote sits on a picture or video of the person quoting it. ✅ *"Would prefer quotes to be on a picture/video of the person quoting it"* (2026-09-30)
- **C9.10** Beats about the speaker: his B-roll from earlier client shoots (`Visual Assets\Frontal Assets\B-roll\` for Alex) plus a node element such as a thought bubble. ✅ *"Show a B-roll of him, with a thought bubble, I used B-rolls of him in the last project"* (2026-09-27) 🎬
- **C9.11** "His team", a team discussion: real team B-roll from the client library, not a graphic. "Engineers working": official office B-roll, not interviews or stock-style shots. ✅ (2026-10-03)
- **C9.12** Named source with a podcast: thumbnail card top-left over A-roll, then the clip full screen with its own audio, then PIP top-left with a two-tone name. 🎬 (TSB)
- **C9.13** A quoted client or a call: his own video plus an AI or podcast-guest prospect with the face blurred (layout in C8.37). ✅ *"use his own video and then an AI video as the client with a blurred out face"* (2026-10-08)
- **C9.14** Generic people beats with no real footage ("business owner confused", a missed meeting): an AI video from Google Flow. ✅🔁2 *"Use my FLow to generate it"* (2026-10-02)
- **C9.15** AI-generated faces are fine when stock sites block scripts. No strangers' faces where they don't belong. ✅ (2026-10-15) ⚙️

### Metaphors, anecdotes, lists, emotions
- **C9.16** A metaphor is shown literally with a stylised, cartoonish Flow B-roll ("fuel you pour on the fire" = fuel poured on a fire), never an abstract glow. ✅🔁3 *"show the fuel pouring on the fire, create a stylyzed cartoonish B-roll with google flow"* (2026-10-21)
- **C9.17** An analogy or action is shown happening, never labelled (TSB: the GPS card "YOU HAVE ARRIVED · NOWHERE" switched off). 🎬
- **C9.18** An anecdote: photoreal AI stills of the scene, one per sentence, plus a speech bubble. AI stills must move. 🎬 🎯
- **C9.19** A list of actions: a montage, one clip per clause (about 1.5 s), each with its clause in a pill. 🎬 (the creative director's variant: each item its own darkened B-roll with one big glowing keyword 🎯)
- **C9.20** Emotion words get emotion B-roll. 🎯
- **C9.21** A composite scene (the speaker and a team member at a big monitor): find real reference images of the people, generate the image with AI, split it into layers (each person, background, monitor) that recombine cleanly so people can animate out. ✅ (2026-10-02)
- **C9.22** A story montage uses real photos and clips from those moments, not the raw B-roll archive. Picks saved numbered by script line, approved before editing. ✅ *"I need to tell the story by showing real pictures and videos from those moments"* (2026-10-16, personal video)

### Google Flow (AI B-roll)
- **C9.23** Model: Omni Flash (about 12 credits a take), never Veo 3.1 Quality (100 credits). ✅🔁2 *"Next time, use the Omni Flash model when generating AI B-roll"* (2026-10-03); *"Use Omni Flash in my Chrome if you need it"* (2026-10-08)
- **C9.24** Run Flow in his Chrome only when the Brand manager's session is idle. He clicks Save As on downloads; if he can't, use existing B-roll, or leave the clip for him with a fallback in its place. ✅ (2026-10-22) ⚙️
- **C9.25** The clip matches the line exactly. Never show the generator's watermark. 🎯
- **C9.26** Pick in-points where hands or garbled screens don't break the shot; flag AI artefacts (e.g. a bad last second) to Samuel. ⚙️
- **C9.27** Where a screen in AI B-roll must be readable, cut while it is still clean to a full-screen rebuild of the app (C8). Never corner-pin onto a screen that turns to garbled text. ⚙️
- **C9.28** Flow clips are 24 fps: place them on the 23.976 timeline with the source frame checked. ⚙️
- **C9.29** Reuse the AI B-roll from the last video where it fits the line. ✅ *"Use the same AI B-roll we used in the last video for this"* (2026-10-25) (see Q-C9.a)

### Placement and treatment
- **C9.30** B-roll animates in only and cuts out hard. 🎬
- **C9.31** No fast zooms on B-roll (zoom rules: B5). ✅ *"It had a lot of fast-paced zooms, especially in the B-rolls"* (2026-10-24)
- **C9.32** Vertical phone footage sits centred with dark blurred sides, never black bars. 🎬 (see Q-C9.b)
- **C9.33** Repeated B-roll is shown differently the second time (e.g. as a polaroid). 🏢 Shots used as a pair must match (not day next to night). 💡
- **C9.34** A real YouTube clip: mute its own audio unless it is meant to be heard (C9.12); a CC BY clip gets a credit line. ⚙️
- **C9.35** AR screen on speaker B-roll (tracked translucent UI plane, hands kept in front): in his AI4 final. Node recipe in [[03-Areas/video-editing/fusion-recipes|Fusion recipes]]. 🎬

## Never
- **C9.40** No stock-website footage; no generic clip that doesn't show the line. ✅ (2026-10-20) 🎬
- **C9.41** Never the client's own Tella or visuals as B-roll unless Samuel names them (C8.11). ✅
- **C9.42** Never leave in visuals made for another video that don't belong to this one. ✅ *"Remove this completely, this whole part of another video visuals"* (2026-10-15)
- **C9.43** No visible AI watermark; no fast zoom on B-roll. 🎯 ✅

## Questions for Samuel
- **Q-C9.a** Reusing old B-roll: *"Use the same AI B-roll we used in the last video for this"* (2026-10-25) vs *"Remove this completely, this whole part of another video visuals"* (2026-10-15). My reading: reuse is fine when it fits the line exactly; you removed visuals whose content belonged to another video. → Options: (1) reuse only where you point to it or it fits the line exactly; (2) reuse freely. My pick: (1).
- **Q-C9.b** Vertical clips: your Apollo final centres them with dark blurred sides; the creative director triples them (outer copies darker and blurred). → Options: (1) centred with blurred sides; (2) tripled. My pick: (1), it is in your final.
- **Q-C9.c** The creative director grades B-roll to carry meaning (grey for dead or quiet, red corners for bad, 2026-10-22). You never ruled on it. → Options: (1) use it on negative lines; (2) not for Route Rise. My pick: (1), on the negative lines only, with colours from C5.
