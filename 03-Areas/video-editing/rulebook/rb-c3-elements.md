# C3 · Motion graphic elements

> **Read when:** planning or building any pill, box, card, circle, icon, logo, list or connector (visual plan, beat builds, beat reviews). **Client values:** corner shape, corner brackets, colours, glow and shadow bans, logo clearance in [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k2-alex-frontal|K2 Alex / Frontal]] and [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k3-gamma|K3 Gamma]]. **Status:** approved by Samuel, 2026-11-05 (issues answered one by one, the rest approved in one go).

## Rules
### Start from what he already approved
- **C3.1** Build every pill, box, card and circle from his own comps and the approved motion gallery, re-skinned for the client. Never invent a new construct where he already has one. ✅ *"we have done something like this before, use that style just with the new branding"* (2026-10-26) 🔁3 (2026-10-01, 2026-10-26, 2026-11-01) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.2** A client brand changes only the skin: font, colours, box shape, background. Treatments stay: shine, light sweep, glow, glass, icons, bold white text. ✅ *"does not mean that we are not going to put the shines that we used to put before"* (2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.3** Never carry one client's look onto another. Mechanics may be reused, looks may not. ✅ *"I told you to make better designs not copy frontals designs"* (2026-10-31) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.4** A recurring device has one design, the same every time it returns. A returning list shows the last visual and reveals the next item. ✅ *"what every visual you came up with in the last one, reuse here and reveal the next pill"* (2026-10-10) 🔁6 (5x 2026-09-27, 2026-10-10) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.5** Motion gallery verdicts. Approved: P01 medallion pills, P02 status-tag pill (without the background wave), P03 ghost-numeral chapter pill, P04 corner-badge pills, P08 CAN/CANNOT pair, P11 flow chips, P13 header-tab pill, B01 checklist with blurred upcoming rows, B05 tabbed steps box, C06 wrong vs right cards, C08 notification stack, O03 orbit, O04 big-numeral circle, O06 avatar row, O09 hand-drawn marker circle, O10 social buttons. Liked: Dynamic Island, border beam, search bar, chat, leaderboard, flags, Venn. Dropped: P12 toggle, B06 receipt, O08 rotating seal. ✅ *"All of them are approved"* (2026-09-30) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.6** His approved recipes, with exact sizes and timings, are in edit grammar. Open the matching one before building: chapter lower third `tsb_V3_000480_1`, statement bar `tsb_V3_002481_1`, montage caption pill `tsb_V4_003130_1` (a new pill on every cut), logo tile over B-roll `tsb_V4_000169_1`, signal/noise cards `tsb_V3_001154_1`, stacked pill list from the left `tsb_V3_003533_1`, hub with arrows `tsb_V2_002829_1`, hub with medallion pills `apollo_V2_000028`, question pills `apollo_V5_001063`, numbered list `apollo_V2_011420`, branch tree `apollo_V7_006981`, card reveal `ai4_V3_000723_1`, glass panel beside the speaker `ai4_V3_001401_1`, data cards each side `apollo_V3_001190`, recap lower third `ai4_V6_013809_1` (one combined comp, not one per tile), speech bubble `tsb_V5_008031_1`. 🎬 Their colours are the July orange look: take colours from the client overlay. · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.7** Agenda and preview beats: start from his "in this video" list comp ($7M / TSB) and his "COMING UP..." comp (AI4). Book-a-call: the style of past videos. ✅ *"This is not how we do book a call, I hate too much not needed boxes"* (2026-10-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Variety
- **C3.8** Never one box, pill or card design across a video. Rotate constructs and fills; neighbouring visuals never share one. Colour side: [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-c5-colour-and-backgrounds|C5]]. ✅ *"I don't want repeated boxes. I want it that the design has different variants"* (2026-10-31) 🔁5 (2026-09-30, 10-15, 10-22, 10-31 twice) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.9** The kit to draw from: pills, glass boxes (as in the LinkedIn video), full boxes, circles, cards. ✅ *"You can use pills, you can use glass boxes ... You can use a full box."* (2026-10-31) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.10** Plain boxes in a row, and random text boxes, read as AI-made. ✅ *"without rubbish random animations and text boxes that all look like AI"* (2026-11-01) (EN-04 three-box row rejected 2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Fewer boxes, only the words said
- **C3.11** No box unless it earns its place. Labels are plain text. No box behind text over the speaker, a screen recording or a page. ✅ *"no need for boxes everywhere especiall on screen"* (2026-10-24) 🔁4 (2026-10-24, 10-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.12** A key phrase goes as text at the centre of the frame, not in a dark pill. ✅ *"remove the pill, let the text be at the center of the page as I used to do it"* (2026-10-14) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.13** Pills and tags carry nouns and lists only. An action gets real UI, a screenshot, a mockup demo or real footage. ✅ *"Text in pills can show list or Nouns, but not for actions"* (2026-10-02) 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.14** Only text that he asked for or the speaker says. No helper labels ("connected", "NOT INCLUDED", "same on paper"), no invented sub-lines ("BY ANTHROPIC"), no side elements nobody asked for. Drop any pill that isn't on this sentence's words. A card carries only what the speaker says. ✅ (2026-09-26, 2026-10-02, 2026-10-08) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.15** Remove visuals between words that don't need them (the DREAM ICP bubble went). ✅ (2026-10-07) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.16** Never let a card sit empty waiting for its words: split it so the second card lands with its content. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Every element carries a treatment
- **C3.17** Every pill, circle and box carries a design treatment: shine, bevel, glow, light sweep or outline. ✅ *"Pills, circles boxes, must have a design aspect to it, either a Shine, a bevel, Glow, light sweep, outine"* (2026-10-03) 🔁4 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.18** Every box gets a static NeoLightSweep, a sheen through its centre, not animated: band 0.07-0.1142, SoftEdge 0.0551, Angle -40, Edge Intensity 0.291, Intensity ~0.3 (0.6 on very dark fills). ✅ *"light sweep is a node I use to give a box a shiny look, not neccessarily animated"* (2026-10-25) 🔁9 (7x 2026-10-25, 10-28, 10-31) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.19** Animated shine is rare: major moments only (about one per section), on the hero box after it lands, one for the whole set, never one per item. Numbers and the full shine rules: [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-c7-blurs-glows-shines|C7]]. ✅ *"sometimes on only major occasions, shine should come in"* (2026-10-31) 🔁~30 (2026-09-26, M-R3 2026-10-24). The creative director's "a shine across each item as he names it" (2026-10-22) is overruled by this. · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.20** Boxes are opaque. No see-through "hollow" panels: they read as too many layers. 🎯 (2026-10-22) Glass is blurred and tinted enough that nothing behind reads as a second layer. 💡 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.21** Use glass when he asks for it, but not heavily in one video. ✅ *"use a glass background like we have done in previous videos"* (2026-10-15) 🔁3 · ✅ *"We have used Glass alot in the beginning don't use it too much again"* (2026-10-03) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.22** No box drop shadows. A pill shadow is small and tight (distance 0.008, blur 0.31). 🎬 (2026-09-26) The client overlay may ban shadows altogether. · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Size and layout
- **C3.23** Size for the job: readable on a phone, never hiding the main visual. Side graphics and card decks stay small (about a third smaller than first built). ✅ *"make it bigger boxes, that are in different lines, and reduce the text sizxe"* and *"make thema smaller so we can see the circle better"* (both 2026-10-25) · *"I always prefer this smaller"* (2026-09-30) 🔁6 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.24** Text and numbers sit well inside their box. Size a card to its longest measured line, equal padding on all sides. ✅ *"The numbers shouls sit well inside the box"* (2026-10-25) ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.25** Twin cards in a set are pixel-identical in layout: same header inset, badge, row alignment. 🎬 (he moved one header to its twin's inset, B2B final, 2026-10-13) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.26** Step and chapter pills: one design, one width, a small dark fade behind them (STEP 1 the same as STEP 2-7). ✅ (2026-10-10) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.27** When he asks for "a box" on a beat, build a true square, not a rectangle. ✅ *"I want the box to be an actual square not rectangle"* (2026-09-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.28** The layout tells the segment's story: title, then the question, then the items, then the speaker with every line coming out of him. The title is text, not a header pill. 🎬 (2026-09-26) · ✅ no header pill on question-pill beats (2026-10-02) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.29** Results go in one card, not a bento grid of tiles. ✅ *"Make it one card, not multiple"* (2026-09-30) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.30** Six items: 3 + 3 (two rows or two columns), big, each numbered, a small title pill under each (blurred until said), a short title on top. ✅ *"three posts on one line and three posts on another line"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Lists
- **C3.31** A spoken list = numbered pills or cards, each highlighted as it is said. ✅ *"if he's listing something, use either pews or use either cards"* (2026-10-06) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.32** "Five different X" = five boxes, each highlighted differently. ✅ (2026-10-06) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.33** All items show from the first frame; items not yet said are mosaic-blurred (mosaic 200), numbers stay clear; each is revealed when said. ✅ *"Mosaic blur the text of each of them till he mentions it, then reveal"* (2026-10-16) 🔁2 (2026-10-08) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.34** The item being said is lit in the accent, earlier items settle to a neutral dark, upcoming items stay blurred. 🎬 (Apollo, AI B2B) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.35** Chapters 1-4 are each a pill revealed on its own; the intro stays as built. ✅ (2026-10-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.36** A list of steps needs a proper visual: full screen, big boxes with text, the client's colours, the same every time it returns, kept light. ✅ *"always keep consistency around this type of visuals"* (2026-10-10) · *"We need a proper Visual showing the 7 Steps"* (2026-10-08) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.37** Tiers = a pyramid (his $7M Founder 6:30 comp) for the whole tier section, walked tier by tier, the named tier lit, a title on top. ✅ *"Show Pyramind tier like I did in his first video"* (2026-10-08) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.38** Tool reveals copy his Clay reveal (V3 adj clip, Cut v6 0:30.16): all tool cards on screen, the named card lit and pushed in, the others desaturated, opacity down and mosaic-blurred. Reuse it for every later reveal and the closing all-tools shot. ✅ *"I told you to reuse this visual for even the ending parts"* (2026-09-27) 🔁5 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.39** Ghost (faded) numbers go on chapter pills and section devices, and sometimes in boxes, but only where there is no number there already. Never on list pills that already carry a number. ✅ *"we can use them 1 and sometimes in boxes but where we don't have numbers already"* (2026-11-05) (was: none on pills and boxes, *"remove the extra fade number and make the pills smaller"*, 2026-09-27) · ✏ 2026-11-05
- **C3.40** Numbered list cards: the number in a circle left of the bar, an icon circle inside the bar. 🎬 (Cold Outreach) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Circles, hubs, badges
- **C3.41** A hub is a circle with the tool's logo and name, not a screen, with a text-motion heading. ✅ (2026-09-27) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.42** Number badge: a small premium circle top right that animates in; its number changes 1 → 2 → 3. ✅ *"make sure the circle looks premium and animates in"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.43** A group of people: human-looking icons inside one circle, not loose dots. ✅ *"looks to casual, have a circle round them all"* (2026-09-27) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Lines and arrows
- **C3.44** Connectors have two points, are curved, and all start from one point on the speaker's card or circle, fanning out. No right-angle paths. ✅ 🔁2 (2026-09-26, 09-27) 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.45** Pointing to a new state or the right option: an arrow, not a line. ✅ *"Instead of a line, use and arrow to point to the new state"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.46** Arrows are clean. ✅ *"Look at the bad arrowns"* (2026-10-08) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.47** Dashed lines sparingly; a simple polygon line often works better. ✅ *"Don't use Dashed lines too much, sometimes just a simple polygon line on a BG makes better sense"* (2026-10-03) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.48** Pills hung off a hub sit on one straight line connected to it. ✅ *"all pills on a straight line connected to the GTM OS"* (2026-10-03) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.49** Two-pill comparison: along the bottom of frame, lighter accent shades, a straight arrow between, the outcome pill in green. ✅ *"Straight line arrow, and product in green"* (2026-09-27) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.50** A "don't / do" rule: one red box (what he doesn't do), one normal box (what he does), an arrow to the good one. ✅ (2026-10-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Icons
- **C3.51** Icons are solid black Flaticon icons used as masks on a Background node, no white border, with a light sweep. Look in his Assets folder first (his Flaticon library, 255 solid PNGs, 512 px); Lucide for app icon sets; placeholders only if nothing exists. ✅ *"all my Icons come from Flaticons, I use solid black Icons, to mask bg nodes"* (2026-09-27) 🔁3 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.52** Use icons a lot: solid and custom, never emoji-style. ✅ *"I use icons a lot. Solid icons... I must still see icons."* (2026-10-22) · 🎯 emoji-style looks "childish" · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.53** For a real object, show the real thing, not an icon of it. ✅ *"a real thing always beats a fake thing"* (2026-10-19) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.54** Engagement on our own designed cards: one simple icon in the client's colour plus a small count pill, numbers small. Real or rebuilt LinkedIn UI keeps its own reaction row. ✅ *"use a simple one Icon in every linkedin engagement, make the icon on brand"* (2026-10-15) 🔁4 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.55** Made-up posts carry a profile picture (AI face), never a grey default avatar. "PP" in a marker means profile picture. ✅ (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Logos
- **C3.56** Real logos only (the tool's own mark), all at one resolution with their box backgrounds. Reuse the logo designs from his own reveal. Never a screenshot thumbnail or a drawn stand-in, no extra branding. ✅ *"use the logo designs I used in my reveal"* (2026-09-27) 🔁8 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.57** Logo + name = the P01 medallion pill (white circle, accent ring, logo clipped inside). Logo tiles keep the brand's real colours, never grey. ✅ (2026-10-01, 2026-10-10) 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.58** A company is introduced by its logo and name, with no website page behind it. ✅ (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.59** Which real companies may be shown by logo is a client rule (overlay). Made-up company names and logos are fine; use made-up names in demos that make claims about a company. ✅ *"Yes made up companies are ok"* (2026-10-20) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Creative director patterns (use when they fit)
- **C3.60** Chapter cards get a number, a title and an icon ([[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-c12-section-cards|C12]]). 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.61** Simple and exact beats complex: logo + logo = result; one icon grid split into labelled groups; ratios as countable icons that fill. 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.62** Tool logos in circles (glow per overlay); many tool logos orbiting the speaker; a waterfall built step by step. 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.63** Dim the item he isn't talking about; tell groups apart by colour and logo. 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.64** Extend a graphic by building its next layer instead of cutting away; pop new things onto the shot instead of cutting to another screenshot; reuse a diagram swapping one element. 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.65** A quote sits on the footage of the person who said it. ✅ (2026-09-30) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.66** Best-editor vocabulary (Nate Herk): glass cards with a dark-to-accent gradient and top rim light, numbered diamond badges, card rows with slow drift and a pixelated not-yet-spoken card, sonar rings, orbits around a hub, node graphs, counters in glass cards. 🎯 (2026-09-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### How to build
- **C3.67** Box with outline: shape mask → dark fill Background (accent × ~0.10) + an Instance of the same mask (Solid 0, BorderWidth ≈ 0.00184) → accent Background, merged over the fill. Borders: pills 0.0012-0.0016, rings 0.0021-0.0046. Corner radius from the overlay. 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.68** Every fill, border and ground Background has alpha 1; only a clear canvas is alpha 0 (a card at TopLeftAlpha 0 rendered invisible). ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.69** Circle avatar: MediaIn → Transform → MatteControl ← EllipseMask (crop with MatteControl, not a mask on the Merge). Frosted glass: RectangleMask → Background → GaussianBlur → Merge → Merge (EffectMask = the same rect). 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.70** Build each element at its final place in frame; Neo Anim stays at Global Center 0.5/0.5 and only animates. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.71** Build 2D elements with the Python generators (`pk.py` + `pillkit.py`: `bgorange()`, `macro()`, `copy_his()`, `arrow()`, `pill()`), never node by node. How: [[03-Areas/video-editing/sops/fusion-build|Fusion build SOP]]. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.72** Icon traps: Flaticon icons with white detail (check, checked, no) render as solid discs when used as masks; glyphs (✓ ✕ →) go in their own Text+ or as an icon, never Segoe UI Symbol on a Text+ that holds a word. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.79** A roadmap runs horizontally, left to right, not vertically. ✅ *"Make the roadmap horizontal isntead of vertical"* (2026-11-06)

## Never (strict)
- **C3.73** Boxes everywhere, or a box behind text over the speaker, a page or a screen. ❌ *"no need for boxes every where bro"* (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.74** The same generic box design through a video. ❌ *"You are using the same gemeric box designs in the whole video"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.75** A circle crop of the speaker, or a black circle in mid-frame (darken the whole frame instead). ❌ *"Don't show a circle of him"* · *"Why this black circle at the middle"* (2026-10-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.76** Tall glass side boxes or circle wipes over the A-roll (rejected and rebuilt on 10,000 SaaS). ❌ (2026-10-24) → Q-C3.a · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.77** Emoji-style icons, icons with a white border, screenshot thumbnails as logos, grey logo tiles. ❌ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C3.78** A background wave inside a pill, dot meters instead of a fill bar, the toggle, receipt and rotating-seal designs. ❌ *"Just don't use that background wave"* (2026-09-30) · ✔ 2026-11-05
