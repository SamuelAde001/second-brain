# C6 · Highlighting and brightness areas

> **Read when:** planning or building any page, article, screen recording, UI or text-over-footage beat, and when reviewing them (Editor, editor-builder, editor-worker). **Client values:** highlighter colour only, in the client overlays: [[03-Areas/video-editing/clients/frontal-brand-v2|Frontal brand v2]] (K2), [[03-Areas/video-editing/clients/gamma|Gamma]] (K3). **Status:** approved by Samuel, 2026-11-05 (issues answered one by one, the rest approved in one go).

## Rules

### One focus point
- **C6.1** Every page or UI frame has exactly one thing the eye goes to. Make it with light, zoom and highlight. ✅ *"there must be one thing that the user is trying to focus on"* (2026-10-06) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.2** Three points of focus on every page, article and UI: (1) the page reads as what it is, (2) the area of interest brightens while the rest goes darker, (3) the exact words get highlighted. ✅ 🔁×2 *"show the general context, then highlilght the area of interest with brightness, and then highlight the specific word"* (2026-10-25) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.3** Light the focus with a BrightnessContrast that darkens everything else, on an adjustment clip above the media, never inside the clip's own comp. ✅ 🔁×3 *"highlight with Brightness contrast, what you want people to focus on"* (2026-09-27, 2026-10-03) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.4** Every light page, dashboard and UI gets the lighting trick: a lit spot near the centre, everything else clearly darker. A flat white page with only a highlighter or a pill on it is "lacking lighting tricks". 🎯 🔁×3 *"lacking lighting tricks"* (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.5** What is being read sits in the centre of the frame, not the top. 🎯 *"eyes shouldnt go to top here pls try to centralize this"* (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.6** In motion graphics, one element is lit at a time: the one being spoken is fully lit or in the accent; the others dim through his Opacity macro (Gain 0.42-0.62, Saturation 0-0.64, over 6-8 f). 🎬 To pick one out, dim the others. ✅ *"just reduce the brightnest for the other"* (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.7** In a busy frame, zoom in and darken the rest. Steer the eye with light (plus zoom, an arrow, a one-word label) so "even a 75-year-old viewer" gets it. 🎯 (2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Spotlight settings
- **C6.8** Spotlight: BrightnessContrast outside one inverted soft rounded rect (CornerRadius ~0.12, SoftEdge 0.004), padded 14 px sideways and 5 px up and down. Its edge never crosses text. 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.9** Outside the spot: Gain ~0.40-0.50, Saturation ~0.5. Don't go darker: LinkedIn came back *"the screen is too dark"* (2026-10-15). ✅ 🎬 Exact number: Q-C6.b. · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.10** Fade the spot in over 5-14 f (12 f eased). Cutting in from a dark frame, the light is on from frame 0, or the page flashes white. 🎬 ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.11** The spot moves with the words: it re-targets every 1.3-3.8 s, ~10 f per move, and the zoom moves with it. For text in a doc it shrinks to the one line. A gap over 24 f fades it out. 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.12** Between boxes the spot cross-fades; it never slides over blank lines. ⚙️ (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.13** On dark pages keep the surround around median 35-40 (8-bit) so it still reads. 🎯 ⚙️ (2026-10-27) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Highlighting words
- **C6.14** Highlight the whole phrase at once, never word by word, and not too fast. ✅ 🔁×3 *"Highlight this words at once, not one by one"* (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.15** Highlight on the word, early, never late. Move to the next item as soon as it's said; hold it while it's still being talked about. ✅ 🔁×6 *"Highlight the one post from here, it highlights too late"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.16** Highlight the whole thing he names, and the exact detail the line names (the view count when he says views). Light the whole message when the whole message matters. ✅ *"Highlight the whole signup list not just the numbers"* (2026-10-15) 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.17** Articles and documents get a double highlight: darken everything except the line, then mark the exact words. Leaving the page bright is a mistake. Mix styles (marker, underline). 🎯 🔁×2 (2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.18** The marker is Neo Highlighter Pro, one stroke per line, in the highlighter colour (client overlay). On a dark page, an accent band with light text. Settings: B3.38. 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.19** A list he walks through is revealed in the same page over consecutive shots, each part highlighted as he says it. ✅ *"Highlight what is said in the page, and reveal them as he says them"* (2026-10-03) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.20** A stat from a named report shows the report itself, marker strokes landing line by line, not a stat card. 🎬 (TSB) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.21** If a source page shows a figure that contradicts what Alex says, highlight the title or a matching line, crop it, or darken it under our number. On screen we show his number. ⚙️ (2026-10-20) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.22** Screen recordings get exactly this treatment: highlight the area in focus, cursor movement, his media adjustment clip. ✅ *"we always normally highlight the area that is on focus, then we use cursor movement"* (2026-11-01) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### When not to highlight
- **C6.23** Not every visual needs a highlight. When the zoom is already close on the text, or he says so, just zoom in gradually. ✅ 🔁×3 *"Since we are close to the text box here no need to highlight it"* (2026-10-25); *"Remove highlights here and just let it zoom in gradually"* (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Darkening for text
- **C6.24** Text over footage or a page always sits on darkened footage (Gain 0.34-0.42; a quote down to 0.17) or on his Down fade. 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.25** Darker still behind all text, the whole video (luminance ×0.70 under every text). 🎯 *"should darken behind all texts a bit more this entire vid prob"* (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.26** Text over undarkened A-roll sits low (y ≥ 900 px), centred, on the Down fade, never over his face. A darkened statement layout is not a lower third. Placement: C4. 🎯 (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.27** Darken neutrally with BrightnessContrast, moderately: dark base, bright elements on top, never bright on bright. 🎯 (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.34** The dark shade behind text depends on what is under the text and where the text sits. Lower third: darken only the lower third, faded the way he does it (his Down fade). Big text in the middle over a visual: darken the whole screen so the text pops. Never extremely dark. Context decides; this rule governs C6.24-C6.26. ✅ *"The dark shade behind text depends on the visuals underneath the text and the position of the text, if it's lower third the dark shade should be just on the lower third and faded like I do it, if it's a big text over a visual in the middle of the screen the dark shade should darken the whole screen so the text can pop out, but it all depends on the context, and the dark shade shouldn't be extremely dark"* (2026-11-05) · ✔ 2026-11-05

## Never (strict)
- **C6.28** No uniform dark or grey shade over his visuals unless it steers the eye to one spot. ✅ 🔁×2 *"The dark shade is not needful"*, *"Don't put this shade on screen on his visual"* (2026-10-24). See Q-C6.a. · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.29** No green (or any coloured) glow to mark the right one. ✅ *"no need for the green glow"* (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.30** No brown or orange backdrop to darken. 🎯 (2026-10-15) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.31** No highlight boxes or row highlighters drawn into a UI mockup; the highlight comes from the spotlight above it. ✅ *"Don't add that row highlighter"* (2026-09-27). (Its "only when he asks" part is superseded by C6.2.) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.32** Never blur what is being said or highlighted. 🎬 (2026-10-13, C7) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C6.33** No text or motion graphics on screen recordings (C8). ✅ (2026-11-01) · ✔ 2026-11-05

## Questions for Samuel
- **Q-C6.a** Dark shade. Your video 2 rev 3 markers (2026-10-24): *"The dark shade is not needful"*; the Editor then cut the vignette 0.835 → 0.4 and softened spotlights to Gain 0.65. The creative director (2026-10-26): lighting tricks on every page, darker behind all text. Your order puts your markers above the CD. → Options: (1) both stand: darkening is fine when it lights one spot or sits behind text; a uniform shade over a visual is banned (2) your marker wins: mild spotlights only, no extra darkening behind text (3) the CD wins. My pick: (1). → Samuel: the shade depends on the text's position and what is under it (new C6.34): lower third = shade on the lower third only, faded; big centre text = darken the whole screen; never extremely dark (2026-11-05).
- **Q-C6.b** The number outside the spot: 0.16 (settled with the CD for video 4, 2026-10-27), 0.40-0.42 (your Apollo final), 0.50 (video 4, after your LinkedIn "too dark"), 0.65 (video 2, after "dark shade not needful"). → Options: (1) 0.45 ±0.05 as standard (2) 0.65 mild everywhere (3) your number. My pick: (1). → Samuel: (1), 0.45 ±0.05 as the standard (2026-11-05).
