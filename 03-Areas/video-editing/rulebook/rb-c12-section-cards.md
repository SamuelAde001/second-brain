# C12 · Section cards and chapter markers

> **Read when:** planning or building a section start, a chapter return or a step marker. **Client values:** pill shape (square or rounded), fonts, colours and the chapter templates in [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k2-alex-frontal|K2 Alex / Frontal]] and [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k3-gamma|K3 Gamma]]. **Status:** draft, not yet approved by Samuel.

## Rules

### The chapter marker
- **C12.1** A chapter starts with a pill at the bottom of the frame over the A-roll, revealed on screen as he names it. Not a full-screen card. 🔁 ✅ (2026-10-07 TSB, 2026-10-25 video 2) 🎬
- **C12.2** Chapter returns use that bottom pill, never the intro list again. ✅ (2026-10-25)
- **C12.3** All chapter and step pills in a video are one size, with a small dark fade behind. 🔁 ✅ (2026-10-10, 2026-10-26)
- **C12.4** Working size at 1080 (video 4): pill text 76 px caps, capsule 112 px high, centred at y 926. ⚙️ (2026-10-28) Font and shape: client overlay.
- **C12.5** A chapter marker is a lower third: centred, one line, big, low in the bottom band. ✅ 🎯 (2026-10-25, 2026-10-26) Rules: C4.30.
- **C12.6** Chapter card content: number + title + icon. Number white, written 1, 2, never 01. Text phone-size. 🎯 (2026-10-22)
- **C12.7** A step inside a chapter gets its own step pill, e.g. "STEP 1 · NAME", same family as the chapter pill. 🎬 (B2B, 2026-10-08)
- **C12.8** A ghost numeral behind the chapter pill is an approved option. ✅ liked (2026-09-30) 🎬

### The section start
- **C12.9** Each section start gets the section slam on the A-roll (zoom-out with blur, ~28 f) together with the chapter pill. 🎬 (TSB, AI4, B2B) Slam settings: B5.
- **C12.10** A recurring section device keeps one design every time it returns. It may double as a progress bar: the teaser blurred, then each section lit as it comes, all lit at the end. ✅ (2026-10-10) 🎬 (AI4)
- **C12.11** A new chapter changes the song (in a gap). 🎯 ✅ Detail: D1.
- **C12.12** Vary chapter styles across videos; keep a library of chapter designs so no video repeats the last one. ✅ *"different lists styles, different chapter styles, different lower thirds"* (2026-10-20)
- **C12.13** Build a section intro from his marker comments, not from a guess; a section intro he marked is rebuilt to his marker. ✅ *"you did a very bad work in the other time I tolled you to build an intro"* (2026-10-03)

### The chapter overview
- **C12.14** The chapters are first shown in "In this video" as numbered boxes, blurred and revealed as he names each. ✅ (2026-10-24) 🎯 Detail: C13.

## Never
- **C12.15** A full-screen chapter card. ✅ (2026-10-07 switched off in TSB, 2026-10-25) ❌
- **C12.16** Repeating the intro list as a chapter return (C12.2). ✅ ❌
- **C12.17** Chapter pills of different sizes in one video. ✅ ❌
