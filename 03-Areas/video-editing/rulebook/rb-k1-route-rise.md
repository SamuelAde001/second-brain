# K1 · Route Rise agency rules

> **Read when:** setting up any new Route Rise job, before a review goes to the creative director, and before delivery. **Client values:** [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k2-alex-frontal|K2 Alex / Frontal]] · [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k3-gamma|K3 Gamma]] · new client: [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k4-new-client|K4]]. **Status:** draft, not yet approved by Samuel.

## Rules
### Who is who
- **K1.1** Route Rise Media LTD is the agency above Samuel and his only payer. End clients so far: Alex (Frontal) and Gamma. Payment contact: Lewis Mountstephens. Maria Fernanda Alvarez runs the month-end EOM Team Huddle. 🏢 ✅ (2026-09-20, 2026-10-30)
- **K1.2** Rates are set by Route Rise: Alex USD 333.33 a video, Gamma USD 175 a video. One monthly invoice covers both. A new client's rate comes from Samuel, never guessed. 🏢 ✅ (2026-09-20, 2026-10-30)
- **K1.3** One rulebook for every Route Rise client. Between clients only the fonts, the colours and (for Alex) square boxes change. The style, the typography approach and the premium chain stay. ✅ *"what would change between clients are the fonts, colors and for alex, square boxes instead of rounded corners"* (2026-11-03) 🔁 (also 2026-10-22)
- **K1.4** The Route Rise creative director (Shawn) sets the edit standard. He judges every edit on visual frequency, visual style, the look and the sound design, and asks why each edit was made. His breakdowns are the standard: [[03-Areas/video-editing/creative-director/creative-director-standard|creative director standard]]. 🎯 (2026-10-22)

### How a job arrives
- **K1.5** A job comes as a ClickUp card: the card's dates set the order and the deadline. The script comes as a Notion idea doc (Alex) or a ClickUp script doc (Gamma); footage as a Google Drive folder; screen recordings often via Tella. Setup steps: [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-a1-download-and-sorting|A1]]. 🏢 (2026-09-24, 2026-10-30)
- **K1.6** The deadline is the one on the ClickUp card. *"we follow the deadline giving on clickup"* (2026-11-05) (Q-K1.b; the 5-day cap of 2026-09-02 is no longer the measure.) Samuel doesn't work Sundays. 🏢 · ✏ 2026-11-05
- **K1.23** Revisions: as many rounds as needed, but most times one round from the creative director and one from the main client. Plan the job for those two. ✅ *"we do as many revisions as needed if needed but most times ones from the creative director and one from the main client"* (2026-11-05) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **K1.7** Volume: at least 5 videos a month, and faster each time. ✅ *"my company said they want me to edit at least 5 videos"* (2026-10-04) 🔁 (2026-09-29)
- **K1.8** The client's production notes on the script are rules for that video (Alex: anonymous quotes, the CTA link, numbers as written) unless Samuel settles otherwise with the client (K0.10). 🏢 (2026-10-06)
- **K1.9** Footage often arrives incomplete. List what is still needed from the client on day one, ranked, and send it to Samuel. 🏢 ⚙️

### What Route Rise always wants
- **K1.10** Graphics are editable native Fusion nodes. No PNG sequences or rasterised stand-ins. 🏢 ✅ (TSB, 2026-09-19)
- **K1.11** Show over tell: picture every point, with clear calls for when to cut back to the A-roll. B-roll shows, animation explains. Treat viewers like kids: simple and literal. 🏢 (Route Rise editing guide, Module 1, 2026-09-25)
- **K1.12** Retention devices from the same guide: blur signposting, progress bars and visual timelines, zoom plus text on key words, moving B-roll with text. Detail: [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-c15-pacing|C15]]. 🏢 (2026-09-25)
- **K1.13** Every on-screen claim matches what the speaker says and the script. Numbers on screen are the numbers the speaker says, checked on the audio. 🎯 (CD, 2026-10-26)
- **K1.14** The accent colour is per client, never fixed: take it from the client overlay. 🏢 (2026-09-22)
- **K1.15** The client films on macOS. Any browser insert Samuel records himself is made in Chrome, F11 full screen, so no window chrome shows Windows. 🏢 (2026-09-22)
- **K1.16** Blur or crop private data on screen: emails, phone numbers, card numbers, business addresses, store URLs. 🏢 (cut sheets)

### Review and delivery
- **K1.17** Versions go to the creative director on Frame.io. His notes there become rules for the whole video and the next ones (video 2: 21 notes on v6). 🎯 (2026-10-26)
- **K1.18** CD corrections come after Samuel's final. Apply them, re-render the same way as before and name it the next version (v2). ✅ *"now I want to render it again same way as before, name it v2"* (2026-10-16)
- **K1.19** Samuel uploads and delivers. No agent sends anything to Route Rise, Frame.io, Slack or the client. ⚙️ (AGENTS.md §5 rule 1)

## Never
- **K1.20** Never Dynamic Zoom; MagicZoom V3 instead ([[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-b5-zooms-and-crops|B5]]). ❌ 🏢 ✅ *"I prefer MagicZoom V3 instead of Dynamic Zoom"* (2026-09-22)
- **K1.21** Never a transition on the A-roll. Cuts only. ❌ 🏢 🎬 (0 dissolves in 503 clips, $7M Founder)
- **K1.22** Never use Ifeoma's notes or personal-brand rules on a Route Rise edit (K0.12). ❌ ✅ (2026-10-28)
