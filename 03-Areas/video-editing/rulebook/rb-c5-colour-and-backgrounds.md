# C5 · Colours, variety, backgrounds

> **Read when:** choosing fills, the ground, darkening under text, or any red, green or grey on a beat (visual plan, beat builds, beat reviews). **Client values:** palette hex values, accent, the ground macro, which shades replace green/red: [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k2-alex-frontal|K2 Alex / Frontal]], [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k3-gamma|K3 Gamma]]. **Status:** draft, not yet approved by Samuel.

## Rules
### Stay in the client's palette
- **C5.1** Every colour comes from the client's palette (overlay). For a client, the colours, the box types and the background change to their brand; everything else in his style stays. ✅ *"The colors will change to his brand. So the colors, the type of boxes, and the background"* (2026-10-22)
- **C5.2** Use the colours the client's kit gives, never another client's. Each client's look comes from that client's own reference. ✅ *"please use the colors I told you to"* (2026-10-31) 🔁2
- **C5.3** Never recolour an element to a tool's brand colour (no purple for Railway). Real logos keep their own colours; a logo tile is the logo brand's real colour, never grey. ✅ *"don't change the color to purple, it most be on brand"* (2026-09-27) · logo tiles (2026-10-10)
- **C5.4** Don't put a client's brand font or colours on things built for Samuel himself (e.g. his B-roll pages) unless he asks. ✅ (before 2026-09-19)
- **C5.5** Use the client's current website and brand file, never an old one. ✅ *"tHIS IS THEIR OLD WEBSITE, CHECK THE BRAND FILE FOR THE NEW WEBSITE"* (2026-10-25)

### Variety inside the palette
- **C5.6** Mix shades: light and dark accent, white, sometimes grey. Same font, same scheme, more creative ways. ✅ *"mix it up, use light and dark orange and white, and sometimes grey"* (2026-09-30) 🔁3 (2026-09-25, 09-30, 10-22 *"Not the same colors of boxes all the time"*)
- **C5.7** Neighbouring visuals never share a fill recipe (solid accent, white paper, light accent, neutral dark, grey glass sparingly, outline). A board of items uses different colours. ✅ *"tHE CALL BOARD SHOULD HAVE DIFFERENT COLORS"* (2026-10-25)
- **C5.8** Variety check: no variant twice in any 4 graphic beats in a row, at least 9 variants per part. ⚙️ (Gamma, 2026-10-31)
- **C5.9** Rank items with tints of one brand colour: the shade varies, the hue stays. 🎯
- **C5.10** Grey is a fill tone for elements, never a changed background (see C5.11). ✅ (2026-09-30)
- **C5.11** Text and graphics are almost always white. 🎯

### The ground
- **C5.12** The ground never changes: the client's background macro or the A-roll. Fills change, the ground doesn't. ✅ *"I want a consistent background, so, not this grey background"* (2026-09-30) 🔁 (13 gallery designs sent back) · *"Don't change background from the default one"* (2026-09-27)
- **C5.13** Never a grey, cream, full-accent or flat-colour ground, never a tool's brand colour, never flat black. A ground is a dark base with a brand-colour gradient or radial glow, a faint grid, a vignette, or a blurred, dimmed real screenshot. 🎬
- **C5.14** Never bright on bright: bright elements on a dark base. 🎯 (a beige chapter background was the one bad thing in Apollo)
- **C5.15** UI demos in dark mode, unless the real product isn't. ✅ *"Dark mode instead"* (2026-10-02) 🔁3
- **C5.16** No white text on a white surface. ⚙️

### Darkening under text
- **C5.17** To put text over footage, darken the whole frame neutrally and moderately with a Brightness Contrast node. Never a brown or orange backdrop. ✅ *"use brightness and contrast node to darken the whole video when you want to put text on top"* (2026-10-15) 🔁5 · *"I hate the orange backdrop"*
- **C5.18** Not too dark, and no black frame by accident: find out why it happened. ✅ *"we have a black backgroiund here, figure out why"* (2026-10-24) · *"the screen is too dark"* (2026-10-15)
- **C5.19** No dark shade over his screen visuals unless it steers the eye to one spot ([[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-c6-highlighting|C6]]). ✅ (2026-10-24)
- **C5.20** A light web page under a number: Blur 8-14 + a dark overlay at 75-85 %, or dark glass behind the number. Never dim the page to grey (Gain 0.4-0.55 looks flat and cheap) or to black (Gain 0.16). Page lighting gain 0.42 → 0.50; LinkedIn surround 125-145/255. ⚙️ (2026-10-21, 10-27, 10-29)
- **C5.21** Lighting-trick sweeps never flash white on a cut from a dark frame; no flat white page without the lighting trick. ⚙️ (2026-10-27)
- **C5.22** A person's picture under text is dimmed (Gain 0.34). 🎬 (Gamma, 2026-11-01)
- **C5.23** Every B-roll clip gets a vignette and a slight glow. 🏢 (2026-09-25)

### Red, green and grey carry meaning
- **C5.24** Green and red only where truly needed. Green = money up, revenue, something done right, advice to follow. Red = something not to do, a revenue stall, what failed. Everything else in the client's own shades. ✅ *"you used so so so much green and red in the video when they aren't needed"* (2026-10-23) 🔁2 (2026-10-31)
- **C5.25** A stated negative: red coloration on the word, with a red vignette and desaturation. ✅ *"When they say something negative use a red coloration at the right timing with a red vignette, and destaturation"* (2026-10-25) → Q-C5.a Only on B-roll or screen UI on top, never on the A-roll (B2.17, 2026-11-05).
- **C5.26** Something that just isn't working or is stuck: desaturate or grey it, don't paint it red. ✅ *"thsi red is not needed, just disaturated instead"* (2026-10-24)
- **C5.27** A failure vs success pair: the failing company in red, the succeeding one in green. ✅ (2026-10-25)
- **C5.28** Pick out a winner by dimming the others, not with a green glow. ✅ (2026-10-24)
- **C5.29** Grey out the dismissed thing (logo, footage, dashboard) on its beat, with a sound effect; show the alternatives first, then grey them. 🎯 *"grey is more chill"*
- **C5.30** A chart line turns red where it stalls ([[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-c10-graphs-and-stats|C10]]). ✅

### Build method
- **C5.31** Default fill = accent × ~0.10, border = the accent or its lighter tint, when the kit names no fill. 🎬 💡 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **C5.32** Background node colours are not premultiplied: multiply RGB by alpha, or low-alpha fills show bright. Straight-alpha PNG Loaders: PostMultiplyByAlpha = 1. ⚙️
- **C5.33** Kill gradient banding with a light FastNoise overlay (3-5 %) or grain, or float16 on the ground macro. ⚙️

## Never
- **C5.34** A red strike or cancel line through something. What failed turns red; what is stuck turns grey. ❌ (2026-10-25; replaces the BAD FIT strike-through shipped in Apollo, 2026-10-03)
- **C5.35** Green and red all over a video. ❌ (2026-10-23)
- **C5.36** A brown or orange backdrop under text. ❌ *"I hate the orange backdrop you added to this kind of text"* (2026-10-15)
- **C5.37** Changing the ground for a beat (grey, cream, full accent, flat colour). ❌ (2026-09-30)
- **C5.38** Another client's colours, or a tool's brand colour on our elements. ❌ (2026-09-27, 2026-10-31)
