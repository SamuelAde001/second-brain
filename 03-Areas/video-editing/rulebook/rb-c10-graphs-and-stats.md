# C10 · Graphs and stats

> **Read when:** a beat has a number, a percentage, money, a ratio or a trend (visual plan, beat builds, beat reviews, the number check). **Client values:** chart series colours, gridlines, 3D and gradient bans in [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k2-alex-frontal|K2 Alex / Frontal]] and [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k3-gamma|K3 Gamma]]. **Status:** draft, not yet approved by Samuel.

## Rules
### Show the data, not text
- **C10.1** Show the chart, not text about it. ✅ *"Show the actual revenue Chart of them, not text, show and not tell"* (2026-10-24) 🔁4 (2026-10-24, 10-25)
- **C10.2** Every revenue chart is a real chart: money values on the right axis, years along the bottom, labelled lines, a title on top. ✅ *"always show numbers in a chart, like a real chart"* (2026-10-25)
- **C10.3** Years and dates go in the graph, not as separate text. ✅ *"Don't show 2019 here, show it in the graph"* (2026-10-25)
- **C10.4** Pick the chart by the number. Percentage → an animated pie with the number counting. Growth → a bar chart. Ratio ("1 in 10", "8 of 10") → countable cells or icons that fill (e.g. ten cells). ✅ *"Use a bar chart instead to show the growth"* (2026-10-25) 🔁5 (2026-10-08, 10-25) · 🎯 cells (2026-10-26)
- **C10.5** A pie chart carries little text. ✅ *"Show a pie chart istead, with less texts"* (2026-10-08)
- **C10.6** A stat or progress bar is one bar that fills up: no segment dots, no multi-part progress bar. ✅ *"don't use this kind of progress bar, use one ling bar"* (2026-10-25) · *"I prefer a bar that fills up instead of the dots"* (2026-09-30)
- **C10.7** An abstract claim gets one simple chart. Back a claim with a real chart, article or search result when one exists. 🎯
- **C10.8** A number gets the real UI or page it came from under it, when there is one. ✅ (2026-10-15)

### Animate on the word
- **C10.9** Numbers count up or animate in; the counter rolls inside the clip. ✅ (19 markers, video 2, 2026-10-25) 🔁19
- **C10.10** Start on the word. No chart before he talks about it; the line draws each year as it is said. ✅ *"I dont like the way you started showing appolos revenue when they haven't started talking about it"* (2026-10-25)
- **C10.11** A line chart grows at one constant rate from start to end while he speaks. ✅ *"let it be a constant growth"* (2026-10-24)
- **C10.12** A flat stretch is the line growing flat, not a "flat" label box. ✅ *"remove the flat name box, and,let the line growth to flat, as it is animated"* (2026-10-24)
- **C10.13** The line turns red where growth stalls. ✅ *"change the color of the line to red, to show how it stopped"* (2026-10-24)
- **C10.14** The tip dot rides its line on every frame. ✅ (2026-10-25)
- **C10.15** Axis labels and gridlines don't all land together or before the word. A counter's number, label and box don't enter on the same frame. ⚙️ (7 of 9 video 2 charts and 10 of 16 counters failed this, 2026-10-28)
- **C10.16** Judge a counter by where its count ends against the end of the spoken number. ⚙️
- **C10.17** His approved animations: a 90 % pie ring 0 → 0.9 over 22 f with a Numbers counter; a data card with a thin border, area gradient, count-up and card flash (`tsb_V2_000210_1`). 🎬

### True numbers
- **C10.18** Every number on screen is heard in its beat (±1 s) or elsewhere in the video, and matches what the speaker says, not the script. Listen to the audio before changing a number (Whisper heard "1,700" where the creative director heard 700). 🎯 ⚙️ (2026-10-26) · ✅ show Alex's number (2026-10-20)
- **C10.19** Facts on charts hold up and are to scale. A label or bracket spans exactly what it names (the "7 years" bracket covers all seven). 🎯 *"is the years at the bottom accurate? its 2020"* (2026-10-26)
- **C10.20** Never invent a stat. Real numbers come from the footage. Illustrative numbers in made-up UI stay inside real ranges and never pass as a client's stat. ⚙️
- **C10.21** No source or citation line on a stat card. 🎬 (he removed "6sense · 2025 Buyer Experience Report" in the B2B final, 2026-10-13; overrules the creative director's source line and the 2026-10-10 source line on the deal tokens)

### Stat devices
- **C10.22** Small stats: small pills at the bottom of frame, not full screen. ✅ *"Put this as small pills on screen, they don't need full screen, small pills down"* (2026-10-25) 🔁4
- **C10.23** A number sits inside real UI or a data card, never as a giant number on its own. 🎬 (Apollo; the "66%" overlay was removed in TSB)
- **C10.24** Money is never a bare counter: a box, a subline and the business logo. A stat card = the big number in the accent, a detail line in a different text style, the logo; a small qualifier he says ("generated in 2026") goes in a sub-bubble under the number. 🎯
- **C10.25** For a stat block, reuse his past stat style in the client's skin; don't invent a new box. ✅ *"we have done something like this before, use that style just with the new branding"* (2026-10-26)
- **C10.26** Two contrasting numbers can flank the speaker: a red dot-grid circle "5,000" on one side, a green circle of 25 person icons on the other. 🎬 (AI4)
- **C10.27** A bad number pops, shakes and turns grey with a sound. Money lost is a falling red number in real-looking UI. Benchmarks are glowing labelled bars. A wrong claim and the true one sit side by side. 🎯 → Q-C10.a
- **C10.28** Build charts from real data with the chart kit (`frdata.py`, kit v3). ⚙️

## Never
- **C10.29** Lines without numbers on an axis. ❌ *"I don't want to see just lines"* (2026-10-25)
- **C10.30** Text where a chart should be. ❌ (2026-10-24)
- **C10.31** Dot meters or multi-part progress bars. ❌ (2026-09-30, 2026-10-25)
- **C10.32** A chart or number on screen before it is said. ❌ (2026-10-25)
- **C10.33** A citation line on a stat card; an invented stat. ❌
