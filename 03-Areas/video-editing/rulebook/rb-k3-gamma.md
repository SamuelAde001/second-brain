# K3 · Gamma (client overlay)

> **Read when:** any step of a Gamma job that touches the look: planning, every build, every review. **Client values:** this note. **Status:** draft, not yet approved by Samuel.

## Rules
### Who and what
- **K3.1** Gamma is a Route Rise end client, not Alex: the Frontal kit does not apply. Same client as the August 2026 "40 Social Media Posts" job. ✅ *"still from my company Routerise, but not the same client Alex, this client name is gamma"* (2026-10-30)
- **K3.2** The presenter is a woman whose name we don't know: never name her on screen. In rebuilt UIs use the stand-in name "Casey". ⚙️ (2026-10-31)
- **K3.3** Frame: 1920×1080, 25 fps everywhere. Two cameras (A + B): multicam rules in [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-a2-import-and-sync|A2]]. ⚙️ (2026-10-30)
- **K3.4** Gamma is logged in on his Chrome: capture the real app for every Gamma visual. ✅ *"I have logged in on Gamma for more accurate visuals"* (2026-10-31)

### The reference
- **K3.5** The one-to-one reference is his own Gamma edit *"Claude Just Changed Freelancing Forever!"* (YouTube 5YlpIKqN8Ek). Resolve project in `Routerise\Delivered`, timeline *Sequence 01 - FLAT*, 25 fps, 13,531 frames; 105 comps exported in the job's `Docs\Gamma style study (Editor)\ref comps\`. ✅ (2026-10-31, 2026-11-01)
- **K3.6** Study it by opening the timeline: census every track (what sits over each screen recording, over her, how many, how long), read the comps and nodes, and watch it. Copy his grammar; don't just measure his shapes. Clone his own comps with new text (`refclone.py`). 🔁 (2026-10-31, 2026-11-01) ✅ *"I told you to study how I edited the gamma video"* (2026-11-01)
- **K3.7** It must not be a replica, but it must be creative inside his styles, and better than his own video. ✅ *"It must not be replica ... but still creative"* (2026-10-31)

### Colours
- **K3.8** Only two brand colours, for fills, strokes and glows: navy `#002253` and sky `#84c1fa`. White is for text only. ✅ *"The colours are : #84c1fa and: #002253"* (2026-10-31) 🔁 *"please use the colors I told you to"* (2026-10-31)
- **K3.9** Green `#3ddc84` for a positive, red `#ff4d5e` for a negative, only to reinforce meaning. ✅ *"when there's something that needs to be reinforced as negative, you can put it red"* (2026-10-31)
- **K3.10** Ground: the LinkedIn video background in Gamma's colours (a new BGGAMMA file; his macros stay untouched). Keep it calm: not over-animated. ✅ *"The background is the same background as that LinkedIn video, just with a different color"* (2026-10-31)

### Font
- **K3.11** ES Build, Bold ALL CAPS for headings and keywords. ✅ *"The font is ES build"* (2026-10-31). See Q-K3.a on the label font.

### Shape: rounded
- **K3.12** Rounded corners on every box. ✅ *"the boxes are wrong, she always had rounder corners"* (2026-10-31)
- **K3.13** Pills CornerRadius 0.8-1.0. Cards 48-80 px radius (CornerRadius about 0.22-0.55 by box size). No visible corner under ~28 px. Number bubbles are always circles. 🎬 ⚙️ (measured from his comps, 2026-10-31)
- **K3.14** Elements: pills, glass boxes as in the LinkedIn video, full boxes. From his reference: glossy navy glass pills, circle bubbles, lower pills, a roadmap, sky accents; bottom pills as a numbered step pill, a price pill, three circles, a header bar, a two-line statement (red line for a negative). ✅ *"You can use pills, you can use glass boxes"* (2026-10-31) 🎬 (2026-11-01)
- **K3.15** Shines: the light sweep is always on hero boxes; an animated shine only on major moments, about one per section. ✅ *"the light sweep is always there to give a little bit depth"* (2026-10-31)

### Over her, and on screens
- **K3.16** Most graphics go on the main screen over her, not on navy. Graphics sit in her free side third (~560 px); she stays centred. Full-frame navy only for major moments, 90 s at most in the whole video. ✅ *"you did too much of the animation on the blue background and not enough on the main screen"* (2026-10-31)
- **K3.17** Text over her: short ALL-CAPS lines, about 14 characters a line, in NeoTextMotion; the accent (sky) is the last word; a negative second line is red as a whole. Her picture dims to Gain 0.34 under text. White text only on boxes. 🎬 (his reference, read 2026-11-01)
- **K3.18** Boxes vary: never all the same design. ✅ *"the boxes all look the same, no much variance"* (2026-10-31)
- **K3.19** Screen recordings get only adjustment clips: his NeoHighlighter plus darken-the-rest, cursor movement, NeoAnim entrance with a small vignette (Blend 0.638) and a slow MagicZoom. Rules: [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-c8-screens-and-ui|C8]], [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-b2-comps-and-adjustment-clips|B2]]. ✅ 🎬 (2026-11-01)
- **K3.20** She talks through many tools: demo each one properly, with clicks, dropdowns and the list items shown as they are said. ✅ *"I feel they didn't demo enough"* (2026-10-31)

## Never
- **K3.21** Never Frontal's design on Gamma: no square boxes, corner brackets, tabs, die-cut stickers, hard offset shadows, square bars, white card bodies, `#1862d4`, Frontal blues or orange. ❌ ✅ *"you are using the same design of Alex for Gamma ... I told you to make better designs not copy frontals designs"* (2026-10-31)
- **K3.22** Never motion graphics or text on her screen recordings. ❌ ✅ *"Since when did we start putting motion graphics on screen recording visuals"* (2026-11-01)
- **K3.23** Never invent a style: no bouncing animations, no overshoot pop scale-ins, no random text popping on screen, and none of the constructs that read as AI on v4 (stickers, toasts, checklists, panels, counter chips, float pills, marker sweeps). ❌ ✅ *"the visuals all look AI, with bouncing animations and too much text"* (2026-11-01)

## Questions for Samuel
- **Q-K3.a** Which font for labels and small text? You said *"The font is ES build"* (2026-10-31). Measured from your Gamma comps: Open Sans Bold on labels, ES Build on hero numbers; the Editor's read of your timeline (2026-11-01) also found PP Mori. → Options: (1) ES Build for everything; (2) ES Build for headings and numbers, Open Sans Bold for labels; (3) as your reference uses it, ES Build plus PP Mori. My pick: (2) if your comps really use it, else (1). Your words win until you answer.
- **Q-K3.b** Reference order. On 2026-10-31 you pointed to the LinkedIn video's styles; on 2026-11-01 you called LinkedIn *"just a side refrence"* and your Gamma edit the one to copy. → Options: (1) your Gamma edit leads, LinkedIn only for pills, glass and the background (as K3.5-K3.10 read now); (2) both equal. My pick: (1).
- **Q-K3.c** Does Gamma have a brand kit or logo rules (logo files, where the Gamma logo may appear)? → Options: (1) you send it; (2) none, use the app's own UI only. My pick: (2) until you say.
