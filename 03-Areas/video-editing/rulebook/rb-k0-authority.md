# K0 · Order of authority

> **Read when:** any rule seems to clash with another, a new correction or marker comes in, or a creative director note arrives. Every agent, every job. **Client values:** none (the overlays are [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k2-alex-frontal|K2 Alex / Frontal]] and [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k3-gamma|K3 Gamma]]). **Status:** draft, not yet approved by Samuel.

## Rules
### The order
- **K0.1** This rulebook is the number-one rule for every Route Rise edit, for Samuel and for every agent, unless Samuel says otherwise. ✅ *"This rule book would be your number one rule, unless I tell you not to"* (2026-11-03)
- **K0.2** Only Samuel's own corrections and markers rank above it. Full order, top wins: ✅ *"The only overruling of everything is my corrections and markers"* (2026-10-22)
  1. Samuel's live corrections and markers on the job in hand (chat, timeline markers, his own edits on his timeline).
  2. This rulebook. It already holds his past corrections, the creative director standard and the house notes, ranked in that order.
  3. The client overlay (K2, K3 …) for fonts, colours, corner shape, logos and brand rules.
  4. Older Brain notes and the client's brand kit, only where the rulebook says nothing.
- **K0.3** Inside the rulebook the ranking Samuel set still decides any new tie: his corrections and markers, then the creative director standard, then older house notes, then the client's brand kit (subtle, but present throughout). 🔁 (4 sources) ✅ *"The Creative directors standard stays top, older house notes second, client brand kit third"* (2026-10-22)
- **K0.4** The brand kit sits last, but his own brand rulings count as his corrections and rank first. For Alex: fonts, colours and square boxes come from the kit because he ruled them; where the kit and the standard disagree on anything else (glow, red keywords, motion, density), the standard wins. ✅ (2026-10-22)
- **K0.5** Within his own words the newest wins. A later brief beats the review card where they differ (the video 4 brief of 2026-10-28 beat the card). ✅ (2026-10-25, 2026-10-28)
- **K0.6** If two of his rulings conflict and the later one does not clearly replace the earlier, do not choose. Write it as a question for him in the section and ask. ⚙️

### What counts as his correction
- **K0.7** A timeline marker beats any check or lint rule: if his marker asks for something a check flags, the marker wins and the check is marked accepted. ⚙️ (video 4, 2026-10-29)
- **K0.8** His own corrected timeline is the truth to learn from: what he changed by hand is a correction even if he never says it. Diff his version against ours at the end of every job. ✅ *"this timeline is the final cut take"* (2026-09-25)
- **K0.9** The client's script decides what must stay in the cut; Samuel's cut decides how it is cut. ✅ (2026-09-25)
- **K0.10** A settlement Samuel makes with the client beats the client's own note on the script. ✅ *"Don't worry about what he said about not showing logos... I have settled it with him."* (2026-10-07)
- **K0.11** Creative director notes that Samuel passes on are his corrections: apply them on that video, then fold them into the rulebook. 🎯 *"Did some corrections on the video, from my creative director"* (2026-10-16)
- **K0.12** Only the Route Rise creative director counts for these videos. Ifeoma is his personal-brand creative director and never touches company edits. ✅ *"Ifeoma is for my personal brand, she doesn't touch my company edits"* (2026-10-28)

### When the rulebook is silent
- **K0.13** Draft sections apply now. Where a draft section says nothing, the older notes under its *Sources* still apply. Approval locks the wording. ⚙️ (index, 2026-11-03)
- **K0.14** Not covered anywhere: check the client's own reference edit (his last finished video for that client) first, then the client kit's own tie-breakers (for Alex, Frontal's five principles in [[03-Areas/video-editing/clients/frontal-brand-v2|Frontal brand v2.3.1]] §1). If it still isn't clear, ask Samuel. Never invent a style. 💡
- **K0.15** When two sections seem to disagree, the more specific one wins: the client overlay for brand values, the beat-type section (C6, C8, C10 …) over a general one. Flag the clash so the general rule gets fixed. 💡

### Folding in a new correction
- **K0.16** Every new correction from Samuel goes into the rulebook **in the same session** it is given. Never "later". ✅ (2026-11-03)
- **K0.17** Find the rule it touches by number. Rewrite that rule in place, keep its number, add his words (20 words max) and the date. Repeated corrections get 🔁 with the count. ⚙️
- **K0.18** A reversal: the old rule leaves the rulebook for [[08-Archive/brain-build/rulebook-removed-rules|removed rules]] (word for word, dated) and the new one takes the next free number; numbers are never reused. ✅ *"The replaced rules should be replaced and removed from the rule book"* (2026-11-03)
- **K0.19** A new rule goes at the next free number under the right subheading. If it fits two sections, put it in the section the step that would break it reads, and link it from the other. ⚙️
- **K0.20** Brand values (font, hex, corner shape, logos) go only in that client's overlay. Every other section stays client-neutral and points to the overlay. ⚙️
- **K0.21** A fix for one beat only stays in the job note. It becomes a rule when he says "always", "never" or "every", when he gives it a second time, or when it would apply to the next video. If unsure, ask. 💡
- **K0.22** Add one line to [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rulebook-changelog|the changelog]] for every change: date · rule number · what changed · his words. Then commit. ⚙️ (2026-11-03)
- **K0.23** A change Samuel dictated needs no new approval. A change an agent infers carries 💡 and waits for his yes. ⚙️

## Never
- **K0.24** Never let a client's brand kit, an older Brain note or an agent's own taste override his correction. ❌ ✅ (2026-10-22)
- **K0.25** Never take creative direction for a Route Rise edit from anyone but Samuel and the Route Rise creative director. ❌ ✅ (2026-10-28)
- **K0.26** Never edit a rule without a changelog line. Never delete a rule. ❌ ⚙️

## Questions for Samuel
- **Q-K0.a** A creative director note read straight off Frame.io clashes with your earlier marker. On video 2 your rev 3 marker said *"dark shade not needful"* (2026-10-24); the CD's notes (2026-10-26) asked for lighting and darker text over footage. The Editor followed the CD and flagged it. Your order puts your markers above the CD. → Options: (1) your marker wins until you say otherwise; (2) the newer CD note wins on that video and you decide if it becomes a rule. My pick: (1) for notes you didn't pass on, flagged to you the same hour; CD notes you pass on count as yours (K0.11).
- **Q-K0.b** Do draft sections bind before you approve them? Your words (2026-11-03) set no condition. → Options: (1) drafts bind now, approval only locks wording (K0.13); (2) drafts are advisory until approved. My pick: (1).
- **Q-K0.c** The agent profiles (Editor, editor-builder rule 7) still state the 2026-10-22 order with no mention of the rulebook. → Options: (1) point them to K0 once you approve this section; (2) leave them. My pick: (1).
