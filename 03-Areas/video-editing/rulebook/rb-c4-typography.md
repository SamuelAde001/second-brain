# C4 · Typography and numbers

> **Read when:** planning or building any beat with text or a number on screen, and when reviewing stills for size, placement and wording. **Client values:** fonts, weights, case, accent colour in [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k2-alex-frontal|K2 Alex / Frontal]] and [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-k3-gamma|K3 Gamma]]. **Status:** draft, not yet approved by Samuel.

## Rules

### How much text (his most repeated correction here)
- **C4.1** Show, don't tell. Text only on important statements; everything else is shown. 🔁 5 (2026-09-25, 2026-10-03, 2026-10-13, 2026-10-25, 2026-11-01) ✅ *"I need things to be showing more than they are telling"* (2026-09-25); ✅ *"TOO MUCH TEXT ON SCREEN"* (2026-10-25)
- **C4.2** Every text means something: short nouns, never long lines, never paragraphs, never random helper text or invented sub-lines. ✅ *"Every text must actually be something meaningful. I think we settled with something like nouns."* (2026-10-13)
- **C4.3** Mobile first: one or two things on screen, fewer words, bigger. 🔁 ✅ (2026-10-22) 🎯
- **C4.4** Remove text that isn't a chapter or isn't needed, but never drop a word arbitrarily from a line you keep. ✅ *"Remove this text, it's not a chapter"*; ✅ *"Why did Stuck text disappoear"* (2026-10-25)
- **C4.5** When he asks for a phrase card, the phrase alone. ✅ *"Just put the text common context alone"* (2026-10-02)
- **C4.6** Captions over B-roll are the words animating in, nothing else: no pill, no chips. 🔁 ✅ (2026-10-10)
- **C4.7** No running subtitles. 🎬

### Wording
- **C4.8** On-screen text matches his spoken words (~97 %); change a word or two only when it reads better. When the script and his words differ, follow his words. 🎯 (2026-10-22)
- **C4.9** One accent word per line, the word that carries the meaning, in the accent colour (client overlay). 🎯 🎬
- **C4.10** Only the negative words turn red; the rest stays normal. ✅ *"Fewer than half should be wtitten in red, while the rest is normal"* (2026-10-25)
- **C4.11** A title on top of every list, chart, diagram and group. ✅ *"Put the text "The 6 posts" on the top"* (2026-10-14) 🎯
- **C4.12** A quote gets quote marks, the person's picture and the attribution. 🎯 (2026-10-22)
- **C4.13** No brackets, no bracketed eyebrows, no dots between words, no comma clutter, no filler. 🔁 2 ✅ *"I don't like the bracktes you are putting on text, I would never do that"* (2026-10-25)

### Style
- **C4.14** A new brand changes the fonts only, never his way of styling type. ✅ *"does not mean that we are changing any other thing about the style of the way we do typography"* (2026-10-22)
- **C4.15** Text is white and bold. 🔁 2 ✅ *"use white please on text and make it bold"* (2026-10-25) Weights: client overlay.
- **C4.16** Vary the text treatment across a video (gradient, a second style for a number or year, italics now and then, text in a box where a box is allowed); never basic subtitle text. 🎯 (2026-10-22)
- **C4.17** Text keeps his NeoTextMotion shines. ✅ (2026-10-22) Detail: C7.
- **C4.18** ALL CAPS for titles, chips and pills; sentence case for longer lines, on every client (Q-C4.a, 2026-11-05). ✅ · ✏ 2026-11-05

### Size
- **C4.19** Big enough to read on a phone, key words bigger; but never so big it crowds the frame. 🔁 ✅ *"Make the texts always bigger expecially winners and stucl"* (2026-10-25); ✅ *"the text is too big"* (2026-10-24)
- **C4.20** Floors (lint C7): 56 px over footage (64+ better), 40 px on a ground or page, 36 px in a UI rebuild. Never under 20 px anywhere. 🎯 ⚙️ (2026-10-26) 🎬
- **C4.21** Statements over darkened footage: 90-130 px, centred. ⚙️ (2026-10-28)
- **C4.22** No small text over footage: small mono caps labels are unreadable on a phone. 🎯 *"text is not readable"* (2026-10-26)
- **C4.23** No small, short text on a full-screen colour background: a full-frame colour moment carries type big enough to own the frame, or isn't used. 🔁 ✅ *"I don't like small short text on a full screen background"* (2026-10-21)
- **C4.24** Check size on a rendered frame, not by formula alone; the Fusion size formulas disagree between fonts and measures. ⚙️ Open [[03-Areas/video-editing/resolve-automation-lessons|Resolve automation lessons]] for the px-to-Size maths.

### Placement
- **C4.25** Text centred, vertically too; nothing touching or leaving the edge of the frame. 🔁 4 ✅ *"the $100M is touching the edge"* (2026-10-24)
- **C4.26** Text well aligned in every design; centre the equals sign; put labels where the eye already is. 🔁 ✅ *"so the viewer doesn't move his eyes too much"* (2026-10-15)
- **C4.27** Over his A-roll, text sits low with the dark fade down low. 🔁 2 ✅ *"Put this text on screen, lower like I normally do"* (2026-10-15); ✅ *"The dark fade is too high, make it lower"* (2026-10-25)
- **C4.28** Over darkened footage or a page: its own text-over beat, neutral darken, big centred white text, one accent word, no box, pill or label strip behind. ✅ (2026-10-14) ⚙️
- **C4.29** Text that goes with a screen recording or web capture sits centred and big in its own visual, never at the top of the capture. ✅ *"never put text on a screen record up, I don't do that"* (2026-10-21)
- **C4.30** Lower thirds: centred, one line, big, the method on top, in the bottom band (lint y ≥ 900), never to the side, never on his chest. 🔁 3 ✅ *"All lower thirds, must be centered, one line and big with the method on top"* (2026-10-25); 🎯 *"why is it so high up"* (2026-10-26)
- **C4.31** A CTA ("link in the description") is NeoTextMotion over the A-roll with a dark gradient at the bottom, text only, placed low. ✅ *"Bring it lower a bit"* (2026-09-27)
- **C4.32** Dynamic typography can sit by the speaker's side (e.g. a counter plus a short label). ✅ *"typography by Alex side"* (2026-10-14)
- **C4.33** Text lands first, then each supporting visual. 🎯 (2026-10-22)

### Numbers
- **C4.34** Every main number he says counts up (his Number Counter, Text+ named "Count"), landing 0-3 f after its word. 🔁 ~19 ✅ *"Numbers must count in, any main number mentioned must count in"* (2026-10-25)
- **C4.35** Exception: a number inside a sentence or title is plain text rising with the other words. Lint P2: a number said in the beat and shown alone at 72 px+ is a counter; never a counter on one line with same-size words; skip axis ticks, 0 and ordinals 1-9. ✅ 🎯 *"why was the 1m a money counter just do normal text"* (2026-10-26, he agreed the same day)
- **C4.36** A hero number stands big and bold with a short label under it; no hedge word like "almost". ✅ *"Remove the word almost and just have the number, Big and bol"* (2026-10-14)
- **C4.37** Money is never a bare counter: give it a box, a subline or the logo. 🎯
- **C4.38** Every number on screen is the number he says: listen, don't trust the transcript; if a website differs, show what he says. 🎯 *"he said 700??"* (2026-10-26)
- **C4.39** Numbers written 1, 2, never 01, 02. Chapter numbers white so they don't blend into the box. 🎯 (2026-10-22)
- **C4.40** No source or citation line on a stat card. 🎬 ✅ (2026-10-02, 2026-10-13)
- **C4.41** Counters: force the thousands comma, right-justify so the number grows leftwards. ⚙️ 🎬 How-to: [[03-Areas/video-editing/tsb-graphics-pipeline|TSB graphics pipeline]].

## Never
- **C4.42** Text over his face. If at all, put it under. 🔁 2 ✅ *"NEVER EVER PUT TEXT OVER HIS FACE, if at all put it under"* (2026-10-15) ❌
- **C4.43** Anything, text included, directly behind his back. ✅ *"don't put anything directly behind his back it's bad"* (2026-10-21) ❌
- **C4.44** Text pushed to the top of a frame where it doesn't belong. ✅ *"p3 o5 text up is bad there"* (2026-10-21) ❌
- **C4.45** A red strike through text (C2.44). ✅ ❌

## Questions for Samuel
- **Q-C4.a** Case. Your orange-era finals used ALL CAPS titles and chips; the Frontal kit says sentence case; the creative director uses all caps; your Gamma reference is short ALL-CAPS lines. → Options: (1) ALL CAPS for titles, chips, pills; sentence case for longer lines (2) per client overlay only. My pick: (2), with (1) as the default for a new client. → Samuel: (1), ALL CAPS for titles, chips, pills; sentence case for longer lines (2026-11-05). C4.18 updated.
- **Q-C4.b** Glow on big titles. Apollo: no glow ever on text; TSB: giant names and titles carry a soft glow (0.04-0.12). → My pick: no glow on titles, except a giant name behind the speaker. → Samuel: yes, no glow on titles except a giant name behind the speaker (2026-11-05).
- **Q-C4.c** Lower-third height: the lint says y ≥ 900, video 2 notes say y ≥ ~950. → My pick: 900 as the floor, lower is fine. → Samuel: yes, 900 as the floor, lower is fine (2026-11-05).
