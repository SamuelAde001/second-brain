# D1 · Music

> **Read when:** the sound pass of any Route Rise job (choosing songs, levels, song changes), and when a reviewer checks the mix. Sound effects are in [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-d2-sound-effects|D2]]. **Client values:** a client may ask for no music under the voice (K2 Alex overlay, [[03-Areas/video-editing/clients/alex|Alex]]). **Status:** approved by Samuel, 2026-11-05 (issues answered one by one, the rest approved in one go).

Two creative directors appear below. The **Route Rise CD** gives Frame.io notes on our videos; Samuel counts those as his own corrections (2026-10-15). The **CD standard** is Ifeoma's breakdowns of other editors' videos ([[03-Areas/video-editing/creative-director/creative-director-standard|creative director standard]]); it ranks below Samuel and the Route Rise CD.

## Rules
### When the sound pass happens
- **D1.1** Sound (music and SFX) comes last, after Samuel has approved the visuals. 🔁 ×4 ✅ *"sound design is always going to be the last that we'll work on"* (2026-10-14) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.2** Before the sound pass, duplicate the timeline as a backup. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.3** Finished means render-ready: after his last watch he should only have to render. ✅ *"once I'm watching the video, I will just need to render it after the music."* (2026-10-16) See Q-D1.a. · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Which songs
- **D1.4** Use his music from his library, the songs he used in the last video, in the same order, one song per major segment, at his in-points. 🔁 ×3 ✅ *"use the music I used in the last video ... in the sequence. I used them for each major segment"* (2026-10-16) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.5** Never use the same song twice in one video. A hook song may come back in a later video. 🔁 ×3 ✅ *"never repeat music"* (2026-09-28) 🏢 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.6** Music only where it carries a feeling the video needs. Never plaster a song on for no reason. ✅ *"someone else can just look at and plaster sad music for no reason"* (2026-10-19) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.7** High-energy song in the hook and intro, calmer songs in the body. 🎯 (CD standard, 2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.8** A long section can take a second song (his B2B practice). Never jump back to an earlier song. 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Where the song changes
- **D1.9** Change the song at section boundaries: after the hook, after the intro, at each chapter and for the outro. 🎬 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.10** Change only in a speech gap, on an A-roll cut. Never change while he is talking. 🎯 (CD standard, 2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.11** Fades: 6 f in, 12 f out at a boundary; 48 f out at the end of the video. ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.12** At each section reveal the new song is already at level. No hit over near-silence. 🎯 *"something felt too dramatic here maybe sfx volume or BG song not loud compared"* (Route Rise CD, 2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.13** Songs run about 1-2 minutes each. 🎯 See Q-D1.c. · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Levels
- **D1.14** Music goes on its own track (A2 Music) so it can be muted in one click. 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.15** Match the volume he used in past videos. One level per song, set once on the clip, never keyframed per clip. His range is −25.1 to −41.9 dB, usually −30 to −34 dB, outro −41.9 dB (e.g. Bahamas −32.1, A Pinpoint of Light −30.2, Road to Nowhere −32.8). ✅ *"the volume should be similar to the volume I used in past videos"* (2026-09-28) 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.16** Music never competes with the voice. If in doubt, take it down 3-5 %. 🎯 (CD standard, 2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.17** No breakdown or drop inside the part of a song you use. Scan the used span. If it dips more than about 4 dB under speech, move the in-point. 🎯 *"what happeend to BG song"* (Route Rise CD, 2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.18** No SFX louder than the song (see [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-d2-sound-effects|D2]]). 🎯 (Route Rise CD, 2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05

### Where music stops
- **D1.19** No music under a screen-recording demo (Tella + PIP stretch, long screen recordings). Cut it or disable it; bring it back when the demo ends. 🎬 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.20** Music stays under rebuilt full-screen UI visuals (those are graphics, not demos). 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05

### The voice
- **D1.21** A-roll lav at +6.0 dB on every clip, set once, never automated. 🎬 (7M reference final) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.22** Voice Isolation on A1 only, at Samuel's own value for that video (44 on video 2, 31 on video 4). Keep his value; don't change it. 🎬 *"lot of audio issues w alex audio"* (Route Rise CD, 2026-10-26) See Q-D1.d. · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.23** Loudness of the final mix: see [[03-Areas/video-editing/rulebook/rb-d2-sound-effects|D2]] → Mix and loudness (target not confirmed). · ✔ 2026-11-05

## Never (strict)
- **D1.24** Never the same song twice in one video. ❌ ✅ (2026-09-28) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.25** Never a song change while he speaks. ❌ 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.26** Never a song breakdown or near-silent stretch under the voice. ❌ 🎯 (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **D1.27** Never music under a screen-recording demo. ❌ 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-05

## Questions for Samuel
- **Q-D1.a** When does sound go in? You ruled four times (2026-10-07 to 2026-10-15) that sound comes only after you approve the visuals. On 2026-10-24 you said *"go ahead and add music and sound effects ... and render it"*, and on video 4 (2026-10-28) the Editor put sound in before your morning review so you watch the finished video once. → Options: (1) always after your visual sign-off; (2) in before your review so you review once, muted if you want to judge visuals alone. My pick: (2) for overnight runs, since you review once and only render after; (1) when you are reviewing visuals live. → Samuel: (1), always after his visual sign-off, *"so that we don't change sounds after visual changes"* (2026-11-05) D1.1 stands.
- **Q-D1.b** Alex asked for "no music under the voice" (2026-10-06). Every later video shipped with music under the voice. → Options: (1) that note is dead, music as above; (2) it still holds for Alex, mute A2 under speech. My pick: (1), keeping music on A2 so it mutes in one click. → Samuel: (1), that note is dead; music stays on its own track (K2.25) (2026-11-05).
- **Q-D1.c** Song length: Route Rise's notes say never a song over 90 s (2026-09-25); the CD standard says about 1-2 minutes a song (2026-10-22). → Options: (1) 90 s cap; (2) 1-2 min, change at section boundaries only. My pick: (2), because changes must land on section boundaries. → Samuel: (2), 1-2 min a song, changing at section boundaries only (2026-11-05).
- **Q-D1.d** Voice clean-up: the CD standard says Adobe Podcast (Enhance Speech) on every video (2026-10-22); you fixed video 2 and video 4 with Resolve's Voice Isolation on A1. → Options: (1) Voice Isolation at your value only; (2) Adobe Podcast first, then your value. My pick: (1), it is what you did yourself. → Samuel: (1), Voice Isolation at his value only (2026-11-05).
