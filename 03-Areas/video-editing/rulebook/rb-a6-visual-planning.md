# A6 · Visual ideation and planning

> **Read when:** after Samuel approves the cut and before any build: studying the reference, writing the per-sentence plan, hero frames for a new look, the preview gate, turning his approvals into the build list. Editor and the Opus planner (editor-builder). **Client values:** which reference video is the recipe and which final is the bar (client overlays K2/K3: [[03-Areas/video-editing/clients/alex|Alex]], [[03-Areas/video-editing/clients/gamma|Gamma]]). **Status:** draft, not yet approved by Samuel.

## Rules
### Study before planning
- **A6.1** Understand the whole video first: what it means and what helps the viewer digest it. Plan for the viewer, not for Samuel. Every visual has a one-sentence reason. ✅ 🔁×2 *"the viewer is the main person to understand"* (2026-10-13; 2026-10-22) 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.2** Where this rulebook has a fixed method for the client or the construct, it is the guide. Study the client's reference edit in Resolve only where the rulebook is silent or you are not sure: census every track (what sits over each screen recording, over the presenter, how many, how long), then its comps, nodes, colours, animations and UI visuals. ✅ *"I said study the timeline in resolve, the comps the nodes the colors, the animations the screen UI visuals"* (2026-11-01) · *"if we have a fixed method in this book, use it as guide and still go back to study past videos if not sure"* (2026-11-04) · ✏ 2026-11-04
- **A6.3** That reference is the 1:1 recipe for this client. Another client's finals are side references only. Never build one client's look on another client's kit. ✅ 🔁×2 *"That video shows a one to one representation of exactly how I edit gamma videos"* (2026-11-01); *"you are using the same design of Alex for Gamma"* (2026-10-31). See Q-A6.b. · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.4** Before building variants, render the client's own reference comps beside your proofs. ✅ (2026-11-01) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.5** Plan from this rulebook first: it is the standard guide, so old videos are not studied over again. Then the comp libraries (his templates, the client's template library, the last video's comps): open a comp or still only to copy the construct you are building. What a study teaches goes into the rulebook, so it is never studied twice. ✅ *"without it learning again from the old videos"* (2026-10-08) · *"I want us to have a standard guide that doesn't need us to study old videos, cause we can't be studying them over again that's what this guide book is meant to help"* (2026-11-04) · ✏ 2026-11-04
- **A6.6** Start from what shipped on the last video (contact-sheet its delivered render, reuse its components), never from random ideas. ✅ *"learn from what we finalised in the last video… don't do lazy work"* (2026-09-29) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.7** Use the previous video as the foundation, and make this one better. Work on copies; never change delivered files. The bar per client is in its overlay. ✅ 🔁×2 *"I wanted that every video will get better"* (2026-10-20; 2026-10-28) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.8** Read his markers first, on the timeline he wrote them on. Blue markers on the cut before visuals are his intro guides: check the hook plan against every one. ✅ *"those are part of things you should do"* (2026-10-08) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.9** Check facts against the footage before naming or offering options: how Alex really uses a tool (frames from his past Tellas), which tools a company really uses. ✅ 🔁×2 *"are you sure this are the apps. he uses in his prospecting system? crosscheck that"* (2026-10-01); *"Desktop app, as Alex uses it"* (2026-10-20) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.10** Learn the client's niche so the right proof comes quickly; add fitting examples he didn't say. 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-04

### The plan
- **A6.11** Fixed order: transcript → written plan of the best visual for every section → how each is built (nodes, adjustment clips on top) → gather every resource (B-roll, screen recordings, pictures, UI) in Chrome, with a source link for each → build. ✅ 🔁×2 *"Plan the visuals in a written form, right? Then plan how you're going to do it in nodes"* (2026-10-20; 2026-10-06) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.12** Plan the screen recordings first (what each demo needs), then the motion graphics. ✅ (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.13** A visual for every main sentence. One beat per main sentence; join very short ones; one visual may run 2-4 sentences, building on the words. Every sentence still appears on the plan, as a visual or as plain A-roll. ✅ 🔁×2 *"every main sentence, you will write a visual that makes sense with it"* (2026-09-25; 2026-09-29) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.14** Format: a table per section, one row per sentence: ID, frames, what Alex says, the visual, the build (track, kit, nodes), SFX, timed to word frames on the approved cut. Delivered as a page, never a chapter-level summary in chat. ✅ (corrected once, before 2026-09-19) ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.15** The plan must narrate and visualise what is said, on the right timings. Nothing placed "anyhow"; a visual that shows nothing useful comes out. ✅ 🔁×2 *"Make sure that it actually narrates and visualizes what is being said."* (2026-10-06); *"it isn't showing anythig useful"* (2026-10-08) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.16** Show, not tell. Tool talk shows the tool, really demoed (cursor clicks, dropdowns, interactions). A listing is listed. Every named company gets a website or logo beat (how: C8). Real UI, articles and B-roll beat abstract icon or text graphics. ✅ 🔁×4 *"Be properly demoed with cursor clicking on it, drop downs, interactions"* (2026-10-31; also 09-25, 10-07, 10-26) 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.17** Every screen recording looks exactly like a live demo of the real thing: not too zoomed in or out, the actual website, UI one-to-one identical. No text or motion graphics on screen recordings (treatment: C8). ✅ 🔁×2 *"the UI must be one-to-one identical"* (2026-10-26); *"Since when did we start putting motion graphics on screen recording visuals"* (2026-11-01) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.18** Where the speaker should be seen, put nothing on top. His own screen-recording stretches need no new visual. ✅ (2026-09-25) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.19** Density like his finals: about 85-89 % of the runtime covered; the video 4 plan aimed at hook 100 %, intro ~93 %, body ~80 %. Hook and intro denser than the body. Pacing detail: C15. 🎬 · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.20** Follow the style already used in the edited part of the video; visual notes in a client's cut guide are often unrealistic. ✅ (2026-09-18) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.21** Before designing any process, split or percentage graphic, find the construct his finals used for the same idea and re-skin it in the client's brand; cross-check the creative director breakdowns. A redesign starts from his own precedent. ✅ *"we have done something like this before, use that style just with the new branding"* (2026-10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.22** Libraries (motion gallery, template libraries) are a vocabulary, never templates: take parts and combine them for the sentence. Reuse a device for the same idea with slight changes, never a template with only the text swapped. Gallery sample copy is never fact. ✅ 🔁×2 *"this designs must not be exact like this on my edits, but this designs are impirations"* (2026-09-30; 2026-09-18) 🎯 · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.23** Be creative inside his grammar: no construct type, animation or text density he has never used. ✅ *"being creative doesn't mean you should do things I don't normally do in any of my videos"* (2026-11-01) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.24** Vary the designs: never the same device twice the same way in one video (use a different device or a deliberate echo), and never the intro visual again for a body beat. ✅ 🔁×2 *"Mak a completly different vsiual for this, not the same intro visual"* (2026-10-25); *"I don't want repeated boxes"* (2026-10-31) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.25** A long video gets recurring motifs that carry its story (a steps tracker, a tier board, a recurring person). A board that returns several times is built once as a reusable generator. 💡 ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.26** When Alex numbers methods, number each one on screen; chapter cards are one-size bottom pills, not the intro list again (detail: C12). ✅ *"please number ieach method if it is part of his 4 listings"* (2026-10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.27** Simplest rig that works; add complexity only when a real problem shows up. ✅ (before 2026-09-19) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.28** Every effect in a preview maps 1:1 to the Fusion node that makes it, named on the plan. Not sure which node? Ask; don't invent a workaround. ✅ (2026-09-25) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.29** Needs his yes before it is planned: any behind-Alex idea (MagicMask). Put your own calls (made-up names, zoom caps, layout) in a short list with the hero sheet. ✅ (2026-10-21) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.30** A visual runs to the next beat when the next starts within 8 frames, so A-roll never flashes for 1-3 frames (timing: C14). ⚙️ · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.31** If he limits a run (e.g. no motion graphics for a client whose style you don't know yet), the plates still need full demos and timing to the words. Static plates are a fault. ✅ *"I feel they didn't demo enough"* (2026-10-31) · ✔ 2026-11-04

### The intro
- **A6.32** The intro is the most important part: the strongest, most creative visuals, wall to wall, real things on screen, every element on its word. No generic visual. ✅ 🔁×3 *"this is the intro, it most be good"* (2026-10-25; also 09-20, 10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.33** The Editor builds the intro, from his suggested visuals (job note, `Docs\`, blue markers, Notion comments). If none have come, ideate it yourself and mark it as your own idea. Devices: C13. ✅ 🔁×2 *"Ideate yourself if I haven't sent the suggested visuals"* (2026-10-13); *"You will do the intro"* (2026-10-12) · ✔ 2026-11-04

### Quality bar and checks before he sees it
- **A6.34** Test every visual with his three questions: the best editors' kind of creative work? would the creative director like it? does it belong on a 10M-follower channel? ✅ 🔁×2 *"does it look like a video that belongs to a 10m followers channel?"* (2026-10-21; 2026-10-22) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.35** Revise the plan until it meets the bar before anything goes on his timeline. Cross-check it against everything he has said since the job started, the review card, the creative director standard and the videos the creative director approved. ✅ 🔁×3 *"so better revise your visual plan before you put it on my timeline"* (2026-10-21; 10-22, 10-24) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.36** Every correction he or the creative director gave, on any earlier video, is already in the plan. He never repeats himself. ✅ 🔁×4 *"Anything I corrected in the last video or in the last previous videos, I should not repeat myself."* (2026-10-13; 10-20, 10-21, 10-26) · ✔ 2026-11-04
- **A6.37** Aim for a first version he and the creative director approve with no corrections. Don't hand it over until you're sure; getting it right first beats speed. ✅ 🔁×5 *"until you are sure Samuel will love this and will be mind blown by it, don't give me yet"* (2026-10-06 to 10-15; 10-22, 10-28) · ✔ 2026-11-04

### Look lock, previews and approvals
- **A6.38** New look (new client, new brand, or a rejected look): render 3-4 hero frames and get his yes before a full plan or any build fans out. Judge them beside his last finals, not against written rules. Start from frames he liked. ✅ *"the visuals look cheap bro ... using their brand guide doesn't mean you should do very medicocre looking visuals"* (2026-10-21) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **A6.39** Settle the new look rules with him at job start and put them on the review card. The review card plus the job card is the brief; no separate "bible" stage. ⚙️ (2026-10-30) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **A6.40** Show him a picture before building: a still mock-up on a frame of his video, before any node structure or timeline placement. ✅ 🔁×2 *"Give me a picture of what you want to do before doing the node structure"* (2026-09-27) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **A6.41** Every review shows the actual visuals, never a text-only sheet. ✅ 🔁×2 *"you are meant to create the actual visuals and not just the text"* (2026-09-29) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **A6.42** Preview gate: one page per section; each motion-graphic beat (kinds `motion-graphic`, `overlay`, `aroll-fx` with a comp) as a short clip beside its line, Approve or Note. Only approved beats go on the timeline. Screen recordings, captures, UI demos and B-roll skip it. Command: [[03-Areas/video-editing/sops/video-pipeline-tools|video pipeline tools]] → `preview_page.py`. ✅ *"maybe if I can review the visuals before they get on the timeline, especially the motion graphic beats"* (2026-10-28) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **A6.43** Show the gate early: the anchors and the first section's motion beats before the other sections are built. Send sample renders early. ⚙️ (video 4 markers came after the 4K render) ✅ *"The sample renders you showed me looks good, just that you did too much of the animation"* (2026-10-31) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **A6.44** Put each note from the gate on the review card the same session and turn it into a fix spec. A rule he gives on one visual applies to every similar construct. ✅ *"some of the rules I have used applies for the rest with similar construct"* (2026-09-27) · ✔ 2026-11-05
- **A6.45** He may approve gradually as he goes through the edit, or ask for specific visuals. When his review covers only part of the video, build new visuals only where his marker covers (corrections still apply everywhere, E2). ✅ *"I only worked with the part my marker shows, so build the visuals there"* (2026-10-02)
- **A6.46** Before acting on his notes, list the extra fixes you planned so he can veto them. ✅ *"Can you list me the other fixes you planned to do?"* (2026-10-29)
- **A6.47** Ask short either/or questions with the default already chosen ("answer only where you disagree"). For a structurally unclear decision, ask one targeted question before rebuilding; otherwise go on sensible defaults and flag them. ✅ (before 2026-09-19) ⚙️ (2026-10-08)
- **A6.48** When a look is rejected, keep the plan's structure (beats, timing, captures) and re-plan only the compositions. ⚙️ (2026-10-21)
- **A6.49** Unattended runs he names (overnight, "all at once"): no approval stops. Build on defaults; save the hero stills, previews and every choice for his morning review; versioned timelines. Otherwise the gates above apply. ✅ 🔁×3 *"No storyboard gate tonight: build on your defaults and save the storyboard stills"* (2026-10-08; 10-13, 10-28). See Q-A6.a.
- **A6.50** Planner helpers hold ~15-20 beats each; the hook gets its own planner. After planning run `plan_check.py`, `boundary_check.py`, `make_build_list.py`, `make_plan_docs.py` ([[03-Areas/video-editing/sops/video-pipeline-tools|video pipeline tools]]). ⚙️

## Never
- **A6.57** Nothing on his timeline before the plan is revised and the hero frames have his yes. ❌ *"so better revise your visual plan before you put it on my timeline"* (2026-10-21)
- **A6.58** No styles he has never used: bouncing animations, random text popping on screen, made-up constructs. ❌ *"the visuals all look AI, with bouncing animations and too much text"* (2026-11-01)
- **A6.59** No row of equal labelled boxes with icons joined by arrows. ❌ *"don't let things look like it is AI"* (2026-10-26)
- **A6.60** No mediocre, random or swapped-text visuals. ❌ *"the visuals that you plan is always kind of very mediocre"* (2026-10-06)
- **A6.61** Never change a visual he liked and didn't mark. ❌ *"I liked the visual you used in the last version ... I didn't tell you to change it"* (2026-10-15)
- **A6.62** Never start from frames he rejected. ❌ *"I liked the brand guide examples you gave, but you made it worse"* (2026-10-21)

## Questions for Samuel
- **Q-A6.a** Gates vs unattended runs. Gates: plan revised and hero frames approved before any build (2026-10-21), preview page for motion beats (2026-10-28). No gates: "do it all at once and have reviews in versions" (2026-10-13), overnight runs with no approval stops (2026-10-28, same day as the preview gate). → Options: (1) gates by default; you lift them per run by saying "overnight" or "no gates"; (2) unattended by default, gates only when you ask. My pick: (1), and in an unattended run still send the hero frames before the build starts. → Samuel: (1), gates by default (2026-11-05); he lifts them per run.
- **Q-A6.b** Which reference wins for a client. On 2026-10-31 you said Gamma's style is the LinkedIn video's with Gamma colours and font. On 2026-11-01 you said both "the best refrence example is Alex linkedin video" and that the Gamma edit is "one to one" while LinkedIn is "just a side refrence". → Options: (1) the client's own past edit by you is the recipe; another client's final is a side reference for craft (screen-recording treatment, pills, cards); (2) the latest Alex final is the main bar for every client. My pick: (1).
- **Q-A6.c** Gap: a new client with no past edit by you. What is the reference? → Options: (1) ask you for a reference video and the style at job start (K4); (2) use the latest Alex final with the client's colours and font. My pick: (1).
