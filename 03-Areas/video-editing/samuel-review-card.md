# Samuel's review card: every correction he has given, one line each

Samuel, 2026-10-13: *"Anything I corrected in the last video or in the last previous videos, I should not repeat myself."* And 2026-10-24: *"I really need it to be good... and remember things that we've done in the past."*

**What this is.** The short form of his corrections, for every builder and reviewer on every video. A helper reads this card, the client's brand note and its own beat record, instead of the long notes. The long notes stay the source and hold the detail: [[03-Areas/video-editing/ways-of-working|ways of working]] (his corrections, dated), [[03-Areas/video-editing/creative-director/creative-director-standard|creative director standard]], [[03-Areas/video-editing/sops/fusion-build|Fusion build SOP]]. If this card and a long note disagree, the long note wins and this card gets fixed.

**Kept up to date the same day.** Every time Samuel gives markers or a correction, the Editor adds the new rule here (one line, dated) in the same session, as well as in ways of working. The end-of-job review checks nothing was missed. Compiled 2026-10-24 by the General Manager from ways of working (to 2026-10-23), the creative director standard and video 2's REVIEW CARD v3.

## Authority and the bar

- Order: **Samuel's corrections and markers** > creative director standard > older house notes > the client's brand kit (subtle but present). (2026-10-22)
- The three questions on every visual: is it the best video editors' kind of creative work? would his creative director like it? does it belong on a 10M-follower channel? "Cheap", "flat" and "like AI did it" are the failures. (2026-10-21, 2026-10-22)
- Keep the standard of the last finals, then do better. A brand never excuses a plain visual: *"use the style and work on it."* (2026-10-21)
- A fix round touches only what he marked, plus the same correction everywhere else it applies. Don't change what he didn't mark. (2026-10-10, 2026-10-15)

## His pattern: never static, never "AI-edited" (2026-10-25, 73 markers on video 2; overrules anything below)

Samuel, 2026-10-25: *"Nothing just comes on screen randomly. Everything is animated individually and well-timed. But this one, everything is just very static and it feels very AI generated."* Check every beat against these ten before anything else.
- **P1** Every element animates in on its own, one at a time, on the word that names it (readable 0-3 f after the word, never before it). Lists, logos and companies come in one by one, fast. Nothing lands all at once; no element sits on screen empty waiting for its content.
- **P2** Every main number Alex says counts up (his Number Counter, Text+ named "Count"), landing on its word. **Except a number inside a sentence or title: plain text, rising in like the other words** (CD on video 2 v6, *"why was the 1m a money counter just do normal text"*; Samuel, 2026-10-26: numbers inside a sentence plain, hero numbers on their own still count).
- **P3** Main text goes word by word with NeoTextMotion, each word rising from below (PosDir 0 = Up; 3 = Left is the frfx.ntext default and reads as "slides from the left"). Separate word pieces need a real space between them.
- **P4** No boxes he doesn't need: plain text labels, no "matched by" or initial boxes, no box behind text over Alex.
- **P5** Never a red strike or cancel line: what failed turns red, what is stuck turns grey/dim.
- **P6** Never a box animated over a box: change the existing box's colour and swap its logo.
- **P7** Blur only what must stay hidden (the intro list items revealed later); never blur made-up names or companies.
- **P8** A chart dot rides its line at every frame; percentages are animated pies with the number counting, growth is a bar chart.
- **P9** Pages zoom to what is being highlighted (not so close the text crowds the edge); small stats are pills at the bottom, not full screen.
- **P10** Every page, screen recording and B-roll gets his media adjustment clip (slide 0.021 + blur in). Chapter returns are bottom pills, not the intro list again.

## Route Rise's creative director on video 2 v6 (2026-10-26, 21 Frame.io notes; Samuel: apply everywhere it fits, and on the next videos)

- **C1 Lighting tricks on every page and UI:** the eye goes to one spot because everything else is darker and that spot is lit. A flat white page or dashboard with a pill on it is "lacking lighting tricks". (#2, #11, #16)
- **C2 Darker behind all text** over footage and pages, the whole video. (#10)
- **C3 Time to read:** a card's last element is on screen fully landed for at least ~1.5 s before the cut. (#3 "never did was too fast")
- **C4 Not too much at once:** a frame the viewer can't take in before it changes is wrong; slow the beat down, fewer elements, longer hold. (#11, #12)
- **C5 The thing being read sits in the centre** of the frame, not at the top. (#2)
- **C6 Lower thirds sit low** (bottom band), still centred and big, never on his chest. (#7)
- **C7 No small text over footage:** small mono caps labels are unreadable on a phone. (#19, #21)
- **C8 Transitions gentle:** no fast fly-off stacked on a jump cut. (#8)
- **C9 Every on-screen number is the number Alex says**, checked against the audio, not the transcript alone (he said 700, the screen said 1,700). On-screen wording follows the script when the transcript is unsure. (#15, #17)
- **C10 Facts on charts hold up:** a label spans exactly what it names (the "7 years" bracket covers the whole 7 years). (#1)

## Show, don't tell

- Triple attack: he says it, the text says it, a visual shows it. Every visual has a one-sentence reason. (CD standard)
- Actions are shown being done (real UI, a demo built from scratch, real B-roll). Pills carry nouns and lists only. (2026-10-02)
- A number gets the real UI or source under it. Real proof first: the creator's footage, real UI, real pages, real posts; AI B-roll only when nothing real exists, matched to the line, no watermark. (2026-10-13, 2026-10-15, CD standard)
- Metaphors are shown literally: a stylised, cartoonish Flow B-roll (Omni Flash model). (2026-10-21, 2026-10-03)
- Calls are shown as calls: a video-call screen with Alex's real podcast footage and guests, "name - position", name blurred. (2026-10-10, 2026-10-15)
- No helper text he didn't ask for, no invented sub-lines, no source line on a stat card. (2026-10-02, 2026-09-26, 2026-10-13)

## Look: premium, never flat

- **No shines by default** (2026-10-24, overrules 2026-10-22): no moving shine or light sweep on boxes, pages or screen recordings. At most one, on one key box, only that box, on its turn, travelling fully off it. Boxes keep glass where it fits, solid icons or real logos; glow only where it means something. Bevel off. (2026-10-22, 2026-10-23, 2026-10-24)
- Effects are real nodes: NeoGlow, DropShadow, NeoLightSweep, ResolveFX Gaussian Blur. Never a faked glow or shadow box. (2026-09-25, 2026-10-15)
- Vary fills; neighbouring visuals never share one. No single box style carries a video. Gallery designs are inspiration, never templates. (2026-09-30, 2026-10-15)
- Boxes are opaque or properly blurred glass; nothing behind reads as a second layer. Shapes in layers, sizes and shades. (CD standard)
- Real logos in their real colours, never grey, never a screenshot thumbnail. Icons solid, never emoji-style. (2026-10-10, CD standard)
- Colour carries judgement, used sparingly: green for money up and advice to follow, red for a "don't" and a stall, grey for dead or the competitor; everything else in the client's shades. (2026-10-22, 2026-10-23)
- Dark base, bright elements on top. Never bright on bright. Darken neutrally with Brightness Contrast, moderately; never a brown or orange backdrop. (CD standard, 2026-10-15)

## Text

- **All text in Inter** (bold for headlines); Season Serif is out for Alex. Not too big, centred (vertically too), never off screen or touching an edge. Typing is character by character. (2026-10-24)
- **No boxes everywhere, especially on screen:** text over a screen recording or page has no box. (2026-10-24)

- White, bold, big for a phone; mobile first, fewer words, one or two things on screen. (2026-10-22)
- Text lands on the words he says (97 % match). No brackets, no dot separators, no filler, numbers as 1, 2 (never 01). One accent word. (2026-10-22, CD standard)
- No small, short text on a full-screen background. (2026-10-21)
- Over darkened footage: big, centred, no pill behind. Over Alex: low, with the Down fade. **Never over his face.** (2026-10-14, 2026-10-15)
- Lower thirds centred, one line, big. Never to the side. (2026-10-22)
- A title on top of every list, chart and diagram. (CD standard)
- In any list he walks through, each item's text is MosaicBlurred (PixelFrequency 200) until its word. Tease answers and chapters with blur. (2026-10-15, 2026-10-16, CD standard)

## Placement

- **One thing on screen at a time** (2026-10-24). No dark shade over his screen visuals unless it steers the eye to one spot. Desaturate (not red) for "not working"; dim the others rather than a green glow. A company is introduced by its logo and name, no website behind it. (2026-10-24)

- **Nothing directly behind Alex's back.** Any behind-Alex idea needs his yes first. (2026-10-21)
- **No text at the top of a screen recording or web capture.** Text that goes with a capture is centred, big, in its own visual. (2026-10-21)
- One focal point; the eye never chases. Labels near the centre, never far corners. Margins kept. Twin cards share one layout. (CD standard, 2026-10-13)

## UI, pages and screen recordings

- Full screen, viewport only: no browser bars, tabs, Windows UI, wallpaper, tilt or floating window. (2026-09-27, 2026-10-13)
- Real products get their real UI (his Chrome, then vendor screenshots); HTML rebuilds only for what doesn't exist, accurate "down to the last dot". Never copy Alex's own visuals or Tella unless Samuel names them. (2026-09-27, 2026-10-02, 2026-10-13)
- Three points of focus: the page reads as what it is, then the area brightens, then the exact words get highlighted, **all at once, never word by word** (2026-10-24). **Zooms gradual (2026-10-24): open just a little wider than the line (never the whole page, never right on the sentence), then ease in slowly across the beat; never deep, never fast, never jittery; not every page needs one.** On his own screen recordings: highlight only, no zoom. (2026-09-27, 2026-10-03, 2026-10-15, 2026-10-22, 2026-10-24)
- No motion graphics or text on UI visuals. Never blur what is being said or highlighted. Hide a person with mosaic, not soft blur. (2026-09-27, 2026-10-13)
- His adjustment clip (animation in + vignette) on every screen recording, page and B-roll. Focus, zoom and darkening go on an adjustment clip above, never in the clip's own comp. (2026-09-27, 2026-10-22)

## Charts and data

- Real charts: money on the right axis with numbers, years along the bottom, labelled lines drawn on cue, red where it stalls, a title on top. (2026-10-22)
- Money is never a bare counter: a box, a subline, the logo. Every readable number is Alex's. (CD standard, REVIEW CARD v3)

## Motion and timing

- **Slow and smooth beats fast** (2026-10-24): nothing that makes the viewer chase; line charts grow at one constant rate while he speaks; one thing moves at a time; elements animate or fade out. Comps light enough to play back smoothly on his PC.

- Every element in and out with NeoAnim (slide + blur + fade, Expo ease). Smooth Bezier keys. Entrances 16-22 f with a long ease-out, exits 10-14 f, transitions 14 f or more. Never a pop, overshoot, bounce or jitter; never a Transform where NeoAnim does it. (2026-10-03, 2026-10-15)
- Things land on their word (±3 f), never before it. A visual starts on the word that names it. One element per word, staggered. (2026-10-03, 2026-10-22)
- Never static: a slow push (~6-12 % over 2-4 s) on A-roll, B-roll, pages and graphics. No fast zooms. (2026-10-13, 2026-10-14)
- Hard cut from full-frame visuals into A-roll; no fade-in on inserts. (2026-10-10)
- Logos enter gently (NeoAnim ~25 f, short offset, from opacity 0). (2026-10-10)
- Visual frequency: every 1 s in the hook, 2 s in the intro, 5-10 s in the body, 15 s at most. Many A-roll sentences may breathe with no visual. (CD standard, 2026-09-27)
- The hook gets full-screen, concrete visuals. The intro is the most important part: well paced, smooth, premium. (2026-10-10, 2026-10-14)
- A recurring device keeps one design every time it returns; a list reveal reuses the last visual and reveals the next item. Step and chapter pills are one size. (2026-10-10)

## Fusion builds (he edits them later)

- Visuals are always editable Fusion nodes; rendered video only for real-UI demos. (2026-09-27)
- Node names carry their type (`Transform_D01`); never rename his nodes. (house rule)
- Never position with a Merge (Center 0.5/0.5, Size 1, Angle 0); position on a Transform. Opacity through his Opacity macro, never Merge Blend. (2026-09-27, 2026-09-26)
- Node tree: one main line left to right; each group over its own clear canvas with a collector column, joining the main line once; compact, so he never scrolls to find a group; no stray nodes. (2026-10-02, 2026-10-14)
- Zooms and punch-ins are adjustment clips (MagicZoom), never a Fusion comp. Use adjustment clips a lot. (2026-10-14)
- A device that returns is kept light (a heavy one crashed his cache). (2026-10-10)

## Sound (last, after he approves the visuals)

- Music: a new song per major segment and chapter, changed in a gap, never the same song twice in a video; the last video's music in its order. (2026-10-16, CD standard)
- ~~Every animated item gets its own low, varied hit; vary his beeps~~ (2026-10-16, **superseded 2026-10-26**).
- **SFX = the first videos' sound design (2026-10-26, overrules 2026-10-16).** Samuel: *"the sound effects I used initially in the first two or three videos that we have worked on are the ones that are best. I don't use the sound effect of the last video."* The creative director on video 2 v6: *"didnt like the techno style SFXs ... too many in a row"*, *"random sfx?"*, *"can u remove that clack sound"*, *"too dramatic ... sfx volume or BG song not loud compared"*. Model = his delivered #3 (122 SFX in 9.9 min from 9 files) and the $7M Cold Outreach reference: a small palette reused (UI Double Beep 03 at -43 to -48 dB, Arpeggiated UI Beep 03, Multiple UI Beeps 01, Swoosh_03, Camera_01 on photo cuts, Mouse Click (5) on UI clicks, ES_Low Hit + one riser only at big reveals), about a third of reveals get a sound, never a run of beeps, no sub drops, no cash registers or money counters, no "techno" data sounds as filler. - Music never drops under him: no song breakdown or near-silent stretch inside a cue; the SFX never sits louder than the song. (CD, 2026-10-26)
- Finished means render-ready. Cache the whole timeline before his review. (2026-10-14, 2026-10-16)
- **2026-10-26 (video 4 plan, hero EN-04 "10-80-10"): plain boxes in a row read as AI-made.** Samuel: *"I don't like the design of the boxes for the 80% part, we have done something like this before, use that style just with the new branding ... don't let things look like it is AI, be creative and look at our past videos ... crosscheck your design with the top videos in my company and just fit new design concepts with the clients brand style, don't let me say this video is trash"*. Before designing any process/split/percentage graphic, find the construct his past finals already used for the same idea and re-skin it in the client's brand; cross-check against the creative director breakdowns (top videos). A row of equal labelled boxes with icons is never the answer.
