# Video editing

## What this is

The freelance client work. It is Samuel's full-time job and his income. It is also, in his own words, his biggest time bottleneck — the thing the rest of the system exists to buy time back from.

## Why it matters

Everything else — the brand, HighSignals, the app, the course — is funded by this and constrained by it. Any hour this area wastes is an hour the ventures don't get.

## Current status — as of 2026-09-20

What the vault holds is deep on **how the work gets done** and empty on **how the business runs**. The Routerise client is documented in real detail; rates, capacity, pipeline and turnaround are not recorded anywhere. That gap is Phase 3 interview material.
