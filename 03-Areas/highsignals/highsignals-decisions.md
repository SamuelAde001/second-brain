# Decisions — highsignals

Append-only.

## 2026-09-20 — HighSignals is the umbrella brand; the personal brand sits outside it
**Decided:** HighSignals is the whole brand and contains HighSignals Community, HighSignals Academy, Mentorship and the Scripnals app. Samuel's personal brand (@SamuelSignals) is a separate brand that funnels people into HighSignals.
**Why:** Samuel stated it directly on 2026-09-20, correcting both the build spec and his own earlier shorthand.
**Who decided:** Samuel.

## 2026-09-22 — A new HighSignals system from 2027

**Decision:** *"From next year, we would have a new system."* Samuel, 2026-09-22.
**Why:** not stated. For context: no branch earns, Academy is paused, and the community is being restarted around video editing.
**What it replaces:** presumably the ascension model (Academy → Community → Mentorship → Scripnals). Not confirmed.
**Details:** none yet (open question 71).
**Who decided:** Samuel.

## 2026-10-20 — Called to Edit becomes the brand; HighSignals stops being the umbrella

**Decision (Samuel, voice note, his words):** *"instead of High Signals, I'm moving my brand to Call to Edit fully"* · *"this brand Call to Edit should just be everything. So Call to Edit Webinar, right? Call to Edit Masterclass, Call to Edit Cohort ... Call to Edit Community"* · *"eventually I'll have the Call to Edit website"* · *"Instead of known as the founder of High Signals, I'll be known as the founder of Call to Edit"*. His voice typing writes "Call to Edit"; the brand on the landing page is **Called to Edit** (to confirm, open question 114).
**Ambition:** *"I want to become the best or the most known or the most influential video editing coach in Nigeria"*, and one day host a physical Called to Edit seminar.
**Positioning:** *"every video editing coach will come and train you on how to do flashy edits, but me, I'm coming in to teach you how to build a career in video editing"*. He still teaches the skill, *"with a better mindset towards career growth"*. Audience: video editors building a career (working for clients, getting content creators), mostly Nigerian, not only; *"not people that are just learning video editing for fancy sake"*.
**What stays:** the personal brand. *"Samuel Signals is still my personal brand that will funnel people to Call to Edit and Call to Create."* No separate Called to Edit social account yet: *"right now I don't have the capacity for that"*. The branding he uses now carries over, and a Called to Edit logo is wanted *"eventually"*.
**HighSignals Community:** keeps running for posting accountability; *"I'm not sure it might continue next year"*. If it does, it becomes **Called to Create** (voice typing: "Code to Create"): *"High Signals will eventually be turned to Code to Create"*.
**Creative director:** the new creative director *"would eventually be the creative director of Call to Edit brand"* ([[03-Areas/personal-brand/creative-director|creative director]]).
**Not yet decided:** where Mentorship and Scripnals sit, the exact list of Called to Edit products, and when the Brain areas are restructured (open question 114). Replaces the 2026-09-20 umbrella decision above; partly answers open questions 71 and 107.
**Who decided:** Samuel.
