# HighSignals

## What this is

**HighSignals is the whole brand** — the umbrella. It contains four branches, each of which has its own area in this vault:

| Branch | Area | What it does |
|--------|------|--------------|
| HighSignals Community | [[../community/_index]] | Where people implement what they learn |
| HighSignals Academy | [[../academy/_index]] | The three courses — equips |
| Mentorship | [[../mentorship/_index]] | Personalised, one-to-one |
| Scripnals | [[../scripnals/_index]] | The app — daily execution |

This area holds what belongs to the brand as a whole: positioning, the relationship between the branches, ecosystem-wide goals, and decisions that cut across more than one branch. Anything that belongs to a single branch lives in that branch's area, not here.

## Samuel's personal brand is NOT under HighSignals

Confirmed by Samuel on 2026-09-20. [[../personal-brand/_index]] — Samuel himself, Instagram @SamuelSignals — sits **outside** HighSignals as a separate brand. Its relationship to HighSignals is a funnel: the personal brand builds attention and trust, and feeds people into HighSignals.

This matters for every content and goal decision. Content made as Samuel is not HighSignals content, even when it points at HighSignals.

```
  @SamuelSignals (personal brand)
          │  funnels people to
          ▼
     HIGHSIGNALS (brand)
          ├── Community
          ├── Academy
          ├── Mentorship
          └── Scripnals
```

## The ascension model

From the project-setup docs, 2026-07-23 — recorded as the model those docs describe, not yet reconfirmed by Samuel:

> Academy → Community → Mentorship → App.
> "Academy equips; Community implements; Mentorship personalizes; Scripnals executes daily."

Whether Samuel still works this way, and whether people actually move along it, is interview material.

## Why it matters

Four branches sharing one name only works if the boundaries are clear and the promise is the same in all four. When that slips, the brand stops meaning anything specific and the funnel from the personal brand leads nowhere.

## Current status — as of 2026-09-20

Structure confirmed. Everything else — positioning statement, who it is for, revenue by branch, which branch is actually working — is unknown and waiting on the Phase 3 interview.
