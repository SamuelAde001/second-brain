01 · The brand
About Called
to Edit
Called to Edit helps video editors build a career. Most training teaches effects and flashy edits. Called to Edit teaches the career itself: how to start, how to find clients, how to price your work and how to grow, alongside the editing skill.
The line
I coach video editors to get paid.
Who it's for

Video editors building a career. Some are starting with no plan. Others can already edit but don't yet get clients or get paid. Most are in Nigeria, and editors anywhere are welcome.

Founder

Samuel Adebayo, video editor and coach. He is the face of the brand.

The name

Always Called to Edit, with a lowercase "to" in sentences. Never "Call to Edit". "Called" means a calling: from God, and to the craft.

01 · The brand
Brand principles
Four rules. When a case isn't covered in this guide, decide it with these.
1
Navy first

Every piece starts on navy. White and champagne sit on top. Use real photos of Samuel; never stock photos of people.

2
One gold, one word

Champagne marks the single thing that matters in a design: the main word, the number, the price or the button.

3
Gloock for the big word

The big word is set in Gloock. Everything people read is set in Plus Jakarta Sans.

4
Clear hierarchy

One big thing per design, then a clear second level, then the small print.

01 · The brand
Voice and tone
Called to Edit sounds like a working editor who has done it and is showing you how.
We sound like
A coach, not a guru. "Here's what worked for me."
Simple English a 15-year-old can follow. One or two Pidgin phrases for warmth.
Nigerian by default, open to editors everywhere.
Bold and true hooks: a stake, a pain or a dream in the first line.
Loose, like speech. Talking to editors directly.
We never
✕  Say "you must" or act perfect.
✕  Promise income or use the word "guaranteed".
✕  Show money as flaunting. Money is proof and possibility.
✕  Use "Nigerians only" wording.
✕  End with more than one call to action.

02 · Logo
Marker or round dot
Both versions are correct. The marker is the main one. The round dot is the alternative wherever the marker can't be built, for example a title typed live in a video tool.
Marker · main
Round dot · alternative

Never mix the two in one piece. Files for both are in the logo pack (names ending in -dot for the round dot).

02 · Logo

02 · Logo
Clear space and size
Keep empty space on every side equal to the height of the "Called to" letters. Nothing enters it, including the edge of the page or frame.

02 · Logo
Logo misuse
✕  Don't stretch or squash it.
✕  Don't change its colours.
✕  Don't rotate it.
✕  Don't add glows, outlines or bevels.
✕  Don't put champagne on white.
✕  Don't place it on busy areas without the shadow.
Called to Edit
✕  Don't retype it in any font.
EditCalled to
✕  Don't split or reorder the lines.

03 · Type
Aa
Text typeface
Plus Jakarta Sans
ExtraBold 800 · Headlines
Bold 700 · Titles and labels
SemiBold 600 · Top lines and captions
Medium 500 · Body text
For everything people read. Letter spacing −3 % on headlines, 0 on body, +14 % on all-caps labels. Free Google Font (SIL Open Font License). Install it on every machine that designs or edits for the brand.
