# Daily routine and habits

Samuel's day as he confirmed it on 2026-09-22, correcting the Accountability Engine's last version (2026-09-15). How it got here is history, in `08-Archive/accountability-engine/context/habits.md`, and is not repeated.

## The day

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 6:00am | Wake |
| 6:00–6:30am | Prayer and Bible study |
| 12:00pm | First meal. Social media allowed while eating, to 12:45pm |
| 1:00pm | Nap, about an hour |
| 5:00pm | Gym |
| ~6:30pm | Dinner, social media allowed while eating. **No hard stop** — he sometimes works past 6:30pm |
| 9:00–10:00pm | Call with his girlfriend |
| ~11:00pm | Phone away and sleep |

The hours between the anchors are client and brand work; the day's plan lives in TickTick, not here.

## Habits

- **Movement:** gym at 5:00pm. No morning exercise any more. *(Which days of the week: not stated yet.)*
- **Sleep:** by about 11:00pm — *"mostly."*
- **Phone away:** by 11:00pm — *"not always."*
- **Prayer and Bible study:** 6:00–6:30am. A practice, not a tracked habit.
- **Social media:** only while eating, at lunch and dinner. His reason: *"when I am eating so it's a relaxation with it."* Outside meals there is no rule.

11:00pm to 6:00am is 7 hours, with no buffer: the phone cut-off and bedtime are now the same time, and both hold only some nights. Late nights are the upstream cause of P4 in [[patterns]].
