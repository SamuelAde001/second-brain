# Spirit

Samuel's faith practice. Built 2026-09-22 from the Accountability Engine's `spirit.md` (2026-08-23 / 2026-08-26), checked with Samuel.

## The goal

**Time with God every single day, without a miss.** When the engine asked about his life, it was the first thing he named, ahead of the work list. Still the goal, 2026-09-22.

- **Standard: 7 out of 7.** *"No acceptable miss rate."* Confirmed 2026-09-22. No penalty attaches ([[stakes-and-accountability]]).

## The practice

- **Prayer and Bible study, 6:00–6:30am** every day ([[daily-routine]]). It moved from the engine's 5:30–6:15am block when his wake time moved to 6:00am.
- It is a practice, not a tracked habit. The nightly habit tick was dropped on 2026-09-02.

## Rules for agents

- **The block is an anchor, not a task. Never schedule work over it.** Confirmed 2026-09-22.
- **The chain holds:** *"Spirit is downstream of sleep, and sleep is downstream of not finishing client work at 1am."* A missed morning block is usually a scheduling failure from the day before, not a spiritual one ([[patterns|P4]]). Confirmed 2026-09-22.
- **Do not ask him what time he went to bed** when the block is missed. Samuel, 2026-09-22 — the engine's rule to ask this is dropped.

## Church

- **Sunday is not a work buffer. Plan Sunday from 3:00pm.** Confirmed 2026-09-22 as the rule to keep.
- The shape behind the rule: service at 9:00am; he leaves around 7:30am, up to 1h30 each way on public transport; the service ends 12:30pm and he is home around 3:00pm.
- **He is on the church media team** — Sundays only, inside the service, no extra time cost. Confirmed 2026-09-22.
- Samuel, 2026-09-22: *"I have not been going to church for a while now"* — but keep the Sunday rule.
- Church media work gets no area of its own ([[00-System/decisions|decision, 2026-09-20]]). If it ever needs tracking, it becomes a client note under video-editing.
