# Failure patterns

Five patterns that run Samuel's days. The Accountability Engine wrote them from his ledger between 2026-08-24 and 2026-09-13. **Samuel confirmed all five as still true on 2026-09-22.** The evidence under each is dated history from the engine; the pattern itself is current.

In his words, 2026-09-22: *"things changes under pressure and less prioritised things gets dropped"* · *"sometimes money saved gets eaten again"* · *"when my morning is messed up, it affects my day"* · *"I get distracted a lot."*

The engine's one cross-cutting observation: P1 and P3 are the same shape — **the line with no external party watching it is the line that gets cut**, whether it is a content shoot or a savings transfer.

## P1 — Selective dropping, not effort collapse

Under pressure his effort does not collapse. The work nobody outside is waiting on gets cut — content, Scripnals, the course — while client work continues. The thing cut is always the thing with no external deadline.

- **Evidence.** Week of 2026-08-24: content shoot cancelled while client blocks ran — 5.95h worked of 14.7h planned (40%). All three content tasks deleted: the first zero-content week. 2026-08-26: the Scripnals review and the course-plan talk dropped; the client download continued.
- **Engine-confirmed 2026-09-02** after three consecutive zero-content weeks (w/c 2026-08-24, 25–31 Aug, 1–2 Sep). The afternoon content block meant to protect content was eaten by client work every week it was tried — dead on the evidence.
- **The engine's constraint on any new content design:** it must survive an ordinary pressured week (11–13 Sep was the example), because the old design could not survive an ordinary Tuesday.

## P2 — The debt gets paid with the body

Work not done on the planned day is repaid in a single death-march session. Dangerous because the deliverable still ships, which rewards the behaviour.

- **Evidence.** 2026-08-24/25: 19h06m, ending 1:42am, 16.23h logged focus, no nap.
- **Watch line:** a day over 12h of logged focus is not a good day — *"it is an invoice."*

## P3 — Money booked is not money moved

Savings get booked, then eaten.

- **Evidence** (June–August budget sheets, noted 2026-08-26): NGN 900,250 booked to Savings and Emergency across three months. NGN 305,000 survives, as the Cowrywise investment. NGN 595,250 is gone, and the August building payment dropped from NGN 500,000 to NGN 200,000 to cover it.
- Money rules and pots: [[03-Areas/finances/finances|Finances]].

## P4 — The day is lost in the morning, not the evening

Losses happen in unstructured, unsupervised gaps, and the phone fills them. **A gap opening at 6am costs a day; a gap opening at 5pm costs a block.**

- **Evidence.** 2026-08-28: a 6:00am–4:00pm gap swallowed the whole day — doomscrolling, masturbation, a broken fast, no prayer, no Bible, no editing. 2026-08-27: a 5:00–6:30pm gap cost a block.
- **Sleep is upstream.** Bedtimes 25–27 Aug: 1:42am, 1:30am, 1:30am — 4h, 5h, 5.5h against a 7h floor, on day 4 of a fast. Sleep explains the late wake; it does not explain the ten hours after it. The phone does.
- **Countermeasures:** the phone charges outside the bedroom from 10:00pm; the 5:30am prayer block closes the 6am gap. **Samuel, 2026-09-22: both in use, not consistently.**

## P5 — The scope is estimated once and never re-estimated

A job is a number of hours, not a number of days, and the estimate never moves once the job starts.

- **Evidence.** Client #2 was planned as 5 days and took 8 (27 Aug – 2 Sep), against Route Rise's contractual cap of 5 days from start. Roughly three of the eight were productive: about a 30-hour job spread across however many days it was allowed to occupy. No day re-cut the plan to the truth.
- **The variable** was not scope or skill but whether the day was built into timed chunks before it started. 2 Sep was, and closed the job in about eight hours. 28 Aug was not.
- The engine's conclusion: plan weeks from real hours, not a day count. The Client #3 test result was never recorded; Samuel, 2026-09-22: not necessary.
