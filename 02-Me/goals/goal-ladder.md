# Goal ladder

What Samuel is working toward and in what order. Built 2026-09-22 from the Accountability Engine's `mission.md` (written 2026-08-26), checked line by line with Samuel.

## The horizon: 12 months

Samuel, 2026-09-22, on 3-year and 10-year goals: *"Skip it, let's hit the goals for 12 months first."* The ladder starts at 12 months on purpose. Longer horizons get added when he chooses.

## The 12-month picture

Written by Samuel on 2026-08-26. On 2026-09-22 he said: *"those goals are still clear and my plan for the next 12 months."*

- **The house project completed** — he moves into the new house and gets established there.
- **No longer editing full-time.** Income no longer depends on it.
- **A coach for video editors**, with **his own agency for video editors**.
- **Courses making money.**
- **Big on social media** — huge following, invited to conferences and seminars, a person of authority.
- **Impact on skilled entrepreneurs**; teaching and mentoring; making an impact in lives, an example to others.
- **Married, with money not a challenge.**

The money targets behind the house and the wedding live in [[03-Areas/finances/finances-goals|Finances goals]]: NGN 1,000,000 for the house by 2026-12-31, and the wedding in July 2027.

## Priority order — the Big 3, as of 2026-09-22

The tiebreaker whenever two things want the same block.

1. **Video editing** — client work. Still top priority.
2. **Content creation** — the personal brand.
3. **Mentorship and coaching programs** — *"not yet started."*

The 2026-08-26 order was editing · course · content. **The course is not in the Big 3 now.** It was paused on 2026-09-20 ([[00-System/decisions|decisions]]). **Scripnals** sits outside the Big 3, as it did before.

## Per domain

### 1. Video editing
- **Target:** hit every stated per-project deadline — no deadline moves. Confirmed 2026-09-22.

- Rate and payment terms: [[03-Areas/video-editing/video-editing|Video editing]].

### 2. Content creation
- **Target: 5,000 followers by December 2026.** His words, 2026-09-22: *"I know it's possible."* The engine's arithmetic was for Instagram.
- **Cadence, from 2026-09-22: daily.** *"Some kind of content is going out everyday from now on"* — *"as long as I have time."* This replaces the once-a-week cadence recorded on 2026-09-20.
- **Stated once, as the arithmetic.** Instagram was 671 on 2026-09-20, up 211 in about 25 days (~8 a day). Reaching 5,000 by 2026-12-31 needs about 4,330 more in about 100 days — roughly 43 a day, five times the recent rate. The daily cadence sits squarely in [[patterns|P1]]: content is the thing with no external deadline, so it is the first thing cut under pressure. The engine's standing constraint applies: a content design has to survive an ordinary pressured week.
- Current state and the series that is working: [[03-Areas/personal-brand/personal-brand|Personal brand]].

- Area: [[03-Areas/mentorship/mentorship|Mentorship]].
