# Stakes and accountability

What Samuel is risking, and how he does — and does not — want to be held to account. Built 2026-09-22 from the Accountability Engine's `stakes.md` (2026-08-23 / 2026-08-26), checked with Samuel.

## The risk — his words, 2026-08-23, still true 2026-09-22

If he keeps operating at his current pace, in 12 months: *"procrastinating a lot, getting distracted, will not finish projects on time so money doesn't come, won't do course, social media stays stagnant — even if moving to new house will still have to edit videos to survive."*

## The fear — still true 2026-09-22

*"To still be editing videos to survive and not being able to finish my building and get married."*

Set against the [[02-Me/goals/goal-ladder|goal ladder]], it is the 12-month picture failing: still editing full-time, the house not finished, not married.

## What agents do

- **Remind him of his goals and stakes when he drifts.** Samuel, 2026-09-22: *"Yeah do remind me of my goals and stakes."* The original instruction behind it (2026-08-26): quote them back in his own words, and do not soften them into something gentler.
- Drifting looks like the [[patterns]]: the no-deadline work dropped, a lost morning, a death-march day, savings eaten.

## What does not work — tried, and dropped 2026-09-22

| Mechanism | What it was | His verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Self-enforced penalty | Miss the weekly ship, or break a habit twice in a week → no YouTube for 2 days, checked by his own screenshot | *"Doesn't work, we may find better punishments later."* |
| Scorecard to his girlfriend | A nightly card — score, shipped, missed, habits, bedtime, money — screenshot and sent to her | *"Nope doesn't work."* |
| Streak as reward | Four consecutive shipped days = the streak; the streak is the reward | *"Nope."* |

**So, as of 2026-09-22: no penalty, no outside checker, no reward mechanism.** A better consequence may be found later, by him. Nothing goes into `06-Logs/commitments.md` from this review.

## The savings rule

The rule is in [[money-rules]]. Asked what breaking it costs him, 2026-09-22: *"It cost me financial stability and security."* That is the natural cost, not an enforced penalty — consistent with the above: no enforced consequence exists.
