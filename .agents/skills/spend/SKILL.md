# Spend — the any-time update

Samuel, 2026-10-21: *"when I want to update you on my spending I can just call the skill … so when I want to update at anytime I just call a word and we know what to do"*. He also wants, every time, a breakdown of what he updated last.

Built from how his updates already ran (2026-10-14, 2026-10-16, 2026-10-18 in [[07-Agents/finance/log|the finance log]]): he lists spends in short lines ("Food today 9400", "2,100 TP"), each becomes a ledger row against a plan line, he gives his bank figure, the gap is derived, the sheet is rebuilt, and the reply names every line over plan.

**Runs in the main session as the Money man.** Read `07-Agents/finance/profile.md` and `memory.md` first. Every message starts **[Money man]**.

## 1. Where he left off (before he types anything new)

```
python 00-System/scripts/money_ledger.py recap
python 00-System/scripts/budget_sheet.py left
```

Give him, short:

- **Last check-in:** the dates, what went out by line (the `recap` category totals), what moved to or from pots, and the balance he closed it with, with its date.
- **Since then:** every row after that balance (money in, spends), and the derived bank, labelled *derived, as of <date>*. Never as his current balance.
- **Coming up:** the payments still due this month and next:
  - lines in `left` with money still unspent, with any date in the line name (e.g. `Seminar, 2026-10-30`);
  - dated items in [[03-Areas/finances/obligations|obligations]] (the Payday column: `A`, `B` or a day like `19th`) and **Live debts** there;
  - the next payday: invoice on the 29th, Payday A (70%) at month end, Payday B (30%) mid next month ([[03-Areas/finances/income|income]]). Both have landed 1–6 days late, so count days to the late date, not the due date.

Then one line: **"What did you spend?"** If his message already lists spends, skip the question and go to step 2.

## 2. Take the spends

He writes them his way: `"5k transport, 9400 food, 2.5k to babe"`. For each:

- **Amount** in NGN. "5k" = 5,000. If a figure is unclear, ask.
- **Line**: match it to a line in this month's plan (`03-Areas/finances/plans/plan-YYYY-MM.md`), written exactly as the plan writes it, or the spend lands in `Other` on the sheet. His shorthand: TP = Transport, GF / babe = Girlfriend extras, food bought to cook = Feeding, food out = Eating out, recharge / data = Data / airtime. Where a guess decides the line, say it was a guess (as on 2026-10-18).
- **Day**: the day he names, else today, and say the day was assumed.
- **Done or planned?** Memory 2026-10-14: a short line can mean paid or about to be paid. If the tense is unclear, ask in one line before logging. A plan is not a row.
- **Which account?** Personal bank only. Webinar money (separate account and Flutterwave) is recorded in [[03-Areas/finances/pots-and-accounts|pots and accounts]], never in the ledger. If he could mean either, ask.
- Money given to a person is given, never a loan (memory 2026-09-23).

Ask everything unclear **in one message**, numbered, then log.

## 3. Log it

Append to the table at the bottom of [[03-Areas/finances/money-ledger|the money ledger]], UTF-8, one row per spend:

```
| YYYY-MM-DD | bulk | 9,400 | Food today | Feeding | his words: "Food today 9400"; day assumed |
```

- `bulk` for everyday spends; `major` for a transfer sent in the week of pay or the first main shopping (profile → logging policy).
- A pot withdrawal is its own `from-pot` row with the reason in his words (Rule 2), and gets named in the reply.
- Note quotes his words. No row without a source.

**Then ask for the bank balance** (screenshot best). If he gives it:

```
python 00-System/scripts/money_ledger.py pots
```

Gap = derived bank − his balance. A small gap goes in as one `bulk` row, `Other`, `Minor spends <from> to <to>`, note `derived`; then a `balance` row with his figure. A gap over NGN 5,000, ask what it was before logging it. If he gives no balance, log no gap and say the derived bank is unchecked. **Never invent a figure.**

Then:

```
python 00-System/scripts/budget_sheet.py build
python 00-System/scripts/budget_sheet.py left
```

## 4. Tell him, short

```
Logged (N): <amount> <line>, <amount> <line> …
Bank: NGN X (his words / derived, as of <date>)
Over plan: <line> −NGN X, … (or: none)
Left to <next payday, late date>: NGN X across the lines, <days> days
Coming up: <date> <payment> NGN X, …
```

- Every line over plan by name and amount, and what covers it (Extra cash, then Rule 8's cut order). No softening, no praise.
- If a spend was off-plan and he didn't ask first ([[06-Logs/commitments|commitments]]: ask before any off-plan spend), say so once, in his words.
- If a line breaks the month, say so and ask the one question that matters. A plan change goes through [[00-System/skills/budget/budget|budget]].

## 5. Close

- One line in `07-Agents/finance/log.md`: what was logged, the bank figure, what's over.
- A dated line in [[03-Areas/finances/finances-log|the finances log]] only if something changed beyond small spends (a pot touched, a line broken, a plan change).
- Commit: `finance: spend update <date>`, then push.
