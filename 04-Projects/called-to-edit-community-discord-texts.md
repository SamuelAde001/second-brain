# Called to Edit Community: Discord texts

Part of [[04-Projects/called-to-edit-community-launch|community launch]] · [[03-Areas/called-to-edit/called-to-edit|Called to Edit]].

What the bot posts in the server. **This note is the source:** edit a section here (Obsidian is fine), then run `python 00-System/scripts/community_admin.py post-texts` and the bot edits its message in place. Rules from Samuel's answers on 2026-10-22 (no fights or abuse, no scams, be kind); the wording is the Brand manager's draft. `#name` becomes a channel link; `{SITE}` becomes the site address. Each `## #channel` section is one pinned message in that channel. ## #welcome

**Welcome to Called to Edit.** 🎬
This is where video editors do the work together: the 60 days, real feedback, people going the same way.

New here? Go to #start-here first.
(Join messages land in this channel, so the chat stays clear.)

## #rules

**The rules**

**1. Be kind to one another.** Give honest feedback, and give it with respect.
**2. No fights, no abuse, no insults.** Disagree with the idea, never attack the person.
**3. No scams.** No investment, forex or crypto offers, no fake jobs, no asking anyone for money. If something looks off, tell Samuel.
**4. No selling or self-promotion.** Don't pitch your services, courses or pages to members, here or in their DMs.
**5. What's shared here stays here.** Don't share the workbook, call recordings or other members' work outside the community.
**6. Your check-ins go in your own post** in #daily-check-in. Go at your own pace.
**7. English**, so everyone can follow.

The bot removes messages that break these rules. Breaking them on purpose means removal from the community.

## #start-here

**How this works**

**1. Introduce yourself** in #introduce-yourself: your name, where you edit from, what you edit on (phone or computer), and who you want to edit for.

**2. Get the workbook** in #workbook. It's your 60 days, one task a day.

**3. Make your check-in post.** In #daily-check-in, tap **New Post**, call it "<your name>'s 60 days", and post your Day 1 there. Every day after, reply in that same post with what you did. One post per person, so your whole journey sits in one place and Samuel can follow it.

**4. Ask for feedback** in #portfolio-and-profile, #outreach and #niche. Share the link or a screenshot and say what you want feedback on.

**5. Stuck?** Ask in #questions, #davinci-help or #gear.

**Your day 1 is the day you start.** The group starts on Sunday, 1 November. If you join later, start your own day 1 and go at your pace.

**Streaks:** each morning the bot adds 🔥 to yesterday's check-ins. Every Sunday evening, #leaderboard shows the longest streaks.

**Every morning** there's a nugget from Samuel's teaching in #daily-nugget.

**Your subscription:** see it, or stop card auto-renew, at {SITE}/community/account
