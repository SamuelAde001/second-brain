# Called to Edit Webinar: Project Plan

**Host:** Samuel Adebayo (@SamuelSignals01) · **Project Manager:** Ifeoma · **Last updated:** 5 October 2026

The working brief for the Called to Edit webinar: what we are doing, when, who does what, and what happens each week until the event.

## 1. The event at a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Event** | Called to Edit: a live webinar on how to start a career as a video editor |
| **Date and time** | Sunday 25 October 2026, 7:00pm WAT, about 2½ hours (to about 9:30pm) |
| **Platform** | Google Meet, recorded |
| **Host** | Samuel, solo |
| **Price** | NGN 5,000 per seat. No refunds. No early-bird price. Sales close when the webinar starts |
| **Seats** | Target 200 in the room. Meet on the current plan holds 100, so going past 100 needs an upgrade first |
| **Registration** | Opens Monday 5 October, 20 days before the event |
| **Sign-up path** | calledtoedit.netlify.app → form → Flutterwave payment → WhatsApp group |
| **After the webinar** | Payers get the replay. Closes with the offer for Called to Edit Academy: 6-week live DaVinci Resolve cohort, NGN 50,000, from November |
| **Ads budget** | NGN 20,000 to NGN 30,000 |

## 2. Goals and targets

Three jobs: become a recognised authority on video editing careers in Nigeria; help editors who don't know what to do next; help editors establish themselves. It also sets up the Academy: people leave thinking "I have a plan, now I need the tools", and want a guide who can really edit.

Targets: **200** people in the room; **50** Academy students at NGN 50,000 (a target, not a cap).

Checkpoints, cumulative paid seats: Sat 10 Oct → 30 · Sat 17 Oct → 90 · Wed 21 Oct → 150 · Sat 24 Oct → 200. Sales bunch up in the last 72 hours; a slow first week is normal. A slow second week means more outreach and ads.

## 3. Team

- **Samuel (host):** teaches, records the videos, approves everything before it goes out.
- **Ifeoma (Project Manager):** runs the plan: tracker, timeline, volunteers, weekly report, chasing tasks.
- **Volunteers (5 or 6):** recruited by a public call-out video; sign up in one place that redirects to a WhatsApp group; Samuel assigns roles; free access to the webinar.

Volunteer roles: outreach and admin (DMs, community posting, following up payers) · video editor (cuts Samuel's footage into Reels) · graphic designer (flyers, countdown cards, covers, slide template) · distribution (posting and scheduling) · scripts and copy (captions, DM templates, draft scripts).

Rhythm: one 30-minute team check-in every Saturday, plus a written weekly report from the PM.

## 4. The webinar

**Promise.** "This is not a video editing tutorial." The pathway to becoming a professional video editor who earns from editing, on any device.

**For.** Editors who want to start but have no plan, and editors already learning who don't know how to get clients, position themselves or get paid. Main audience Nigerian; everyone welcome.

**Format.** Google Meet, recorded in OBS as the master. Samuel alone. A chat prompt every ten minutes or so, one poll ("phone or laptop?"). Q&A runs past 9:30pm for those who stay.

| Time | Part |
|---|---|
| 6:30–7:00pm | Doors open: music, welcome slide, chat prompt |
| 7:00–7:10 | Opening |
| 7:10–7:45 | Part 1: Start (learn, tools and money, niche) |
| 7:45–8:05 | Part 2: Establish yourself |
| 8:05–8:10 | Break |
| 8:10–8:40 | Part 3: Get clients |
| 8:40–8:55 | Part 4: Pricing and working with clients |
| 8:55–9:05 | Part 5: Network and grow |
| 9:05–9:20 | Your first 30 days, then the Academy offer |
| 9:20–9:30+ | Q&A |

- **Opening:** Samuel's story, military to editing for a foreign company from Abuja. What the evening is and isn't. Treat it as a business from day one.
- **Part 1:** what to learn first and how (recreate videos you like); working for free or low pay as a way to learn; how long until you can charge; tools, phone against laptop; how much money to start and the computer each niche needs; the decisions an editor makes; the niches (talking heads, short-form, YouTube, ads, weddings and events, music videos, corporate), pros and cons, how to choose.
- **Part 2:** profile page and a new Twitter account; which platform suits which editor; a personal brand as an editor; portfolio, including with no clients yet; presenting yourself professionally; showing your work.
- **Part 3:** inbound and outbound; cold DMs and emails, Loom video outreach, following up; value-first (editing someone's video free); pitching; Upwork, Fiverr, job boards; Nigeria against abroad and why you learn to work for free first.
- **Part 4:** how to price and what moves the price; deposits and contracts; briefs, revisions, deadlines, communication; turning one job into a testimonial and referral.
- **Part 5:** networking and editor communities; one-off gigs against recurring clients; packaging gigs; add-on services (content strategy, thumbnails, scripting); AI to edit faster; agency (mentioned, not taught); coaching once you're good.
- **Close:** a week-by-week first 30 days, the Academy offer, Q&A.

## 5. Registration, payment and joining

1. Someone lands on calledtoedit.netlify.app from a post, DM, community or ad.
2. They tap "Get my seat" and fill the form (name, email, phone, WhatsApp number). A row lands in the Sign-ups tab of the Registrations Sheet, with a source tag.
3. They pay NGN 5,000 on Flutterwave.
4. Flutterwave returns them to the welcome page, which records the payment (Paid tick) and opens the WhatsApp group. No payment, no group link.
5. Meet access: each payer's email is added to the Google Calendar event as a guest, daily. The Meet link is never posted publicly.
6. Reminders in the WhatsApp group: 7 days, 1 day, 3 hours, 30 minutes before, and when live.
7. Sales close at 7:00pm on 25 October: Flutterwave link off, page changes to "registration closed".

**Admin check.** Daily: match the WhatsApp group against Flutterwave's transaction list; remove anyone who didn't pay.
**Seat cap.** Meet holds 100 on the current plan. At about 80 paid seats, decide on the Workspace upgrade (it takes setup time). The landing page says 100 seats, so it changes with the cap.
**Replay.** Payers get a view-only Drive link, targeted within 24 hours.

## 6. Marketing

**Message and rules.** "I coach video editors to get paid." Announcement line: "This is not a video editing tutorial. It's the plan to become an editor who gets paid." Samuel teaches the business side and his DaVinci Resolve methods. No income promises, no guru tone, no misleading hooks, proof over claims, seat counts only when true, every DM personal.

**Channels.** Landing page (link in every bio, post, DM) · Instagram and TikTok (Reels, daily stories with countdown, pinned announcement) · WhatsApp status daily from 5 Oct · communities (WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, campus, church media, X, LinkedIn; value first, link second; includes HighSignals Community and its channels) · warm outreach (people who asked how to start, contacts, mentees, editors Samuel knows) · paid ads · SMFest Sat 17 Oct (printed flyers with QR).

**Videos.** Three big announcement videos, well edited like a major advert, one also runs as the ad: (1) announcement, Mon 5 Oct; (2) Samuel's story, Fri 9 Oct (the ad); (3) "what you will leave with", Wed 21 Oct. Simple talking heads fill the days between. Reels four a week (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sun), posted 8:00–10:00am, each ending with one call to action ("Comment PLAN and I'll send the link").

Hooks are drafts; Samuel rewrites each in his voice.

**Paid ads.** NGN 20,000–30,000 (four to six registrations covers it). Creative: the story video. Run Sat 10 Oct to the morning of 25 Oct; check every two days and move spend to what works; source tag on the link so the Sheet shows seats from ads. Any extra boost approved by Samuel first.

**Outreach targets.** 30–50 personal messages a day across the outreach volunteers, each logged (who, where, date, reply, paid). 20 communities by Fri 9 Oct, 40 by Fri 16 Oct. Follow up once after two days.

**Design pieces.** Flyer set (feed, story, WhatsApp status) · countdown set (14, 7, 3, 1 days, Today) · who-it-is-for card · agenda card · testimonial cards · Reel cover template · slide template · SMFest print flyer with QR. Look follows the landing page: navy, gold, cream.

**Proof.** Showreel of client work (30–45 seconds); screenshots of people asking how to start, names hidden; a testimonial from one of Samuel's mentees.

**Tracked weekly.** Paid seats against the checkpoint; page taps, form fills, payments by source (in the Sheet); Reel views, shares, saves; DMs started; followers.

## 9. What could go wrong

| Risk | Fix |
|---|---|
| Power or internet fails | Inverter or generator, second network, backup device logged in; the co-host holds the room for five minutes |
| More than 100 sign up | Check the Meet limit now; decide on the upgrade at about 80 paid; update the page |
| Meet link leaks | Calendar guests only; never post the link |
| Unpaid person in the WhatsApp group | Daily check against Flutterwave's list; remove them |
| Payment link fails | Test first on Monday; one person on WhatsApp for failed payments |
| A volunteer role stays empty | PM covers admin; daily DM target halves |
| Sales stall | Checkpoint plan in section 2 |
| Paid people don't attend | Reminders and the replay |
