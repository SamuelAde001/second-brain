## For every design

**Size:** 4:5 portrait, 1080 × 1350 px (2160 × 2700 for the master). Posted on Instagram feed and WhatsApp.

**Repeat on every piece (footer):**
- Called to Edit (logo / title)
- Fri 23 Oct · 7:00pm WAT · Google Meet
- NGN 5,000
- Only 100 seats
- Button: **Get my seat**
- calledtoedit.netlify.app
- @samuelsignals01

**Look (approximate, not final brand values):**
- Mood: premium, editorial, dark navy with gold. His room is the brand: navy marble wall with gold veining.
- Colours: navy `#0B1322`, gold `#D6B25E` (light gold `#F6DE9C`, deep gold `#B8862E`), cream text `#F2EBDD`, muted grey `#B7BDCA`.
- Type: a bold high-contrast gold serif for the title (Playfair Display Bold is the closest free match), clean sans for body text.
- What Samuel liked from Pinterest: giant heavy condensed title, Samuel cut out on the right overlapping the title, clean sans text, details in a divided labelled row, a badge for urgency.
- Cutouts with soft edges, no hard outline around him.

**Do not write:** "guaranteed", any income promise, the name of the 1M+ YouTuber, or "HighSignals". This is Samuel's personal brand.

**Files to send the designer:** photos of Samuel from `C:\Users\repzy\Desktop\landing-page\flyer\` (`photo-full.webp`, plus `subject-full.webp` as a ready cutout), the Pinterest picks in `flyer\inspiration\`, and the live page calledtoedit.netlify.app for reference.

## 2. Who it's for

**Title:** Is this for you?
**Subtitle:** This is for you if…

1. **You're starting out.** You want to start video editing, but you don't know where to begin or what to learn first.
2. **You're already learning.** You can edit, but you don't know how to get clients or get people to pay you.
3. **Any device, any app.** You edit on a phone, a laptop or CapCut? It doesn't matter.

**Line at the bottom:** Skill isn't the whole problem. Nobody told you the business side.
**Optional small line:** Not for you if you want a button-by-button editing tutorial. This is the path, not the software lesson.
**Footer:** standard (above).

## 3. Agenda

**Title:** The roadmap, stop by stop.
**Intro:** Seven stops, in the order you'll need them, from your first lesson to your first paying client and beyond.

**Phase 1 — Get started**
1. **A learning plan:** what to learn first, where, and free assets
2. **Your niche:** the niches in editing and how to choose yours
3. **The tools and the money:** what you need and the right computer

**Phase 2 — Get clients**
4. **A profile clients trust:** your pages, your portfolio, presenting yourself
5. **How to get clients:** inbound, cold DMs and emails, pitching your value

**Phase 3 — Get paid and grow**
6. **How to price yourself:** what moves your price up, and what moves it down
7. **Where it can go:** recurring clients, add-on services, coaching editors

**Closing line:** You leave with a clear plan for your next 30 days.
**Footer:** standard. Design idea: a road or timeline with 7 stops in 3 sections.
