## What it covers

Samuel said "full brand build" and gave no list. What is already open and waiting for it:

- **The look:** which existing branding carries over (open question 115): the navy/gold brand guide, the blue/yellow webinar flyer, or the cobalt/Poppins system.
- **The logo:** who makes it and when (*"I haven't decided yet"*, question 115).
- **The announcement:** *"I would announce it all after the webinar"*, plus the bio change from "Founder of HighSignals" to Called to Edit.
- **Ifeoma's role** as creative director of Called to Edit, and her access (question 112).
- Everything in the [[03-Areas/called-to-edit/called-to-edit-brand-foundation|brand foundation]]. Its "Brand manager suggestions" section waits for his yes or no.

The scope, the order and the deadline get set with him when it starts.

## Round 1 (2026-10-21, Brand manager)

Concept board: https://claude.ai/artifact/XPmTaWHUsHiW47CAdM62z9 (private). Built from his own work, per his brief.

- **Logo directions:** 1 *Title Card* (his Life of a Video Editor title build: small white sans "Called to" over a big Gloock "Edit"; the Brand manager's pick), 2 *Footnote* (his flyer: heavy sans, two lines, the asterisk as "the part nobody told you"), 3 *Playhead* (Gloock wordmark, the i's dot is a playhead; flagged against his 2026-10-07 dislike of timeline details). A seal for certificates works with any of them.
- **Palette suggested:** Called Navy `#0A1F44`, Midnight `#071226`, Marble `#1E3150`, Champagne `#FCDD9E` (his title gold), Bronze `#8A5E17` (gold on white only), White, Mist `#B9C3D6`. 60/30/10. Champagne on navy 12.4:1. Flyer yellow dropped.
- **Type suggested:** keep Gloock for display (free, OFL; one weight, display sizes only), Plus Jakarta Sans for everything else. Playfair, Montserrat, Poppins, Anton out.
- **Suggestions, not decisions.** His answers needed: (1) logo 1, 2, 3 or a mix; (2) champagne only, or keep the yellow; (3) keep Gloock; (4) Plus Jakarta Sans; (5) what "Called" means to him (faith, the craft, both).
- **Next round:** the picked logo as vector files (SVG, PNG), then the full guide as a design system: logo rules, colour, type, video title template, flyer, carousel and certificate layouts.

Sources looked at: his Life of a Video Editor Ep 1-2 and My Story ep 3, the landing page CSS, the designer's flyer, Pinterest (navy-gold logos, film studio and editor logos), published brand guides (Foothill-De Anza, Vizrt, Ivey Academy), the structure of Alex's Frontal kit.
