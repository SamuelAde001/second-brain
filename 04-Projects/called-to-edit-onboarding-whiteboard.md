## 4 · Setting up Discord (LIVE DEMO)

**Hub:** *Set up* / **Discord**

Demo badge: **LIVE DEMO · switch to Discord** → his phone screen or the Discord app.

Steps (numbered chain):
1. **Get the app** on your phone and your computer. Log in with the account you joined with.
2. **Your profile:** your real name as your server nickname, a clear photo of your face. (Your face and name: same rule as your Instagram page.)
3. **Notifications:** turn on 📢 announcements and 🎙️ weekly-live, set the rest to @mentions only, so you're not buried.
4. **Find your way:** the channel list on the left (on phone, swipe right). Categories fold open and closed.
5. **Forums:** ✅ accountability, 🎞️ feedback and 📚 resources are forums. You tap **New Post**, give it a title and a tag.
6. **The stage:** 🎙️ weekly-live. To talk, tap **Request to Speak**; I bring you up.
7. **Editors Bot:** type **/** to see the commands. Try **/help**.
8. **Your subscription:** **/subscription** shows when your month ends. Renewal is automatic on a card; transfer payers get a reminder 3 days before.

Rules card (sticky, the six rules short): be kind · no fights or abuse · no scams · no selling to members · what's shared here stays here · English.

## 6 · Using the community to the fullest

## 7 · Your turn on stage

**Hub:** *Your* / **Turn**

How it works (chain): tap **Request to Speak** → I bring you up → 2–3 minutes → I tell you your next step → back to the audience.

**Tell me** (five stickies, the same as the #welcome prompt plus the stage):
1. Your name, and where you edit from.
2. What you edit on: phone or computer, which software.
3. The videos you want to edit, and who for.
4. **Where you are right now** (pick a stop on the map below).
5. One thing you want done in the next 30 days.

**Where are you right now?** Brand manager's proposal: a road of six stops, each with the next step he gives. He points at their stop as they talk.

| Stop | Where they are | The next step (from the teachings) | Channel to live in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Just starting.** Haven't edited much yet. | Pick one tool and learn on real videos, not tutorials alone. Learn, then implement straight away. | ✂️ capcut / 🎛️ davinci-resolve / 🟪 premiere-pro |
| 2 | **Can edit, no direction.** Editing everything. | Choose your niche. Write your offer in one sentence. **/niche** | 🌟 personal-brand |
| 3 | **Have a niche, nothing to show.** | Three full videos in your niche. Practice work is real work. | 🎬 showcase · 🎞️ feedback |
| 4 | **Have work, nobody sees it.** | Fix your profile, build the portfolio, post 2–3 a day. | 🪪 profile-and-portfolio · 🔗 share-your-posts |
| 5 | **Posting, no clients yet.** | Dream client list, warm up, value-first outreach, follow up up to 4 times. | 📨 outreach |
| 6 | **Got clients, want more or better.** | Rate card, testimonials, raise your prices, ask for referrals. | 💰 pricing-and-clients · 🏆 wins |

**Space for his notes:** a large blank area beside the road, one empty sticky per member (name on top), for him to scribble their next step as they talk. Ten stickies to start; he adds more by hand.
