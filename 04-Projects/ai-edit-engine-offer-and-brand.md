## The brand

### Name: **Housecut** (recommended)
- *House style* + *cut*: "your house style, cut by AI, on your own machine". It says what's different in one word.
- A web search on 2026-11-05 found **no video product called Housecut or SignalCut**. Similar names in the field: FireCut, ChatCut, Cutback, CapCut. **Before using it:** check the domain (housecut.ai / gethousecut.com), the trademark (USPTO, UK IPO) and the X / LinkedIn / YouTube handles.
- Backups: **SignalCut** (ties to @SamuelSignals; "signal" also means "no noise"), **Stylecut**.

### Its own brand, not Called to Edit
Called to Edit serves **editors building a career**. Housecut serves **companies that want their videos edited without hiring**. Mixing them confuses both. Samuel is the link: *"Built by Samuel Adebayo, a working editor whose system edits for an international agency."* His personal brand can tell the build story; Housecut sells.

### Positioning
- **For:** B2B, SaaS and AI companies (and small video agencies) that post long-form YouTube every week and already pay an editor or an agency.
- **The problem:** good editors are expensive, slow, and leave, and AI editors give everyone the same template look.
- **The promise:** *your* style, learned from your own videos, edited on your own machine, reviewed in one screen.
- **Why believe it:** built by a working editor from his own rulebook of over 1,000 rules, proven on real client-grade videos (case study after the pilot).

### Taglines (pick one)
1. **Your house style. On autopilot.**
2. **Record it. Review it. Ship it.**
3. **An editor that learned your channel.**

### Voice
Plain, confident, specific. Numbers over adjectives: "4 videos a month, about 20 minutes of your time each". Never "perfect" or "zero mistakes": promise the review gate, not perfection (the R5 lesson). No hype words: revolutionary, game-changing, magic.

### Look (first pass)
- Built around the product's own screen: the review UI *is* the hero image. Show the before/after.
- Palette: near-black `#0E0F12`, off-white `#F4F2EE`, one signal colour, electric lime `#C6F432`, for the playhead, the approve button and the logo mark. That's a starting point; a designer or Ifeoma refines it.
- Type: a grotesk for headings (Inter Tight or Geist), mono for numbers and timecodes (JetBrains Mono).
- Logo idea: a house outline whose roof is a playhead / cut mark.

## The done-for-you offer

### Name: **The Housecut Build**
*"In 6 weeks we study your channel, build your own AI editor in your style, install it on your machine, and prove it on 3 of your videos. If the pilots don't hit the bar we agree on, you get the build fee back."*

### What they get
| # | Piece | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Style Study** | We break down 3-5 of your best videos and write your **House Style**: brand file, rulebook, pacing, caption and graphics rules. You approve it |
| 2 | **Your graphics kit** | Your lower thirds, lists, callouts, screen-demo zooms and title cards, built as editable components in your brand |
| 3 | **The engine, installed** | On your PC, in your own Claude Code: one-command install, every permission set, `/doctor` health check |
| 4 | **Studio UI** | New project → drop footage → watch progress → **review screen** (click a moment, type a note, approve) → final MP4 |
| 5 | **Style editor** | Change colours, fonts, pacing or rules yourself when the brand changes; preview before saving |
| 6 | **3 pilot videos** | Your real videos through the engine, with us beside you |
| 7 | **60 days of support** | Fixes, tuning, one style revision, a weekly check-in |
| 8 | **Handover** | A 20-minute walkthrough video and a one-page guide for whoever runs it |

### What they need
A Windows PC (Mac later) with a decent GPU, a **Claude Max plan of their own** (about USD 200 a month), their own footage, logo and fonts, and someone to do the review (about 20 minutes a video, target to prove).

### The price (draft)
- **USD 9,500 total**, in three payments:
  1. **USD 1,500: Style Study.** Paid first, delivered in week 1, **counted toward the total**. If they stop here, they keep the House Style document.
  2. **USD 5,000 at build start** (week 2).
  3. **USD 3,000 after the 3 pilots pass.**
- **After 60 days: Housecut Care, USD 750 a month** (updates when Claude Code / HyperFrames change, a style refresh each quarter, priority fixes). Optional, cancel any month.
- Why USD 9,500 is easy to say yes to: an agency-grade B2B editor costs USD 1,000-5,000 a video ([increditors](https://increditors.com/youtube-video-editing-cost-per-video-full-breakdown-2026/)), and SaaS editing retainers run USD 5,000-16,000 a month ([Video Editing Company](https://videoeditingcompany.com/youtube-video-editing-cost/)). At 4 videos a month the build earns itself back in 1-3 months. Vendor prices; check them before quoting.

### The guarantee
**Pilot guarantee:** before the build, we agree in writing what a passing video looks like (a checklist, plus "you'd post it after one round of notes"). If the 3 pilots don't pass after one revision round each, the USD 5,000 build payment is refunded. The Style Study is kept, because it's already delivered.

### Capacity, said out loud
**2 builds a quarter.** It's true (Samuel's hours and tokens, alongside Route Rise), and it's a real reason to book now.

### The 6 weeks
| Week | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Kickoff call, sample videos in, Style Study delivered and approved |
| 2-3 | Graphics kit + engine customised to their House Style |
| 4 | Install on their machine, permissions, `/doctor` green, Studio UI walkthrough |
| 5 | Pilots 1-2 |
| 6 | Pilot 3, handover, 60-day support starts |

### Who it's not for (put it on the page)
Solo creators who want zero involvement; anyone who won't review a video; channels whose look depends on heavy VFX or cinema colour grading.

## How it gets sold

1. **Proof first:** Phase 0, then pilot customer 1 at USD 3,000-5,000 (no guarantee, a case study in return).
2. **The case-study video:** raw footage → Studio UI → final, a side-by-side with the human edit, with real numbers.
3. **Housecut page**: one page, the hero is the review screen, the offer above, *Book a Style Call*.
4. **Outreach:** a list of 50 companies (B2B / SaaS / AI, weekly long-form, 1k-100k subscribers, with a content lead). For each: re-edit 60 seconds of their own public video in their style → a 2-minute Loom → LinkedIn or email DM. Sample DM:
   > *"Hi {name}, I re-cut 60 seconds of your last video in your own style with the AI editor I build for B2B channels (link). I install it on your machine, trained on your videos, and you review each edit in one screen. Worth a 15-minute look?"*
5. **Build in public** on LinkedIn and X: weekly progress clips. Samuel's own brand tells the story; Housecut takes the bookings.
