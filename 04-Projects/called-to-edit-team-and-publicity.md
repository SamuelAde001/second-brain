Part of [[04-Projects/called-to-edit-webinar-and-academy|Called to Edit webinar → Academy]]. Area: [[03-Areas/personal-brand/personal-brand|Personal brand]].

## The rule it runs under

Samuel, 2026-09-30: *"I need to also be able to do this while still being able to do my work as a video editor working for my company. So I need to make sure that this doesn't distract me working for my company. That's why I need people and hands."* He'll use AI tools where they help.

Client work comes first: Route Rise (5 videos a month is the target, [[03-Areas/video-editing/video-editing-decisions|video editing decisions]]) and the [[04-Projects/personal-client-project-2026-10|October personal-client job]].

### Terms (Samuel, 2026-09-30)

- **Pitched as an internship, unpaid.** *"if they want to work for me for free, they should come and work for me for free."*
- **Every intern gets a free cohort seat**, *"for sure"*.
- **Privately, a percentage of the whole money goes to the workers.** Not settled, not told to them yet: *"I'm not going to tell them they're going to be paid yet so that they can want to work for me regardless of the payment. Then I will still pay them, just make them feel welcome with what they did."*
- **Ads budget: none for now.** *"if I see that I need to do ads, I might do ads"*.
- Nobody found yet besides Ifeoma.

### Proof for the publicity

Route Rise work and previous clients' work. *"I can show them I have the rights to do so."*

## Publicity

*"It must go public, like the publicity must be huge."*

- **DMs and cold outreach**, sent by him.
- **Communities:** posting it in every community he can get into.
- **Reels:** him on camera, edited by the team's video editor, posted on his profile and other profiles.
- **Flyers and carousels.**
- **Ads**, *"if I have the money to run it"*. Budget not set.
- Every form of publicity that reaches the most people.

Then the same again for the cohort after the webinar.

## Suggestions (General Manager, 2026-09-30, not decided)

- The admin runs the outreach tracker: who was DM'd, who replied, who paid. Without one, 200 seats can't be chased.
- Other editors, creators and community owners with bigger audiences than 700 sharing the webinar is the cheapest reach there is. A list of them is worth building this week.
- The Brand manager (content agent) drafts the promo reel scripts and the carousel copy; the team produces and posts them.
- Interns' terms written down before they start, even if unpaid: what they do, for how long, what they get (a certificate, a free cohort seat, a credit, a cut).
