## Talk outline (the PA's draft, built from his own teachings; change anything)

Thread: **habits build the skill, and reading is the habit under every skill.** The stories are his, from [[05-Knowledge/teachings/teachings-01-story-and-mindset|story and mindset]] and [[05-Knowledge/teachings/teachings-02-learning-the-skill|learning the skill]].

**8:30 · Open (3 min)**
- Thank the host and Phenomenal Bibliophiles. Pick up from the Mindset speaker: "mindset decides where you want to go; habits and skills are how you get there."

**8:33 · His story (7 min)**
- 8 years in the Nigerian Air Force. 2022: *"Is there any skill I can learn that can make me money outside of my military work?"*
- Picked editing because he already knew a little. Now he edits full-time for clients, including a YouTuber with about 1 million subscribers.
- He was self-taught: *"I never bought one course. The only thing I ever paid for in my video editing journey was a community."*

**8:40 · Habits (15 min)**
- A habit is a small thing done on a schedule, not a mood. Use his **60 Days of Greatness** (started 2026-11-01): prayer and Bible study every morning, gym on Mon/Tue/Thu, posting every day, planning tomorrow the night before. Say how it's going in his own words.
- **Consistency beats talent.** The Robuik story: a nobody who showed up online every day and *"after 30 days of consistency, it felt like he blew up overnight"* ([[03-Areas/personal-brand/series/cinematic-visibility-script|the script]]).
- **Learn in public.** *"Instead of learning in private, I started learning in public."* Document the journey: *"if you did any edit, post it, talk about it."*
- **Reading as the first habit.** His mornings start with reading the Bible (6:00–6:30am, every day). The habit that shapes him is a reading habit.

**8:55 · Skills (12 min)**
- **You learn a skill, but you sell a service.** *"Video editing is not a skill, it's a service."* The skill has to serve someone.
- **Break it into small pieces.** Colour grading, typography: each one can be learnt on its own. With focus, *"just one week"* for one piece. *"Try and master it in 30 days instead of 60 days."*
- **Readers learn faster.** He read a **1,000-page DaVinci Resolve book on Fusion** to master it. *"If you don't know how to learn things by yourself, you'll never become a master."* Reading is how you teach yourself any skill: manuals, books, other people's work.
- **The finished test:** you're ready when you can finish the whole thing start to end. *"There's no real time. It is more of your skill level."*

**9:07 · Reading, and the plug (5 min)**
- Tie it together: reading builds the habit (you show up to a page daily) and the skill (you learn without waiting for a teacher).
- His proof that a community pays: the one thing he paid for was a community, and a fellow editor's recommendation got him his biggest client. *"There's a reason why you need to be able to build your network."*
- So: join Phenomenal Bibliophiles, and its next-year cohort (details from the host, question 2).
- One line at the end, if the host says yes (question 4): he runs Called to Edit, for video editors.

**9:12 · Q&A (15–18 min)**
- Likely questions: how to stay consistent when motivation drops; which book to start with; how to learn a skill with no money for courses (his answer: YouTube, recreate other people's work, a community).

**Takeaway line for the end (draft):** "Your mindset picks the destination. Your habits get you there daily. Reading is the habit that teaches you every skill on the way."
