# Called to Edit Academy: the cohort

Part of [[04-Projects/editing-career-webinar-and-live-class|Called to Edit webinar → Academy]]. Area: [[03-Areas/personal-brand/personal-brand|Personal brand]] until [[00-System/open-questions|question 99]] says otherwise.

This replaces what the project called the "DaVinci Resolve live class" on 2026-09-29. Everything below is Samuel's, 2026-09-30. Where he hedged, it says so.

## What it is

- **Name:** Called to Edit Academy.
- **Sold at:** the end of the Called to Edit webinar. He'll still promote it separately.
- **Price:** **NGN 50,000** for one cohort.
- **Length:** *"The cohort might be six weeks."* Not firm.
- **Format:** mostly live teaching. *"I'm thinking two live teachings in a week"*, depending on how the outline comes out. Not firm.
- **Assignments:** given out, students bring them back, he keeps training them over the six weeks.
- **Certificate:** students graduate with a certificate from Called to Edit Academy.
- **Target:** 50 students. 50 × NGN 50,000 = NGN 2,500,000, before fees.
- **Starts:** November 2026 (from 2026-09-29). Date not set.

## Who it's for

Mainly people **already using DaVinci Resolve**. It teaches the skill of editing, where the webinar teaches the career.

## What it teaches

Majorly DaVinci Resolve:
- Cutting videos
- Sound design, and the Fairlight page
- Fusion
- A little colour grading
- Different types of animation

## What students get

- His assets, some of his sound effects, some of the tools he uses.

## How it relates to what's already in the Brain

- **HighSignals Academy** already had a course line named *Called to Edit* (Beginners on CapCut, Advanced on DaVinci Resolve), paused since 2026-09-20 ([[03-Areas/academy/academy|Academy]]). This cohort is closest to *Called to Edit: Advanced*: Resolve, Fusion, motion, monetisation. Whether this cohort *is* the Academy restarting, or a personal-brand product that borrows the name, is question 99.
- The paused CapCut curriculum ([[03-Areas/academy/called-to-edit-beginners/called-to-edit-beginners|Called to Edit: Beginners]]) has a module map for cut, audio, colour, motion and workflow. Its structure and the [[03-Areas/academy/called-to-edit-beginners/teaching-rules|teaching rules]] may be reusable for the outline, even though the software is different. A suggestion, not a decision.
- His own Resolve know-how is already written down: [[03-Areas/video-editing/fusion-node-system|Fusion node system]], [[03-Areas/video-editing/fusion-recipes|Fusion recipes]], [[03-Areas/video-editing/workflow|Workflow]], the [[03-Areas/video-editing/motion-gallery/motion-gallery|Motion gallery]]. That's cohort material that already exists.
