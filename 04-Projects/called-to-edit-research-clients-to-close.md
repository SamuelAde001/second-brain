## 5.1 Inbound

- No data exists on how editors get inbound clients. Every guide repeats "niche, reel, before/after, process posts, referrals" with no numbers ([Fueler, 2026-08-12](https://www.fueler.io/blog/how-to-get-your-first-video-editing-client), read). Your own story is the evidence.
- Useful tactics: screen-record the timeline and post the before/after **with the reason for each cut**; check favourite creators' own careers pages; creator newsletters carry job posts (Passionfruit, The Publish Press) ([Descript blog, 2026-07-29](https://descript.com/blog/article/video-content-creator-jobs), read).
- Line: inbound compounds slowly; outbound gets the first client.

## 5.2 Outbound: where to look

- **YT Jobs (ytjobs.co):** Paddy Galloway's YouTube job board. Lots of editor and thumbnail posts, profiles that verify your work, "a lot of extremely low-paying jobs" (Descript blog, read). Whether it accepts Nigerians wasn't confirmed.
- **Other boards** named by Descript: Roster (pays more, fewer posts), Vouch (pitch big creators), Creator Economy Jobs (noisy), LinkedIn and Indeed alerts for "YouTube".
- **Contra:** see 4.4 in [[04-Projects/called-to-edit-research-learning-to-brand|the other note]]. Nigerian editors from Lagos and Abuja are listed there.
- **Reddit:** r/forhire about 694k members. Rules couldn't be read (Reddit blocked); an old version required a stated rate. Check the rules before posting.
- **Agencies and white-label:** guides suggest offering overflow editing to agencies at a lower rate for steady volume (Fueler, Pixflow, read). This fits your 0.3 take on agencies.
- **Skool communities:** creators running them post for recurring short-form editors. That makes them a client source more than a peer network (unverified).
- Line: two or three channels, not ten. One board, direct outreach, agencies. Qualify leads by budget first.

## 5.3 Cold DMs and emails

- **Reply-rate benchmarks are sales-tool vendors' and disagree:**
  - Instantly: 3.43% average, top 10% get 10.7%+ (2025 data, [report](https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026), read).
  - Belkins: about 0.5% per email step; LinkedIn sequences 7%+ (7.5M emails, [Belkins](https://belkins.io/blog/sales-follow-up-statistics), read).
  - All are B2B sales emails; no editor-to-creator data exists.
  - Line: "a few in a hundred reply, so personal beats volume."
- **Shape of a good editor DM** (guides; opinion, not tested):
  - Open with *their* specific video.
  - One concrete observation (slow first 30 seconds, no captions, no shorts from the long video).
  - Why it matters.
  - A low-pressure offer ("want me to cut 30-60s of your latest?").
  - One reel link. Short.
- **Platform limits:**
  - X free accounts: 50 posts, 200 replies, **500 DMs a day** ([Android Headlines, 2026-05-18](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/05/x-limits-unverified-free-accounts-daily-posts-replies.html), read).
  - LinkedIn: about 100 connection requests a week, 20-30 for new accounts (vendor estimate; LinkedIn publishes none).
  - No automation tools; bans are real.
- **Email:** Gmail's bulk-sender rules (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) apply above 5,000 emails a day ([Google](https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126), read). A beginner sending 20-50 is far below that, but a proper domain, plain text, one link and no blast tools still matter.

## 5.5 Value-first: the free sample edit

- What guides advise: pick a creator with good content but weak editing; re-edit about 60s of their latest video; one sample per prospect; paid packages ready; ask for a testimonial if they like it (Fueler, Pixflow, read).
- **Legal catch:** YouTube's Terms forbid downloading content without the rights holder's permission ([YouTube Terms](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), read). A re-edit is a derivative work under US law ([17 USC 106](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/106), read). Clean route: ask first ("can I send you a 30s re-cut?"), or ask for the raw clip. Don't post it publicly.
- Time-box it to about an hour. Never give away a full video. A small paid test beats a long free one.

## 5.6 Pitching your value

- Pitch one number the client cares about: hours saved a week, upload consistency, how many viewers stay past 30 seconds. Prove it on their own content.
- Never promise a retention percentage for a channel you haven't edited. Say "I'd test X", not "I'll get you Y%".

## 5.7 Nigeria vs abroad

- **Pay gap:** a Nigerian 2026 guide says skills paying NGN 20,000 locally earn USD 30-100 from international clients ([ProGigFinder](https://www.progigfinder.com/blog/how-to-price-your-gig-in-nigeria-a-freelancer-pricing-guide-for-2026), reported). Quote foreigners in USD. For local contracts over 3 months, add a clause tying price to the CBN rate.
- **Bias against Nigerians:** the only evidence is old anecdote (developers, 2021, [BuiltInAfrica](https://www.builtinafrica.io/blog-post/overcoming-stereotypes-life-as-a-nigerian-developer), read). Don't claim "all foreign clients think Nigerians are scammers". Counters: testimonials, verified credits, a paid small first job, normal payment rails, reliable overlap hours.
- **Payment rails:** set one up *before* you win the client (next section).

## 5.8 Following up

- Instantly: 58% of replies come from the first email, 42% from follow-ups. Belkins: 41% from the first, 59% from follow-ups. Same lesson either way: follow-ups bring a big share. Belkins says 3-5 steps, then switch channel (both read).
- No solid source exists for "80% of sales need 5 follow-ups". Don't use it.
- Sane cadence (suggestion): day 0; day 3-4 with something new; day 8-10 a last "closing the loop"; then try in 60-90 days or on another channel.

## 5.9 Upwork, Fiverr, job boards

- **Upwork:**
  - Freelancer fee **0-15%, variable, since 2025-05-01**, set per contract ([Golance, 2026-06-23](https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-fees-explained-2026), read). Old "20/10/5%" and "flat 10%" are outdated.
  - Connects USD 0.15 each; a proposal costs about 16-32 Connects, so USD 2.40-4.80 ([Snipework](https://snipework.com/blog/upwork-connects-cost-2026), read).
  - **Nigerians pay 7.5% VAT** on Upwork fees and Connects ([Raenest blog](https://www.raenest.com/blog/upwork-fees-for-african-freelancers), read).
  - Withdrawals: see the next section.
  - Upwork's own page lists editors at "USD 6-25/hr typical" (unverified). That's the floor you compete against.
- **Fiverr:**
  - Flat 20% seller fee; earnings held 14 days (7 for top-rated) ([Vaultleap](https://vaultleap.com/blog/fiverr-fees-explained-2026), read).
  - Cut 250 staff (about 30%) on 2025-09-16 to become "AI-first" ([Engadget](https://engadget.com/ai/fiverr-is-laying-off-250-employees-to-become-an-ai-first-company-215730063.html), read). No documented effect on sellers yet.
  - Fiverr Go (Feb 2025): top sellers train AI models on their own work.
- Line: marketplaces are for early reviews and proof, not a career. Test a payout route before the first job.

## 5.10 Loom outreach

- **Loom free:** 25 videos total, 5 minutes each, 720p ([loom.com/pricing](https://www.loom.com/pricing), read).
- **Alternatives:** Cap free is personal use only, so prospecting needs the USD 29/year licence ([cap.so](https://cap.so/pricing), read); Tella from USD 13/month; Vidyard free has 5 videos a month.
- **Does video outreach work?** The "4x replies" figure is Loom's own claim, with no independent study ([FirstSales, 2026-07-10](https://firstsales.io/blog/video-cold-email-loom), read). It only works when it's under about 60s and specific to the person.
- Use: a 60-90s audit of their latest video with 2-3 fixes, for your top 10 prospects only.

## Scams aimed at editors and creators

**No documented campaign of malware disguised as video "project files" sent to editors was found.** These are the real, sourced patterns:

1. **Fake clients with malicious files or links.**
   - A US DOJ case (reported 2026-09-04): 255 fake client accounts sent macro-laced files to about 80,000 freelancers ([Cloaked](https://www.cloaked.com/post/could-you-be-the-next-target-in-a-phishing-campaign-hiding-in-freelance-job-messages), read).
   - A fake client on Contra pushed "project files" from an external link and a fake booking page asking for Google sign-in ([Contra community](https://contra.com/community/76K7NqHS-protect-freelancers-beware-de-cruiz-labs-scam), read).
2. **Fake sponsorship emails to creators (your clients).** Password-protected .rar files containing an .exe named like a contract; the infostealer copies browser cookies and hijacks the channel even with 2FA ([CloudSEK](https://www.cloudsek.com/blog/how-threat-actors-exploit-brand-collaborations-to-target-popular-youtube-channels), [Bitdefender](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/hotforsecurity/how-fake-sponsorship-emails-are-targeting-youtube-creators), [TubeBuddy, 2026](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/youtube-sponsorship-scams-protect-youtube-channel/), read). A hacked client can then send *you* bad links.
3. **Fake editing software and "AI video tools."** Fake CapCut sites, fake AI video generators delivering stealers (reported). Cracked plugins and pirated editors are a likelier way to get infected than client files.
4. **Fake recruiters who make you run a command** ("fix your camera, paste this") (reported, KnowBe4 2026). Never run commands for an interviewer.
5. **Unpaid "test edits"**, edited chat history, and crypto-only payment (Finzar, read).
6. **Overpayment and "pay for software first"** scams (reported). Upwork can't recover money paid off-platform.

**Safe habits:** footage only from the client's own Drive/Dropbox; extensions must be .mp4, .mov, .mxf or .wav. Never open .exe, .scr, .lnk, .bat or .iso files, macro files, or password-protected archives from someone unverified. A 5MB "video" or a double extension is a trap. Use a separate browser profile and passkeys for channels and money accounts. Take a deposit. Never pay to get a job.

## 6.1 How to price

- **Models:** per video (easiest start, scope in writing); per finished minute (common in Nigeria); hourly (punishes you as you get faster); retainer; packages; value-based.
- **Floor rate:** monthly costs (data, power, transport, rent, savings) ÷ about 100 billable hours = never go below ([ProGigFinder, 2026-04-16](https://www.progigfinder.com/blog/how-to-price-your-gig-in-nigeria-a-freelancer-pricing-guide-for-2026), read).
- **Benchmarks** (no proper survey exists; vendors disagree wildly, and that spread is the lesson):
  - Short-form USD 5-25 entry, 25-100 mid ([The Creator's Assistant, 2026-09-25](https://www.thecreatorsassistant.com/youtube-video-editor-cost), read), up to USD 100-500 at agency pricing.
  - Long-form talking head USD 30-150 (budget) to USD 800-1,500 (agency).
  - 10 Shorts in a package USD 250-500.
  - Retainers cut the per-video rate about 20-25%.
- **Nigerian local:** per minute NGN 5,000-15,000 entry, NGN 20,000-60,000 experienced (ProGigFinder, read; no method given).
- Your own path (USD 20 → 30 → 60 a short; USD 250 a video from an agency) sits inside these ranges. That's useful proof that the price rises with proof.

## 6.2 What moves the price

From one compiled guide (The Creator's Assistant, read):
- Messy multicam footage: 2-3x.
- Motion graphics, B-roll, sound design: +50-150%.
- 24-48 hour rush: +25-50%.
- Extra revision round: USD 50-100, or +15-25% beyond two rounds ([whatshouldicharge.biz](https://whatshouldicharge.biz/), read).
- Thumbnail USD 20-50; adapting for another platform USD 15-40.

No numbers were found for retention-based pricing or usage-rights fees; teach those as principles. Put the rush fee and revision price in writing **before** work starts.

## 6.4 Deposits, contracts, not getting scammed

- **Bonsai's free templates** ([creator](https://www.hellobonsai.com/contract-template/freelance-creator), [fixed-price](https://www.hellobonsai.com/contract-template/fixed-price), read):
  - 25% non-refundable deposit at signing; payment due in 15 days; 1.5% monthly late fee.
  - **Ownership transfers only on full payment.**
  - Pay for work done if the client cancels, with 14 days' notice.
  - No revision cap: add one yourself.
  - "Free" is a 7-day trial for the editable version.
- No source supports "50% upfront is the norm". Say "take a deposit; 25-50% is common".
- **Clauses to add** (standard practice, not sourced): scope; two consolidated revision rounds and the price of extras; kill fee; late fee; watermarked previews until paid; final files after payment; client feedback deadlines; who supplies music and footage licences.

Say only "there are new tax rules; check NRS."

## Tax: Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (not tax advice)

- **Solid** ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/capital-gains-tax/1726922/understanding-personal-income-tax-under-the-nigerian-tax-act-2025), [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/nigeria-tax-act-2025-has-been-signed-highlights), [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/?p=1071243), read):
  - Signed 2025-06-26, applies from **2026-01-01**.
  - **Residents taxed on worldwide income.** Resident means a home in Nigeria, substantial economic or family ties, or 183+ days a year.
  - **Bands on taxable income:** first NGN 800,000 at 0%; next NGN 2.2m at 15%; next NGN 9m at 18%; next NGN 13m at 21%; next NGN 25m at 23%; above NGN 50m at 25%.
  - **Reliefs:** rent relief of 20% of rent, up to NGN 500,000; pension and NHF deductible.
  - **Penalties:** not filing costs NGN 100,000 for the first month, then NGN 50,000 a month; not registering NGN 50,000, then NGN 25,000.
  - **Returns are due even when nothing is owed.** The 2026 deadline (for 2025 income) was 31 March. Personal tax is run by the **state** revenue service.
  - Convert foreign income at the official CBN rate on the day received (Cleva, read; no section cited).
- **Unsettled:**
  - Whether foreign **fees** brought in through banks are exempt. Only dividends, interest, rent and royalties are confirmed ([BusinessDay, 2026-06-17](https://businessday.ng/business-economy/article/nigeria-retains-foreign-investment-tax-relief-as-tax-law-clarifies-treatment-of-offshore-earnings/), read). **Assume editing income is taxable.**
  - Whether "NGN 800,000 tax-free" is gross or after deductions (sources differ).
  - Bank reporting thresholds (sources conflict; don't give a number).
- **Bank accounts and tax ID:** accounts must link to a tax ID or NIN; Fidelity said accounts without a TIN keep working ([Gazette NGR, 2026-01-01](https://gazettengr.com/customers-without-tin-can-continue-to-run-their-bank-accounts-fidelity/), read).
- Line: keep every invoice, contract, and USD-to-NGN record; use a separate account for the work; see an accountant once you're earning.

## 7.1-7.2 Working with clients (light touch, as you decided)

- **Revisions:** a round = one consolidated list from one person. Write down the number of rounds, the price of an extra round, and the feedback deadline before you start. Answer scope creep with "happy to; it's outside scope; here's the price and new date" ([Filmmaking Lifestyle, 2026-02-11](https://filmlifestyle.com/how-to-protect-yourself-from-scope-creep-in-video-projects/), read). "Two rounds" is a common template number, not an industry rule.
- **File sharing, free tiers** (read 2026-10-19):
  - Frame.io: 2 members, 2GB, 2 projects; Pro USD 15/member ([frame.io/pricing](https://frame.io/pricing)).
  - WeTransfer: 3GB or 10 transfers per 30 days ([help, 2026-05-30](https://getcomics.wetransfer.com/help-center/subscriptions/plan-limits)). Its July 2025 AI-training wording was withdrawn on 2025-07-15 ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/wetransfer-modifies-terms-service-following-user-backlash-over-ai-rights/)). Don't say they train on your files.
  - Google: 5GB for new accounts from 2026-03-09 (+10GB with phone; checked).
  - Dropbox: 2GB free.
  - Wormhole: up to 10GB, end-to-end encrypted.
- **Nigerian internet:** fixed broadband median 34.5 Mbps down, 25.2 up in H1 2026, but the slowest tenth get about 6 Mbps ([Nairametrics, 2026-08-17](https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/17/nigerias-broadband-speed-jumps-155-as-fibre-connections-triple/), read). Send a compressed review copy, keep masters local, upload big files overnight with a tool that resumes, and use proxies for huge raws.

## 7.3 Testimonials into case studies (your emphasis)

- **Ask at peak happiness**, right after warm feedback. Give 3 questions (the problem before; favourite part; one result since). Offer to draft it from their own words. A 30-second selfie video is strongest. Keep a folder of kind messages ([Freelancing Females, 2026-04-21](https://freelancingfemales.substack.com/p/how-to-ask-for-testimonials-without), read).
- **Case-study structure:** snapshot (one-line result) → client context → challenge → what you did → result with numbers and a quote → lesson ([Artfolio](https://www.artfolio.com/article/structuring-case-studies-inside-your-portfolio-to-solve-real-client-pain-points), read). For editors, add retention charts, views, whether you were the sole editor, and the full video ([Fueler](https://www.fueler.io/blog/what-should-a-video-editing-portfolio-include-complete-guide), read).
- **Teachable skeleton:** Problem (with a number) → What I did (hook, pacing, captions, sound) → Result (retention, views, time saved) + a one-line quote.
- **No numbers allowed (NDA)?** Use ranges or soft results (time saved, consistent posting). Get written permission for each place you'll use it and for sharing metrics.
- **US FTC testimonial rule** (in force 2024-10-21): no fake or bought testimonials; incentives are fine if positivity isn't required ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers), read). A good standard for your own Academy pitch too.
- **Referral ask:** after sign-off and results; never inside the invoice; short; name the work they liked ([Bonsai, 2026-01-08](https://www.hellobonsai.com/blog/how-to-ask-for-referrals), read).
- Line: one finished job = three assets: a quote, a 30-second client video, a one-page case study.

## 9.1-9.3 Growing the income

- **One-off → retainer** ([Filmmaking Lifestyle](https://filmlifestyle.com/how-to-turn-one-off-video-jobs-into-monthly-retainers/), [retainer services](https://filmlifestyle.com/how-to-offer-retainer-video-services/), read):
  - Frame job one as stage one; listen for "we post every week".
  - Propose the retainer in the review after delivery.
  - Sell fixed outputs, turnaround and revision count, not "10 hours a month".
  - Set terms for unused capacity, rush and paused months.
  - Start the renewal talk in month two.
  - The real sell is relief from re-briefing a new editor every time.
- **Real listing:** 12+ short-form videos a month from USD 1,500, 24-hour turnaround, 2 revisions per video ([Contra](https://contra.com/s/qsLFcr6L-monthly-short-form-video-editing-12-videos), read).
- **Packaging:** "one long video becomes 8 shorts, a thumbnail and captions" for one monthly price. Agency tiers seen: 4 clips USD 349, 8 clips USD 699 (unverified). AI clippers undercut per-clip prices, so the human value is choosing the moment, the hook and the brand fit.
- **Thumbnails:** USD 5-30 budget, 30-50 mid, 100-300 premium ([Ad Snipper, 2026-07-01](https://adsnipper.com/?p=2496), read; a vendor). No sourced rates for scripting, strategy or channel management.
- **YouTube Test & Compare** ([Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en), read): test up to 3 titles and/or thumbnails, desktop Studio only, advanced features on; no Shorts or Premieres; **the winner is picked by watch-time share, not click-through rate**; a few days to two weeks. Add-on idea: supply 2-3 thumbnail or title variants and read the result. Don't promise CTR gains.

## Burnout and capacity

- WHO: burn-out is chronic, unmanaged work stress (exhaustion, cynicism, reduced effectiveness) ([WHO](https://www.who.int/news/item/28-05-2019-burn-out-an-occupational-phenomenon-international-classification-of-diseases), read).
- About 25 billable hours a week is a ceiling for most freelancers (blog, no method; [SoloHourly](https://solohourly.com/guides/how-many-billable-hours-per-week-is-realistic), read). No source gives a "clients per editor" number. Your three-client story is the best evidence.

## 10.1 First 30 days

No source gives a week-by-week plan. This skeleton is built from first-client guides ([Fueler](https://www.fueler.io/blog/how-tharun-speaks-teaches-video-editors-to-find-their-first-clients), read):

- **Week 1:** one niche, one offer sentence, 3 spec samples (labelled), one portfolio link, a rate card in NGN and USD, a revision clause.
- **Week 2:** reel under 60s, a before/after post, profile fixed, 50 prospects listed.
- **Week 3:** about 10 personal pitches a day with a free hook re-edit or audit; join 2-3 communities and be useful.
- **Week 4:** follow up on every pitch; offer a small paid test; deliver; ask for a testimonial the day they're happy.

Guides say **30-50 personal pitches before the first paying client**. Don't promise a client in 30 days. This fits your 0.4 homework (skill, client, kind of video, package).

## 10.2 The Academy pitch

**Your audience paid** (29 seats, NGN 145,000 by 2026-10-16), so it's warm. The benchmarks are B2B webinar vendors' with no African data:

- **Show-up:** 47.7% of registrants attend live; half of registrations come in the final week and 15% on the day ([Livestorm 2026](https://livestorm.co/webinar-benchmark-report-2026), read; internal inconsistencies). Paid seats usually show up better, but no source measures that.
- **Buying:**
  - Cold audiences: 2-5% of attendees buy; warm, dedicated audiences 10-15% ([Learnybox, 2026-07-16](https://learnybox.com/en/blog/webinar-conversion-rate/), read).
  - 5-15% "solid" for a course ([Ruzuku](https://www.ruzuku.com/learn/glossary/webinar-funnel), read).
  - Replay viewers convert at 60-70% of live attendees if followed up ([ClickMeeting](https://blog.clickmeeting.com/?p=33384), read; partly unsourced).
  - Rough arithmetic for 29-100 attendees at 5-15%: 1-15 buyers. Plan with the low end.
- **What makes it convert without feeling salesy:**
  - Give real value; the offer is "the next logical step".
  - Teach the what and why fully; keep the hands-on, feedback and accountability for the Academy. Don't bait-and-switch.
  - Bridge from the last lesson into the offer; don't stop and switch modes.
  - Show one real student or client result (the 7.3 structure).
  - Take objections in Q&A and restate the call to action every few answers. *"The people who stay for Q&A are your buyers"* (Amy Porterfield, dated 2015).
  - Polls early, then tailor the pitch to the answers.
- **Timing:** guides put the pitch in the last 10-15 minutes of a 60-minute webinar. No source studied 2½ hours; scale it, don't copy it.
- **Deadlines, honestly:** a real deadline you'll honour; no countdown that resets. Bonuses tied to the outcome (templates, a review call). Replay watchers get the **same** deadline. Clear price, a guarantee if you offer one, payment options. Fake urgency is a dark pattern regulators name (FTC 2022).
- **Follow-up:** three emails over 3-5 days (replay, objections, final call) (Ruzuku). Personal messages within hours to people who showed intent (ClickMeeting).
