**2026-11-01 (day 1) to 2026-12-30 (day 60).** Samuel's own challenge for his life, not the Called to Edit Community's 60-day challenge (which also starts 2026-11-01) and wider than his 60-day posting challenge ([[03-Areas/personal-brand/personal-brand-decisions|personal brand decisions]], 2026-10-29), which it includes.

## What he committed to (his list)

1. Prayer and Bible study every morning.
2. Church every Sunday.
3. Called to Edit Community meetings, Sundays and Wednesdays at 8:00pm.
4. Fasting Wednesdays and Fridays, broken at 2:00pm or 6:00pm.
5. Gym Monday, Tuesday and Thursday.
6. Batch-create content on Monday or Wednesday.
7. Finish all major tasks on TickTick before the day ends.
8. Post content every day.
9. Post a gratitude picture every morning.
10. No masturbation at all.
11. No doom scrolling: social media discipline.
12. Good rest and sleep.
13. He plans his own day on TickTick the night before. *"Sometimes I would tell you to do it or add something to my TikTik for me, sometimes I would do it myself"*.
