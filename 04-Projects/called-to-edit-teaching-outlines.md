## Part 1: the webinar, 2 hours

**Shape:** one story, one framework, eight stages, one action plan, one offer. Each stage runs the same way, borrowed from the old course's [[03-Areas/academy/called-to-edit-beginners/teaching-rules|teaching rules]]: **the claim** (one sentence) → **the principle** → **his proof or story** → **the trap** beginners fall into → **the action** they take this week.

**The framework they screenshot: the Called to Edit Pathway**
`Learn → Niche → Tools → Brand → Clients → Price → Network → Grow`

**Where the time is tight:** his list is bigger than 2 hours. This draft keeps stages 1–6 full and makes 7 and most of 8 headlines, with the detail in the Starter Kit. His call which parts get cut, and whether the webinar runs 2 hours or 2½.

**Slides:** ~35. One idea per slide, his footage and screenshots as proof, the Pathway slide repeated at the start of every stage so people know where they are.

**Engagement:** a chat prompt every ~10 minutes; one poll (*"phone or laptop?"*); name people who answer.

**Gaps only he can fill** (needed before slides, 10-19):
- The order he learned things in, and the first thing he'd learn again.
- His niche list and his view of each.
- What he actually paid for to start, and what he'd buy first now.
- How he got his first paying client, and how he got Route Rise.
- A real DM or pitch he sent (or received) to take apart.
- How he prices now, and what he charged at the start.
- What "work for free first" looked like for him.

## Part 2: Called to Edit Academy, 6 weeks × 2 classes (Editor)

**For:** people already on DaVinci Resolve (free version covers most of it; every Studio-only move gets a free route, his decision). **Shape of every class:** last assignment reviewed (20 min) → the principle → live demo, narrating the decision, not the click → the trap → this week's assignment. **Every assignment is a portfolio piece**, so the career half of the webinar keeps working. Classes recorded, on Google Meet (his decisions).

| Week | Class A | Class B | Assignment (portfolio piece) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Foundations and the cut** | Resolve set up right: project settings, media management, proxies and optimized media for weak computers, his keyboard workflow | The cut: pacing, J and L cuts, cutting a talking head clean (his waveform-first method, [[03-Areas/video-editing/sops/routerise-cut-workflow|cut workflow]]) | Cut a 60s talking head from supplied raw footage |
| **2. Story and short form** | Structure and retention: the hook, B-roll that matches the feeling, not the words | Text and captions: subtitles, typography on screen | A captioned short-form edit |
| **3. Sound** | Fairlight: dialogue clean-up, EQ, compression, loudness for each platform, music and ducking | Sound design: layering SFX, whooshes, risers, using his SFX pack | Full sound pass on a supplied edit |
| **4. Fusion** | Thinking in nodes ([[03-Areas/video-editing/fusion-node-system|Fusion node system]]): Text+, Merge, Transform, keyframes and easing | Motion graphics: animated titles, lower thirds, callouts; his macros and tools | An animated title and lower third |
| **5. Animation and colour** | Types of animation: kinetic type, shapes and UI, punch-ins, tracking, masking, animated screen recordings. [[03-Areas/video-editing/motion-gallery/motion-gallery|Motion gallery]] as inspiration | A little colour: white balance, primaries, matching shots, a simple look, LUTs | A 30–60s ad-style piece with motion and colour |
| **6. Finish and get paid** | Delivery: render settings per platform, client handover, revisions, archiving projects | Final project review, graduation, career next steps (portfolio, pricing refresher) | Final project: a full edit for their portfolio |

**What students get** (his list + suggestions): his assets, some of his SFX, some of his tools (his) · class recordings · raw footage packs · a certificate (his) · a cohort WhatsApp group · *suggested:* one office-hours call a fortnight.

**Hard rule:** no Route Rise or client footage goes to students. Practice footage comes from his own shoots or royalty-free packs.

**Logistics to settle:** Google Classroom (free on a personal Google account) handles assignments, hand-ins and recordings in one place, and Meet links inside it. A shared Drive folder is the simpler fallback. Certificate criteria: e.g. attend most classes and submit the final project. His call.

**Prep load for him:** 12 classes. If each class needs ~2 hours of prep (slides, project files, footage), that's ~24 hours before or during the cohort. Building weeks 1–2 before the start date and the rest a week ahead keeps it inside his evenings.
