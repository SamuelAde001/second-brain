## Questions for Samuel (2026-10-03)

Asked in chat on 2026-10-03; answers recorded here as they come. Answer by number, skip any.

**The webinar**
1. What hour on Sat 10-24?
2. Strict 2 hours, or 2½ with Q&A?
3. Which parts of your list get only a headline (draft: networking, agency, coaching)?
4. The Starter Kit bonus: yes or no?
5. Refunds: none after the webinar, or another policy?
6. Do you want a co-speaker or guest (a client, another editor) for 10 minutes, or just you?

**Selling**
7. Paystack, Selar, or Paystack with Selar as backup? Is your Paystack business already registered, or starting from zero?
8. Partners get a cut: yes or no, and how much a seat?
9. Can it be promoted inside HighSignals Community and your other HighSignals channels?
10. Will you send the top-15 partner messages yourself?
11. Is registration open from Mon 10-05, or earlier (tomorrow)?
12. Do sales close at a set time (e.g. Sat noon), or stay open until it starts?
13. Free warm-up live mid-campaign: yes or no, IG Live or X Space?

**Team**
14. What has Ifeoma already agreed to, and how many hours a week can she give?
15. Is the PM also the co-host on the day, or someone else?
16. Do you already know anyone for designer, video editor, admin or social? Names?
17. The intern call-out: post it publicly, or ask people you know first?
18. How long does the internship run: to 10-24, or to the end of the cohort?
19. Who approves posts when you're on client work: you, or Ifeoma inside your brand rules?

**Your time**
20. Do you accept the PA's October time plan (afternoons until 10-10, afternoons and evenings 10-12 → 10-23)?
21. Which two evenings can be shoot nights for the batch recording?
22. Move the outline from 10-12 to this week: yes or no?

**Proof and content**
23. Which client pieces can go in the showreel, and is there anything Route Rise wouldn't want public?
24. Who can give you a testimonial (mentees, people you've helped)?
25. Ep 4 of Life of a Video Editor: keep its schedule with a webinar pointer, or pause the series until 10-24?

**Money**
26. What can you spend before seats sell, if anything (designer tools, printing, data, fuel)? The Money man checks it against October.
27. If 10-17 is missed by 20+ seats, will you put money into a boost?
28. The team's percentage: what figure, and of what (webinar, cohort, both; gross or after fees)?

**The Academy**
29. Start date, and which two days and times for classes?
30. Is 6 weeks firm?
31. Payment plan (e.g. 2 × NGN 25,000), or full payment only?
32. Sales deadline for the cohort, and a seat cap if you want one?
33. Google Classroom or a Drive folder for assignments?
34. What exactly goes in the asset pack: which SFX, which tools and macros?
35. What earns the certificate?
