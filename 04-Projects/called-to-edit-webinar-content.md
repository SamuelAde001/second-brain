# Called to Edit webinar: what it covers

Part of [[04-Projects/called-to-edit-webinar-and-academy|Called to Edit webinar → Academy]]. Area: [[03-Areas/personal-brand/personal-brand|Personal brand]].

**Raw material, not the outline.** This is everything Samuel said on 2026-09-30 that he wants the webinar to cover, grouped by the General Manager into sections but otherwise as he said it. He'll add his own research, and the outline gets built from this in a later session (step 4 of the plan, Mon 2026-10-12, or earlier).

**Theme:** how to start a career as a video editor.
**For:** anyone, whatever they edit on: phone or laptop, CapCut or anything else.
**Not:** *"This is not a video editing tutorial"*. It's *"the steps, the pathway to become a professional video editor that earns money from video editing"*.
**Where it comes from:** *"these are the things off my head that helped me as a video editor"*.

## 1. Learning the skill

- How to learn video editing properly when you're starting and don't know what to learn first.
- Where to learn: tutorials.
- Where to get assets and templates, including free templates.

## 2. Choosing a niche

- The different niches in video editing, with their pros and cons.
- How to choose one.

## 3. Tools and money to start

- The different editing tools, with their pros and cons.
- How much money you actually need to start.
- The computer you need, and how powerful, depending on the niche.
- The decisions an editor has to make, and what should drive them.

## 4. Establishing yourself

- Setting up a profile page and a new Twitter account.
- Which platforms suit which kind of editor: Instagram favours some types, YouTube others, Twitter and LinkedIn others, TikTok others.
- Building a personal brand as a video editor.
- Where to build a portfolio, and how to present yourself.
- Presenting yourself professionally; packaging your skill.

## 5. Getting clients

- Getting clients to come to you (inbound).
- Reaching out to clients: where to find them, which platforms.
- Cold outreach, cold DMs, emails.
- Value-first outreach, including editing people's videos for them for free.
- How to pitch yourself and show your value in people's eyes.
- **Working for Nigerians vs working for people abroad.** Many people who reach out to him want to work abroad. He wants to tell them they have to learn to work for free first, and how to.

## 6. Pricing

- How to price yourself.
- What makes your price go up or down.

## 7. Networking

- Networking with other video editors.
- Joining communities of video editors.

## 8. Career paths and extra income

- **Freelancing vs recurring clients:** one gig at a time vs retained clients, and how to get recurring ones.
- Packaging several gigs into one deal.
- **Add-on services** that raise what you earn: content strategy, thumbnail design, scripting.
- **Agency:** mentioned as possible, not taught. *"I'm not going to cover those, but I'm going to tell them that, okay, Agency is something that is possible."*
- **Coaching / teaching editing** once you're good: attracting other editors, which can also attract clients.
- Showing your work.

## 9. The close

- The upsell into [[04-Projects/called-to-edit-academy-cohort|Called to Edit Academy]].
