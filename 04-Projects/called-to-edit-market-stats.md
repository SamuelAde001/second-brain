# Called to Edit: market statistics for topic 0.3

The numbers for **0.3 "The truth about the market"** in [[04-Projects/called-to-edit-webinar-teachings|the teachings note]], for Samuel's Canva whiteboard. Part of [[04-Projects/called-to-edit-webinar-and-academy|the project]] · [[03-Areas/personal-brand/personal-brand|Personal brand]].

Gathered 2026-10-19 by the Brand manager. Nothing is rounded up or invented; every number is as printed at the source. Samuel asked for stats instead of "a lot" (*"billions of users"*).

**How to read the Checked column**
- **Read** = I opened the source page and read the number there.
- **Reported** = I read a news report that quotes the original source. The original page was blocked or not reachable.
- **Not seen** = only a search-result summary showed it. Not on the board until someone opens the link.

**Dated** = older than 2024. Say "as of 2019", never "right now".
