# Called to Edit: the playbook (brainstorm)

Part of [[04-Projects/called-to-edit-webinar-and-academy|Called to Edit webinar → Academy]]. Area: [[03-Areas/personal-brand/personal-brand|Personal brand]]. Teaching side: [[04-Projects/called-to-edit-teaching-outlines|Teaching outlines]].

**Everything here is a proposal**, written 2026-10-03 by the General Manager, Brand manager and Editor at Samuel's ask: *"brain storm everything for the event, from publicity, marketing, setting up, videos I need to create, the whole teaching outline and everything needed for this to be a success"*. ## 1. The maths that drives everything

Targets (his): 200 paid at NGN 5,000, 50 in the cohort at NGN 50,000. His own audience is ~700 per platform, so **the campaign is an outreach operation first and a content operation second.**

**Where 200 seats could come from** (a planning split to steer by, not a forecast):

| Source | Seats | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| Partners (editors, creators, community owners who share it) | 80 | 15–20 partners live, each bringing 4–6 |
| Personal DMs and WhatsApp (warm and cold) | 60 | ~600 personal conversations if ~1 in 10 buys. The real rate shows after week 1 |
| Communities (WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, X, campus, church) | 40 | 40+ groups posted in, with a person answering replies |
| His own followers (IG, TikTok, status) | 20 | Daily stories, 3+ reels a week |

**Checkpoints** (paid seats, cumulative): Sat 10-10 → 30 · Sat 10-17 → 90 · Wed 10-21 → 150 · Fri 10-23 → 200. Sales bunch up in the last 72 hours, so a slow week 1 is normal; a slow week 2 is the alarm.

**If a checkpoint is missed**, in this order: more partners → a free live warm-up → more DMs per day → a small paid boost on the best reel (Money man first, `money-check`).

**Meet cap:** 100 on Google One AI Pro (his plan). Decide on Workspace at **80 paid**, not 101. Until then, *"100 seats"* is honest scarcity and can be said out loud.

## 2. The offer, sharpened

- **Name:** Called to Edit.
- **One-line promise** (options, his pick): *"From 'I can edit' to 'I get paid to edit.'"* · *"The full pathway to your first paying editing client, on any device."* · *"Not a tutorial. The career."*
- **Who it's for:** anyone who edits or wants to, on phone or laptop, CapCut or Resolve or anything, who isn't earning from it yet, or is earning small and wants more (foreign clients, retainers).
- **Who it's not for:** people looking for a software tutorial.
- **What they get:** 2 hours live with him · the replay (his decision) · live Q&A · **suggested bonus: the Called to Edit Starter Kit** (a PDF: free places to learn, free assets and templates, the niche table, a bio formula, 3 outreach DM templates, a pricing worksheet, a 30-day action plan). Cheap to make, it carries the parts the 2 hours can't fit, and it's a reason to pay now.
- **No early-bird price** (his decision). A non-price push that respects it: *"First 50 seats get the Starter Kit a week early"*, or *"bring a friend: both get a 1-minute portfolio review in the Q&A"*. Suggestion.
- **FAQ the admin answers from** (objections → answers):
  - *"I only edit on my phone."* → It's built for any device; part of it is what phone editors should do first.
  - *"NGN 5,000 is much."* → One small client job pays it back. (Never promise income.)
  - *"I'm a total beginner."* → It starts from zero.
  - *"I can't make that time."* → Every payer gets the replay.
  - *"Is it a tutorial?"* → No. That's the Academy.
  - *"Refunds?"* → Needs a policy line before launch. Suggestion: no refunds after the webinar, replay instead.

## 5. Publicity and marketing (Brand manager)

### Partners — the biggest lever
- Build a list of **50** this week: Nigerian editors and creators with bigger audiences, editing-community owners, tech and creative community leads, campus media clubs, church media teams, people who already know his work.
- **Offer them a cut** (needs his yes and the Money man): e.g. NGN 1,000 a seat (20%) through their own coupon code or payment page. Selar does this natively.
- Give each a **share pack**: a 15-second vertical clip of him, a flyer, two captions they can copy, their link.
- The ask is short and personal, from him, not the admin, for the top 15.

### Communities
- WhatsApp and Telegram groups for editors, creatives, freelancers, tech/job seekers · Facebook groups for Nigerian creatives and editors · campus groups (students are the core "start a career" buyer) · church youth and media groups (his Called to Create audience) · X creative community · LinkedIn for the B2B angle.
- Rule: post with value first (a tip or a carousel), the link second. Ask the admin before posting where rules forbid promo.
- **HighSignals Community:** whether the personal brand can be promoted there is his call (brand boundary). Asked below.

### DMs and warm outreach
- His WhatsApp contacts, everyone who ever asked him "how do I start editing", the 3 mentees, past clients' teams who edit, old course interest.
- DM shape: personal line → why them → what it is in one line → the link → *"want me to send the agenda?"*. Never a copy-paste blast to strangers in his name.

### His own channels
- **Stories every day** from 10-05: countdown, seats taken, BTS of preparing, questions answered, payer shout-outs (with permission).
- **WhatsApp status every day.**
- **Pinned post** on IG and TikTok: the announcement.
- **Link in bio** everywhere.

### A free warm-up live (mid-campaign)
- **Wed 10-14 or Thu 10-15, 30 minutes**, IG Live or X Space: *"3 reasons you can edit and still have no clients"*. Free value, ends with the webinar link. Live viewers convert better than reel viewers. Suggestion.

### In person
- **SMFest, Sat 10-17:** A5 flyers with a QR code to the payment page, and him telling people directly. One afternoon of in-person reach.

### Ads
- No budget (his decision). Trigger to revisit: missing the 10-17 checkpoint by more than 20 seats. Then a boost on the best-performing reel, through `money-check`.

### Message angles (hooks, his voice to rewrite)
1. *"You can edit. So why has nobody paid you?"*
2. *"The worst mistake you can make is picking a niche."* (his own hook, [[brand-context]]) → turned into the niche reel.
3. *"Foreign clients won't hire you. Here's what they hire."* (his "work for free first" point)
4. *"How much money do you actually need to start editing? Less than you think, and more than zero."*
5. *"I went from the military to editing for a foreign company, from Abuja."* (the proof story)
6. *"Stop watching tutorials. Start doing this."*
7. *"This is not a video editing tutorial."* (the announcement line)

Rules from [[brand-context]] apply: no guru tone, no misleading hook, no income promises, proof over claims, brand after value.

### Proof to collect now (admin)
- Route Rise and past client work he has the right to show (his words: *"I have the rights to do so"*). A 30–45s showreel.
- Screenshots of people asking him how to start (names hidden).
- Short selfie testimonials from anyone he has helped (mentees, people from his content).
