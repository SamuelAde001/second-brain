## 1. Hero

**Subhead:** Called to Edit is a live webinar with Samuel Adebayo, a working video editor with 5 years of experience, on how to start and build a career as a video editor and earn from it, wherever you are. Any device. Any app.

**Facts bar:** Friday 23 October · 7:00pm WAT · Live on Google Meet · NGN 5,000

**Button:** Get my seat

## 2. This is for you if

- You want to start video editing but you don't know where to begin or what to learn first.
- You're already learning, but you don't know how to get clients or get people to pay you.
- You edit on a phone, a laptop or CapCut. It doesn't matter.

**Not for you if:** you want a button-by-button editing tutorial. This is the path, not the software lesson.

## 3. Where most editors get stuck

You've watched the tutorials. You can cut a video. But nobody is paying you, you don't know which niche to pick, you don't know what to charge, and you don't know how to message a client without looking desperate.

Skill isn't the whole problem. Nobody told you the business side.

## 4. What you'll leave with

Seven things, one for each part of the path (his webinar sections, as outcomes):

1. **A learning plan:** what to learn first, where to learn it, where to get free assets and templates.
2. **Your niche:** the niches in editing, what each is like, and how to choose.
3. **The tools and the money:** what you really need to start, and the computer for your niche.
4. **A profile clients can trust:** how to set up your pages and portfolio and present yourself professionally.
5. **How to get clients:** inbound, cold DMs and emails, and how to pitch your value, including working for free the right way, especially for clients abroad.
6. **How to price yourself:** what moves your price up or down.
7. **Where it can go:** recurring clients, add-on services, and coaching other editors.

You'll finish with a plan: *I know what to do next.*
