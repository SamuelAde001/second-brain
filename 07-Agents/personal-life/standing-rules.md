# PA — standing rules

**What Samuel has settled about his day, his habits and his commitments, as they stand now.** One line each, dated, in his words where he gave them. The morning brief, the night plan and the weekly review read this file **first**, before anything else.

Why it exists. Samuel, 2026-09-25: *"they forget things that I have settled before on another chat"*. On 2026-09-24 the night plan asked him about "3 videos" after he had already settled "one video" in the morning. The rule was only in a replan note, not anywhere the routine looked.

**How it's kept:**
- **Any session, any agent, any chat:** when Samuel settles something about his schedule, habits, must-dos or commitments, update this file in that session, commit and push. A cloud routine only sees what has been pushed.
- This file holds the **current** rule. When a rule changes, edit its line and add the date. The history is in [[07-Agents/personal-life/memory|memory]] and [[07-Agents/personal-life/log|the log]], which stay append-only.
- If a rule here clashes with an older line in the profile, a skill or memory, **this file wins**. Fix the other file in the same session.
- Only what he said. Nothing here is a suggestion (AGENTS.md rule 7).

## His day

- 7:00am–1:00pm client work · 1:00pm meal · 2:00pm nap · 3:00–4:30pm content · 5:00pm gym (on gym days) · ~6:30pm dinner · 7:00–9:00pm content, or client work in a deadline week · 9:00–10:00pm call with his girlfriend. (2026-09-22)
- Nothing after 10:00pm. Sunday is planned from 3:00pm only. (2026-09-22)
- **On a client day, client work owns the whole morning. No content before 1:00pm.** *"when there is client video, it takes top priority of mornings, no content in the morning"* (2026-09-24). Make-ups that aren't client work go in the first block after the nap.

## Planning

- **Everything planned is a timed TickTick task** with a start and an end, so it shows on his TickTick calendar. Reminders at the start and 5 minutes before. Routine blocks too (nap, gym, dinner, the call). *"Always put what ever is planned in a schedule so I can see it on my tiktik calender, don't schedule on google cal"* (2026-09-23)
- **Google Calendar is read only.** Nothing is written there, meetings included. (2026-09-23)
- **TickTick is the record of what got done.** A ticked task is done, so don't ask about it. If he says something is done and it isn't ticked, tick it. (2026-09-22: *"It can do everything on ticktick"*)
- **At most three must-dos a day**, priority 5. A missed must-do becomes the next day's must-do #1. (2026-09-22)
- A plan goes into TickTick only after he answers. No answer, nothing written. (2026-09-22)

## Content

- **The next episode waits until the client edit is delivered.** *"we would do the next one another day after my clients edit is done"* (2026-09-24)
- Daily posting is reported on Sundays, not held as a commitment. (2026-09-22)

## How the reports must read (2026-09-25)

Samuel: *"it sends report not well formatted for me to understand like it's talking to itself"* · *"it should list the tasks for the day, not just the calender, but the main task for the day"*.

- Write to him, not to yourself. No internal terms: not "escalation level", "make-up rule", "cap", "P5", "unknown, not counted", "KEPT/MISS". Say what happened in plain words.
- Short headed sections, one item per line, a blank line between sections. Never one run-on paragraph. His phone shows markdown, and lines without a bullet or a blank line between them run together.
- **The main tasks for the day come first**, from TickTick, with their times. Then the rest of the schedule.
- Questions go at the end, numbered, so he can answer "1 yes, 2 no".
