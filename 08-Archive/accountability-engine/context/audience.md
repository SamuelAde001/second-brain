# Audience

Content, course, community, mentorship, Scripnals — everything audience-facing.

## SMART goal

**One high-quality video published Wednesday 7:00pm WAT, every week, to Instagram
and TikTok (same video, both platforms).**

Stated target: **5,000 followers by end of year.**

**5,000 is an AMBITION, not a SMART goal.** 460 → 5,000 on Instagram in four months
is roughly 11x. At one post a week that is very unlikely, and it fails the R in
SMART. His goal stays in his file — but the SMART version underneath it is the one
that gets scored:

> **18 consecutive Wednesdays without a miss, between now and 31 December.**

That is the thing he controls. Followers are the thing he does not.

## The one lever

**Depth over volume. The cadence is the point, not the count.** His own reading of
his own posted content: reach follows focus, not frequency.

## Weekly minimum

One video published, **on the day, at the time**. Thursday is a missed week.

## Cost of breaking it

No YouTube for 2 days (`context/stakes.md`). **Missing the TIME counts as missing
the week.** Self-enforced, therefore currently unenforced — see stakes.md.

## How it is verified

The post exists at the URL by 7:00pm Wednesday. Not "it's rendering", not "it's
uploading". Live, or missed.
