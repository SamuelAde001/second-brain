# Things to buy

Opened 2026-09-02 at Samuel's request. Two lists, and the split matters.

**BUYING THIS MONTH** is money already inside a budget line. Nothing here is extra
spending — it is the September plan written as objects instead of categories, so
he can see what the Feeding and Household lines actually turn into.

**THE WISH LIST** is what gets bought when a month closes with spare cash and every
pot is funded. It is not a plan and it has no date. Its whole job is to exist in
writing, so that spare cash meets a decided list instead of an impulse — which is
how ₦595,250 disappeared June–August.

**The rule for the wish list:** nothing moves off it until the month's bills, the
one-offs, Cowrywise, the Buffer and Goal 1 are all paid in full. A wish bought out
of Goal 1 is the house move slipping, and it will not be recorded as anything else.

**Ten of eleven items have no price on them.** That makes the list unplannable — a

convenient; nothing here is urgent.

hangers are probably a few thousand each. If September really closes with ₦58,027
free, several of those clear at once without touching a single pot. The PS5, the
guitar and GTA 6 are a different order of money and belong to a month that has not
happened yet.

## How something moves off this list

1. The month closes. Bills paid, one-offs paid, Cowrywise, Buffer and Goal 1 all funded.
2. The surplus is real — the ledger says so, not the plan.
3. The item has a price on it.
4. It gets logged in `context/money-ledger.md` like any other spend, and named here.

Anything that skips step 1 is not a purchase, it is a raid.
