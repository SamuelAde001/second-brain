# The constraint chain

Written 2026-09-20, after the video-editing, finances and personal-brand interviews. It is the one thing in the Brain that spans every area, and it explains why the areas keep making the same trade.

## The chain

```
  client editing hours
          │ consume
          ▼
       his time
          │ which limits
          ▼
    content cadence  ──────►  audience  ──────►  the course
   (1 video/week now)          (671 IG)        (paused, unbuilt)
                                                     │ which is meant to fund
                                                     ▼
                                          the only income line he controls
```

Every link is documented, and none of it is inference:

1. **Client editing owns his time.** *"It solves income, but it's my biggest time bottleneck"* ([[brand-context]]). The content production block was moved from morning to afternoon in August for exactly this reason — client edits own his mornings almost every day.
2. **Time limits cadence.** Asked why he posts once a week, 2026-09-20: *"that cadence would improve once I speed up my video editing for my client using Claude."*
3. **Cadence drives the audience.** The one series he shipped twice — [[03-Areas/personal-brand/series/life-of-a-video-editor|The Life of a Video Editor]] — moved Instagram 460 → 671 in about 25 days.
4. **The audience is the course's market**, and the course is *"the only income line whose existence he controls"* ([[03-Areas/finances/income|Income]]). Route Rise sets his volume and his rate and does not tell him the volume in advance.
5. **Which loops back:** the course is meant to carry roughly NGN 42,530/month from January 2027. It is paused for lack of time — time taken by client editing.
   - *Update 2026-09-22:* Samuel has handed that job to **mentorship** ([[03-Areas/mentorship/mentorship|Mentorship]]). The chain doesn't change: mentorship is also Samuel's own hours, and also waits on demand.

## What that makes the editing agent

Not a convenience. **[[03-Areas/video-editing/agent-plan|The video-editing agent]] is the intervention at the top of the chain** — the only one that adds hours rather than reallocating them. Samuel already said it plainly, before this chain was written down:

> "I need to free up time to be able to start my own brand and build my own business."

His target for it is **60–80% of the editing work**. He also called it *"my most important agent in this whole second brain."* The chain says he is right, and says why: everything else in the system is downstream of the hours it would return.

## What it means for planning

- **Any plan that adds work without removing client hours is borrowing against the same constraint.** That is how three audience-facing commitments died in ten days in early September.
- **The order matters.** Editing agent → hours → cadence → audience → course. Starting at the course end, with no hours freed, is what produced the 16 October beta that never happened.
- **The failure mode is documented and recurring:** the thing with no external deadline is always the thing that gets cut. The client edit has a deadline; the course does not.

## The honest caveat — updated 2026-09-21

This chain assumes the editing agent actually works. **Step 6 — turning visuals into editable Fusion comps on a real timeline — is no longer pure hypothesis:** on 2026-09-21 a house-style card was built programmatically as Fusion nodes on a duplicated timeline via the Resolve MCP and rendered over the footage ([[07-Agents/video-editor/profile|the agent]], [[07-Agents/video-editor/memory|its memory]]). That proves the *bridge*, not the *product* — one hand-specced card is not an approved storyboard rebuilt at the density of his own edit, and the shine layer (Fusion Instances + Neo* macros) is still unproven over the bridge. **Until the full pipeline runs on one real video end to end, the chain is a hypothesis with good evidence and one proven link, not yet a plan.**
