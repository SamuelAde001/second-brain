# Video editing niche rates, 2026

Web research for Samuel's request (2026-10-30: *"Research the top 30 niches in video editing and how much they pay on average for shorts and for longform, and drop the list on discord general chat, tag everyone"*). Posted to #general-chat on the Called to Edit Community Discord with @everyone, 2026-10-30, as three messages (below, verbatim).

**Reliability.** Almost no source publishes rates by niche. ★ rows carry a figure published for that niche; the rest use the general 2026 band (shorts USD 50–150 a clip, long USD 300–1,500 a video). All figures are USD asking prices from agency and freelancer blogs, Upwork and Twine, mostly US/global clients. Not a survey.

Sources: pixflow.net/blog/freelance-video-editing-rates · thecreatorsassistant.com/youtube-video-editor-cost · increditors.com (podcast, pricing survey) · markstudios.com/blog/documentary-editing-cost-2026 · samfusion.com/faceless-youtube-channel-editing · tryvibeworker.com/blog/best-upwork-niches-for-video-editors-2026 · clipmasters.io/video-editing-rates · twine.net/find/video-editors/with/music-video · upwork.com (real estate listing, USD 50/video) · ruahcreativehouse.org (church, editing rates) · vidico.com/news/video-editor-cost · freelancinginst.com (gaming, INR).

## The post

@everyone 🎬 **30 video editing niches and what they pay (2026, USD)**

How to read it: **Shorts** = per short clip (Reel/TikTok/Short). **Long** = per long video or project. ★ = a figure published for that niche. No ★ = nobody publishes niche rates for it, so it shows the general 2026 band: **Shorts $50–150 per clip, Long $300–1,500 per video**. Price climbs with skill, turnaround and how much the client's business makes.

**Creator / YouTube**
1. **Podcasts** ★ — Shorts $10–40 per clip · Long $100–400 per episode (agencies $775–1,750)
2. **Documentary-style YouTube** ★ — Long $300–1,500 · full documentaries $3,000–15,000+
3. **Faceless channels** ★ — Long $50–150 compilations, $150–400 motion-graphics explainers, $400+ doc-grade
4. **Talking-head YouTube** ★ — Long $150–400 basic 10-min edit, $800–1,500+ highly produced
5. **High-retention / big-creator YouTube** ★ — Long $500–2,500 per video
6. **Finance & money** — general band; sources say these channels have more budget than lifestyle
7. **Business & entrepreneurship** — general band, same budget note as finance
8. **Tech reviews** — general band, same budget note
9. **Gaming** — general band; one India guide puts it at ₹3,000–15,000 per video
10. **Education & tutorials** — general band

11. **Online courses** ★ — Long $200–1,000 per module (Upwork median job budget $200)
12. **Coaches & personal brands** — general band; monthly reel packages run $800–1,500
13. **Fitness** — general band
14. **Beauty & fashion** — general band
15. **Travel vlogs** — general band
16. **Food & cooking** — general band
17. **Commentary & news** — general band
18. **True crime & storytelling** — doc-style rates apply ($300–1,500 long)
19. **Sports highlights** — general band

**Business / brand clients**
20. **Ads & performance creative** ★ — Shorts $250–1,200 per ad creative (Upwork median ad job $100). Sources rank it top for per-project income
21. **UGC ads** ★ — same band as ad creative: $250–1,200 per ad
22. **Corporate** ★ — Shorts $50–400 per social clip · Long $400–2,000 per project
23. **Testimonials & case studies** ★ — Long $1,000–5,000 all-in (with the shoot)
24. **Explainer & animation** ★ — Long $3,000–15,000+ all-in; motion graphics editing $50–200+ per finished minute
25. **Real estate** ★ — Shorts ~$50 per property reel (Upwork listing) · Long $300–1,500 per listing video (shoot + edit)
26. **SaaS & product demos** — corporate band ($400–2,000 long)
27. **E-commerce product videos** — ad band for short ($250–1,200), general band otherwise

**Events / music / church**
28. **Weddings** ★ — Long $800–3,500 per wedding film (Upwork median $250; jobs rarely repeat)
29. **Events** ★ — Long $500–2,500 per highlight edit
30. **Church & sermons** ★ — Shorts $50–400 per sermon clip · $500–1,500 a month on retainer
➕ **Music videos** ★ — $20–150 an hour by level (no per-video figure published)

**What the numbers say**
• Shorts pay least per piece; money comes from volume and monthly retainers (e.g. 4 long + ~20 shorts = $2,500–3,000 a month on one published sheet).
• Retainer niches: podcasts, churches, coaches, YouTube channels. One-off niches: weddings, events, music videos.
• The highest rates sit where the client's video makes them money: ads, finance/business, courses, corporate.

⚠️ These are 2026 asking prices from agency and freelancer guides, Upwork and Twine, mostly for US/global clients. Treat them as a range to pitch in, not a promise.
