# Teachings archive

Everything Samuel teaches about building a career as a video editor and getting paid, in one place, in his own words. Area: [[03-Areas/called-to-edit/called-to-edit|Called to Edit]].

## What it's for

- **Content.** Every teaching here is a video waiting to be made. Ask the Brand manager for educational video ideas from it ("Brand manager, give me 5 educational video ideas from the pricing teachings"). It pitches from what's written here, quoting his own lines, and never invents a teaching he didn't give.
- **Editors Bot.** The bot in the Called to Edit Community answers from these notes. `community_admin.py knowledge` rebuilds its knowledge from this folder on every site deploy.
- **The Academy, the book, the workbook and future webinars.** Start here instead of from a blank page.

## Adding to it

**Community calls file themselves** the same night (2026-10-28): the recorder transcribes each main call and the Brand manager adds what's new to the topic notes, with a call note in `calls/` ([[00-System/automations/meeting-recorder|meeting recorder]]).

Anything new he teaches (community calls, Academy classes, a voice note, a post that lands) goes into the topic note it belongs to, marked with the date and the source. One teaching per heading. His words stay his: record what he said, never fill a gap with a guess (AGENTS.md §5 rule 6). New topic, new note: `teachings-13-…`, then a line in the list above.
