## Questions asked

- *Most of the people who see my posts on X are video editors. Will that content attract clients?* [0:56:02] → Clients and agencies scout for talent and check your socials after you reach out; editors who love your edit boost it to the clients and recommend you.
- *LinkedIn, X and Instagram: should I narrow down to one platform?* [1:02:53] → One major platform where you do the work, and a secondary one where you just repost; growing several at once burns you out.
- *Will the community have sessions breaking down edits?* [1:06:53] → Rarely: editing styles depend on niche, software and clients, so learn the basics on YouTube and the skills your niche needs; the community focuses on getting paid, clients and visibility. He'll still give feedback on your edits.
- *As a motion designer reaching out to a company, do I send to the company email or an employee?* [1:11:53] → Both: multiple points of contact, because you don't know where the opportunity will come from.
