2. **80% of editing is removing the fluff** [0:09:25]: what fluff is, why it kills retention, *"remove things more than add things."*

4. **Four questions before you edit** [0:06:05]: what kind of video, who's watching, where, what they should feel and do.
5. **One focus per frame** [0:39:53]: face, captions close to it, motion graphics on one line of focus.
6. **Sound design passes the feeling** [0:15:07]: voice, music, sound effects.
7. **Clients value speed more than quality** [0:24:47]: shortcuts, waveform cutting, deadlines.

9. **What to ask a client before you sign** [0:58:52]: the questions, and why the pain point changes the edit.
10. **"I don't care if you're good"** [1:05:18]: pain points and dreams vs *"I am a video editor, I'm very good."*
