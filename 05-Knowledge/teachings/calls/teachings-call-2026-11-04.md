## Carousel and post ideas

From his own points; suggestions, not his decisions.
1. **The 8 steps in the journey of a video editor** [0:27:28], one slide each; open on *"learning the software and learning the skill is only step one and step two."*
2. **Knowing the software isn't knowing how to edit** [0:10:47]: the gun, as a talking head.
3. **The five things that get you clients** [0:33:01]: competence at the centre, then visibility, outreach, recommendation, networking; close on *"Recommendation is the pinnacle of video editing."*
4. **Volume or quality outreach?** [0:50:09]: about 2% reply to simple emails; quality outreach gets more, and better clients.
5. **Public outreach** [0:45:41]: re-edit a big creator's video publicly; his MKBHD edit landed someone else as a client.
6. **Don't promise views yet** [0:58:41]: what views need beyond editing: pacing, the hook, strategy, scriptwriting, storytelling. *"You are more of a content partner than a video editor."*
7. **Four client pain points** [0:59:36]: just editing, more views, more customers, no time; and the pitch line that goes with each.
8. **Visibility vs outreach** [0:20:16]: visibility brings clients you didn't pick; outreach lets you pick them.

## Questions asked

- *How much outreach should I do a day, when a value-first re-edit takes time and I can only make one or two?* [0:48:51] → Volume outreach (many simple emails, about 2% reply) and quality outreach (few value-first re-edits, more replies) both work; quality outreach brings better clients, because big creators ignore simple emails.
- *Where do I find videos to practise on?* (a motion designer who edits business coach videos) [0:55:45] → On YouTube: take a section of a big video that isn't edited much, cut out only that part and re-edit it, as he did with MKBHD.
- *If my offer promises views and the video I edit doesn't bring them, does that make me less competent?* [0:56:46] → No. Views need more than editing (content psychology, pacing, hooks, strategy, scriptwriting, storytelling); don't promise views until you've delivered them for someone before.
- He also asked learners for their biggest challenge with the software [0:54:01]; one answer was choosing the right software, to be picked up in the skill classes.
