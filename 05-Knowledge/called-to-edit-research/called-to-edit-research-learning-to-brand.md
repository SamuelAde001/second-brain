## 0.3 The market (adds to [[04-Projects/called-to-edit-market-stats|Market statistics]])

- **Upwork In-Demand Skills 2026** (released 2026-02-04, 2025 earnings data, US demand): video editing is the **#2** skill in Design & Creative (graphic design #1, video production #4). "AI video generation and editing" grew **+329%**, the fastest in the category. ([Finviz reprint of the release](https://finviz.com/news/299253/upworks-in-demand-skills-2026-demand-for-top-ai-skills-more-than-doubles-as-ai-is-embedded-into-everyday-work), read.) This closes the open "Upwork" check in the market-stats note.
- **Fiverr Business Trends Index, Fall 2025** (2025-12-10): AI-video freelancer demand +66% in six months; "faceless YouTube video editor" +59%. These are **search** changes, not money spent ([Finviz reprint](https://finviz.com/news/250509/demand-for-ai-video-creators-surges-66-as-businesses-race-to-scale-marketing-campaigns), read).
- **Creators work with help:** 62% of creators had teams or outsourced help in 2025, 38% solo (CreatorIQ, via [MangoThrive](https://mangothrive.com/creator-economy-statistics/), reported). Not editor-specific.
- **Fresher creator count:** Goldman ~67m creators in 2025, 107m by 2030 (reported via MangoThrive; Goldman's page blocked). More than half of creators earn under USD 15,000 a year ([Neal Schaffer, 2026-09-28](https://nealschaffer.com/creator-economy-statistics/), reported). So most creators can't pay. Editors get paid by the minority with budgets: businesses, agencies, full-time channels. That backs your "who pays" point.
- **AI makes more editing, not less:** 57% of creators say AI output needs moderate or extensive editing before posting (Adobe Creators' Toolkit 2026, via MangoThrive, reported; Adobe is an interested party).
- **No independent data shows editor demand or rates falling.** The "AI is killing editors" lines on blogs are unsourced.
- **Data costs in Nigeria:** average 1GB went from NGN 287.50 to NGN 475 after the Jan 2025 tariff rise ([BusinessDay, 2025-03-13](https://businessday.ng/?p=949775), read). Learning from YouTube costs real naira: download once, learn offline.

**Don't say:** "X% of creators hire editors" (no such stat) · "+329% demand for editors".

## 0.4 Mindset: editing as a service

- Offer sentence that matches your "work backwards" teaching: *"I help [type of client] get [result] by [how I work, how fast]."* Positioning on a format or audience avoids price races ([Fueler, Aug 2026](https://www.fueler.io/blog/how-to-get-your-first-video-editing-client), read; practitioner opinion).
- Productized service = fixed package, price, scope, turnaround. Justin Welsh's editor example: 5 videos of up to 40s, 1 revision, monthly price; anything else is out of scope ([2023-12-23, dated](https://justinwelsh.me/essays/unlock-service-business-scale), read). His USD 2,000/month is an illustration, not a Nigerian rate.
- Hormozi's value equation (dream outcome × likelihood ÷ time delay × effort) fits "why should a client pay you". It's a sales-book idea, not research.
- Reliability is the product: the client complaints in every source are missed deadlines and going quiet.

## 1.1 What to learn first

- No creator survey exists on what skills clients want. Practitioner consensus: **pacing, clean audio, captions, then storytelling**, before effects. Matches your "foundations every paid edit needs".
- **Retention graph = feedback.** YouTube's own Help: the "Intro" figure is the share still watching after 30 seconds; dips are where people skip; YouTube's advice is to fix the first 30 seconds and move strong content earlier ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415), read). Teaching a beginner to read a client's retention graph turns taste into evidence.
- "Cut every 3-7 seconds" is one agency's rule of thumb ([C&I Studios, 2026-01-14](https://c-istudios.com/youtube-video-editing-in-2026-what-you-can-and-cant-do/), read). A business talking head isn't a MrBeast video.
- Auto-captions are better "especially for English", but Nigerian accents and Pidgin are untested. Tell them to proofread every caption.

## 1.3 Learning by recreating videos

- Videomaker's exercises: recreate a scene shot for shot, edit the same footage several ways, swap genres, cut to music ([Videomaker](https://www.videomaker.com/how-to/editing/editing-technique/6-exercises-to-become-a-better-editor/), read, likely 2023).
- Science: deliberate practice means one specific goal, quick feedback, rising difficulty. "10,000 hours" is a myth Ericsson rejected. A 2014 meta-analysis found practice explains far less of skill than claimed ([summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practice_(learning_method)), read). Nobody has studied video editing specifically.
- How to make a recreation count: one skill per recreation (hook pacing, J/L cuts, caption timing); compare side by side and list 3 differences; get feedback from someone better.
- Label recreations as practice. Re-editing someone's footage and posting it publicly invites takedowns (see Copyright below).

## 1.6 How long until good enough to charge

- No real data. One blog: "foundational skills in 3-6 months of daily practice" ([Fueler](https://www.fueler.io/blog/how-to-become-a-video-editor-with-no-experience), read), with no time to first client and no quality bar.
- A usable bar, from what clients complain about: delivers on time, from a brief, clean audio, tight pacing, no hand-holding. Your own timeline is the best evidence you have.

## 1.7 Free or low-pay work to learn

- Guides say: a **short free sample** (e.g. a 60-second re-edit), never a full project for free "without a strategy"; or a paid small test, a 14-day trial, or a three-video test package ([Fueler](https://www.fueler.io/blog/how-to-get-your-first-video-editing-client), read).
- Guardrails (synthesis, not sourced): fixed scope, a deadline, written permission to show it, the next paid step agreed before you start, a cap on unpaid pieces.
- Real risk: one editor did three unpaid shorts; a client edited an old message to claim free work was agreed, another offered GBP 2 per short, another wanted crypto ([Finzar, 2025-04-04](https://finzar.substack.com/p/how-i-got-scammed-by-shorts-editors), read).

## 2.3 Computer per niche

- **Blackmagic's floor for Resolve 21.1.1** ([readme, 2026-10-01](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/support/content/readme/62817b75bdf744718e93909ca6d58f0d), read):
  - Windows: 16GB RAM (32GB with Fusion), a GPU with 4GB VRAM or integrated graphics, NVIDIA Studio driver 581.57+.
  - Mac: macOS 15+, **Apple Silicon only**.
  - Studio's advanced AI needs 16GB VRAM; background rendering needs 32GB RAM and 12GB VRAM (DaVinci Resolve Club quoting the readmes, read).
- **By niche** (third-party guidance):
  - Short-form and talking heads: RAM and SSD matter more than GPU; proxies make a weak laptop workable.
  - Long-form: 16GB minimum, 32GB comfortable.
  - Fusion and motion graphics: 32GB.
  - 4K colour and VFX: about 8-12GB VRAM.
- **Windows 10 support ended 2025-10-14.** Before buying a used laptop, check it can run Windows 11.

## 2.4 What drives an editor's decisions

- Tie every cut to the retention graph: dips mean dead air, repeats or tangents to remove. The first 30 seconds must deliver what the title and thumbnail promised (YouTube Help above). B-roll, captions and sound earn their place by holding attention, not as decoration.
- YouTube gives no universal "good retention" number. Don't quote one.

## 3.1-3.2 Niches and what pays

No real survey; these are agency pages.

- **Long-form YouTube:** USD 300-2,000 per video ([C&I](https://c-istudios.com/youtube-video-editing-in-2026-what-you-can-and-cant-do/)), USD 200-800 for talking heads ([Videotto, 2026-04-16](https://www.videotto.com/blog/ai-video-editing-vs-freelance-editor-cost)). Talking heads sit at the low end; the plus is recurring work. Retainers about USD 1,700-3,500/month for about 4 long-form videos plus cutdowns (Ad Snipper, read).
- **Podcasts:** steady recurring demand, but AI clippers at about USD 15/month push down pure clip-cutting.
- **Ads, corporate, documentary:** pay more, harder to enter (USD 1,500-5,000+ per project, studio-level).
- **Faceless / YouTube automation:** policy risk after the July 2025 and July 2026 YouTube changes; the client can lose monetisation and stop paying.
- **The price floor Nigerians compete with:** Filipino editors USD 5-7/hr; short-form USD 20-100 a video ([HireTalent.ph, 2025-12-25](https://hiretalent.ph/), recruiter).
- **Nigerian local** (blog estimates, no method): short social video NGN 15,000-80,000; corporate 1-5 minutes NGN 80,000-300,000 ([Profolio.ng](https://www.profolio.ng/video-editor/salary-guide)); per minute NGN 5,000-15,000 entry, NGN 20,000-60,000 experienced ([ProGigFinder, 2026-04-16](https://www.progigfinder.com/blog/how-to-price-your-gig-in-nigeria-a-freelancer-pricing-guide-for-2026)). NGN 15,000-80,000 is about USD 11-60, so international clients pay several times more.
- **Not found:** real estate, wedding, UGC and gaming rates. Your own paying niches (AI/SaaS, real estate, logistics, solar) remain your call.

## 3.3 Short-form vs long-form

- **Shorts views:** since 2025-03-31 a Short counts a view on every start or replay. Earnings and YPP eligibility still use **engaged views** ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/), read). Bigger view counts didn't mean bigger pay.
- **YPP:** 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 long-form watch hours in 12 months or 10M Shorts views in 90 days; Shorts watch time doesn't count toward the 4,000 ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851), read).
- **Short-form** gets the first client faster (small jobs, many clients). **Long-form** is where retainers and raises sit. Price per finished minute; one finished minute can take 1-4 hours.
- **Instagram:** Reels up to 3 minutes (Jan 2025); Trial Reels need 1,000+ followers.
- **TikTok:** the US joint venture closed 2026-01-22 and the US feed is being retrained. The effect outside the US is unknown.

## 4.1 Profile and a new X account

- **X's algorithm** ([xai-org/x-algorithm](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm), read, updated Aug-Sep 2026):
  - A model called "Phoenix" predicts likes, replies, reposts, profile clicks, link clicks, video views, dwell time and follows, minus not-interested, mutes, blocks and reports.
  - It filters out posts older than 48 hours and caps any one author's share of a feed.
  - **There is no Premium boost and no link penalty in the published code, and the weights weren't released.** The old "reply = 13.5 likes" weights are from the 2023 code and outdated.
- **Buffer's study** (18.8M posts, Aug 2024-Aug 2025): Premium accounts get about 10x the median reach (correlation, not proof). Non-Premium link posts had a median engagement of 0% from March 2025 ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/x-premium-review/), [links](https://buffer.com/resources/links-on-x/), read). Habit: native video with captions in the post, the link in a reply.
- **X prices in Nigeria** ([The Condia, 2026-09-13](https://thecondia.com/x-monetization-nigeria/), read): Basic NGN 1,368.75, Premium NGN 3,650, Premium+ NGN 60,390 per month. Optional; not a requirement to get clients.
- **Creator payouts:** Revenue Sharing ended 2026-09-07 → Original Content Rewards: Premium (not Basic), 500 verified followers, 500k verified impressions in 90 days; Nigeria is supported; paid via Stripe ([Gigazine](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260808-x-original-content-rewards-program/), [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=193612)).
- **Profile setup:**
  - Bio: who you edit for and what, with keywords (YouTube, short-form, DaVinci Resolve). DMs open; a link to your reel; a pinned best before/after.
  - Search X for "video editor needed" ([FasterGig, 2025-08-27](https://fastergig.substack.com/p/how-to-use-x-formerly-twitter-to-land-video-editing-gigs-in-2025), read).
  - A YouTube editor who hires (Tony Santos) finds most jobs on X and wants work linked in the first message ([Creator Science, 2024](https://www.creatorscience.com/tony-santos/), read).

## 4.2 Which platform for which editor

- **X:** creators, YouTubers, indie founders post "hiring" openly. Trap: an audience of editors isn't an audience of clients. One editor's 2,000 followers were "an audience of Editors, not clients" ([Mantu, 2023](https://mantu.beehiiv.com/p/i-fucked-up-with-twitter), read).
- **LinkedIn:** agencies, SaaS, B2B founders. It has a vertical video feed, but per-video reach on personal profiles **fell 36%** year on year ([AuthoredUp, 2026](https://authoredup.com/blog/linkedin-video-posts), read). Not free reach. Good for pitching agencies white-label overflow work.
- **Instagram and TikTok:** proof of style and personal brand; local businesses. Weaker for being found by agencies.
- **Threads** (about 147M daily, July 2026) and **Bluesky** (10.4M monthly, down 27%): no evidence editors get clients there.
- Rule: one main platform where your buyer already posts.

## 4.3 Personal brand

- Post for the buyer, not for editors: before/after, the reasoning behind cuts, breakdowns of a project, short tips. Count DMs from non-editors, not likes.
- No well-sourced named case of an editor growing by building in public was found, other than small self-reported ones. **Your own story (first client and Route Rise both came from your posts) is the strongest case available.**

## 4.4 Portfolio: where

- **Contra** ([pricing](https://contra.com/pricing), read): commission-free, but on the Free plan the *client* pays USD 15 or 29 per payment (since 2026-08-10); Pro is USD 29/month or 199/year. Cleva says Nigerians can be paid on Contra and that Pro is needed for the Opportunities board (Cleva blog, promotional).
- **Own site:** Carrd custom domain USD 19/year ([carrd.com/pro](https://carrd.com/pro), read); Framer free has no custom domain, paid from USD 10/month.
- **Vimeo:** pricing in flux; check before recommending. YouTube unlisted works.
- **Google Drive** as the main portfolio: advised against (no context). Fine as a delivery link.
- **What hirers want:** a 30-60s reel matching their format plus 2-3 full projects as mini case studies (goal, role, result) ([HireTalent.ph](https://hiretalent.ph/evaluate-video-editor-portfolio), read). Many decide on a **paid test edit on their own footage** (Ad Snipper, read).

## 4.5 Portfolio with no clients

- **Legal footage:**
  - Cinestudy "EDIT THIS" (Feb 2026): free podcast and documentary footage; post your cut with their hashtags and credits ([Adobe forum](https://community.adobe.com/questions-105/anyone-want-a-free-editing-project-cinestudy-offers-several-1550931), read).
  - Pexels: free to use and modify ([licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/), read).
  - Your own phone footage: cleanest of all.
- **Spec ideas:** re-cut an ad shorter; podcast into clips; a mini-doc from public-domain footage; pro bono for a local NGO. Label them "spec" ([Fueler, 2026-08-09](https://www.fueler.io/blog/how-to-build-a-video-editing-portfolio-with-no-clients), read). Never pass spec off as client work.
- **Re-editing a famous creator's video and posting it** is a derivative work: takedown and strike risk. Copy the *style* with your own or licensed footage, or get written permission.

## 4.6-4.7 Packaging and showing your work

- Sell a unit and an outcome ("4 shorts a week from one long video"), not hours. Have a one-page package list ready before the first DM.
- Quoting far below others reads as a red flag to hirers (HireTalent, read).
- Ask the client in writing before posting their work; show NDA work only as far as approved; blur logos and unreleased content in before/afters. No source covered this; it's standard practice, and you can confirm it from your Route Rise experience.

## Platform rules on AI

- **YouTube disclosure** ([Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491), read):
  - Disclose: a real person saying or doing something they didn't, altered real events, realistic made-up scenes, AI music as the main focus.
  - **No disclosure needed:** captions, upscaling, audio repair, AI-assisted scripts, titles or thumbnails, **cloning your own voice**.
- **YouTube monetisation:** see briefing item 12 ([policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392), [TechCrunch, 2026-07-20](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/), read). Reused clips can be unmonetisable *even with the original creator's permission*.
- **Meta:** "AI info" label when detected or disclosed ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/governance/tracking-impact/labeling-ai-content/), read).
- **TikTok:** auto-labels content that carries Content Credentials (2024 announcement; 2026 wording unverified). Adobe's AI features add Content Credentials, so Premiere AI edits can trigger labels.
- **Nigeria:** no AI-labelling rule found. The Digital Economy and E-Governance bill was at third reading in July 2026; status since then unknown.

## Copyright for editors

- **Music** is what gets client videos claimed or demonetised.
  - YouTube Audio Library tracks are safe on YouTube; Creative Commons tracks need credit ([Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882), read).
  - Musicbed's Individual plan excludes client work; Business covers it ([Musicbed](https://www.musicbed.com/pricing), read).
  - Epidemic: blogs say client work needs Pro/Commercial with sublicensing (unverified).
  - Licences usually attach to the channel or account, so the cleanest setup is the **client's own subscription** or client-supplied music.
  - CapCut's library is not for client or ad work.
- **"Fair use" is American.** Nigeria's Copyright Act 2022 has open-ended **fair dealing** (purpose, nature, amount, market effect) ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/copyright/1445818/modifications-to-fair-dealing-and-other-exceptions-under-the-new-copyright-act), reported). It will not stop a YouTube Content ID claim.
- **Who owns the edit:** under the 2022 Act, the "author" of an audiovisual work is whoever made the arrangements for making it (usually the producer or client), unless a contract says otherwise ([Banwo & Ighodalo](https://www.banwo-ighodalo.com/grey-matter/implications-of-the-copyright-act-on-the-entertainment-and-advertising-industries-in-nigeria/), read). Nothing settles who owns the project file, so **put footage, final video, project files and licensed assets in writing.**
- **AI output:** the US Copyright Office (2025-01-29) says prompts alone don't earn copyright, but human arrangement and editing of AI material can ([copyright.gov](https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html), read). A fully AI clip is probably unprotected; your edit around it is protected. Not legal advice.
