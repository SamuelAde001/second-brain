# Every known way video editors get clients

**Reference only, for Samuel's own knowledge.** Samuel, 2026-10-21: *"research deeply how video editors can get clients, all the known methods... don't add it to the webinar teachings, I am just updating my knowledge"*. Nothing here goes into the teachings, the outline or the webinar project notes.

The methods already in his outline (inbound posting, outbound platforms, cold DMs and email, free sample edits, pitching, following up, Upwork/Fiverr/job boards, Loom, testimonials and referrals, networking, retainers) are only named here. Their detail is in [[05-Knowledge/called-to-edit-research/called-to-edit-research-clients-to-close|Research: sections 5-10]]. This note is about what sits **outside** that list.

**How it was done.** Four research helpers (Sonnet) on 2026-10-21: platforms and contractor networks, partnerships, inbound and offers, buyer groups (with Nigeria). Labels: **read** = the helper opened the page · **unverified** = search snippet only · **checked** = the Brand manager opened it on 2026-10-21. **Honest limit:** almost everything on this topic online is written by agencies, platforms and course sellers. There is no study of what gets video editors clients and no first-person story of a Nigerian editor landing foreign clients was found. Prices are indications, not market rates.

## 1. What the data says works (all freelancers, none video-specific)

| Source | Top channels |
|---|---|
| Fiverr Freelance Economic Impact Report 2024 (fieldwork 2023) | Referrals and reputation **67%**, social media 44%, marketplaces 29%, job sites 27% ([Freelance Informer](https://www.freelanceinformer.com/news/word-of-mouth-still-top-way-to-land-freelance-work/), read) |
| Localogy / Mono Solutions, 2022 | Word of mouth 65%, platforms 54%, social 50%, agencies 46%, job boards 44% ([Localogy](http://www.localogy.com/2022/02/benchmark-bytes-through-what-channels-do-freelancers-find-work/), read) |
| Upwork via Statista, 2020, US | 46% named a previous client as a main source ([Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/530909/sources-for-freelancers-to-find-work-us), read) |

Every source agrees: repeat clients and referrals first, social media as support. A video-editor survey: not found.

## 2. The full map

**A** = already in his outline · **New** = not in it.

### Direct outreach
- **A** Cold DM, cold email, Loom, free sample, follow-up.
- **New: the custom audit / re-edit pitch to a short list.** Re-edit 30-60 s of a named creator's public video and send *"I re-edited your video. Thoughts?"* to 50-100 picked prospects. A course seller claims 10-20% response vs ~5% for Upwork proposals and <1% for blind email; unsourced ([Fueler](https://www.fueler.io/blog/how-tharun-speaks-teaches-video-editors-to-find-their-first-clients), read). Risk: free work with no paid pilot after it.
- **New: trigger-based outreach.** Reach a company the week something happens: a funding round, a product launch, a new podcast, a founder starting to post raw video on LinkedIn ([FilmLifestyle](https://filmlifestyle.com/how-videographers-can-use-linkedin-to-land-b2b-clients/), read; advice only).
- **New: complaint prospecting.** Search X for podcasters and creators saying they have no time to clip or edit ([ClipSpeed](https://clipspeed.ai/blog/podcast-clipping-business.html), read; vendor).
- **New: the paid test first.** One solo editor DM'd 10 LinkedIn "growth marketing" titles, one replied, sold a **USD 1,150 test video**, then a **USD 4,995/month** subscription for unlimited motion videos ([Indie Hustle](https://indiehustle.beehiiv.com/p/making-4000-monthly-oneman-productized-videoediting-agency), read; the article's numbers don't fully add up).

### Marketplaces and job boards
- **A** Upwork, Fiverr, Contra, YT Jobs, Roster, Vouch, Creator Economy Jobs, LinkedIn/Indeed alerts, r/forhire.
- **New: Jobgether with the Nigeria filter.** 71 video-editor roles under that filter on the day, many tagged "Remote from Africa"; check each one ([Jobgether](https://jobgether.com/remote-jobs/nigeria/video-editor), read).
- **New: Pavago.** Employers browse profiles; Nigerian and South African editors are listed, one senior South African at USD 2,000-2,500/month ([Pavago](https://www.pavago.co/), read).
- **New: Working Not Working** (owned by Fiverr). Vetted creative network, ~10% accepted (secondhand), unvetted members can still be hired ([WNW](https://magazine.workingnotworking.com/future-member-faqs), unverified).
- **New: Mandy, Behance, Glimmer.io.** Mandy is film/TV, mostly contract and staff; Glimmer is freelance only. Nigeria eligibility not checked.
- **New: Marketing Monk talent bench.** Pass one assessment and join a bench instead of applying job by job ([Marketing Monk](https://www.marketingmonk.so/jobboard/jobs/video-editors-at-head-hunter-snznp1), unverified).
- **New: NetInfluencer Creator Economy Job Center.** Carries Superside, Scale Army, Snowball roles ([NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/jobs/all/61/), unverified).
- **New: OnlineJobs.ph.** Creators and agencies post full-time editor jobs there; built for Filipinos, so pay is anchored low. Useful as a list of who hires, not as a channel.
- **Low volume:** RemoteOK (12 editor results on the day, read).
- **Not found** for Nigeria: ProductionHUB, StaffMeUp, Production Beast, Twine, Malt, PeoplePerHour, Freelancer.com, Wellfound, We Work Remotely.

### Contractor networks, staffing and placement firms
You work for a firm that sells editing to clients. Pay is lower than direct; the upside is volume and no selling.
- **Scale Army Careers.** Places editors with US agencies; listings say open to **LATAM, Africa and Eastern Europe**, US business hours, contract. Pay seen: USD 950-1,800/month by listing ([Remotive](https://remotive.com/remote/jobs/design/video-editor-6157633), checked by search). Process: form, showcase, take-home test, video interview.
- **Superside.** Design-and-video-as-a-service, network in 60+ countries, contractor or employee by role ([NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/jobs/video-editor-at-superside/), unverified).
- **Snowball, Creatora Studio, Team Hummus.** Done-for-you YouTube editing with editor rosters; posts ask for 3-15 long videos a week following SOPs (unverified).
- **Subscription editing companies** that hire contractors: Vidpros (Philippines-run, 200+ editors, editors paid USD 500-1,800/month, [read](https://vidpros.com/hire-video-editor/)), Video Husky, Vidchops, beCreatives, Oneedit, ThisGirlEdits, The Editor's Connection.
- **Offshore headhunters:** Somewhere (Philippines, South Africa, LATAM; a case at USD 900/month, read), Kinetic Staffing. Nigeria not named.
- **US creative staffing agencies** (Aquent, Creative Circle / Vitamin T): tied to US work authorisation. Skip unless a post says international.

### Subcontract and white-label
- **New: back-end editor for agencies and production houses.** You edit under their brand. A vendor guide says resellers mark up 30-50% on standard work and 100-300% on rush or complex work ([Vidpros](https://vidpros.com/how-to-start-a-video-editing-agency/), read; vendor). What agencies pay subcontractors: not found.
- **New: wedding white-label.** Wedding filmmakers outsource editing; houses like Bride&Groom.video, S&D Media, EditWeddings, CutProMedia are both the competition and a subcontract route. A wedding takes 40-60 edit hours (unverified). Fotober lists from USD 35 (teaser) to 80+ (documentary edit) per film, and a USD 750 example for 600 GB of 4K ([Fotober](https://fotober.com/wedding-video-editing-pricing), read). Buyers test with a trial on their own footage and want peak-season cover.
- **New: podcast production houses.** Video is the add-on they need an editor for ([Podglomerate](https://podglomerate.com/?p=6011), read). One hired full-time at USD 1,000/month through a headhunter (Somewhere, read).
- **A, extended:** Samuel's outline already covers agency overflow.

### The creator economy's own routes
- **YT Jobs as a hiring hub.** MrBeast and Mark Rober post there; editors are the most wanted role; its recruitment service is for roles of USD 50k+ a year ([NetInfluencer, 2025-10-07](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-talent-gap-spurs-rise-of-yt-jobs-as-hiring-hub-for-top-creators/), read).
- **New: editing contests.** Airrack ran one through Roster in May 2025: USD 2,000 prize and a shot at a role up to USD 100k, relocation to Los Angeles ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/05/21/airrack-youtube-video-editing-competition-roster/), read).
- **New: Discord servers run by creators' staff.** One editor posted his reel in a server of people who work for YouTubers and got connected to a 2.3M channel; another used a MrBeast job to get introduced to Jacksepticeye ([Passionfruit](https://passionfru.it/video-editors-behind-youtubers-2253/), read; undated). Job Discords (Editing Hunt ~4.9k, Edit Big ~12k), r/HireAnEditor, r/CreatorServices exist but are unvetted and scam-heavy (unverified).
- **New: clipping, paid per view.** Whop Content Rewards: brand funds a budget and sets a rate per 1,000 views. 50 live campaigns on 2026-09-28: **median USD 1.00 per 1,000 views**, range 0.05-3.00; Whop's 10% fee is on top of the brand's payout ([Foundry](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/what-is-clipping-1-dollar-per-1000-views-50-campaigns), checked). Vyro (MrBeast's) USD 1.50-2.00 per 1,000 (unverified). ClippaPay launched 2026-03-23 for African creators, no rates ([Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/brandpress/clippapay-launches-africas-first-creator-powered-content-creation-clipping-distribution-ugc-affiliate-platform/), read). Risks: rejections after views land, emptied budgets, bot flags at payout. Poor pay for a pro editor; the business is running a clipping team, not clipping.
- **UGC platforms:** Billo is US/Canada/UK/Australia only. Not a route.

### Referral partners and editor overflow
- **A** Referrals and networking with editors.
- **New: paid referral partners.** Videographers, thumbnail designers, scriptwriters, web designers, social media managers whose clients need editing. Finder's fee norm quoted at 5-10%, some 15-25%; most freelancers swap work instead of paying ([DVinfo, 2009](https://dvinfo.net/forum/taking-care-business/143968-shooting-referral-commission.html), read; old). A studio programme pays 10% after the first project (unverified). Put the deal in writing.
- **New: being the second editor.** Senior editors and small editing agencies pass overflow to someone they trust; creators ask their editor for other editors (unverified).
- **New: bundling.** Channels want one person for editing plus thumbnails; partner with a thumbnail designer and sell both (unverified).

### Inbound
- **A** Before/after and process posts, portfolio, personal brand.
- **New: keyword call to action.** Faceless before/after Reels captioned *"Comment 'Fix' and I'll re-edit your video."* One editor reports a USD 1,600 first client in 2023 this way and nothing from Upwork or Fiverr ([Victor Tosky](https://victortosky.substack.com/p/the-blueprint-to-landing-your-first), read; one case).
- **New: teaching-led inbound.** Tutorial channels, toolkits and templates (Premiere Gal: 500k+ subscribers, a paid toolkit, a team on three continents, [Adobe](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2024/06/28/professional-youtube-video-editing-tips-from-team-behind-premiere-gal), read). Evidence that it turns into client work: not found. Called to Edit is his version of this.
- **New: case studies with numbers.** Baseline, result, the editing change that did it (e.g. retention 42% to 65% after re-pacing; unverified). Outlier research (a video's views against the channel's norm) as a pitch opener; advice from tool sellers.
- **Weak for him:** local SEO and Google Business Profile (his buyers are abroad), paid ads (no editor evidence; studios report both wins and nothing).

### Offers that pull clients
- **New: productized subscription.** Published prices per month: Vidchops from USD 495, The Editor's Connection 539 / 915 / 1,665, Oneedit 599 vertical / 989 horizontal (up to 12 videos), ThisGirlEdits 749 / 1,333, beCreatives from 899, Vidpros 1,000 (2 h a day) or 4,000 full time with a USD 100 trial week ([Dreamgrow](https://www.dreamgrow.com/best-unlimited-video-editing-services/), [Vidpros](https://vidpros.com/pricing/), read). "Unlimited" is always capped by active requests, hours or length.
- **New: podcast-to-clips retainer.** Suggested tiers USD 300-500 for 20 clips up to 1,500-2,500 for 60+; claimed retention 6-12 months; entry is clipping one episode free ([ClipSpeed](https://clipspeed.ai/blog/podcast-clipping-business.html), read; vendor).
- **New: ad-creative volume retainer.** DTC brands and ad agencies want 15-25+ Meta and TikTok variants a month (one body, swapped hooks). USD 30-150 per clip, USD 75 per concept of 1 ad + 3 hooks; postings start with a paid trial. The hiring contact is the agency's creative strategist (unverified).
- **A** Retainers, packages, add-ons.

### Growing inside clients
- **A** One-off to recurring.
- **New: revenue share on top of a base fee.** One editor gets base plus 10% of the revenue of the videos he edits and calls 10% "the standard"; Ludwig pays his editors a percentage and says they earn "hundreds of thousands" ([Passionfruit](https://passionfru.it/video-editors-behind-youtubers-2253/), [Dexerto, 2026-07-21](https://www.dexerto.com/youtube/ludwig-explains-why-he-pays-his-youtube-editors-hundreds-of-thousands-3389582/), read). German streamers cut editors' shares from 50% to 30-40% once channels grew, or dropped them ([Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/twitch-streamer-does-not-understand-why-he-should-give-so-much-of-his-money-to-an-employee,1215090/), read). Safe shape: written contract, fixed base, capped bonus, minimum term.
- **New: account expansion.** More channels, more formats, a second brand from the same client; ask for introductions after a win. Data on how much editor income is repeat: not found.

### Institutions and tenders (Abuja-specific)
- **New: UN and INGO framework agreements.** UNICEF Nigeria in Abuja issued a two-year videography and photography Long-Term Agreement in 2026 with editing and SRT subtitling in scope; closed 2026-04-06; bidders had to be companies or NGOs registered on UNGM ([UNGM notice 294179](https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/294179), read). Also a UNDP Nigeria RFP and a Plan International tender (unverified). Route: a registered company on UNGM, watch UNGM and ReliefWeb, or subcontract to whoever wins the agreement.

### Events
- VidCon: networking sessions, no evidence of editors hired there; US travel. BNI-style chapters: one seat per trade, local clients only. CANEX WKND, Lagos, 2026-11-05 to 08: African creative-economy deal-making, nothing editor-specific (unverified). Low value for a USD remote editor.

## 4. Getting paid: one change that affects him

**Upwork started charging USD 2.99 per Direct to U.S. Bank withdrawal on 2026-09-01** for freelancers whose tax address is outside the US; it was the only free route. The fee follows the tax address on the profile, not the receiving account ([Cleva blog](https://www.getcleva.com/blog/upworks-new-2-99-withdrawal-fee-dont-let-it-cost-you-twice), checked; Cleva sells the alternative). Whop payouts to Nigeria: not confirmed.

## 5. The Brand manager's read (a suggestion, not a decision)

The five that look least used by Nigerian editors and fit a remote USD pro:
1. **Productized offer with a paid test** (B2B and podcasts): the one documented case went from a cold DM to USD 4,995/month.
2. **White-label for wedding houses, podcast houses and agencies:** dollar pay, no client-facing work, they already hire remotely.
3. **Paid referral partners and the thumbnail bundle:** costs nothing, matches the survey finding that referrals win.
4. **UN and INGO framework agreements in Abuja:** Abuja is the base and the 2026 UNICEF one included editing; needs a registered company.
5. **Base fee plus capped revenue share** on a creator client who already trusts him: only with a contract and a floor.

Placement firms (Scale Army, Vidpros) pay USD 500-1,800 a month full time: a floor for a beginner, below his level.
