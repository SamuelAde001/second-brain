**How it was done.** Seven research helpers (Sonnet), one per block of sections, then the Brand manager checked the most surprising claims at source. Every fact carries a link and a label: **read** (the page was opened), **reported** (a news or blog piece quoting it), **unverified** (search summary only). **Checked** = the Brand manager opened the source itself on 2026-10-19. The helpers ran out of search budget part-way, so some gaps remain; they're named in each note.

**Honest limit:** nearly all rate, pricing and "how to get clients" material online is written by agencies, platforms and course sellers. There is no proper survey of editor rates, no data on Nigerian editors, and no study of what gets editors clients. **Your own story is better evidence than anything found.** Use the numbers here as context, never as "the market rate".

## The 15 things most worth knowing

1. **Your audience paid.** 29 paid seats, NGN 145,000, by 2026-10-16 ([[03-Areas/personal-brand/personal-brand-log|log]]). Webinar-sales benchmarks for *free, cold* audiences say 2-5% of attendees buy; paid, warm audiences sit higher (vendor figures 10-15%). Details in 10.2.
2. **DaVinci Resolve 21.1 (2026-09-08) officially works with Claude, Claude Code and ChatGPT Codex.** Blackmagic's own press release: ask your AI assistant to organise media, batch render, or *"create highlight edits from long form video"*. **Checked.** Your machine runs Studio 21.1, so you can show it live. Your "I use AI to get your videos out faster" pitch now matches Blackmagic's own marketing. Advanced scripting needs Studio (reported).
3. **Resolve 21's headline AI tools are Studio-only (USD 295, one-time).** Free Resolve lacks text-based editing, Magic Mask and AI noise reduction (Blackmagic product page, read). A beginner watching AI tutorials on free Resolve will think their copy is broken. Say it.
4. **Adobe Premiere went free on Android on 2026-09-22.** No watermark, 4K export, but **Android 13+ and 5GB+ RAM**, which rules out many budget phones in Nigeria. Two independent reports (9to5Google, IT Brief). Premiere Rush retired 2026-09-30 (unverified).
5. **CapCut Pro roughly doubled: USD 19.99/month** (was about USD 78/year). In Nigeria CapCut's own pricing page showed **NGN 17,900/month** (read). Its music and many templates are **not cleared for client work**, and one export that mixes personal-only and commercial assets becomes personal-only (CapCut Materials Licence, updated 2026-01-22, read).
6. **OpenAI announced on 2026-03-24 that it is shutting down Sora** (app and API). **Checked** (Engadget). Don't teach it.
7. **The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 has applied since 2026-01-01.** Residents are taxed on worldwide income. The first NGN 800,000 of taxable income is at 0%, then 15% and up. **Annual returns are due even when no tax is owed.** Foreign freelance *fees* are not confirmed as exempt; assume editing income is taxable. Not tax advice; tell them to see an accountant once they earn.
8. **Getting paid has changed.**
   - PayPal works again in Nigeria through Paga (receive since 2026-01-27; two sources). Fiverr reportedly rejected a Nigerian PayPal in June 2026.
   - Upwork now charges **USD 2.99 per "Direct to U.S. Bank" withdrawal** for freelancers with a non-US tax address, from 2026-09-01. That was the free route through Cleva, Raenest or Grey USD accounts. Several outlets confirm it; Upwork's own page wasn't opened.
   - **Gigbanc shut down** (withdraw by 2026-07-31) and Chimoney closed. Don't park money in small apps.
   - **Wise for Nigerian residents is unconfirmed**; sources conflict. Don't say "use Wise".
9. **Naira cards are usable for dollar tools again.** GTBank USD 40,000/quarter, FirstBank USD 10,000/quarter, Zenith USD 50,000/year (Nairametrics, 2026-09-07, **checked**). The old "USD 100 a month" cap is gone.
10. **The naira is about NGN 1,332/USD official, NGN 1,365-1,375 parallel** (2026-10-13/14). In March 2026 Cleva's examples used NGN 1,570. A dollar price is worth less in naira than it was in spring.
11. **Blackmagic's minimum for Resolve 21 on Windows is 16GB RAM** (32GB for Fusion), and **Apple Silicon only on Mac**. Intel Macs are dropped (21.1.1 readme, 2026-10-01, read). An 8GB UK-used laptop is not a starting point. RAM and SSD prices are rising in 2026 (AI data-centre demand; Gartner/TrendForce, unverified), so laptops are getting dearer.
12. **YouTube's "inauthentic content" rules are a client risk.**
    - 2025-07-15: YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" (read).
    - 2026-07-16 clarification (TechCrunch, read): three kinds of content can't be monetised: templated or mass-produced content; content made to upset or manipulate for views; and AI personas talking about finance, health, legal or political topics.
    - Faceless and template channels can lose income, and then can't pay their editor. Ask what a client's channel does.
13. **X changed.**
    - Creator Revenue Sharing ended 2026-09-07. The replacement, "Original Content Rewards", needs Premium (not Basic), 500 verified followers and 500,000 verified impressions in 90 days. Several outlets confirm it.
    - "X buries links by 80%" and "Premium gives 10x reach" are not in X's published algorithm code. The 10x is a correlation from Buffer's 2025 study.
    - Free accounts are capped at 500 DMs a day (May 2026, read).
14. **Free tiers shrank.**
    - New Google accounts get **5GB** from 2026-03-09, plus 10GB with a verified phone (Google Help, **checked**).
    - WeTransfer free is 3GB or 10 transfers per 30 days (read).
    - Loom free is 25 videos of up to 5 minutes (read).
    - Contra is commission-free, but clients on its Free plan pay USD 15-29 per payment since 2026-08-10 (read).
15. **Upwork ranks video editing #2** in its Design & Creative category (2025 data, released 2026-02). The famous **+329%** is for *AI video generation and editing*, not editing in general.
